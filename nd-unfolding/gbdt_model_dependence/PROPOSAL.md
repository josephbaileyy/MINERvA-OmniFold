## Concrete first request

Once the scheduling conditions above are satisfied, consider authorizing **one development panel capped at 70 CPU node-hours, zero GPUs, at most two CPU nodes concurrently and 10 GiB of new saved products**, after a committed design and required independent review. No existing campaign jobs or allocations are reused, stopped or changed. This is a proposal, not an authorization recorded by this document.

**Settings (development-informed):**

1. R at K=5, 100 trees/8 leaves on the three OmniFold estimators.
2. R at K=20, same capacity. This spans the earlier affordable-iteration range; W3 has already plateaued while some other truths improve.
3. R at K=5, 400 trees/31 leaves on those estimators. This tests the separate capacity intervention at the affordable historical iteration. All settings retain the **same** 400/31 background-refinement classifier, regression treatment, learning rate, dtype, thread count and background method.

There is no scan over alternatives and no tuning after looking at panel results. Store common per-iteration fine-grid outputs when available so the K=5 and K=20 paths share their initial iterations; the cost below conservatively prices them separately. Verify checkpoint equivalence before using that saving.

**Truths:** nominal, the **flux-repaired W3-like joint muon/hadronic ratio** already constructed for the s5p P1r alternative, and the **flux-repaired conditional q3 alternative** P2r/D5. Resolve these from the committed production design, hash them, and freeze the actual numerical definitions, amplitude 1, support, positive weights and normalization before running. They are development truths, not validation. P2r is not the old unanchored q3 stress: its choice asks whether the trend transfers to a corrected physical departure. Verify EW preservation numerically, rather than inheriting a label. If these exact artifacts are unavailable, stop at the design stage; do not silently substitute an older ratio.

**Pairing:** 160 independent pseudo seeds per truth, with the same pseudo-data, signal/background source and template splits and event Poisson draws in all three settings. A fixed detector response and no physical systematic draw in this first panel keep the measured quantity explicit. Preserve split keys, truth arrays, estimator/jitter seeds, all settings, source hashes, expected rates, detector-fold residuals and wall times. Use the existing guarded execution and a new namespace. No sample is excluded for a poor scientific result; failed/incomplete tasks receive a predeclared missing-data disposition.

**Seed factor:** on ten preselected pseudo seeds per truth, add two further estimator seeds for each setting (180 extra unfolds). Treat this as a paired diagnostic at fixed events. Do not mix it into the 160-repeat spread or claim it estimates a wide seed distribution precisely. If the deterministic seeds remain inert, record the null result; if not, design a crossed event/estimator decomposition before any unconditional claim. Coordinate rounding stays fixed in this first panel; its effect was separately measured for R5 and has not automatically transferred to K20/capacity.

**Estimands and precision:** J-cell integrals are primary, with the same 109-cell support and full-domain rate; H2 and EW are predeclared reaggregations, never a selection of favorable cells. Record each cell's ensemble mean residual, SD, covariance across settings, MSE, interval-free detector-fold agreement and cost. Because source-split truth varies, report residual variability explicitly; add fixed truth/source conditioning if the intended claim is an unconditional fixed-truth variance.

Use six planned contrasts (two settings versus R5 × three truths) for a predeclared aggregate functional, and preserve per-cell estimates with uncertainty. The aggregate should be fixed before compute, for example the equally weighted mean squared relative residual over the supported J cells; it cannot replace the separate bias/variance components or hide failed physical regions. For orientation, n=160 gives a mean SE of 0.079 repeat SD and normal-model SD relative SE 5.6%. For a scalar paired contrast, detecting a standardized mean change of 0.30 paired SD with 80% power and two-sided Bonferroni α=0.05/6 needs about 135 pairs under a normal approximation. This calculation **does not guarantee power for an MSE or variance contrast**: those require fourth moments and paired covariance not available here. Report their achieved precision; do not promise that n=160 proves a 20% variance change. Prespecify a smallest scientifically relevant effect (e.g. 20% reduction in a declared residual-bias summary, against its paired uncertainty) at design review and state its observed-precision limitation. Fixed n, no optional stopping or automatic extension.

**Decision and terminal outcomes:** a bias decrease with variance increase on common functionals can support a local tradeoff only with its uncertainty, truth and setting scope stated. Improvement in both is a local improvement, not a tradeoff; no change is a plateau within measured precision; worsening is retained; mixed or wide intervals are inconclusive. Any of these terminates the panel. No outcome adopts an estimator, covariance, real-data central value or total interval, changes historical grades, or triggers a fresh ensemble without a separate decision. Fresh confirmation is required before promoting a relationship chosen using this panel.

Its baseline five-iteration prediction 292 s is close to the receipt median 293.973 s over 423 float32 pseudo products. Capacity scaling comes from noise-free probes, not a matched noisy panel; it is explicitly an extrapolation. The 32 s term includes historical build/refinement overhead; allow new input preparation separately.
