`HANDOFF-20261005-pet-gbdt-existing-outputs.md` (repository root of this branch). Owner: one Opus 5.5 session; at

## 1. Decision this serves

Whether and how to complete the unfolding with PET. This needs to know what PET buys over the strongest GBDT
that can be compared on the same events with the same scoring, and what further evidence would change the next spend.
It does not rank the two PET finalists (the closed study could not; nothing here re-opens that).

## 3. The GBDT comparator (fixed; nothing is tuned)

The matched study's scalar OmniFold, unchanged (`scalar_estimators.run_omnifold`):

- **Classifier** `HGBRatio`, sklearn HistGradientBoosting, frozen config `h1` (learning rate 0.1, ≤ 400 trees,
  31 leaves, ≥ 200 per leaf, early stopping on an external validation split). This was the tuned winner for both
  miss rules on the DEV selection F0 (`frozen_config.json`).
- **Miss rule** efficiency-corrected, as both PET finalists use.
- **Reco inputs** p_T, p‖, reco E_avail, reco q3, stored-cluster ΣE and count. These are the detector summaries the PET
  hybrids receive at step 1, plus the muon kinematics.
- **Truth inputs** `truth4_species`: true E_avail, p_T, p‖, q3 and the truncated-cloud counts of p, n, π±, π⁰ and other.
  This is the richest documented scalar set. It carries the same truncated-cloud species information that PET's
  categorical-PDG truth cloud encodes. These counts are over the stored 12-token truth cloud, not full final-state
  multiplicities. They also define E4's proton classes, so the GBDT receives the topology variable as an input.
- **Iterations** 10. **Operating points:**
  - **primary k = 7.** This is `kF0` for this exact variant: the k maximizing the mean R on F0 over seeds 1–3, chosen
    in the matched study on DEV data before any FB use.
  - **secondary k = 3** (the matched study's declared primary) and **k = 10**.
  - The full k = 1–10 trajectory is reported but is not used to pick a winner.
- **Seed** one estimator seed per draw, `1 + FB index`, the same for every case of a draw. A single-run estimator, like
  each PET look-1 run.
- **What it is not:** it is not the production LightGBM scalar-5D estimator. That estimator uses 5D, real data and
  backgrounds, and is incompatible here. It is not a six-member or bootstrap package. It has no interval procedure.

**Controls passed before freezing** (results committed with this plan):

1. `control_pet_rescore.json`: the local scorer and `row_features.npz` copy re-score `S4F-H2S1T24K5-FB0` k = 5 from its
   own iteration file and reproduce all 952 committed values (max |diff| 1.1e-16).
2. `control_matched_repro.json`: `pgc_fb.build_problem` on the predecessor F0 run arrays gives estimator inputs
   identical to the matched study's extracted selection. A refit of the committed task (F0, dev, efficiency-corrected,
   `truth4_species`, h1, seed 1) reproduces R at every k = 1–10 with |diff| = 0 (sklearn 1.8.0, numpy 1.26.4, Python
   3.12.2, the matched study's versions).

## 4. Tasks, order and budget

`tasks-20261005.json` (from `pgc_plan.py`) lists 352 tasks. They are the same 352 look-1 (stage × draw × case) units
each PET finalist has. Every design's committed score of a unit names the same `replicate_arrays.npz` digest; the
generator refuses otherwise. Execution order:

- **Tier 1, the designated endpoints (200 tasks).** Run in blocks so that an early stop leaves every endpoint
  partially covered:
  - E0 FB0–29, E4 FB0–19, E3 FB0–29, E5 FB0–19;
  - then E0 FB30–59, E4 FB20–39, E3 FB30–59, E5 FB20–39.

  The cases are:
  - E0/E1/E2: S4F development tilt.
  - E3: S4F D1 −0.35.
  - E4: S4S D4c p up, on E_avail × proton class.
  - E5: S4S D3 +0.35, on E_avail × q3.
- **Tier 2, robustness rules (40 tasks).** S4S FB0–7 of null, D4d n down, D1 +0.35, R1 ×1.05 + D1 and R1 ×0.95 + D1.
  These feed B2, B3 and B4.
- **Tier 3, the rest of the library (112 tasks).** S4S FB0–7 of 14 cases, case by case, in the `pgc_plan.py` order.
  These feed B1 and E6.

**CPU ceiling (goal): 8 CPU-core-hours of new GBDT fitting, including the control refit and retries; at most four
fitting threads; no tuning; no cluster allocation.**
- Execution is local: 2 workers × 2 OpenMP threads, with BLAS single-threaded.
- Each task is charged wall seconds × 2 / 3600. This counts reserved cores, an upper bound on use; the process CPU
  time is recorded beside it. Load, scoring and failed tasks are all charged, in `cpu_ledger.jsonl`.
- A task starts only if charged + (in flight + 1) × 1.25 × (largest charge so far) ≤ **7.5** core-h.
- When the stop fires, the analysis uses what completed. A case or rule is reported only if all its draws completed.
  Unfinished tiers are reported as such and costed.

Data are copied from Perlmutter read-only (`pgc_fetch.py`):
- each run's `replicate_arrays.npz`, verified against the committed digest;
- the inventory's signal-MC `reco_scalars` member, fetched by byte range and checked by CRC and sha256;
- the study's `row_features.npz`, checked by sha256.

## 5. Metrics and statistics (frozen)

All scores use the final-design scorer (`score_design.DesignScorer.block`), unchanged:
- **target** = the replicate's own pseudodata truth, at w_truth × distortion;
- **prior** = the prior at w_truth;
- **unfolded** = the prior at w_truth × push;
- each normalized to unit area;
- R = 1 − L1(unfolded − target) / L1(prior − target).

R is not computed when the injected L1 is below its floor. The PET values are the committed look-1 scores at each
finalist's k.

1. **Endpoint values per method:** mean, sd and n over draws for:
   - E0 (aggregate E_avail R, S4F dev);
   - E1 (E_avail R in the low-acceptance, moderate and good regions);
   - E3;
   - E4 (E_avail × proton R);
   - E5 (E_avail × q3 R);
   - B2: the mean of (E_avail residual L1 − injected L1) over D4d n down and over null;
   - B3: the mean spurious L1 over null, for E_avail and for E_avail × proton;
   - B4: |mean E8|, with E8 = R_E0(R1 + D1) − R_E0(D1) paired by draw;
   - B1: the count of units with R < 0 on the natural histogram, for completed cases;
   - an N1 analogue: median ESS/n and p99.9 of w_truth × push over E0 draws, and the maximum over all runs.
2. **Paired differences (the primary comparison).** Per draw, Δ = R_PET − R_GBDT on the same events, target and
   histogram, for each finalist against the GBDT at k = 7, and secondarily at k = 3 and k = 10. Reported for each:
   - n, mean, sd and two-sided 95 % t interval;
   - the number of draws with Δ > 0;
   - the study's bank-effect bound, √0.227 × sd(Δ), reported beside it.

   **Unit:** the FB draw. Draws are independent random subsets *given the frozen banks*; pseudodata draws overlap by
   chance, sampling fraction 0.227. Inference is conditional on the banks, as in the study, and bins are never
   replicates.
   - The intervals are **exploratory and unadjusted** (5 designated endpoints × 2 finalists × 3 k). They support
     statements of the form "on these banks PET exceeds this GBDT by X ± Y". They support no selection rule.
   - The E3/E0 contrasts are also given for the PET finalists against each other, only to show what the frozen study
     already reported; no ranking is drawn.
3. **Per-bin accuracy** for E0 (7 bins), E4 (28) and E5 (28):
   - the mean signed residual (unfolded − target) per bin with its standard error over draws;
   - the RMS residual and the MSE = mean² + variance of the residual over draws;
   - the L1 of the mean residual (the bias part) beside the mean per-draw L1 (bias + spread).

   **The target moves with the draw.** The residual spread is the spread of (estimate − own target). It is not the
   unconditional variance of the estimator. Injections near zero (null, D4d) are reported as residual L1, never as
   ratios.
4. **Variability.** Each FB value mixes event and estimator-seed variation, with one seed per draw for each method;
   they cannot be separated on FB. Seed-only variation comes from existing operands only, with no new fits:
   - **GBDT:** 3 seeds × 2 DEV draws per case in the matched study.
   - **PET:** the closed study's N2 (S3P seed runs).

   These are reported side by side as comparable, not paired.
5. **Exact-paired DEV check, with no new fits.**
   - **Data:** the matched study's GBDT rows (3 seeds per draw) against PET dev3N runs of both finalists on the same
     predecessor selections (F0/F1 dev; T0/T1 D1 −0.35, D4c p up, D4d n up). The `replicate_arrays.npz` digests are
     identical.
   - **Scoring:** PET pushes are re-scored with the matched study's scorer (`scalar_scoring.Scorer`), so both methods
     use one definition (E_avail R; E_avail × proton and × neutron R against pseudodata truth).
   - **Status:** two draws per case, so the check is descriptive.
6. **Uncertainty and coverage.** Only the PET H2S1T24 six-member procedure has constructed intervals with measured
   coverage. The GBDT has none here, and L128S1T24's coverage was never measured. No coverage or width comparison is
   made, and point-estimator outputs are not turned into an uncertainty procedure.
7. **Old PET.** The historical CTL (0.306 on E0) and C anchors are old PET, not GBDT. They appear only as labelled
   context.

## 6. What the result can and cannot support

**Can:**
- On these banks, signal-only simulation, response and sample size, the stated paired differences in point accuracy
  between each PET finalist and this GBDT on the designated endpoints and the completed library cases;
- where the GBDT fails a frozen-rule threshold that PET passes, or the reverse. These are *report-only*
  descriptions; the frozen rules do not apply to the GBDT.

**Cannot:**
- a ranking of the PET finalists;
- the GBDT or PET being best in general;
- any uncertainty, coverage or total-uncertainty claim;
- any real-data, background or systematic statement;
- adoption of anything.

A GBDT that was tuned, ensembled or given other inputs could do differently.
