## 3. The comparator and its controls

- **Classifier:** HistGradientBoosting `h1`: learning rate 0.1, ≤ 400 trees, 31 leaves, ≥ 200 per leaf, early
  stopping. In the matched study this was the tuned winner for both miss rules on DEV F0.
- **Miss rule:** efficiency-corrected, as both finalists use.
- **Reco inputs:** p_T, p‖, reco E_avail, reco q3, stored-cluster ΣE and count.
- **Truth inputs:** E_avail, p_T, p‖, q3, and the **truncated-cloud** counts of p, n, π±, π⁰ and other.
  - These counts are over the stored 12-token cloud, not full multiplicities.
  - They are also the class variables of E4 and E6.
- **Operating points:** primary k = 7. This is the matched study's `kF0` for this variant, chosen on DEV before any FB
  use. Secondary operating points are k = 3 and k = 10.
- **Seed:** one per draw, 1 + FB index.
- **Response cases:**
  - R1 (energy scale): handled as on the PET path.
  - R2 (muon momentum scale): handled by the PET path's own `design_inputs.r2_scalars`, which is blob-pinned. The
    first R2 attempt was refused by a guard (§7.1).

This is not a weak comparator. The documented alternatives are carry-misses (much worse on the tilt), `truth4` without
species (no topology), and IBU (excellent on the tilt but moving away on hadron cases). It is also not the production
LightGBM estimator, not tuned on FB, not an ensemble and not an uncertainty procedure.

## 8. Recommendation: one next action (not launched; owner decision and new authorization required)

**P0: data-scale readiness and embedded closure, for both finalists.**

1. **Real-data nominals.** Two unfoldings, H2S1T24 K5 and L128S1T24 K4.
   - **MC prior:** 2 M rows.
   - **Data side:** all 4,116,128 data rows plus the 564,591 negative-weight background rows.
   - **Diagnostic only:** not scored, not compared with any published result, and supporting no physics claim.
2. **Data-scale closures.** For each finalist: development tilt, null and **D5 NuWro**, × 2 draws, which is 12
   unfoldings.
   - **Pseudodata:** 4.12 M DEV signal rows.
   - **Prior:** a disjoint 2 M-row DEV prior; no backgrounds.
   - **Scoring:** the final-design scorer.
   - **Order:** dev, then null, then NuWro.
3. **Matched GBDT.** This report's recipe, on the same 6 closure draws, on CPU, ≤ 6 core-h.

**It measures:**
- the A100-h per data-scale unfolding, which removes the about 4× uncertainty in every later route;
- whether the jobs fit in memory at two per GPU;
- the truth-weight tails;
- each finalist's R and per-bin bias against the GBDT at 7× the pseudodata size, including the generator-model case
  where PET is weak.

**Request:**
- **GPU:** 14 unfoldings × about 7 A100-h ≈ 98 A100-h (49–196). The cap is **125 A100-h ≈ 31 GPU node-hours**, qos
  shared, two per GPU; it includes the verification and retry reserve. Closures run in the declared order until the
  cap.
- **CPU:** ≤ 2 CPU node-hours for input builds and extraction, plus ≤ 6 core-h of GBDT.
- **Elapsed:** about 1–2 days.

  GBDT on the same draws.
- **(d) Generator-model flag.** Flag as a production blocker, to be resolved before P1, if both finalists stay worse
  than the GBDT on NuWro by more than 0.10.

**It can authorize nothing beyond a decision on P1.**

## 9. Limits

- **Population:** signal-only simulation, the inventory response, generator histogram reweightings and the frozen
  banks.
- **Sample:** exploratory reuse of already-inspected FB rows, at study scale (600 k pseudodata events).
- **Statistics:** intervals are unadjusted, and the library cases have only 8 draws each.
- **Seeds:** there is one seed per draw for every method, so seed and event variation are not separated on FB.
- **Comparator:** a tuned, ensembled or differently informed GBDT could do differently, for example one given the reco
  cloud. This one was neither tuned nor weakened. The topology comparison depends on its k.
- **R2 inputs:** for R2 cases the GBDT's scaled muon inputs are rebuilt with the PET path's function. They cannot be
  byte-compared with the PET run's inputs, which are not stored.
- **Not compared at all:** uncertainty, coverage and real-data behaviour.
- **Costs:** data-scale costs are scaled, not measured. That is the gap P0 closes.
