## 0. Answer in brief

   final-bank units of the two finalists, with the same events, weights, distortions, targets and scorer as PET.
   - **Recipe:** HistGradientBoosting `h1`, efficiency-corrected; the detector summaries of the PET hybrids plus muon
     kinematics; truth including truncated-cloud species counts; the DEV-chosen k = 7.
   - **Cost:** **5.31 core-hours charged, 4.42 CPU core-hours**, on local CPU; nothing was tuned. The goal's ceiling
     was 8 core-hours.
2. **PET's point estimate wins on the E_avail tilt endpoints.** Both finalists reproduce the development and opposite
   E_avail tilts better than this GBDT, in every acceptance region.
   - Paired over 60 draws, E0 is **+0.102 [0.084, 0.119]** for H2 and **+0.070 [0.051, 0.089]** for L128; E3 is
     +0.076 and +0.070.

   - The GBDT's error is 96 % systematic under-correction.
   - PET also leads on most hadron-species reweightings: π⁰ +0.12, neutron up +0.055, proton down +0.07, π± +0.05
     (H2). On the muon-scale case (+0.135) the lead is mostly the underlying tilt gain. The response-specific E8
     contrast is +0.016 [−0.005, 0.037] (H2) and +0.028 [0.013, 0.043] (L128).
   - **This lead is over this HGB OmniFold, not over scalar unfolding in general.** On the same DEV development units,
     the matched study's binned IBU (efficiency-corrected, k = 3) reaches 0.945 / 0.902 and AUSSIE λ = 0 reaches
     0.870 / 0.919, against H2's 0.909 / 0.900. Both collapse on hadron-content or generator cases (§5).
3. **Elsewhere PET does not win.**
   - On the designated proton topology, E4, H2 leads by +0.055 [0.035, 0.075] and L128 is level. A GBDT iterated to
     k = 10 is level with H2 (+0.009 [−0.012, 0.030]).

   - **The GBDT is markedly better on all three generator-model reweightings:** NuWro, NuWro′ and GiBUU, by 0.12–0.25

## 3. The comparator and its controls

**The comparator** is `final_design/scalar/scalar_estimators.run_omnifold`, unchanged.
- **Classifier:** HistGradientBoosting `h1`: learning rate 0.1, ≤ 400 trees, 31 leaves, ≥ 200 per leaf, early
  stopping. In the matched study this was the tuned winner for both miss rules on DEV F0.
- **Miss rule:** efficiency-corrected, as both finalists use.
- **Reco inputs:** p_T, p‖, reco E_avail, reco q3, stored-cluster ΣE and count.
- **Truth inputs:** E_avail, p_T, p‖, q3, and the **truncated-cloud** counts of p, n, π±, π⁰ and other.
  - These counts are over the stored 12-token cloud, not full multiplicities.
  - They are also the class variables of E4 and E6.
- **Operating points:** primary k = 7. This is the matched study's `kF0` for this variant, chosen on DEV before any FB
  use. Secondary operating points are k = 3 and k = 10.
- **Seed:** one per draw, 1 + FB index.
- **Response cases:**
  - R1 (energy scale): handled as on the PET path.
  - R2 (muon momentum scale): handled by the PET path's own `design_inputs.r2_scalars`, which is blob-pinned. The
    first R2 attempt was refused by a guard (§7.1).

**Comparator scope.** This is the strongest *GBDT OmniFold* recipe the repository documents for these cases. It is not
the strongest scalar unfolding on every endpoint.
- `h1` was tuned with `truth4` inputs at k = 3. It is the least-regularized corner of the 4-point grid, and it was not
  re-tuned for `truth4_species`.
- On the 1D development tilt, two other methods already in the matched study do better on the same DEV units (§5):
  binned IBU (efficiency-corrected) and AUSSIE λ = 0.
- Those methods collapse on hadron-content or generator cases. IBU's D5 NuWro R is 0.06–0.12; AUSSIE's D4c is −3.9.
- The other documented GBDT alternatives are carry-misses (much worse on the tilt) and `truth4` without species (no
  topology).
- It is also not the production LightGBM estimator, not tuned on FB, not an ensemble and not an uncertainty procedure.
- PET's tilt lead is therefore a lead over this GBDT. The lead over a binned unfolding of the same seven bins is
  smaller or absent.

**Controls** (`results/controls/`), committed with the plan:

  `S4F-H2S1T24K5-FB0`, max |diff| 1.1e-16.

## 6. Disposition of each finalist against the GBDT tested

These dispositions hold only for these banks, signal-only simulation, the inventory response and this sample size. No
universal winner is named, and the finalists are not ranked.

- **H2S1T24 K5.**
  - Better on the E_avail tilt endpoints (E0, all E1 regions, E3) by 0.076–0.102 in R, with about one third of the
    GBDT's E0 MSE.
  - Better on most species reweightings. On the muon-scale case its lead is mostly the tilt (E8 contrast +0.016
    [−0.005, 0.037]).
  - Better on E4 against the GBDT's primary k, but not against k = 10. Level on E5.
  - **Worse on all three generator-model reweightings,** by 0.21–0.25, and on the narrow bump.
  - Its frozen rules pass. Its uncertainty procedure is uncalibrated, which is unchanged.
- **L128S1T24 K4.**
  - Better on the tilt endpoints by 0.04–0.08, with about half the GBDT's E0 MSE.
  - Level on E4 and E5.
  - Worse on the generator reweightings (0.12–0.21), the 0.3 GeV bump and R1 ×1.05.
  - Its frozen B2 failure stands: 0.0122, against 0.0033 for the GBDT on the same draws. Its coverage was never
    measured, so it is neither certified nor presumed to fail.
- **The GBDT (exploratory).** A reproducible, light-tailed point estimator with small residual spread and DEV seed
  spread. It is biased on the tilt and
  more robust to generator-model changes. It has no uncertainty procedure here.

**Practical reading for the decision.** PET's point estimate is worth carrying forward if the measurement depends on
E_avail-shape and hadron-content fidelity. The generator-model weakness is the main risk to a PET result whose
uncertainty must cover model dependence, and the data-scale step should test it directly.

## 9. Limits

- **Population:** signal-only simulation, the inventory response, generator histogram reweightings and the frozen
  banks.
- **Sample:** exploratory reuse of already-inspected FB rows, at study scale (600 k pseudodata events).
- **Statistics:** intervals are unadjusted, and the library cases have only 8 draws each.
- **Seeds:** there is one seed per draw for every method, so seed and event variation are not separated on FB.
- **Comparator:** a tuned, ensembled or differently informed GBDT could do differently, for example one given the reco
  cloud. This one was neither tuned nor weakened. The topology comparison depends on its k.
- **R2 inputs:** for R2 cases the GBDT's scaled muon inputs are rebuilt with the PET path's function. They cannot be
  byte-compared with the PET run's inputs, which are not stored.
- **Not compared at all:** uncertainty, coverage and real-data behaviour.
- **Costs:** data-scale costs are scaled, not measured, and the prior size is an open choice. That is the gap P0
  closes.
- **Comparator scope:** PET's tilt lead is over this GBDT OmniFold. Binned IBU and AUSSIE do better on the 1D tilt on
  DEV and fail elsewhere.
