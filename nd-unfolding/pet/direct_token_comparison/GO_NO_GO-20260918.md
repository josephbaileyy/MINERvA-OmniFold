## 1. What production conditions the experiment represents

Measured on the two pinned tuples, entries read in full (job 58470099; 17,930 data and
186,439 MC entries; 9 and 94 entries cross-checked against the production builder, all
agreeing).

| quantity | data 1B | MC 1A | represented in the experiment? |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| non-muon clusters per event, **pre**-truncation | mean 74.6, median 25, max 1651 | mean 102.6, median 60, max 2349 | **yes** — the generic cloud is built variable-length and then capped |
| events where the cap binds | **64.1%** | **84.4%** | yes |
| cluster energy beyond rank 12 | median 0.208, mean 0.286, p90 0.715 | median 0.449, mean 0.407, p90 0.754 | yes, and it sets the tail signal share |
| blobs per event | mean 11.2, median 4, max 158 | mean 12.5, median 6, max 153 | **mean yes, tail no** (gated to ≤18) |
| prongs per event | mean 1.5, median 1, max 7 | mean 1.8, median 2, max 12 | yes |
| photons per event | mean 0.35, max 2 | mean 0.35, max 2 | yes — the source provides only `gamma1`/`gamma2` |

Three consequences, and the first changes the case for the experiment:

* **The reco cap is not trimming a thin tail.** In most events production keeps a
  minority of clusters and discards a large fraction of their energy. The note's
  reco-cluster means of 11.09 and 11.15 are **post**-truncation figures over a selected,
  POT-weighted sample; they saturate against the cap of 12 and cannot show this.
* **The routing question is a real question at production multiplicity.** Pooling a
  family means summing a median of 4-6 blob embeddings — not the two prong embeddings of
  the finished matrix. That is why the finished null does not settle it.
* **Scope, stated because it errs in a knowable direction.** This read takes tuple
  entries 0..N with **no analysis selection**, while the note's figure is selected and
  POT-weighted. The two files are also different playlists of different sizes, so the
  difference between them is **not** a data/MC statement. If selection reduces
  multiplicity, the cap binds less often than the table says; nothing here bounds that.

**Qualification Q1 stands unchanged:** measured tail *energy* bounds how much the cap
*could* matter. It does not establish that the discarded clusters carry predictive
information. Those are different quantities and only the first is measured.

## 2. Measured GPU cost

Job 58470448, medians of three replicates, at the multiplicity A1 measured.

| arm | mean operating point (0 photons, 12 blobs, 2 prongs; C carries 27 tokens vs 16) | tail point (85 blobs; C carries 100 tokens) |
|---|---|---|
| **A** incumbent | 10.7 ms/step — 1.000x | 11.5 ms/step — 1.000x |
| **B** typed pooled | 10.3 ms/step — **0.967x** | 11.2 ms/step — 0.975x |
| **C** typed individual | 13.3 ms/step — **1.244x** | 21.6 ms/step — **1.879x** |
| **D** aggregate overflow | 10.8 ms/step — **1.007x** | 11.4 ms/step — 0.994x |

Inference ratios track it: C is 1.257x at the operating point and 1.318x in the tail;
B and D are within 2% of A.

* **A and D agree to 0.7%**, which is the designed parity — identical token counts — so
  aggregate overflow costs nothing to buy.
* **Adding typed objects with pooled routing is free within noise.** A ratio below one
  is noise, not a speedup.
* **Individual routing is the only arm with a price**, and it grows with exactly the
  multiplicity that would motivate it.

Per seed-regime job, four arms: 29,310 training steps per arm at the run card's 1M rows,
batch 1,024, 5 epochs, 2 legs, 3 iterations → 1,322 s of fit plus the frozen campaign's
measured ~1,200 s of per-job overhead (fixture build, normalization, serialization) ≈
**0.70-0.90 GPU-hours**. The variable-length fixture build will sit at the upper end.

## 3. The gate: where the experiment may and may not run

Jobs 58471178 and 58471470. Both controls land in both jobs — the **nominal** geometry
PASSes, reproducing the campaign's recorded verdict, and the frozen **variable**
geometry FAILs, reproducing the recorded stress failure — so the instrument is
exercising the gate rather than agreeing with itself.

| family width (photons, blobs, prongs) | pooled | direct | max_abs at failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| (0, 0, 1) | PASS | PASS | — |
| (0, 18, 1), (0, 18, 4), (2, 18, 4) | PASS | PASS | — |
| (0, 24, 1) | **FAIL** | **PASS** | 1.35e-05 |
| (0, 32, 1) | FAIL | FAIL | 1.89e-04 |
| (0, 48, 1) | FAIL | FAIL | 3.47e-04 |
| (0, 64, 1) | FAIL | FAIL | 4.88e-04 |
| (0, 85, 1), (2, 85, 4) | FAIL | FAIL | 6.28e-04 |

**The usable ceiling is 18 objects in a family; 24 already fails.** The discrepancy
grows monotonically with multiplicity. Bucketing removed the padding, as intended, and
did not make a long sequence agree across devices.

One observation that bears directly on the original question: **the first failure as
multiplicity grows hits the pooled arm, not the individual-object arm.** At 24 blobs
pooled fails at 1.35e-05 while direct passes; from 32 upward both fail identically. So
the cross-device discrepancy is not a property of individual-object routing — at high
multiplicity pooling is affected at least as much. That is now measured rather than
argued.

A limitation of the receipt: passing widths carry no quantified margin, so "18 passes"
has no headroom attached. If that headroom matters, it is one short job to measure.

## 4. Achievable sensitivity within a fixed budget

$\delta$ = 10 percentage points, three primary contrasts at Bonferroni $\alpha$ =
0.0167, 80% power, $t$ quantiles, and 0.80 GPU-hours per seed-regime job with three
regimes per seed.

**Every sd below is a planning assumption, not this endpoint's power.** The 25.8 was
measured on the four-object fixture, with two arms, against a ratio to the pooled arm
rather than to arm A, at a multiplicity the experiment no longer uses. It sizes nothing;
it only shows what a plausible sd costs. The sd that sizes Stage 2 is the one the pilot
measures for **this** endpoint, and it could be larger.

| between-seed sd $s$ | seeds for $\delta$ = 10 | jobs | GPU-hours |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **25.8 — planning assumption, not measured power for this endpoint** | 73 | 219 | **175** |
| 18.0 | 37 | 111 | 89 |
| 12.9 | 21 | 63 | **50** |
| 9.0 | 12 | 36 | 29 |

Inverted — what a fixed budget buys:

| budget | seeds | resolvable effect at $s$ = 25.8 | at $s$ = 12.9 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 30 GPU-h | 12 | 27.5 pts | 13.8 pts |
| 60 GPU-h | 24 | 18.1 pts | 9.1 pts |
| 90 GPU-h | 37 | 14.3 pts | 7.1 pts |
| 130 GPU-h | 54 | 11.7 pts | 5.8 pts |

**The whole question is whether variance reduction works.** At the measured $s$, a
10-point effect costs 175 GPU-hours; at half that $s$ it costs 50. The pilot's job is to
return $s$ after test-sample uncertainty has been capped at $\delta/4$ with inference
rather than with seeds — and if $\sigma_{\rm test}$ turns out to dominate the measured
25.8, the finished matrix was evaluation-limited rather than seed-limited, which is the
most useful thing the pilot can return.

## 5. What each outcome would support

For each primary contrast, and stated now so no outcome gets interpreted after the fact:

| outcome | what it supports |
|---|---|
| **P1 = C−B superior** (CI lower bound > +10) | adopt individual-object routing **on this fixture**, priced at 1.24x training and 1.26x inference at the operating point, and treat it as grounds to run the matched comparison on real inputs. **Neither direction nor magnitude transfers automatically** (§2.5, corrected 2026-09-18) |
| **P1 equivalent** (CI inside ±10) | keep family pooling. This is the positive result the finished matrix could not produce, and pooling then wins on cost and on being flat in multiplicity |
| **P1 inconclusive** | close the routing question at the achieved resolution and keep pooling as incumbent. Do **not** add seeds after the fact |
| **P2 = D−A superior** | adopt aggregate overflow at the production cap. This is the cheapest available change: measured parity with the incumbent, 1.007x, so it costs nothing to buy. Magnitude is conditional on Q1 — the tail's signal share is calibrated to its energy share, not to a measured predictive importance |
| **P2 equivalent** | leave truncation in place and stop treating the discarded tail as a candidate improvement, at least at $E_{\rm avail}$ scale |
| **P3 = B−A not superior** | pooled typed objects do not help. This does **not** make P1 irrelevant, and the three contrasts are read together rather than in sequence: P3 null with P1 positive is coherent and informative — it says typed objects help *only if routed individually*, which is precisely the hypothesis P1 tests. A null P3 would be evidence **for** that reading, not against it |
| **any control fails** (R3 non-tie, R1 non-tie, routing null non-tie) | no contrast is reported in any direction; the diagnosis ladder in §4.3 of the proposal runs first |

**Qualification Q2 stands:** a training-loss signature is *consistent with* a mechanism
and never establishes one. No mechanism is named unless two independent signatures
agree; otherwise the report says undetermined.

## 6. The no-go, and what it would take to lift it

The experiment **cannot** cover the measured multiplicity tail. Blobs reach 153-158 in
the data, the gate fails from 24 upward, and the existing discipline treats a gate
failure as blocking. So the fixture's ladder must cap typed multiplicity at 18, and that
cap is itself a representation choice which keeps the comparison in the regime where
pooling is least stressed — better than the finished matrix's two objects, but not the
regime where the two representations differ most.

Three honest options, none of which I have taken:

1. **Run at the operating point only** (blobs ≤ 18), state the ceiling, and report the
   routing verdict as applying to the bulk of events and not to the tail. Cheapest, and
   it is what the GO above refers to.
2. **Resolve the cross-device discrepancy** before extending the ladder. It is a
   float32 reduction-order effect that grows with sequence length; the earlier
   diagnostic measured it without fixing it. This is a separate piece of work with its
   own decision, not a preparation step.
3. **Run the tail arm GPU-only**, dropping cross-device reproducibility for those
   buckets and recording it as a scoped exemption — the same shape as the 2026-09-16
   stress decision, and a decision only you can make.
