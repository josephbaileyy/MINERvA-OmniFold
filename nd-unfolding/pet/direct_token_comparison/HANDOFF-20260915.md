## Why the gate changed

Read `OPTIMIZER_RESULT-20260915.md` before interpreting earlier failures.
The captured optimizer discrepancy concerns an attention key bias: its
contribution is constant across allowed keys for a query and cancels from
softmax in exact arithmetic. Adam can amplify tiny floating-point gradient
residuals in this redundant parameter. Common-gradient CPU/GPU Adam replay and
predictions pass. This supports the specifically approved gate, not a blanket
relaxation of tolerances and not a performance conclusion.

The earlier diagnostic built all models first, which changed initialization
after the first pair because checkpoint reload consumes the construction
sequence. The amended preflight freezes the original sequence. Do not reuse the
build-all-first bundle as proof of historical reproduction. The earlier failing
weights were not saved, so historical failure reconstruction retains its stated
limits even when the amended preflight passes.
