# Campaign protocol — PET improvement (version 1, 2026-09-22)

Written by the orchestrator **before any confirmatory event is read**. Scope: `SCOPE-HANDOFF-20260922.md` §5–§6.
Changes are made only by a dated amendment appended at the end, committed before the affected sample is
evaluated. PET is diagnostic method development: nothing this protocol produces is a publication adoption, an
uncertainty product, a threshold change for the historical comparison, or a Gate-6 action.

## 1. What is measured, and what a terminal result cannot authorize

- **Measured:** how well OmniFold estimators recover a known injected truth distortion from simulated
  pseudodata, where recovery is lost, whether event statistics and training effort change it, and whether a
  candidate's recovery and uncertainty statements hold under predeclared stress tests.
- **Cannot authorize:** adoption of any estimator for publication; a change to the historical comparison's
  verdict (`NEITHER_ELIGIBLE / NO_SELECTION`, preserved); a change to its thresholds; `C_stat`/`C_ML`; any
  real-data unfolding. A new reference or threshold can only be a **prospective recommendation**.

## 2. Stages and populations

The inventory is `G2_FPS_MEFHC_P12.npz` (sha256 `fa6b3463…`) with identity sidecar
`G2_FPS_MEFHC_P12.identity.npz` (`01e07412…`). Signal MC only; real data is never read by any stage.

| stage | population | used for | may choose anything? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DEV** | the historical final halves (prior half A 600,130 rows; pseudodata half B 600,111 rows) — already public in the historical report | Phase B diagnostics, Phase C feature/schedule/architecture selection, bug-finding | yes — and is therefore never confirmatory |
| **PILOT** | fresh pool P (§3) | variance for sizing the final; pilot rows never re-enter the final interval | sizes only |

| **STRESS** | fresh pool T (§3) | Phase E distortions, identifiability, and simulation-only bias/coverage ensembles | no |

## 3. Event pools

Every inventory row whose event identity appears in ANY historical comparison run artifact (tuning, pilot or
final, both arms — the union of the absolute dump rows the historical weights files record) is **excluded** from
P, F, S and T. The remaining signal-MC rows with `pass_truth` are assigned by
`u = blake2b(identity, salt = "pet-improvement-20260922-pools")` mapped to [0,1):

| pool | u range | expected share |
|---|---|---|
| P (PILOT) | [0.00, 0.08) | 8 % |
| F (FINAL) | [0.08, 0.40) | 32 % |
| S (SCALE) | [0.40, 0.80) | 40 % |
| T (STRESS) | [0.80, 0.97) | 17 % |
| R (reserve, untouched) | [0.97, 1.00) | 3 % |

A pool manifest (row counts, the digest of each pool's sorted identity list, the exclusion count) is committed
before any pool is read for scoring. Within a pool, a replicate draws its prior and pseudodata samples as
disjoint subsets by a second identity hash salted with the replicate id, so replicates are **independent event
draws**, not seed repetitions. Estimator seeds vary independently of event draws, and results report the two
sources of variation separately.

## 4. Endpoints

- **Primary (like-for-like):** the historical seven-bin `E_avail` recovery `R = 1 − residual_L1/injected_L1` on
  normalized spectra, with the historical tilt (amplitude 0.35, standardized coordinate clipped at 3.0), the three
  historical regions, and the historical floors **imported from `configuration_comparison/frozen_design.THRESHOLDS`
  and the historical reference**: aggregate floor `0.8 × 0.6949731569 = 0.5559785255`; regional floor
  `0.6 ×` each region's own reference; non-inferiority margin `0.02`; switching margin `0.04`. These are not
  changed, even though B1 measured that the reference is computed on the (pT, p‖) cell displacement rather than on
  the scored seven-bin spectrum (`phase_b/scalar/SCALAR_REFERENCES-20260922.md`). That finding is reported
  separately (§7) and cannot alter a like-for-like verdict in either direction.
- **Iteration reporting:** every run records every iteration. The like-for-like point is **iteration 3** (the
  historical count). A candidate may declare a different iteration count `K*` at freeze time; it is then scored at
  both 3 and `K*`, and both are reported. Passing at `K*` but not at 3 is reported as exactly that.
- **Secondary:** signed per-bin residual vs injected displacement (direction/overshoot), per-region recovery,
  final truth-weight tails (max, 99.9th percentile), ESS `(Σw)²/Σw²` for positive weights, reco-level closure after
  step 1, pulled-vs-pushed comparison, and wall-clock/GPU cost.

## 5. Candidate freeze and confirmatory design

1. At the end of DEV the orchestrator freezes **at most three candidates** plus two controls: the *repaired
   incumbent* (the historical `ours` configuration with only the confirmed recipe defects repaired) and the
   *historical as-executed* `ours` configuration. Each frozen candidate is a committed `RunConfig` (recipe, inputs,
   feature arm, iterations, seeds policy) with its content hash, recorded in an amendment before any PILOT row is read.
2. **PILOT:** 4 independent replicates, all candidates and controls, paired. The pilot estimates the sd of the
   paired difference; its observations are reported separately and never enter the final interval (the historical
   comparison's rule, kept).
3. **FINAL:** `n` independent replicates from pool F, each with a fresh prior draw (600,130 rows) and a fresh
   pseudodata draw (600,111 rows) at the historical sizes, every estimator run on the same replicate (paired), one
   estimator seed per replicate. `n` is the larger of 8 and the number that gives 80 % power to reject
   non-inferiority failure at margin 0.02 against the 80 % upper confidence bound of the pilot sd — capped by the
   pool size (F supports ≈ 12 fully independent replicates at the historical size; if more are required, the
   prior draw may be shared across replicates and this is reported as a reduction of independence).
4. **Decision inequalities (per candidate vs repaired incumbent), one-sided α = 0.05, Holm-adjusted across the
   frozen candidates:** superiority if the lower bound of the paired difference > 0; non-inferiority if > −0.02;
   switching recommended only if > +0.04. **Adequacy** (like-for-like): mean aggregate recovery ≥ the historical
   floor AND every scoreable region's mean ≥ its historical regional floor; the lower confidence bound of each is
   reported beside it. A candidate that is adequate but not superior is reported as exactly that.
5. The historical `theirs` configuration is not a FINAL control unless DEV shows its repaired recipe changes its
   standing; if it enters, it enters by amendment before PILOT.

## 6. Phase D (does more data help?)

For the strongest ≤ 2 DEV candidates (scalar references alongside): vary separately (i) pseudodata size with prior
fixed, (ii) prior-MC size with pseudodata fixed, (iii) training effort (epochs × iterations) with both fixed. At
least three sizes spanning ≥ 8× (e.g. 150k, 600k, 2.4M rows per sample where cost allows), ≥ 3 independent draws
per size from pool S, all scored against the same population-level target (the tilted spectrum computed on all of
pool S) and additionally on a common untouched evaluation population (a fixed subset of S never used for training).
Fixed-epoch and trained-to-convergence scalings are reported separately, since more events at fixed epochs also
means more optimizer updates. Nested subsets of one draw are never presented as independent.

## 7. Phase E (robustness, reference, coverage)

- Predeclared distortions — both signs and ≥ 3 magnitudes of the `E_avail` tilt; ≥ 1 different `E_avail` shape; a
  hadronic multiplicity/topology reweighting; available alternative-generator or interaction-model reweightings;
  and reco-level detector-response variations kept separate from truth-model changes — are fixed by an amendment
  before any STRESS row is read, once the source inventory is complete (`phase_e/DISTORTION_SOURCES` task).
- For every distortion: measure first whether it produces a distinguishable selected reco distribution (a
  reco-level two-sample statistic with its null distribution from equal-model resamples). A distortion with no
  measured reco-level power is reported as unprobed, not as unrecoverable.
- Reference assessment: the historical reference and floor are retained for like-for-like verdicts; alternative
  references (response-aware IBU, the reference model applied to the scored spectrum, controlled high-statistics
  and known-function diagnostics) are reported as **prospective recommendations only**.
- **Coverage:** only for a candidate that is adequate or best-in-class on FINAL. Its uncertainty procedure,
  nominal levels (68 % and 95 %), the ensemble construction (independent pseudodata draws from pool T with the
  prior varied or fixed, stated), and the Monte Carlo precision target (coverage estimated to ±0.05 at 95 %
  confidence, i.e. ≥ 100 replicates, or the achievable number reported with its binomial interval) are fixed by
  amendment before any coverage replicate is run.

## 8. Stopping rules

- A hypothesis is closed when its discriminating experiment has been run at the precision this protocol states,
  or when a measured cost/precision limit shows further resolution is impractical — reported with the numbers.
- No coverage campaign for a candidate already inadequate under §5.
- Phase F alternatives advance to an expensive evaluation only after a bounded representative benchmark supports
  them; the evidence for trying or deferring each is recorded.
- The campaign ends with either a candidate that satisfies §5 and survives §7, or a decision-resolving diagnosis
  with measured limits and an evidence-supported next choice.

**Truth-model distortions** (a per-event weight on the pseudodata's truth, normalized to unit mean over the

**Detector-response distortions** (applied to the pseudodata's RECO quantities only; truth and the prior are
unchanged, so the correct unfolded answer is the undistorted-response target; these measure sensitivity to
response mismodeling and are reported separately from D1–D5):

| id | definition | magnitudes |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | hadronic energy scale: every reco cluster energy and reco `E_avail` scaled | ×1.05 and ×0.95 |

Each R is run twice: with no truth distortion (spurious displacement) and combined with D1 at +0.35 (recovery under
mismodeling).

**Identifiability, measured before any estimator is scored on a distortion:** a GBDT two-sample classifier on reco

**Reference assessment (Phase E, prospective only):** (a) the historical reference model evaluated on the scored

curve) where the attainable recovery at k iterations is computable, testing whether `1−(1−a)^k` bounds, matches
or undershoots it. None of these changes a like-for-like verdict.

### Amendment 3 (2026-09-23, before any pool P row is read) — pilot size and two execution details

1. **PILOT uses 3 replicates, not 4.** V1 measured that pool P holds only 3 disjoint replicates at the historical
   sizes (`confirm/README.md`); 3 fully independent replicates are preferred over 4 overlapping ones. With 3 pilot
   replicates the pilot sd has 2 degrees of freedom, so the 80 % upper confidence bound used to size FINAL is
   correspondingly wider — FINAL is therefore expected to run at the cap of 12 replicates (pool F's capacity).
2. **Sample sizes follow this protocol, not the historical closure's orientation**: prior 600,130, pseudodata
   600,111 events. The executed historical closure had them the other way round (a 19-event swap, documented by
   V1); it has no effect on any comparison inside this campaign, and it is stated wherever historical and new
   numbers sit side by side.
3. **R1 on the PET path**: the PET runner refused reco-response distortions (V1). R1 is implemented at the raw-array
   level before the stress set runs: every stored reco cluster energy and the reco `E_avail` of the pseudodata are
   scaled by the factor; muon quantities and the selection are unchanged (E1 already records that R1–R3 do not
   re-evaluate the selection). If this cannot be done faithfully, R1 is dropped from the PET stress set and the
   report says so; it is never approximated silently.
