\begin{frame}{What this is, and what it is not}
\begin{block}{Read this first}
\textbf{No comparison has been run.} Neither arm has been trained on the endpoint,
no recovery has been measured, and \textbf{nothing here favours either
configuration.}

\begin{frame}{Production validation: 8 of 11, and one finding I cannot explain}
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
\YES\ pretrained load & 176/176, exact \\
\YES\ masking & padded content, PID and auxiliary: \emph{exactly} 0 \\
\YES\ optimizer & 10 updates, finite, clipping fires, schedule advances \\

\YES\ memory \& timing & 21.7\,GiB, synchronized \\
\NO\ forward XLA vs eager & 2.26e-3 against a 3.7e-7 limit \\

\NO\ batch invariance & \textbf{median 2.37e-3 per row, same program, same rows} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\begin{block}{Two hypotheses tested and rejected}
\textbf{k-NN ties}: rejected --- the row profile shows \emph{100\,\% of rows}
moving, where a tie moves a few a long way. \textbf{TF32}: rejected ---
\texttt{NVIDIA\_TF32\_OVERRIDE=0} changed the deviation by nothing, 2.257e-3 both
times, identical to the digit.
\end{block}
On CPU the same batch comparison gives a median of \textbf{exactly 0.0}. It is a
GPU-kernel property. Limits were \emph{not} widened.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What would unblock this}
\begin{enumerate}
\item A cap-33 dump carrying the 46 branches \texttt{r4\_manifest.py} enumerates,
      from the MasterAnaDev tuples. \textbf{Everything else waits on this.}
\item Regenerate and verify the identity sidecar for that dump.
\item Isolate the XLA batch dependence to an operation before trusting the
      executed path --- the transcendental approximations in \texttt{erf} and
      \texttt{tanh} are the candidates.
\item Then tuning $\rightarrow$ pilot $\rightarrow$ final, under the frozen design.
\end{enumerate}
\vfill
\begin{block}{Not taken}
The degraded arm --- our clusters, no PID, no auxiliary channel, no globals,
cap 12 --- runs today. It is not his complete arm, and reporting its score as his
configuration's would be the asymmetric comparison this lane exists to avoid.
\end{block}
