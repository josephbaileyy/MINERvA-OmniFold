### Things that are established and should not be re-litigated

- **Subsumption:** if `tol < C`, then `dev ≤ tol` *guarantees* the parameter-free check passes. The two
  checks are **not independent**. (Found by an independent agy review; verified against the code.)
- **Soundness condition** of the parameter-free check: `2(1-a)^niter < 1`. At `a=0.4186`: niter=2 → 0.676
  (sound, 1.48× margin); **niter=1 → 1.163, which would reject a correct unfold.** The pinned `niter=2` is
  therefore load-bearing, not bookkeeping.
- **No tolerance ≥ C does anything** on the under-recovery side. You cannot buy margin by widening.
- An absolute `0.055` **goes inert at R ≤ 1.123596** — only 0.391% below the measured R.
- **`structural_floor_worst_case` is NOT a per-seed lower bound.** 10/16 niter=2 and 7/16 niter=3 seeds land
  below it. It is a worst-case expected push. The runbook language invites the wrong reading.
- **The `mean+3sd` criterion from the previous session's mail is defective — do not use it.** It fires the
  alarm branch on *both* arms (6.2467% and 5.6806%, both > C) despite 0/48 and 0/16 realized breaches,

  *wins*, 2.86σ vs 2.11σ. Argue this on **realized exceedance and window width.**
- The gaussian model is unreliable for niter=3: it puts **3.19% of its mass below zero**, impossible for
  `|ratio/R − 1|`. Bound that tail **empirically** from `56375160`, do not extrapolate.
- The niter=3 arm's `mean == F` to five figures is a **verified coincidence**, not degeneracy

- **Refuted:** a `1/√steps` scaling law for `sd` (obs/pred ratio 1.00 → 1.79 → 4.39), and the assumption
