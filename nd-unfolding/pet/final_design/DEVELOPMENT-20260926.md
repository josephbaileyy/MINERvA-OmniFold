# Development stage S1–S2 (DEV bank): candidate screen and finalist choice

**Development evidence only.** Every run here used predecessor-drawn DEV selections (pool F replicates
0–1 for the development tilt, pool T replicates 0–1 for stress cases) with the predecessor's per-replicate
seeds, so each new design is an event- and seed-paired contrast with the committed predecessor runs.
Recoveries are the historical `E_avail` recovery against the replicate's pseudodata truth (the analyzer
reproduces every committed predecessor score to 1e-9); topology recoveries are against the oracle (the exact
distortion on the prior's own truth). Evidence: `results/{dev1,dev2L,dev2S}/` (receipts, scores, post-hoc
JSON), tables generated by `dev/summarize_dev.py` → `dev/DEV_TABLES-20260926.json`, screens applied by
`dev/apply_finalist_rule.py` → `dev/SCREENS-20260926.json` under the rule committed before the dev2S/dev2T/PET2
recoveries were inspected (`dev/FINALIST_RULE-20260926.md`).

## Findings (development)

1. **Detector reco summaries are the lever that transmits both the tilt and hadron content.** H2/H2S1 raise
   the development tilt from C's 0.57 to 0.86–0.88 at k = 3 and the proton-topology recovery from 0.02 to
   0.22–0.26; H1 (truth encoding only) does neither (topology 0.02).
2. **The per-iteration learning-rate policy decides robustness under the hidden-variable case.** With the
   predecessor's forced 1e-5 after iteration 0, H2's neutron-case `E_avail` residual exceeds the 0.021 screen
   from k = 3 (0.025 → 0.034 by k = 6); with a constant rate (H2S1) it stays 0.013–0.020 through k = 6.
3. **Step-1 capacity is not the limit at 8 epochs**: 0.25 M and 0.97 M-parameter step-1 PETs match the
   47 k one within the draw-to-draw spread on every screen, in both schedule lineages.
4. **Optimization effort helps topology and robustness**: 16 epochs (H2S1E16) reach proton topology 0.40
   (k = 4) and 0.50 (k = 5) with neutron residual 0.016–0.017, but its development-tilt recovery is not
   higher (0.88 at k = 4; 0.81 at k = 5).
5. Every efficiency-corrected design is weak on the NuWro reweighting (0.24–0.31) and the 0.3 GeV bump
   (≈ 0.3); these are robustness (non-designated) cases.
