# PET final-design selection study — protocol (version 1, 2026-09-25)

Written by the orchestrator **before any successor candidate has been run or scored**. Authorization:
`docs/orchestration/AUTHORIZATION-20260925-pet-final-design.md`; scope: `SCOPE-HANDOFF-20260925.md`
(byte copy of the brief). Changes are made only by dated amendments appended at the end and committed
before the affected sample is evaluated; an amendment cannot rescue an observed final failure. The
predecessor's protocol (`../improvement_campaign/PROTOCOL-20260922.md`), verdicts, thresholds and terminal
disposition are preserved unmodified. PET is diagnostic method development: nothing here is a
publication adoption, an uncertainty product for publication, a `C_stat`/`C_ML`, a Gate-6 action, a
real-data unfolding, or a change to any historical threshold or verdict.

## 1. What is measured, and what a terminal result cannot authorize

- **Measured:** on simulated signal-only pseudodata with known injected truth changes, the accuracy,
  robustness, numerical stability, cost, and simulation-only uncertainty calibration of complete PET
  OmniFold designs (detector representation, truth representation, model identity/initialization,
  training recipe, miss rule, iteration/stopping rule, uncertainty procedure) at the event scale of §3.
- **Selection target:** one executable recommended design among the declared, meaningfully tested
  candidate family (§5) under the frozen decision table (§6), or a documented "no eligible design" /
  "unresolved" outcome after the discriminating tests or quantified practical limits (§10).
- **Cannot authorize:** publication adoption; any change to the historical comparison's verdict
  (`NEITHER_ELIGIBLE / NO_SELECTION`) or thresholds, or to the predecessor's terminal disposition;
  `C_stat`/`C_ML` or any production uncertainty product; real-data unfolding; reopening `OI-126`;
  changes to the adopted scalar-5D covariance. A selected design is a **study selection for further PET
  method development**, scoped to the simulations actually evaluated (signal-only; the inventory's
  simulated detector response; no background model; no real data).

## 3. Event scale, populations and access rules

**Event scale (fixed):** every replicate uses 600,130 prior rows and 600,111 pseudodata rows (the
predecessor's historical sizes), signal MC only, the inventory's simulated response. Stated as the study's
diagnostic scale; real data are never read.

**Banks (frozen by `banks/build_banks.py`; digests in `banks/BANK_MANIFEST.json` committed before any
bank-FB or bank-RB row is read by an estimator):**

| bank | rows | definition | use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DEV** | all truth-passing rows not in FB or RB (≈ 45.1 M) | every row consumed by the historical comparison or any scored predecessor draw | development, sizing pilot, and the **prior** of every later stage |
| **FB** (final bank) | never-drawn rows of predecessor pools P, F, S, T (2,646,891) | fresh for estimator scoring; entered only predecessor whole-pool aggregate spectra | pseudodata of FINAL confirmation, final stress and coverage |
| **RB** (reserve) | predecessor pool R (1,414,846) | never opened by any predecessor code | revalidation of a repaired design after a failed final (§11), or a predeclared out-of-bank check; sealed otherwise |

**Draws.** A replicate `r` of a stage draws its pseudodata as a uniformly random subset (without
replacement) of the stage's pseudodata bank and its prior as a random subset of DEV, disjoint from its
pseudodata, using identity hashes salted by (stage, r). Replicates are therefore **independent given the
banks** (they overlap by chance: within FB the pseudodata sampling fraction is 0.227). All final-stage
inference is **conditional on the frozen banks**: the target of every score is the replicate's own
pseudodata truth (historical like-for-like) and, reported beside it, the FB population truth under the same
distortion. The conditional scope is stated with every final result; resampling a finite bank does not
create new detector simulations, and the pseudodata-sampling component relative to the FB population is
reduced by the finite-population factor 0.773 (effect on coverage quantified in the coverage record).
Predecessor replicate selections (e.g. pool T replicates 0–1, pool F replicates 0–11) may be reused in
development for event-paired comparison with the committed predecessor runs.

**Access rules (enforced in code):** the study runner refuses FB until amendment "FB release" is committed
after the final set is frozen (§8), and refuses RB unless an amendment releases it for §11 or a predeclared
check. DEV is open. Real-data and background members of the inventory are never read (`SignalOnlyNpz`).

## 4. Endpoints

All recoveries use the historical form `R = 1 − L1(unfolded − target)/L1(prior − target)` on unit-normalized
histograms, with the unfolded histogram the prior weighted by `w_truth × push` over truth-passing events.

| id | endpoint | histogram | target |
|---|---|---|---|
| E0 | **primary**: aggregate `E_avail` recovery under the development tilt D1 +0.35 (historical definition, clip 3.0) | seven historical `E_avail` bins | replicate pseudodata truth (like-for-like); FB population reported |
| E1 | regional recoveries (low-acceptance, moderate, good; historical regions) under D1 +0.35 | 7 bins per region | as E0 |
| E2 | signed per-bin residuals under D1 +0.35 | 7 bins | as E0 |
| E3 | opposite-sign tilt D1 −0.35, aggregate | 7 bins | as E0 |
| E4 | topology: proton multiplicity ×1.3 (D4c up), joint `E_avail` × truncated-cloud proton class (0/1/2/3+) | 28 bins | as E0 |
| E5 | joint energy/momentum-transfer tilt D3 +0.35 (`E_avail/q3`), joint `E_avail` × true `q3` (quartiles of the DEV bank) | 28 bins | as E0 |
| E6 | every other case of the robustness library (§7), on its natural histogram: `E_avail` for D1/D2/D5/R; joint `E_avail` × species class for D4 (the species the case changes) | — | as E0 |
| E7 | null (no distortion): spurious residual L1 on `E_avail` and on `E_avail` × proton class | — | replicate pseudodata truth |
| E8 | response sensitivity: `R_E0` under R1 ×1.05 / ×0.95 combined with D1 +0.35 minus `R_E0` under D1 +0.35 on the same replicates | — | — |
| E9 | numerical stability: final truth-weight tails (max, 99.9th percentile), ESS/n over truth-passing events, non-finite count; estimator-seed sd of `R_E0` at fixed events | — | — |
| E10 | cost: measured GPU-hours (and CPU-hours) per unfolding and per validated result (§6.7) | — | — |
| E11 | uncertainty: coverage and width of the final uncertainty procedure (§9) | — | — |

Species classes are counts over the stored 12-token truth cloud and are labelled **truncated-cloud
counts**; they are exactly the counts the D4 distortions are defined on and exactly what PET's truth step
receives. A recovery is undefined (reported as absolute residual) when `L1(prior − target) < 3 × F`, where
`F = 0.004` is the finite-sample L1 floor between two independent samples at this scale (measured by the
predecessor's oracle anchors: exact reweighting of the prior scores `R ≈ 0.985` on the 0.273 tilt).

## 5. Declared candidate family

Every candidate is a complete design: detector-step model and inputs, truth-step model and inputs,
initialization, per-step training recipe, miss rule, iteration count or stopping rule, and (at the final
stage) the uncertainty procedure. Candidates are committed `RunConfig`s identified by content hash.

**Anchors (diagnostic, not selectable):** CTL (historical recipe, carry-misses, k = 3); C@3 (the
predecessor's efficiency-corrected step 2, baseline inputs, k = 3 — reference candidate; it is **not**
grandfathered through §6). **Failure controls** (development only): C@10, B@3/B@10.

**Compact hybrids (our PET, 47 k parameters per step):** H1 = C + categorical truth PDG (13-category one-hot,
padding distinct from any species); H2 = H1 + detector-only reco summaries at step 1 (reco `E_avail`, reco
`q3`, stored-cluster ΣE and count — whole-event reco `E_avail` is pre-cap, the stored-cluster sums are
post-cap, so their difference carries the overflow); H1/H2 are **new candidates, not additive gains**.
Conditional additions, only if development diagnostics support them: explicit truncated-cloud species
counts as truth-step globals; step-1 learning-rate policy at iterations ≥ 1 (the predecessor forces 1e-5
after iteration 0; the constant-rate alternative is tested because the diagnostics locate a detector-closure
deterioration from iteration 2); iteration count k ∈ {2,…,6}.

**Larger networks (genuinely trained):** L64/L128 = our PET enlarged at step 1 (projection 64 or 128,
4 transformer layers; ≈ 0.25 M / 0.97 M parameters) with an optimization budget chosen from learning curves
(epochs, learning rate), same truth step and miss rule as the best compact hybrid; the complete-package
version may also enlarge the truth step. P2-pre / P2-scr = PET2-small (2,758,702 parameters) at step 1 on
its own compatible 33-token inputs, pretrained (exported state `2480f269…`, verified at the first optimizer
step) versus randomly initialized, with the **declared** PET2 recipe (TorchAdamW, weight decay 0.01, clip 1.0,
warmup/cosine) and an identical tuning opportunity, identical truth step, miss rule, split and seeds policy.

**Algorithmic controls/challengers:** topology-aware scalar GBDT/MLP OmniFold and response-aware binned IBU
(both miss rules) with matched selections and normalization; AUSSIE (arXiv:2602.24282) in a bounded matched
stress test (same scalar inputs, same miss handling, same data and tuning opportunity). AUSSIE advances to a
PET-backbone evaluation only if it beats matched scalar OmniFold on robustness or stability in that test.

**Causal contrasts (development, not selection):** representation (H2 vs H1 vs C at fixed truth step, miss
rule, split, initialization), architecture (L64/L128 vs H2 at fixed truth step), pretraining (P2-pre vs
P2-scr), truth step (one-hot vs raw vs explicit counts at a fixed detector step). Random-number streams of
the two steps are decoupled (per-step seeds). Targets naturally differ after different detector fits.

## 6. Decision table (frozen with this protocol)

Physical meaning is stated with each number. "LB" = one-sided 95 % lower confidence bound, simultaneous
across the candidates entering the decision (Bonferroni over that set); intervals are conditional on the
banks (§3). Historical thresholds are imported unchanged from `configuration_comparison/frozen_design.THRESHOLDS`.

### 6.1 Designated accuracy endpoints (eligibility)

| id | requirement | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| U1 | mean `R_E0` ≥ 0.5559785255 **and** LB(`R_E0`) ≥ 0.5559785255 | the historical adequacy floor (0.8 × 0.6949731569), now required at the simultaneous lower bound — a new safeguard, applied to C@3 as to everyone |
| U2a | each historical region's mean ≥ 0.6 × its historical reference (the historical rule; LBs reported) | preserves like-for-like comparability |
| U2b | moderate and good regions: mean ≥ 0.50 and LB ≥ 0.40; low-acceptance: mean ≥ 0.25 and LB > 0 | where acceptance is moderate/good the unfolding removes at least half of the prior's bias with respect to an injected model difference; where it is low (reference 0.014) at least a quarter, never the wrong sign |
| U2c | no `E_avail` bin with `|injected| ≥ 0.01` moves opposite to its injected displacement by more than 0.005 (mean over replicates) | no bin is pushed materially the wrong way |
| U3 | mean `R_E3` ≥ 0.50 and LB ≥ 0.40 | the opposite-sign tilt is recovered at least half-way: the development point is not special |
| U4 | mean `R_E4` ≥ 0.25 and LB > 0.10 | the unfolded proton-multiplicity distribution moves at least a quarter of the way from the prior to the truth under a reco-identifiable (16 × null threshold) hadron-content change; this is the minimal topology content that justifies a full-event over a scalar unfolding |
| U5 | mean `R_E5` ≥ 0.25 and LB > 0.10 | the joint (energy, momentum-transfer) change is recovered at least a quarter of the way (identifiability 12–13 × the null threshold for D3 ±0.35) |

### 6.2 Robustness (eligibility), over the whole robustness library of §7

| id | requirement | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | unit = (case, replicate) with a defined recovery on the case's natural histogram; failure = `R < 0` (ends farther from the target than the untouched prior). Pooled over the final library, the one-sided 95 % Clopper–Pearson upper bound on the failure probability ≤ 0.10, **and** no case has mean `R` < 0 | "moves away" is rare and bounded, not merely unobserved; zero observed failures is not a zero probability |
| B2 | cases whose `E_avail` injection is below 3F (e.g. D4d): mean `E_avail` residual L1 ≤ injected L1 + 0.010 | under a change that barely moves `E_avail`, the estimator adds no more than 2.5 × the finite-sample floor of spurious `E_avail` displacement |
| B3 | null: mean spurious residual L1 ≤ 0.012 on `E_avail` and ≤ 3 × its null floor on `E_avail` × proton class | applied to data distributed like its own prior, the estimator manufactures no shape difference beyond three times the finite-sample noise floor |
| B4 | response: `|E8|` ≤ 0.15 for both signs of R1 | a ±5 % hadronic energy-scale error changes the recovered fraction of the tilt by at most a quarter of the historical floor. Reported, not covered by any statistical interval |

### 6.3 Numerical stability (eligibility)

| id | requirement | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | all weights finite and non-negative; median over replicates of ESS/n ≥ 0.20 and of the 99.9th-percentile truth weight ≤ 10; max weight ≤ 100 in every replicate | no result rests on a handful of events |
| N2 | estimator-seed sd of `R_E0` at fixed events ≤ 0.05 (measured on ≥ 4 seeds × ≥ 2 event draws) | a single unfolding is reproducible to better than a tenth of the floor |

### 6.4 Uncertainty calibration (eligibility; §9)

| id | requirement | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | development tilt, per-bin marginal coverage of the 95 % intervals pooled over the 7 `E_avail` bins: LB (replicate-cluster bootstrap) ≥ 0.90 and point estimate ≤ 0.99; of the 68 % intervals: LB ≥ 0.60 and point ≤ 0.80 | calibrated within five points at 95 % (ten at 68 %), and not trivially conservative |
| C2 | no single bin's 95 % coverage point estimate < 0.85 | no badly under-covered bin hidden by pooling |
| C3 | moderate and good regions, pooled per-bin 95 % coverage LB ≥ 0.85 | regional calibration |
| C4 | per bin, mean 95 % half-width ≤ 2.5 × the empirical sd of the point estimates across coverage replicates; and the top-bin 95 % half-width ≤ 0.5 × its injected displacement (0.068) | intervals are not inflated to win coverage and can resolve the development tilt |
| C5 | one stress case (D4c up): pooled 95 % coverage LB ≥ 0.85 on its natural histogram | calibration survives a hadron-content change (conditional coverage) |

### 6.5 Non-inferiority and cost (cost-based choice of a smaller package)

A smaller package is selected on cost grounds only if **all** hold, simultaneously at one-sided 95 %:
LB(`R_E0,small − R_E0,large`) > −0.02 (the goal's margin); LB of the paired small − large difference > −0.04 on
each of U2b's moderate/good regions and on E3, and > −0.05 on E4 and E5 (about one replicate sd of each
endpoint at this scale: regional/topology endpoints are noisier than the aggregate); both packages eligible
under §6.1–6.4; and the lower 95 % bound of the total-cost ratio `cost_large/cost_small` ≥ 2.0 (§6.7).
A non-significant difference is never called equivalence. The historical +0.04 switching margin is reported
for improvements over CTL.

### 6.6 Ranking among eligible candidates

1. Strongest scientific performance: highest mean `R_E0`; a candidate is **materially better** if the LB of its
   paired advantage in `R_E0` is > 0 and it is not worse than the other by more than the §6.5 margins on
   U2b/E3/E4/E5. A large gain on the easy tilt never offsets a critical failure (eligibility comes first).
2. Smaller package on cost only under §6.5.
3. Scientifically equivalent (every §6.5 margin met in both directions) but saving < 2×: report the
   equivalence; tie-break by reproducibility (smaller N2 seed sd; then smaller coverage-interval width at
   calibrated coverage), then by cost.
4. Unresolved: spend more on the discriminating contrast within the sequential plan (§8) or a new untouched
   stage (RB, by amendment); stop only at a stated information/resource limit (§10).

### 6.7 Cost definition

Recurring cost of a validated result = GPU-hours (A100, charged, from `sacct`) of all unfoldings the final
estimator plus its uncertainty procedure requires on one data set at the §3 scale, plus inference; reported
separately from one-time costs (development, data preparation, pretraining — the PET2 pretraining is
external and sunk, reported as such), with amortization over 10 validated results stated. Cost uncertainty
= the spread of measured per-unfolding GPU-hours across the final runs (ratio LB by the delta method on
log-costs). Parameter count or one epoch's timing is never used as the cost.

## 8. Stages, budgets and sequential rules

Milestone budgets are A100 GPU-hours on `m3246_g` (planning envelope ≈ 5,000 GPU-h, ≈ 8.5 % of the project's
58,714 remaining on 2026-09-25; revisited after each stage; stop and re-plan if the project balance falls
below 40,000 GPU-h). CPU (`m3246`, 3,430 node-hours left project-wide, scarce) is used only for small tasks.

| stage | content | data | budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| S0 | recovery, capacity, banks, diagnostics of predecessor runs, this protocol | — | ≤ 5 |
| S1 | bounded interventions locating the multiplicity failure (§ diagnostics); compact-hybrid development and screening on the development library, 2 event draws per case, all iterations scored to k = 6 | DEV | ≤ 250 |
| S2 | larger networks and pretraining controls; learning curves in epochs/events for the leading designs; AUSSIE and scalar references on the development library | DEV | ≤ 600 |
| S3 | freeze: ≤ 3 finalists (strongest compact hybrid, strongest large/pretrained package, any justified challenger) + anchors CTL and C@3; exact configs, stopping, seeds, endpoints, hypotheses, multiplicity correction, sizing — committed as the "FB release" amendment **before any FB row is read** | — | — |
| S3p | independent sizing pilot: ≥ 4 fresh DEV event draws × all finalists; its observations never enter the final | DEV | ≤ 150 |
| S4 | FINAL (E0–E3) and final robustness library, finalists + anchors, event-paired | FB | ≤ 1,800 |
| S5 | coverage of each serious eligible finalist at its exact configuration | FB | ≤ 2,200 |
| S6 | independent review, repairs, preservation | — | ≤ 100 |

**Sizing rule (S3p):** `n_F` = the smallest n giving 80 % power for every §6.5 non-inferiority contrast and for
U1 (true values = pilot means, sd = pilot 80 % upper confidence bound), with Bonferroni α over the declared
contrasts; floor 24, cap 60 per stage. The robustness library uses `n_S` = 8 event draws per case (pooled B1
unit count ≥ 8 × cases); the coverage ensemble uses `N_cov` from §9.

**Sequential rule (FINAL and coverage):** at most two looks, α split Bonferroni-style: look 1 at n1 with
one-sided α/2 per simultaneous decision; if a decision is unresolved (its interval straddles its margin), look 2
adds n2 = n1 new replicates (fresh draws from the same bank) and decides at α/2 on the cumulative data. No other
interim inspection of FB outcomes. Ordinary confidence intervals are never repeatedly re-checked.

**Development screening decisions** (S1/S2) are staged ablations, not a Cartesian product: a factor advances
when its paired development effect on E0/E3/E4 exceeds twice its paired spread without an E6 robustness
regression; otherwise it is closed with its measured effect. Development results are never confirmatory.

## 9. Uncertainty procedure and coverage

**Procedure (declared for every finalist, run at its exact configuration):** B = 6 members; member b reweights
the pseudodata events and the prior events each by independent Poisson(1) weights and uses its own estimator
seeds; the reported estimate is the member mean of the unit-normalized histogram; the interval is member mean
± t(B−1) × member sd per bin (68 % and 95 %). It represents pseudodata statistics, prior-MC statistics and
training randomness; it does **not** represent detector-response or physics-model uncertainty (reported
separately via E8 and E6). Any tuning, stopping or ensembling inside the estimator recurs inside every member.

**Coverage ensemble:** `N_cov` replicates, each a fresh random FB pseudodata draw and DEV prior draw; target =
the replicate's own pseudodata truth, and the FB population truth reported. Coverage is **conditional on the
banks**; per-bin marginal (and pooled-over-bins with replicate-cluster bootstrap LBs); simultaneous claims are
not made unless stated. `N_cov` = 120 at look 1 (binomial half-width ≈ 0.04 per bin at 0.95, tighter when pooled),
extended to 240 by the sequential rule if a C1–C5 decision straddles its bound. D4c-up coverage uses 60 replicates.
Bias, pull distributions and widths are reported for every bin and region.

## 10. Selection and stopping the study

Eligibility (§6.1–6.4, plus complete provenance: receipts, config hashes, guard inventories, bank digests)
is applied before ranking (§6.6). The study ends with (a) one executable recommended design with measured
limits and a validated uncertainty procedure, or (b) "no eligible design", with completed discriminating
evidence and a bounded account of the failures, or (c) an unresolved supported set with a practical default and
tie-break, stating what evidence would resolve it. Alternatives are closed with evidence or a quantified
practical limit (compute, queue throughput, event capacity), never by preference.

## 11. Failed-final repair and selection safeguards

A finalist that fails an eligibility criterion on FB is repaired only on DEV evidence, re-frozen by amendment,
and re-validated on RB (never on the failed FB results). Selection among several coverage-tested candidates uses
the prespecified simultaneous inference of §6; otherwise a separate RB validation is required. Provenance-
incomplete runs are rejected before scoring. A coverage study at a different scale, k or recipe does not
certify the selected design.

## 12. Records

Every quotable result lands with its evidence and with the study's RUN_LOG entry
(`nd-unfolding/ND_OMNIFOLD_RUN_LOG.md`), STATUS paragraph (`nd-unfolding/ND_OMNIFOLD_STATUS.md`) and, at
delivery, `VALIDATION_LEDGER.md` rows at the next dense id after integrating `origin/main`. The resource ledger
(`RESOURCE_LEDGER.tsv`, from `sacct`) is updated after each stage.
