# PET final-design selection study — scientific report (draft; final-stage sections pending)

**PET is diagnostic method development.** Everything here is simulation only (signal MC of the ME-FHC
inventory `G2_FPS_MEFHC_P12.npz`); nothing is a publication adoption, an uncertainty product for publication,
a `C_stat`/`C_ML`, a Gate-6 action or a real-data unfolding, and no historical threshold, verdict or the
predecessor's disposition changes. Authorization `docs/orchestration/AUTHORIZATION-20260925-pet-final-design.md`;
design `PROTOCOL-20260925.md` (+ Amendments 1, 2, 2b, 2c); claim sources are the committed receipts, scores and
post-hoc JSON under `results/`.

## 1. Question and outcome space

Select, among a declared and meaningfully tested family, one complete PET design — detector and truth
representations, model and initialization, training recipe, miss rule, iteration count, uncertainty procedure —
with a validated simulation-only uncertainty procedure, or conclude "no eligible design" / "unresolved" with the
discriminating evidence and measured limits. The smaller network is preferred only if it is demonstrably comparable
(simultaneous one-sided 95 % non-inferiority at −0.02 on the primary recovery, plus the frozen regional/topology
margins) and at least twofold cheaper in measured total cost; otherwise the stronger larger/pretrained design.

## 2. Fresh events and independence (§ `CAPACITY-20260925.md`)

Only 4.06 M inventory rows (3.4 historical-size replicates) were never drawn into a scored predecessor run, and no
further same-model simulation exists. The study therefore freezes banks — DEV (45.09 M exposed rows; development and
every prior), FB (2.65 M never-drawn rows; the final stages' pseudodata, sealed until release) and RB (1.41 M, pool R,
sealed reserve) — and makes all final inference **conditional on the banks**: replicates are independent random
subsets given the banks; the bank's own offset from the generator population is reported beside each contrast.

## 3. Where the predecessor's multiplicity failure sits (development; § `DIAGNOSTICS-20260925.md`)

- The multiplicity distortions barely move the scored `E_avail` marginal (neutrons ×1.3: 0.011 L1 against 0.273 for
  the tilt): the predecessor's C "neutron failure" is a near-zero-injection artifact of the recovery ratio; B's is a
  real wrong-direction push of `E_avail` (hidden-variable response).
- Under protons ×1.3 the baseline detector step does not transmit the hadron-content change (stored-cluster energy
  closure worsens from iteration 1; proton-class recovery of the pull ≈ 0.02); step 2 reproduces what it receives —
  given C's real pull every truth representation recovers ≈ 0.01–0.02 of the topology change.
- Given the exact per-event weights, the truth step does learn the change (categorical PDG 0.87 at 8 epochs, 0.97 at
  24) and extrapolates it to the missed events as well as it fits the selected ones; raw PDG codes fail on neutrons.
- The stored reco cloud holds its 12-cluster maximum in 85.5 % of selected events (100 % above 0.8 GeV), so whole-event
  detector summaries carry information the tokens do not.

## 4. Development screen (DEV bank; § `DEVELOPMENT-20260926.md`)

Seed- and event-paired contrasts with the predecessor's runs (two event draws per case):

1. Detector reco summaries at step 1 (H2/H2S1) raise the development tilt from C's 0.57 to 0.86–0.88 at k = 3 and the
   proton topology from 0.02 to 0.22–0.26; categorical truth PDG alone (H1) does neither.
2. With the predecessor's forced 1e-5 learning rate after iteration 0 the same inputs fail the neutron-case screen from
   k = 3; with a constant rate (H2S1) they pass through k = 6.
3. Step-1 capacity is not the limit at 8 epochs (0.25 M / 0.97 M-parameter step 1 ≈ the 47 k one on every screen).
4. Sixteen epochs improve topology (0.40–0.50) and keep the neutron residual low.
5. AUSSIE loses to matched scalar OmniFold on robustness and stability in 12 of 12 matched comparisons and is closed.
6. PET2-small with its pretrained initialization and the declared recipe diverges from k = 3 in the iterative loop
   (detector-step weights up to 5.5 × 10⁵ at k = 5, development tilt −2.8 / −2.0); the randomly initialized PET2 with the
   identical recipe stays stable. (Complete pretrained-vs-scratch contrast: pending.)

The pre-committed finalist rule selects the compact finalist **H2S1 at K = 5**; the large slot is frozen by Amendment 3.
