# Fresh-event capacity and independence (study stage S0, 2026-09-25)

PET is diagnostic method development; simulation only. This record answers the brief's first-stage
question: which simulated events can still serve as untouched validation, and what independence
the study can honestly claim. It is the basis of the banks in `PROTOCOL-20260925.md` §3.

## Method

Every predecessor draw that produced a scored result was rebuilt from committed code
(`improvement_campaign/phase_e/replicates.py`, unchanged; independent re-implementation by a
read-only specialist, then re-derived by `banks/build_banks.py`), and the rebuilt row sets were
checked against the row digests the predecessor receipts record (46 of 48 digests available; the
two E1-references replicates 1–2 carry none and are reconstructed from code). Inputs: identity
sidecar sha256 `01e07412…`, predecessor `pools.npz` sha256 `3d8faeb1…`, inventory
`G2_FPS_MEFHC_P12.npz` sha256 `fa6b3463…` (49,152,885 signal rows; 49,150,928 truth-passing).

**No additional same-model simulation exists.** The inventory already uses all 12 ME-FHC StandardMC
playlists (489 files, equal to today's remote OpenData listing, 1:1 per playlist). The OpenData special
samples (Extended2p2h, diffractive, electron-neutrino, …) are different physics configurations and reuse

triples over the whole inventory; no MC run appears in more than one playlist; no identity-level overlap
between pools. Two rows of the historical 2 M loader subsample were not excluded by the predecessor
(35796924 in S, 35824188 in P); both were drawn by predecessor runs and are in the DEV bank.

## Exposure recorded after this study began (2026-09-25)

The analysis specialist computed a provisional constant for endpoint E5 (quartile edges of true `q3`)
as unweighted quantiles over **all** 49,149,598 truth-passing inventory rows with finite `q3`
(`analysis/provenance/q3_quartiles_provisional_20260925.json`). That is one aggregate statistic over the
truth of every row, FB and RB included; no estimator was trained or scored on those rows and no
per-event information left the computation. It is recorded because the protocol calls RB "never opened":
from this date RB's status is **never drawn and never scored; entered one whole-inventory truth quantile**.
The provisional constant is replaced by DEV-only quartiles (by amendment) before E5 is used for any
decision.

## Consequences for the study design

1. **Fully independent replicates at the historical scale are no longer available in the needed
   number** (3.4 full replicates of fresh events in total). A new hash salt or seed does not create
   events.
2. **Banks and conditional inference (protocol §3).** Fresh rows are reserved for the pseudodata of the
   final stages: FB = the 2,646,891 never-drawn rows of P, F, S, T; RB = pool R (1,414,846), sealed as
   the reserve for a failed-final repair or a predeclared out-of-bank check. Priors of every later stage
   are drawn from the ≈ 45 M exposed rows (DEV). Replicates are independent random subsets **given the
   banks**; FB pseudodata draws overlap by chance (sampling fraction 0.227). Final inference is stated as
   conditional on the frozen banks; the target of every score is the replicate's own pseudodata truth
   (historical like-for-like), with the FB population truth reported beside it.
3. **What this does not establish:** new detector simulations; generator dependence beyond histogram
   reweightings at fixed response; behaviour at the real-data scale (the study's pseudodata scale is fixed
   at the historical 600,111 events).
4. **Precision is not reduced silently:** sample sizes are set by the protocol's sizing rule; if the
   banks cannot support a claim at the required precision, the limit is quantified and reported.
