## Controls (each must pass before its question is answered)

- **C-a, PET re-score.** For every run in P, recompute E_avail R at K from the raw arrays, using the scorer's own
  definition (`final_design/analysis/score_design.py`):
  - unfolded = prior `w_truth × push`;
  - target = pseudo `w_truth × pseudo_distortion`;
  - prior = prior `w_truth`;
  - truth-passing rows with finite E_avail, the protocol edges, unit-normalized.

  It must reproduce the committed `design_scores.json` value to ≤ 1e-9. The oracle anchor must reproduce too.
- **C-b, data integrity.** Each copied `replicate_arrays.npz` must match the sha256 recorded in its committed score
  file (`provenance.replicate_arrays_sha256`). Each iteration file must match `iteration_file_sha256`.
- **C-c, GBDT reproduction (optional, only if Q2 needs GBDT weights).** Refit the committed recipe on one unit. It
  must reproduce the committed R at every k exactly, as the comparison's own control did. If it does not, no GBDT
  weight-level result is reported.

## Questions and reductions (descriptive; unadjusted; 8 draws per case)

**Q1. Where in E_avail, and is it under-movement?** Uses committed P scores and G.
- Per E_avail bin and acceptance region, for PET at K and GBDT at k = 7, on each D5 case:
  - the mean signed residual (unfolded − target);
  - the mean injected change (target − prior);
  - the mean moved change (unfolded − prior).
- The **movement fraction** m = ⟨moved, injected⟩ / ‖injected‖²: 1 means the method moved exactly along the
  injected change; 0 means it did not move.
- The **orthogonal residual** ‖moved − m·injected‖ / ‖injected‖: movement in the wrong direction.
- The same two quantities for the development tilt, as the contrast.

**Q2. Which truth variables does PET's final truth weight fail to reproduce?** Uses raw P arrays.
- Recovery R (same definition) of the unfolded against the target in the **true p_T marginal, the true p_∥
  marginal, and the D5 3D bins**, besides E_avail. The bin edges are fixed below before computing.
- The weight-level agreement between the final truth weight and the exact distortion over prior truth-passing rows:
  - the Pearson correlation of log(push) with log(prior_oracle);
  - the slope of a least-squares fit of log(push) on log(prior_oracle), where 1 means full strength and 0 means no
    response.
- **Contrast:** the same reductions on the development tilt, whose distortion depends on E_avail only.
- **Bins:** the generator table's own edges, from the committed
  `../improvement_campaign/phase_e/calibration/d5_tables.json` (`tables.nuwro.edges`; the same edges for every
  generator). That is 7 E_avail × 16 p_∥ × p_T bins, 1,568 3D cells.
  - The p_T and p_∥ marginals use the same edges.
  - Events outside the table's 3D range form one overflow cell. The D5 weight there is 1.
  - Recovery in the 3D cells is reported over cells where the prior has ≥ 50 weighted rows. The rest are pooled
    into one cell. This threshold is fixed here.

**Q3. Detector step or truth step?** Uses raw P arrays, at each iteration k = 1…K.
- **Step-1 truth-space closure:** R of the truth E_avail spectrum of the prior's **reco-passing** rows weighted by
  `w_truth × pull`, against the same spectrum of the target's reco-passing rows. This is what the detector step
  learned, seen in truth space, on the population it can see.
- **Step-2 closure:** the standard R from `push`, over all truth-passing rows.
- **Detector-level closure:** R of the reco E_avail spectrum of the prior's reco-passing rows weighted by
  `w_reco × pull`, against the pseudodata's reco-passing reco E_avail weighted by `w_reco`. This tests whether step
  1 fits what it was trained on.
- **Reading:**
  - Low detector-level and step-1 closure → the change is poorly visible or poorly fitted at detector level.
  - High step-1 closure but low step-2 closure → the truth step loses it.
  - Every reading is compared with the development tilt.

**Q4. Convergence.** PET R at each k = 1…K (raw P) against GBDT R at k = 1…10 (committed G), on D5 and the dev tilt.
Is PET still rising at K, and at what rate per iteration relative to GBDT?

**Q5. Design dependence (committed D only, two DEV draws, descriptive).** D5 NuWro R by PET design and k:
- CTL (carries misses) against C (efficiency-corrected), which isolates the miss rule;
- C against H1 (one-hot PDG truth);
- H1 against H2 (reco summaries);
- S1 (constant rate).

The matched scalar study's D5 NuWro rows separate the miss rule and the truth set for GBDT, IBU and AUSSIE.
