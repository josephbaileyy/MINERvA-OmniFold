## 0. Answer in brief

The finalists lose to the GBDT on the three generator reweightings (D5 NuWro, D5p NuWro′, D5 GiBUU) by
**0.12–0.25 in R**. Measured on the same 8 final-bank draws per case, with the controls in §1:

1. **Not a sample limit.** The exact distortion applied to the prior (the oracle) recovers **0.96** of each case's
   E_avail change. Both methods move too little in the injected direction: the movement fraction m is
   **0.18–0.46** for PET and **0.41–0.62** for the GBDT, against **0.98–0.99** for the oracle (T1).
2. **Most of the gap is already there after the detector step (step 1): about 0.11–0.16 of the 0.12–0.25** (T3c).
   - **Truth-space closure.** On reconstructed events, the step-1-reweighted truth E_avail spectrum recovers
     **0.46–0.57** for PET and **0.61–0.67** for the GBDT. The oracle ceiling is 0.95–0.96.
   - **Detector-level closure.** At detector level, PET's step 1 fits the pseudodata's reco E_avail to
     **0.78–0.84**, against the GBDT's **0.93–0.94**.
   - **The contrast.** On the development tilt, which PET wins, the two detector steps are level: 0.95 against
     0.96 (T3).
   - **What PET misses.** On NuWro, PET's step 1 fits the reco **muon** marginals nearly as well as the GBDT:
     0.86–0.93 against 0.94–0.96 (T3b). The shortfall is concentrated in the **hadronic (reco E_avail)
     direction**. On GiBUU it is in every reco marginal.
3. **The truth step adds a further 0.02–0.11, in the events that are not reconstructed** (T3c, T2).
   - Both methods lose recovery from the reconstructed population to the full one. PET loses more: 0.13–0.36,
     against 0.10–0.25 for the GBDT.
   - In the **low-acceptance region**, PET moves *away* from the target on NuWro and NuWro′: R from −0.76 to
     −1.46, against the GBDT's −0.26 to +0.10. H2S1T24 also moves away on GiBUU (−0.13).
   - On DEV this is a **miss-rule effect** (T6, two draws, descriptive):
     - PET: carrying misses (CTL 0.30–0.39) beats efficiency correction (C 0.19–0.31). B, which carries misses
       and adds reco summaries, reaches 0.40–0.47.
     - GBDT: the miss rule barely matters (efficiency-corrected 0.46–0.53, carried 0.48–0.58).
     - Binned IBU: efficiency correction collapses it (0.09 at k = 3 and −0.19 at k = 10, against 0.49–0.67 when
       misses are carried).
4. **More iterations would not close it** (T5).
   - On D5 PET's final R is flat or falls with k:
     - NuWro: H2S1T24 0.16 → 0.14 over k = 1–5;
     - GiBUU: it peaks at k = 2–3 and falls to 0.32 at k = 5.

     Its step-1 closures keep rising slowly.
   - The GBDT rises and plateaus by k ≈ 4–7.
5. **It is not an input omission.** PET sees the muon at both steps: at step 1, the reconstructed muon object
   including p_T and p_∥ (`fullevent_fps_dataloader` default schema); at step 2, true p_T and p_∥ (the arm's
   baseline truth globals).
   - **Weights track, but project wrongly.** PET's final truth weights track the generator weight as strongly as
     the GBDT's: the slope of log push on log oracle is 0.87–0.90 for PET on NuWro against 0.82, and 0.49–0.54
     against 0.46 on GiBUU (T3). They mis-project it: in the D5 3D cells PET recovers 0.07–0.21, the GBDT 0.23–0.33,
     and the oracle 0.70–0.77 (T4).

**Best-supported location of the loss:**
- **Mainly:** PET's **detector step under-fits the generator-reweighted pseudodata in the hadronic direction**,
  while it fits the development tilt as well as the GBDT.
- **Secondarily:** the **efficiency-corrected truth step's extrapolation into non-reconstructed events**,
  especially at low acceptance.

**What this cannot exclude:**
- **Why the detector step under-fits.** Training budget (8 epochs per step-1 fit), capacity, or the
  12-cluster truncation (which drops about 40 % of cluster energy) are all open. Testing them needs PET training,
  which is not authorized here.
- **Whether data scale (≈ 16× the pseudodata) changes either effect.**

## 3. Reading for the next decision

This is not a recommendation to launch anything. P0 stays deferred (Joseph, 2026-10-06).

- **Stop condition (d).** P0 flags a generator-model blocker if both finalists stay worse than the GBDT on NuWro by
  more than 0.10. **At study scale that condition already holds** (0.21–0.25 on NuWro). The mechanism found here
  is a detector-step fitting deficit that the development tilt does not expose. A data-scale run could change it,
  because 16× more pseudodata gives step 1 more to fit. The study-scale result gives no reason to expect it
  will.
- **The development tilt does not test this.** The study's frozen accuracy endpoints are all tilts or
  species/topology reweightings. On those, PET's step 1 is level with the GBDT, so they cannot reveal this
  weakness. A future design study should include a generator reweighting among its designated endpoints.
- **Candidate PET-side levers.** These are hypotheses only: none was tested, and all need GPU training and a new
  authorization.
  - More step-1 training or capacity, aimed at structure beyond a smooth tilt.
  - Relieving the 12-cluster truncation at step 1.
  - For the extrapolation, a miss rule that does not rely on the efficiency-corrected truth step at low
    acceptance. B, with carried misses, was the best PET design on DEV NuWro.

  Each would have to keep PET's lead on the tilt endpoints.
