## 0. Answer in brief

The finalists lose to the GBDT on the three generator reweightings (D5 NuWro, D5p NuWro′, D5 GiBUU) by
**0.12–0.25 in R**. Measured on the same 8 final-bank draws per case, with the controls in §1:

1. **Not a sample limit.** Applying the exact distortion to the prior (the oracle) recovers **0.96** of each case's
   E_avail change.
   - **Both methods move too little in the injected direction.** The movement fraction m is **0.18–0.46** for PET
     and **0.41–0.62** for the GBDT, against **0.98–0.99** for the oracle.
   - **PET moves more in the wrong direction.** Its orthogonal residual is 0.10–0.37, against 0.08–0.21 for the
     GBDT (T1).
2. **Deficit 1: the detector step (step 1) under-fits the reweighted pseudodata** (T3, T3b).
   - **Detector-level fit.** PET's step 1 fits the pseudodata's reco E_avail to **0.78–0.84**. The GBDT reaches
     **0.93–0.94**, at the oracle's own detector-level ceiling of 0.92–0.93.
   - **Truth space, reconstructed events.** After step 1, the reweighted truth E_avail spectrum recovers
     **0.46–0.57** for PET, against **0.61–0.67** for the GBDT. The oracle reaches 0.95–0.96.
   - **Present from the first iteration.** At k = 1 PET is at 0.58–0.78 and the GBDT at 0.94–0.95, so the deficit
     is not inherited from earlier step-2 passes.
   - **Specific to the generator reweightings.** On the development tilt, which PET wins, the two detector steps
     are level: 0.95 against 0.96, with the oracle at 0.97.
   - **Which reco marginals PET misses depends on the generator.**
     - **NuWro:** the shortfall against the GBDT is larger in reco E_avail (0.10–0.13) than in the reco muon
       marginals (0.03–0.08).
     - **GiBUU:** it is largest in reco muon p_T (0.23–0.27), then in reco E_avail (0.13–0.14).
     - The GiBUU muon changes are only 1.9–2.2× the scorer's floor.
3. **Deficit 2: the extrapolation to non-reconstructed events** (T3c, T3e, T2).
   - **Reconstructed events.** Step 2 reproduces PET's step-1 result almost losslessly: it costs PET 0.00–0.04,
     against 0.05–0.06 for the GBDT. PET's final R there is 0.44–0.57, against the GBDT's 0.56–0.62.
   - **Non-reconstructed events.** PET recovers **−0.05 to 0.30**, against the GBDT's **0.24–0.44**. The oracle
     reaches 0.92–0.93. This deficit inherits deficit 1, since the truth step learns from the step-1 weights, and
     it is not separable from it here.
   - **It grows with iterations.** PET's non-reconstructed R falls: NuWro′ H2S1T24 goes 0.31 → 0.02, and GiBUU
     H2S1T24 goes 0.41 → 0.18. The GBDT's rises (NuWro′ 0.22 → 0.43, GiBUU 0.37 → 0.44).
   - **Low acceptance.** PET moves away from the target in 8/8 NuWro draws for both finalists, against 6/8 for the
     GBDT. On NuWro′ the counts are 7/8 and 8/8, against 3/8; on GiBUU, 5/8 and 1/8, against 0/8.
   - **The miss rule (DEV, two draws).** The DEV design contrasts are consistent with a miss-rule effect (T6):
     - PET: carrying misses (CTL 0.30–0.39) beats efficiency correction (C 0.19–0.31). B, which carries misses
       and adds reco summaries, reaches 0.40–0.47.
     - GBDT: the miss rule barely matters (efficiency-corrected 0.46–0.53, carried 0.48–0.58).
     - Binned IBU: efficiency correction collapses it (0.09 at k = 3 and −0.19 at k = 10, against 0.49–0.67 when
       misses are carried).

   The two deficits are measured on different populations, each normalized to its own injected change, so they
   are not additive shares of the final gap.
4. **Iterations (within k ≤ K, as run: 4 or 5).** More iterations did not raise PET's final R on D5 (T5):
   - NuWro: H2S1T24 0.16 → 0.14 over k = 1–5.
   - GiBUU: it peaks at k = 2–3, then falls to 0.32.

   Its step-1 closures rose while its extrapolation to non-reconstructed events degraded. The GBDT plateaus by
   k ≈ 4–7. Iterations k > K are untested.
5. **Inputs.**
   - **Step 1: an input omission is ruled out.** PET receives the reconstructed muon object, including p_T and p_∥
     (`fullevent_fps_dataloader` default schema), and the reco summaries: reco E_avail, reco q3, and the
     stored-cluster ΣE and count (arm `reco_summaries_pdg_onehot`). This is a superset of the GBDT's reco inputs,
     plus the cluster cloud.
   - **Step 2: an input omission is not ruled out.** PET's truth step gets true p_T and p_∥ and the one-hot truth
     cloud, but not true E_avail or q3, which the GBDT receives. Their effect on the extrapolation is untested
     here.
6. **Weights.** PET's final truth weights respond to the generator weight with an equal or larger slope, but
   correlate with it less, so they carry more variance unrelated to it (T3):
   - slope: 0.87–0.90 against 0.82 on NuWro, and 0.49–0.54 against 0.46 on GiBUU;
   - r: 0.82–0.86 against 0.90, and 0.53–0.58 against 0.72.

   In the D5 3D cells (NuWro and GiBUU; injected change only 1.2× the floor), PET recovers 0.12–0.21, the GBDT
   0.24–0.33 and the oracle 0.76–0.77. The NuWro′ 3D cells and p_∥ marginal are below the floor and undefined (T4).

**What this cannot exclude:**
- **Why the detector step under-fits.** The study's own contrasts constrain it:
  - **Step-1 capacity alone is disfavored.** The finalists differ about 20× in step-1 size (47 k against
    0.97 M parameters), yet their detector-level closures are equal within spread (0.84 against 0.81, 0.79 against
    0.78, 0.81 against 0.80).
  - **The 12-cluster truncation is a weak candidate for the E_avail marginal,** because PET is given reco E_avail
    as a scalar.
  - **The step-1 training budget remains open.** Each fit is 8 epochs. So do the optimization and the way event
    scalars enter the network. Testing any of these needs PET training, which is not authorized here.
- **The true E_avail / q3 truth inputs** (item 5).
- **Whether data scale (≈ 16× the pseudodata) changes either deficit.**

  Only then were its per-iteration weights used.

  reco-passing row, the same check as the comparison's.
- **The weight semantics match, so the step decomposition compares like with like.**
  - In PET and the GBDT alike, `pull` is the cumulative step-1 weight (the previous `push` × the step-1 ratio, on
    reconstructed rows), and `push` is the step-2 ratio.
  - The efficiency-corrected step 2 trains on accepted events, with classes `w_truth` and `w_truth × pull`, and is
    applied to every truth-passing row.
  - Both methods train step 1 on pseudodata weighted `w_reco × distortion`. No row passes reco but fails truth.
  - Sources: `omnifold_nn/omnifold/omnifold.py:201-203`, `improvement_campaign/phase_b/pet/b2_driver.py:212-234`
    and `final_design/scalar/scalar_estimators.py` (`_omnifold_efficiency_corrected`).
- **The undefined rule.** Every reported R outside E_avail uses the scorer's rule: undefined when the injected
  L1 < 0.012 × √(nbins/7). The injected/floor ratios are printed in T3b and T4.
- **Synthetic tests:** `test_gd.py`, 6 tests. They cover movement, wrong-direction movement, weight slope and r,
  the undefined rule and the D5 bins.
