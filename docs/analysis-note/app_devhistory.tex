\subsection{Defects of the earlier two-dimensional budget}
\label{app:devhistory-2dbudget}

This is the account of the earlier 2D budget's construction defects, which \S\ref{sec:uq-2d-scope} names.

\paragraph{Comparison with the earlier 2D budget.} The saved products and code of the earlier 2D budget~\cite{TwoDFollowup2026} show the following defects in its construction; the quoted budget corrects each. First, its systematic sweep has a simulated truth input that differs from that of its seed-42 reference, while its saved reconstructed inputs agree. A common displacement cancels to first order in the MAT mean-centered covariance: the mismatch does not invalidate the entire covariance and supplies no correction to subtract, and because the earlier input and code revision are not available, it cannot be attributed more specifically. The sweep used by the quoted universe block does not have this mismatch.
% (one paragraph since the history restructure's style pass, 2026-11-15: history-cuts/sec_systematics.md S6)
Second, the bands Rvn1pi and Rvp1pi encode the same shift under the configured nonresonant-pion tune, and the earlier budget counted it twice; the quoted universe block drops Rvp1pi and counts it once (\S\ref{sec:uq-universes}). The band is small, \SI{0.088}{\percent}, but the double count is a defect in the construction of the covariance; no conclusion about the published analysis's implementation follows.
%
Third, the earlier budget did not propagate background-template statistics, whose error exceeds one tenth of the data's Poisson error in 60 of 205 saved reconstructed bins (a reconstructed-space diagnostic, not a measured unfolded uncertainty); the quoted statistical block draws the background simulation as its own bootstrap stream (\S\ref{sec:uq-boot}). The values printed for the earlier budget were not changed by these findings.

\subsection{The completeness-divided two-dimensional statistical band}
\label{app:devhistory-band}

This is the account of the first-built statistical band's secondary analysis and rebuild, whose outcome \S\ref{sec:coverage} states.

Unlike the band's replicas, the central value has completeness exactly~1. A secondary analysis, added after the pilot, applies the band's division to the toys. Scored against the same band, the divided toys overcover: $C_1=\covTwoDRepCOne$, with its interval above the window. The attribution rule was fixed before the full run, but its clause for the undercoverage outcome was written after the pilot pulls had been seen. Under this rule the primary undercoverage is attributed to the completeness term of the band. The attribution is partial: in the replica form, the $\pPar$ \SIrange{40}{60}{GeV/c} column still undercovers.

The band was then rebuilt without this term. The resulting undivided band was rescored only post hoc (\S\ref{sec:coverage}), and the quoted statistical block has not been tested.

\subsection{The sixteen-cell run-period block: its conditions}
\label{app:devhistory-fived16pl}

This is the account of the conditions under which the sixteen-cell run-period block $C_{\mathrm{PL,5D}}$ of \S\ref{sec:fived16-pl} would not be added.

Three conditions under which it would not be added were fixed before any of the measurements that decide them was computed: one existing component, within two standard deviations of its own size, explaining at least half of both period-group patterns; a replica spread of the block's median per-cell width above half that width; or, in the median replica, less than half of the replica-built block's variance lying in the observed block's plane. The conditions were set after the block's reported numbers had been read, and the second and third were not expected to fire. None occurred. That result establishes neither the block's size nor the absence of overlap: descriptively, one detector-response pair, a pion-interaction band with the meson response shift, reproduces one period's offset pattern within noise and part of the other two.

\subsection{Lost pseudo-experiments of the generator tests}
\label{app:devhistory-lost}

This is the account of the generator tests' lost pseudo-experiments and their recovery, whose outcome \S\ref{sec:joint5d-lost} states.

Computing time limits interrupted or prevented \jtLostCal{} of the submitted calibration pseudo-experiments (\jtLostInterrupted{} interrupted, \jtLostNever{} never started) and \jtLostPow{} power pseudo-experiments. The decisions in Table~\ref{tab:joint5d} were evaluated on the completed draws. A sensitivity analysis computed before the lost outcomes were known showed that every decision could change under a realizable assignment of them. Had every interrupted draw exceeded the observed statistic, no test would have been rejected ($p$ \jtWorstIntLo--\jtWorstIntHi). Among completed draws, runtime is not associated with the statistic ($|\rho|\le\jtRuntimeRho$), which supports but does not certify the decisions.

The lost seeds were rerun under a procedure that was fixed, and reviewed independently, before the rerun~\cite{JointTestRecovery2026}. A determinism check fixed before the rerun (16 of 16 completed seeds rerun bitwise identically) shows that each recovered draw is the lost draw, so every submitted draw has been observed. No recovered draw reaches the observed statistic in any test, claim variant or robustness variant, so no assignment of the lost draws changes any decision. The closest margin is 4.6 units of $T$ below the observed value of 868.6 (GENIE~2.12.10 + MEC shape, $\pm\jtMoneKappaRobust\,\delta_{\mathrm{fine}}$ variant). The next is 49.8 (GENIE~2.12.10 shape, same variant). With complete batches the stopping rule, fixed before any production pseudo-experiment, would have stopped the Tune~v1, GENIE~2.12.10, GENIE~2.12.10 + MEC and GiBUU nulls one batch earlier ($B=1200$), with the same decisions.

The recovered draws were evaluated in two ways: with the variant shifts of the primary evaluation held fixed (the \emph{fixed-shift} evaluation), and with the shifts recomputed on all draws (the \emph{recomputed-shift} evaluation). The recovery's checks are narrower in scope than the main recomputation. Only the fixed-shift evaluation was cross-checked independently. The recomputed-shift evaluation was reproduced only by a standalone rerun of the same code, a consistency check rather than an independent recomputation. The earlier stops rest on the calibration run's own re-application of the sequential rule and have no independent check. The decisions of Table~\ref{tab:joint5d} remain those of the evaluation before recovery, at the $B$ at which its rule stopped. The observed outcomes of the lost draws supersede the finding of the sensitivity analysis described above.
