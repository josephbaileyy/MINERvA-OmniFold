\section{Development history}
\label{app:devhistory}

This appendix lists configurations and tests that were superseded, stopped or changed during the analysis, and choices made after results had been seen, where they matter for reading a released result. Where the body states it, each row gives what had already been seen when the change or choice was made; a dash means the note does not state it. The chronology behind the rows is kept in the analysis repository's records~\cite{NoteRecords2026} and its commit history.
