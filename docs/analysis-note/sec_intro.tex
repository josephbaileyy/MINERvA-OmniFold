\subsection{Motivation and previous measurements}

Long-baseline accelerator experiments study neutrino oscillations through changes in beam flavor. Oscillation probabilities depend on neutrino energy, which experiments reconstruct from the observed particles using a model of neutrino--nucleus scattering. At a few GeV, quasielastic scattering, resonance production, and deep-inelastic scattering can produce similar detector signatures, and interactions within the nucleus can change which particles emerge. Differential cross-section measurements constrain the interaction models that experiments such as T2K, NOvA, Hyper-Kamiokande, and DUNE use to predict event rates and reconstruct neutrino energies~\cite{T2K:2023osc,NOvA:2021nfi,HyperK:2018design,DUNE:2020jqi}.

Using the MINERvA detector~\cite{MINERvA:2013zvz} in Fermilab's medium-energy NuMI beam~\cite{MINERvA:2016iqn}, Ruterbories \emph{et al.} reported the inclusive charged-current (CC) $\nu_\mu$ cross section on hydrocarbon at a mean neutrino energy of about $6\,\mathrm{GeV}$, differential in the muon momentum transverse ($p_T$) and longitudinal ($p_\parallel$) to the beam~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq}. That measurement used D'Agostini iterative Bayesian unfolding (IBU) with a binned detector-response matrix~\cite{DAgostini:1994fjx}.

Binned implementations of IBU, singular-value decomposition, and TUnfold require a response grid that grows with the number of observables~\cite{Adye:2011gm,Hocker:1995kb,Schmitt:2012kp}. Unbinned machine-learning methods use individual events~\cite{Arratia:2021otl,Huetsch:2024quz,Canelli:2025guide} to reweight a reference simulation or generate unfolded samples~\cite{Datta:2018mwd,Bellagente:2019uyp,Bellagente:2020piv,Backes:2022vmn}. OmniFold uses classifiers to estimate the likelihood ratios that iteratively reweight simulated events~\cite{Andreassen:2019cjw,Andreassen:2021zzk}. Adding observables changes the classifier inputs, and the resulting event weights can be used for different projections and reporting bins.

H1 pioneered unbinned unfolding on collider data with eight-dimensional lepton--jet correlations and later unfolded high-$Q^2$ jet substructure in deep-inelastic scattering~\cite{H1:2021wkz,H1:2023fzk}. LHCb measured identified-hadron distributions in $Z$-tagged jets~\cite{LHCb:2022rky}. ATLAS jointly unfolded 24 $Z$+jets observables and later measured the full charged-particle phase space~\cite{ATLAS:2024jets,ATLAS:2026fullps}. Recent work has also addressed background treatment in reweighting and generative approaches~\cite{Falcao:2025backgrounds,Butter:2025genfold}.

Huang \emph{et al.} gave the first neutrino cross-section application of OmniFold, comparing an unfolding of muon kinematics in simulated T2K near-detector data with traditional binned methods~\cite{Huang:2025omnifold}.

\subsection{Objectives and scope}

We apply OmniFold to MINERvA's public data. The two-dimensional (2D) muon cross section, $d^2\sigma/(dp_T\,dp_\parallel)$, agrees in its integral with MINERvA's published measurement~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq} within about $1\%$ and shows a correlated shape difference of undetermined origin (Sec.~\ref{sec:muon-cross-section-comparison}). We then add available hadronic energy $E_{\mathrm{avail}}$, the magnitude of the three-momentum transfer $q_3$, and hadronic invariant mass $W$ to test how well the data constrain their distributions and correlations (Sec.~\ref{sec:part-higher-dimensional}).

We report the five-observable cross section in sixteen regions. All three hydrocarbon generator predictions lie below the unfolded integrals in every high-$p_T$ cell (Sec.~\ref{sec:sixteen-cell-generator-comparison}). Agreement in the tested projections checks consistency with the lower-dimensional results. Held-out interval shortfalls, dependence on the starting distribution and unfolding algorithm, sensitivity to rounding-level input changes, and the unexplained run-period difference limit the use of these results to constrain models (Sec.~\ref{sec:result-validity}).
