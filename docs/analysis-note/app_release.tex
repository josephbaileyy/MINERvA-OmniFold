\subsubsection{Scope of reproduction and required inputs}
\label{app:release-scope}

The public ancillary files of Ref.~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq} supply the published inputs for the comparisons to its central values and covariance, the standardized differences, the tension anatomy, and the MINERvA~Tune~v1 model comparison, but not for this analysis's unfolded 2D uncertainty construction.

RC7 does not support extraction from the archived pseudo-experiment products (about \SI{2.3}{GB}), event-level unfolding, the quoted closure and bias measurements, or the measurements behind the covariance's status (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}). Nor does it reproduce the 2D uncertainty and coverage figures, the low-recoil comparisons, the recoil-response sensitivity, or the proxy convergence rate of joint-test condition~(i). The multiplier scan of the joint tests is recomputable from RC7's sufficient inputs with the scan code of Ref.~\cite{JointTestKappa2026}, but it is not part of RC7's own verification. Event processing requires the earlier production of MINERvA \href{https://doi.org/10.15484/3022562}{open-data AnaTuples}, event-loop outputs, and the generator samples used here.

\subsubsection{Bin edges}
\label{app:release-edges}

\noindent The bin edges are the one thing a reader cannot recover from a checksum:
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=2em]
  \item $\pT$, \si{GeV/c}: $0$, $0.07$, $0.15$, $0.25$, $0.33$, $0.40$, $0.47$, $0.55$, $0.70$, $0.85$, $1.00$, $1.25$, $1.50$, $2.50$, $4.50$ (14 bins).
  \item $\pPar$, \si{GeV/c}: $1.5$, $2$, $2.5$, $3$, $3.5$, $4$, $4.5$, $5$, $6$, $7$, $8$, $9$, $10$, $15$, $20$, $40$, $60$ (16 bins).
  \item $\Eavail$, \si{GeV}: $0$, $0.1$, $0.2$, $0.4$, $0.8$, $1.5$, $3.0$, $100$ (7 bins).
  \item $q_{3}$, \si{GeV/c}: $0$, $0.2$, $0.4$, $0.6$, $0.8$, $1.2$, $2.0$, $100$ (7 bins).
  \item $W$, \si{GeV}: $0$, $1.1$, $1.4$, $1.8$, $2.2$, $3.0$, $100$ (6 bins).
\end{itemize}
The last bin on $\Eavail$, $q_{3}$ and $W$ is a catch bin to 100 in the axis unit (\si{GeV}, or \si{GeV/c} for $q_{3}$), and the $\pT\times\pPar$ grid carries the 19 unreported high-$\pT$, low-$\pPar$ acceptance cells of \S\ref{sec:method-binning}.

\paragraph{Using the projected covariance.} The covariance of the $(\Eavail,W)$ projection is $C_{EW}=M\,C_{Z}\,M^{\top}$, with $M$ the projection matrix and $C_{Z}$ the five-axis covariance of the configuration-(a) central value; a worked example that checks the package's ordering, mask and weights end to end is in the records document. The per-cell relative uncertainty is $\sqrt{(C_{EW})_{rr}}/y_{r}$. Because $M$ carries the dropped axes' widths, $\mathbf{y}$ and $C_{EW}$ are on the \emph{density} scale. Converting cell $r$ to an integrated cross section requires multiplying by that cell's own $\Delta\Eavail\,\Delta W$, and the catch bins make that factor large. $C_{EW}$ should \emph{not} be inverted without a specified regularization: its spectrum runs continuously down to the double-precision noise floor, so an inverse is set by the regularizer rather than by the data. The estimator-seed sensitivity, which exceeds its limit (the first measurement of \S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}), is why no significance is computed from it.

\paragraph{Unspecified acceptance field.} The record of the projection carries \texttt{acceptance\_question: UNDECLARED}, stored metadata written when the projection was built, before it had been decided whether an acceptance correction is needed at the destination binning. No separate acceptance weight at the destination binning was specified, and none is applied to Fig.~\ref{fig:eavailWexcess} (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}); whether one is needed remains the open question the field records. The package must carry the field with its stored value until that is decided, so that no default in the data format answers that question.
