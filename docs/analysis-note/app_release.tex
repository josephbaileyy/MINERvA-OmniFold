\section{The result package: what a reader needs, and what is pinned today}
\label{app:release}

Rebuilding the three PDFs of this note is document assembly. It is not
reproduction of any scientific result, and this appendix exists so that the
difference is not left implicit. App.~\ref{app:provenance} lists the internal
records that statements rest on; this appendix specifies the \emph{external}
release that would let a reader outside the collaboration recompute the quoted
numbers, records the identities that are already fixed, and states plainly
which parts do not exist yet.

\paragraph{Status, measured.} \textbf{No release tag and no release manifest
exist.} Measured in this checkout on 2026-09-21: 35 git tags, of which 30 are
\texttt{evidence/*} preservation anchors, one is a \texttt{freeze/*} anchor and
four are deployment tags; none is a publication-results release. The adopted
covariance and the five-axis central values are not in the repository at all
--- they live on \texttt{/pscratch} on the compute system and are identified
here by digest (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-construction}). Consequently the release
below is a \emph{specification plus an identity register}, not an index of
something already published. Assembling it is packaging work on existing
products: it requires no reanalysis and no new compute.

\subsection{What today's reader can and cannot recompute}
\label{app:release-scope}

Recomputable from public inputs: everything in the 2D reproduction that uses
the published data release --- the comparison to
Ref.~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq}'s central values and covariance, the standardized
differences, the tension anatomy, and the MINERvA~Tune~v1 model comparison, all
of which read only the public ancillary files.

Not recomputable from public inputs: every $\Eavail$, $q_{3}$ and $W$ result.
These need the MINERvA production AnaTuples (collaboration-internal), the
event-loop outputs derived from them, the four truth-level generator samples
produced for this analysis, and the adopted five-axis covariance. A reader
without those can check internal consistency of the numbers quoted here and
nothing more.

\subsection{Release schema}
\label{app:release-schema}

The package must carry all of the following. Items marked $\checkmark$ have
their identity or value already fixed and are listed in
\S\ref{app:release-identities}; the rest are packaging.

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Reporting bin edges, per axis, in physical units}
        $\checkmark$: $\pT$ and $\pPar$ in \si{GeV/c}, $\Eavail$, $q_{3}$ and
        $W$ in \si{GeV}, each with its wide terminal catch bin stated as such.
  \item \textbf{The flattened-cell ordering} $\checkmark$: the dense
        $14\times16\times7\times7\times6$ grid in C order, with the reported
        subset given explicitly as an index array rather than implied by a
        positivity cut.
  \item \textbf{Central values}: the five-axis array and every projection
        quoted, each with its own digest, on the density scale, in
        \si{cm^2/nucleon} divided by the product of its kept axes' units.
  \item \textbf{Covariances} $\checkmark$: the adopted five-axis object and its
        $(\Eavail,W)$ projection, with the assembly algebra of
        Eqs.~\eqref{eq:zassembly}--\eqref{eq:zinflation}, the declared
        centering variant, and the band partition.
  \item \textbf{Masks} $\checkmark$: the support mask, the pinned mask (bins
        where the inflation denominator vanished and $g$ was set to $1$ by
        declaration), and the unreported-cell mask.
  \item \textbf{Projection matrices} $\checkmark$: $M$ itself, with its
        content digest, its shape, and the statement that its entries are the
        dropped axes' bin-width products --- without which the destination's
        units are ambiguous.
  \item \textbf{Generator predictions}: the four truth-level $(\Eavail,W)$
        predictions (GENIE-CV, GENIE$+$Valencia~2p2h, NuWro, GiBUU) and the
        MINERvA~Tune~v1 ancillary model, as arrays on the release binning
        rather than as figure curves, each with its generator version,
        configuration, target and flux.
  \item \textbf{Provenance} $\checkmark$: for each object, the producing
        revision, the producing job, and the digest of the bytes as read back
        out of the closed file.
  \item \textbf{One minimal worked example}: \S\ref{app:release-example}.
  \item \textbf{The four measurements} that travel with the adopted covariance
        (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}), carried \emph{in} the package and not
        only in this note, since the package is what a reader will quote from.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{One minimal projection example}
\label{app:release-example}

This is the whole computation a reader should be able to run against the
package, and it is the check that the ordering, the mask and the weights were
released correctly.

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item Load the five-axis central values $\mathbf{x}$ and the reported-cell
        index array. Assert $\mathbf{x}$ has \num{65856} entries and the index
        array \latBins.
  \item Load $M$ (shape $\zprojCells\times\latBins$) and $C_{Z}$ (shape
        $\latBins\times\latBins$).
  \item Form $\mathbf{y}=M\mathbf{x}_{\text{reported}}$ and reshape to
        $7\times6$ \textbf{in C order}. This must reproduce the
        $(\Eavail,W)$ central-value map of Fig.~\ref{fig:eavailWexcess}. The
        F-order reshape is the discriminating control: the C-order reshape
        agrees with the figure to a relative $1.7\times10^{-16}$ and the
        F-order reshape disagrees at relative $1.0$, which is how the ordering
        was established rather than assumed.
  \item Form $C_{EW}=M\,C_{Z}\,M^{\top}$ and check
        $\sqrt{\Tr C_{EW}}=\SI{4.4552e-39}{}$ and exact symmetry
        ($\max|C-C^{\top}|=0$). \textbf{Do not check a rank.} The spectrum
        decays smoothly with no plateau, so the retained count is set by
        whatever cutoff is used --- 26 at $10^{-6}$, 36 at the $10^{-12}$ the
        projector hardcodes, 41 at \texttt{numpy.linalg.matrix\_rank}'s
        default, 42 at none --- and a reader following a ``rank should be 36''
        instruction with NumPy defaults would get 41 and wrongly conclude the
        package is corrupt. Check instead that
        $\lambda_{\min}/\lambda_{\max}\approx2.9\times10^{-15}$ and that
        $\lambda_{\min}$ is positive, and treat the object as ill-conditioned
        rather than as having a definite rank.
  \item Read the per-cell relative uncertainty as
        $\sqrt{(C_{EW})_{rr}}/y_{r}$. Because $M$ carries the dropped axes'
        widths, $\mathbf{y}$ and $C_{EW}$ are on the \emph{density} scale:
        converting cell $r$ to an integrated cross section requires
        multiplying by that cell's own $\Delta\Eavail\,\Delta W$, and the
        catch bins make that factor large.
  \item Do \emph{not} invert $C_{EW}$ without a declared regularization: its
        spectrum runs continuously down to the double-precision noise floor, so
        the inverse is set by the regularizer rather than by the data, and
        \S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}'s first measurement is the reason no
        significance is computed from it at all.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{One field is undeclared and is named rather than defaulted.} The
projection receipt records \texttt{acceptance\_question: UNDECLARED}: whether
an acceptance correction is owed at the destination binning was not decided
when the projection was built. The released package must carry that field with
that value until it is decided, because a silent default here would be a
scientific choice made by a data format.
