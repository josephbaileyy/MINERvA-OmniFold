\subsection{Closure tests across dimensions}
\label{sec:closure-map}

Only the MINERvA simulation supplies a detector response, the conditional distribution of reconstructed events given truth. Other generators (GiBUU, NuWro, GENIE variants) supply truth only. Every closure test here therefore keeps the simulated MINERvA response and changes the truth distribution, by reweighting the simulated events, or changes the analysis chain around it. Reweighting to another generator's shape is a valid known-truth test of the unfolding. It does not test the detector model. An ordinary closure, whose pseudo-data are the simulation itself, is an identity check that a constant estimator would also pass. Table~\ref{tab:closure-map} lists, for each test, what it changes relative to the unfolding's simulation and what its result can detect.

\paragraph{Comparison of injected-bump and generator-shaped closure tests.} The injected-bump and generator-shaped tests differ in deformation, metric and iteration dependence. Their results do not contradict each other.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Deformation.} The closing injections are single smooth bumps in one variable the classifiers see directly. A generator ratio changes correlated structure across $\Eavail$, $W$ and $q_3$, including directions the reconstructed observables constrain weakly (\S\ref{sec:val-departure}, comparison with exact binned unfolding).
  \item \emph{Metric.} The 3D, 4D and 5D closures are judged on one-dimensional marginals, where per-cell errors average out. The 3D test's per-bin spread is already \SI{3.2}{\percent}, and the 5D test's is \SI{3.7}{\percent}. The generator-shaped failures are measured in joint $(\Eavail,W)$ cells.
  \item \emph{Iterations.} At five iterations the estimator retains part of the prior wherever the reconstructed data constrain the truth weakly. Thirty iterations reduce the $\Eavail$ residual by \SI{26}{\percent} while the $q_3$ residual grows (\S\ref{sec:val-departure}).
  \item \emph{Five-dimensional $W$ test.} A $W$ bump at the $\Delta$ peak is recovered to a ratio of 0.180 against a threshold of 0.20, a narrow pass in the marginal only. The same unfolding result leaves joint $(\Eavail,W)$ cells in the peak column up to \SI{9.4}{\percent} low and moves part of the bump into the neighboring $W$ bins. A single smooth bump in a marginal is therefore weaker evidence than the generator-shaped tests, which remain the main five-dimensional recovery evidence of \S\ref{sec:5d}.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Hidden-variable and alternative-model closures.} On the $205$ reported bins the per-amplitude median residuals, $0.57\% / 1.14\% / 3.43\%$ for $A = 0.05/0.10/0.30$, are linear in $A$, as expected for small perturbations, with fitted slope $\kappa_{\text{leak}} = \boxed{0.113}$ (App.~\ref{sec:closure-hv}): about $11\%$ of a hidden $\dpT$ bias leaks into the measured $(\pT,\pPar)$ marginal. Against the $3.0\%$ acceptance bound, two of the three arms pass and the largest does not: $A=0.30$ gives $3.43\%$, which \emph{exceeds} $3.0\%$ (Table~\ref{tab:closure-map}). The scan establishes the linearity and the coefficient: the acceptance bound is satisfied for hidden-$\dpT$ deformations up to roughly $A\simeq0.26$ and is violated above it. No non-closure uncertainty is assigned from this scan either way (\S\ref{sec:validation}). A $\dpT$-shaped mismodeling at the $30\%$ level would leak more than the analysis specified as tolerable; this is an open sensitivity, not a passing test. In-acceptance biases are $-1.68\%$ for \texttt{MaCCQE:0}, removed by extrapolation, and $-7.32\%$ for \texttt{Flux:50}. Both residuals are below the $2.0\%$ bound by at least a factor of $5$. Those residuals are the recovery relative to the constructed reference (App.~\ref{sec:closure-alt}). They do not establish robustness to model variations in general.

\paragraph{Three-dimensional closure.}
\label{sec:3d-closure}
The unfolding recovers a known $+\SI{30}{\percent}$ Gaussian bump injected into pseudo-data in \emph{truth} $\Eavail$ (center \SI{0.3}{GeV}, width \SI{0.15}{GeV}). This reweighting closure tests the 3D unfolding of configuration~(a) only and does not extend to (e). It covers one shape, this Gaussian, injected into the pseudo-data and unfolded \emph{with the same detector response that the unfolding uses}. For this injection the estimator neither removes the injected feature nor creates a spurious one. The recovered step-2 mean weight is $1.048$ against the injected $1.0485$. Residuals against reweighted truth on the $\Eavail$-marginal have median $0.9999$ and standard deviation \textbf{\num{0.0006}} (\SI{0.06}{\percent}). No acceptance threshold set before the test is documented. The test has no pass or fail verdict. The test does \emph{not} exclude estimator bias for other deformations (Table~\ref{tab:closure-map}).
