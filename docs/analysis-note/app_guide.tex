\section*{Guide to the appendices}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Guide to the appendices}

The reader's guide in the front matter gives the routes through the whole note; this guide covers the appendices.

The appendices fall into three groups. The first gives methods and context for the body: statistical methods and the 2D comparison (App.~\ref{app:statmethods}) and those for the multidimensional results and inference (App.~\ref{app:statmethods-nd}); unbinned background subtraction (App.~\ref{app:negweight}); the protocol for a detector-response-mismatch closure, which rescales the reconstructed available energy of the pseudo-data while OmniFold keeps its nominal response (App.~\ref{app:response-mismatch}); and the survey of MINERvA measurement dimensionality (App.~\ref{app:landscape}). The second covers point-cloud (PET) representations, which are diagnostic method development (App.~\ref{sec:pet}). The third describes the result package and what it reproduces (App.~\ref{app:release}) and the code layout (App.~\ref{app:codebase}), with the development history (App.~\ref{app:devhistory}); it is reference material and is not needed to follow the physics results.

The last appendix, App.~\ref{sec:fps}, is the extended-fiducial study, a preliminary exploratory study of acceptance recovery with no finalized cross section (Table~\ref{tab:result-status}).

The provenance of the records the note cites, the fixed identities and audit record of the five-axis covariance of the configuration-(a) central value, the test-suite discipline, and superseded values and withdrawn results are kept in the analysis's records document, Ref.~\cite{NoteRecords2026}; each citation names the entry's number or heading, and the entries cited are designated for inclusion in the Zenodo reproduction package.

\paragraph{Labels used in the repository.} The body describes each object in words: the learner backends (\S\ref{sec:method-config}), the background-subtraction modes (\S\ref{sec:method-algo}, App.~\ref{app:negweight}), the centering of the five-axis covariance (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-construction}), the re-selected universes (\S\ref{sec:uq-higherdim}) and the universe sweeps of the quoted universe block (\S\ref{sec:uq-universes}) and of the earlier 2D budget (\S\ref{sec:uq-2d-scope}). Where a repository label is needed to find the object, it is given once in parentheses at first use. The learner backends are labeled \texttt{lgbm}, \texttt{exact} and \texttt{hist}: LightGBM (the quoted central values and their uncertainty ensembles; also the joint-test estimator), the scikit-learn exact-split estimator (the exact-split cross-check) and the scikit-learn histogram estimator (\S\ref{sec:method-config}).

Products described as flux-corrected are those repaired for an audit finding that the $N$-dimensional code divided every flux universe by the central-value flux; App.~\ref{app:negweight} uses the term.

The calls behind Eq.~\eqref{eq:bootcov}, \S\ref{app:poisson} and \S\ref{sec:invert} (default cutoff) are \texttt{np.cov(Xrep, rowvar=False, ddof=1)}, \texttt{np.random.\allowbreak default\_rng().\allowbreak poisson(1.0)} and \texttt{np.linalg.pinv}.
