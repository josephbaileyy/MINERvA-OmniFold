We report a simultaneous measurement of the $\nu_{\mu}$ charged-current inclusive cross section in five observables: the muon transverse and longitudinal momenta, available hadronic energy, three-momentum transfer and hadronic invariant mass. The cross section is unfolded without binning with OmniFold from MINERvA medium-energy open data. It is reported in sixteen cells, with a covariance whose median per-cell uncertainty is \fvval{sigmed-reported}\%. Three external generator predictions, compared on all sixteen cells, give $\chi^{2}$ between \fvval{g-ext-chi2int-min16} and \fvval{g-ext-chi2int-max16} for 16 degrees of freedom. GiBUU, compared only on the 8 cells its sample covers, gives $\chi^{2}=\fvval{g-gibuu-total-chi2int}$ on those cells. The coverage of the result's intervals has not been validated; a bias test at two held-out GENIE~v3 truths finds the estimator's bias below the reported uncertainty in every cell but does not test interval widths. In a fake-data study, the estimator's bias exceeds the thresholds set against the reported uncertainty at a GiBUU-like truth and at a synthetic $q_{3}$ tilt.
