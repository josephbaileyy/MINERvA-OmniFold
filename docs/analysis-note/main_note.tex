\begin{abstract}
Using unbinned OmniFold on MINERvA's public data at a mean neutrino energy of about $6\,\mathrm{GeV}$, we report the flux-averaged inclusive charged-current $\nu_\mu$ cross section on hydrocarbon in sixteen cells of muon transverse and longitudinal momentum, available hadronic energy, three-momentum transfer, and hadronic invariant mass. The muon-only cross section agrees with MINERvA's published integral to about $1\%$, with a correlated shape difference of undetermined origin. The sixteen cells sum to $\fpeval{round((\fvval{cr-QA-s16}+\fvval{cr-QB-s16}+\fvval{cr-QC-s16}+\fvval{cr-QD-s16})/10,3)}\times10^{-38}\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ per nucleon, and their muon-quadrant sums agree with the muon-only result within $\fpeval{round(abs(\fvval{cr-QC-dpct}),1)}\%$. At two generator tunes held out for validation, simulation tests show a residual unfolding bias and uncertainties too small for the observed deviations.
\end{abstract}
