\begin{abstract}

We study multidimensional medium-energy charged-current neutrino--nucleus
cross sections on MINERvA open data with OmniFold, an unbinned machine-learning
unfolding method. An independent unfolding method reproduces the integral of the published double-differential cross section in muon transverse and longitudinal
momentum within about one percent, with a correlated shape difference, and reproduces its uncertainty scale.
% Methods-paper sync (M8, narrowed: "including its spectrum" contradicted the paper's correlated shape difference;
% main_paper.tex abstract; values.tex:48, :125).
That uncertainty is computed with the
central value's own tree-learning implementation, with its universe and statistical blocks at the
central value's training seed; whether it describes the central value has not been measured.
% FACT (2026-10-18): the "different tree-learning implementation" clause was stale since 2026-10-11 (DECISION-20261011);
% the universe and statistical blocks of Q are at the central value's seed (stageC/production/DESIGN.md §3.4), the
% training-seed block spans five seed settings (§3.5). Review M1.
% J3/J4 provisional (DECISION-20261018, packet C1 and C8: kept; W3: D10 KI-88 in sec:uq-boot).
% plain-language pass (DECISION-20261018-joseph-plain-language-pass)
Adding available energy
($\Eavail$), three-momentum transfer ($q_{3}$), and hadronic invariant mass ($W$)
gives simultaneous \emph{unbinned} three-, four-, and five-observable MINERvA unfolds.
Each passes marginal-normalization checks against the lower-dimensional result to
% plain-language pass, review fix M2, director ruling on base defect note M2 (DECISION-20261018-joseph-plain-language-pass)
$\approx$1--2\,\%. Binned MINERvA measurements already reach triple-differential;
the methodological feature here is simultaneous unbinned unfolding of five
related, not independent, observables. The tested generators
under-predict the central values at low $\Eavail$, in the region of the
established MINERvA low-recoil discrepancy, and the central values show a
descriptive excess over the MINERvA-tuned simulation at high $\Eavail$ and high
$W$, where four external
generators also under-predict the data, each by about its overall shortfall
after accounting for high-energy neutrinos absent from the GiBUU sample, and
with an uncorrected carbon-versus-CH target difference for NuWro and GiBUU. A
fifth of that excess sits in the single widest overflow cell, in ratio it also
extends to low $W$, and the regularization bias is largest there; these
comparisons locate no interaction mechanism.
% Fix round (review MINOR 9, 12): the paper's caveats (paper_body.tex sec:central); grammar.
% Methods-paper sync (M8, removed: the multinucleon attribution of the low-recoil discrepancy; paper_body.tex
% sec:central).
Closure tests that include backgrounds show a bias from the purity subtraction,
which a negative-weight background treatment largely removes; that treatment is
used only for the generator tests, and the central values keep the purity
subtraction and its bias of up to 4\% in the highest-$W$ cells.
% Fix round (review MINOR 13): paper_body.tex sec:conclusions.
Tests that reweight the
truth shape by about the size of the differences between generators show a
residual bias, interpreted as regularization bias, that no quoted uncertainty includes. Coverage and
% plain-language pass, review fix N2 (DECISION-20261018-joseph-plain-language-pass)
estimator-seed studies show further limitations: statistical intervals miscover at the tested truths.
With an envelope over five tested truth deformations, none of the tested
reporting definitions satisfies the adopted 10\% criterion. As an application
illustrating calibrated inference with a biased estimator, calibrated joint
tests reject the hybrid null hypotheses for all five tested generator
predictions, in total and in shape, at a 5\% family-wise error rate under the
stated calibration assumptions; they are not physical exclusions, and NuWro's
total-test rejection is not robust to a hydrocarbon-target correction. These
tests are valid only under stated conditions.
% Methods-paper sync (M8: residual bias interpreted as regularization bias; miscoverage at the tested truths; the
% 10% criterion wording; hybrid nulls; familywise statement; DECISION-20261020-joseph-nuwro-ch-claim.md, provisional).
They recover known
% plain-language pass, review fix m6 (DECISION-20261018-joseph-plain-language-pass)
discrepancies, but do not demonstrate discriminating power beyond that of matched
coarse projections. Their interpretation is limited by the omission of an uncertainty
on the calorimetric recoil response, and by the fine grid on which the null hypotheses
are defined, whose convergence has not been shown. A scalar study examines the effect of removing
the truth-level muon kinematic cuts. Point-cloud (PET) representations of the
full event are future work, documented separately; this note uses no PET result.

\end{abstract}
