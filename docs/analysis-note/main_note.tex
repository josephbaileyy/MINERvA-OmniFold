\begin{abstract}

% Register pass b8 (2026-10-22): one claim per sentence, each with its scope; every number, limitation and
% disclosure kept (ledger.md, rows AB*). The source comments of the earlier passes below are kept.
We study multidimensional medium-energy charged-current neutrino--nucleus
cross sections on MINERvA open data with OmniFold, an unbinned machine-learning
unfolding method. With this method, the double-differential cross section in muon
transverse and longitudinal momentum reproduces the published integral within about one percent, with a
correlated shape difference, and its uncertainty is comparable in size to the published one.
% Methods-paper sync (M8, narrowed: "including its spectrum" contradicted the paper's correlated shape difference;
% main_paper.tex abstract; values.tex:48, :125).
That uncertainty is computed with the central value's own estimator, its universe and statistical blocks at the
central value's training seed, and it has not been independently validated.
% FACT (2026-10-18): the "different tree-learning implementation" clause was stale since 2026-10-11 (DECISION-20261011);
% the universe and statistical blocks of Q are at the central value's seed (stageC/production/DESIGN.md §3.4), the
% training-seed block spans five seed settings (§3.5). Review M1.
% J3/J4 provisional (DECISION-20261018, packet C1 and C8: kept; W3: D10 KI-88 in sec:uq-boot).
% plain-language pass (DECISION-20261018-joseph-plain-language-pass)
% Register pass b8: the earlier not-measured clause restated as the body's status
% (sec_systematics.tex "Status of the 2D result": intervals, bias and covariance not independently validated).
Adding available energy
($\Eavail$), three-momentum transfer ($q_{3}$), and hadronic invariant mass ($W$)
gives simultaneous \emph{unbinned} three-, four-, and five-observable MINERvA unfolds,
each of which passes marginal-normalization checks against the lower-dimensional result to
% plain-language pass, review fix M2, director ruling on base defect note M2 (DECISION-20261018-joseph-plain-language-pass)
$\approx$1--2\,\%. Binned MINERvA measurements already reach triple-differential;
the methodological feature here is simultaneous unbinned unfolding of five
related, not independent, observables. In central values, the tested generators
under-predict the data at low $\Eavail$, in the region of the
established MINERvA low-recoil discrepancy. Relative to the MINERvA-tuned simulation, the data show a
descriptive excess at high $\Eavail$ and high $W$: a fifth of the positive difference from that simulation lies in the single
widest overflow cell, the
regularization bias is largest in this region, and in ratio the excess also extends to low $W$. Four external
generators also under-predict the data there, each by about its overall shortfall
once the high-energy neutrinos absent from the GiBUU sample are accounted for, and the NuWro and GiBUU
comparisons carry an uncorrected carbon-versus-CH target difference. These
comparisons locate no interaction mechanism.
% Fix round (review MINOR 9, 12): the paper's caveats (paper_body.tex sec:central); grammar.
% Methods-paper sync (M8, removed: the multinucleon attribution of the low-recoil discrepancy; paper_body.tex
% sec:central).
Closure tests that include backgrounds show a bias from the purity subtraction,
which a negative-weight background treatment largely removes. The two- and five-dimensional central values and all
quoted uncertainties use the purity subtraction; the five-dimensional central value keeps its bias of up to 4\% in the
highest-$W$ cells, which no quoted uncertainty includes. In two dimensions, such a test puts the purity and refined
negative-weight estimates more than one statistical standard deviation apart in 14 of 205 cells, while the refined
estimate stays within 0.44 statistical standard deviations of the truth in every cell; the two-dimensional result is being moved to the refined
estimator. No full five-dimensional
comparison of the two background treatments has been made, and the revisit of the choice of purity is in progress.
The negative-weight treatment is used for the three- and four-dimensional central values, in the generator tests,
in some five-dimensional validation studies and in two-dimensional equivalence studies.
% Fix round (review MINOR 13): paper_body.tex sec:conclusions.
% Register pass b8: the earlier generator-tests-only scope was incomplete: Table tab:estimators-matrix, configurations
% (d) and (e), give studies of sec:val-negweight--sec:gbdt-model-dependence (sec:coverage-5d uses (c), purity);
% the open revisit and the missing full 5D comparison: app_negweight.tex sec:negweight-footing and "Limits of the
% five-dimensional evidence" (review round 1: S1, the bias belongs to the central values only; B2; S3).
Tests that reweight the
truth shape by about the size of the differences between generators show a
residual bias, interpreted as regularization bias, that no quoted uncertainty includes.
% plain-language pass, review fix N2 (DECISION-20261018-joseph-plain-language-pass)
Statistical intervals miscover at the tested truths, and
with an envelope over five tested truth deformations none of the tested
reporting definitions satisfies the predeclared 10\% criterion. As an application
illustrating calibrated inference with a biased estimator, joint tests calibrated in simulation reject the
hybrid null hypotheses for all five tested generator predictions, in total and in shape, under six stated
conditions and at a 5\% family-wise error rate under the stated calibration assumptions.
% Methods-paper sync (M8: residual bias interpreted as regularization bias; miscoverage at the tested truths; the
% 10% criterion wording; hybrid nulls; familywise statement; DECISION-20261020-joseph-nuwro-ch-claim.md, provisional).
% plain-language pass, review fix m6 (DECISION-20261018-joseph-plain-language-pass)
The tests recover known discrepancies and are not physical exclusions. They show no evidence of
discrimination beyond matched coarse projections, omit an uncertainty on the calorimetric recoil response, and
assume convergence of the fine grid on which the null hypotheses are defined, which has not been shown.
NuWro's total-test rejection is not robust to a hydrocarbon-target correction.
A scalar study examines the effect of removing
the truth-level muon kinematic cuts. Point-cloud (PET) representations of the
full event are future work, documented separately; this note uses no PET result.

\end{abstract}
