The test is a bias test, not a coverage test: its Poisson pseudo-experiments do not draw the systematic parameters, so they cannot test the interval widths, and the detector response is not probed. A coverage test at these two tunes, declared in advance for the covariance without the run-period block, with pseudo-experiments that also draw the flux, interaction and detector uncertainties (the lateral and normalization ones as surrogates, and $C_{\mathrm{model}}$ and $C_{\mathrm{resp}}$ not at all), was stopped before any coverage pseudo-experiment ran. At one of its gates, the ratio of each cell's uncertainty reproduced through the pseudo-experiments to that of the drawn covariance lay outside \fvval{cov1b-win-lo}--\fvval{cov1b-win-hi} in \fvval{cov1b-ncells-out} of the 16 cells, ranging from \fvval{cov1b-rho-min} to \fvval{cov1b-rho-max}. Replacing the pseudo-experiments' values by the covariance's for any \fvval{cov1b-kmax-word} or fewer of the \fvval{cov1b-nbands} drawn bands does not bring every cell inside that window.
The coverage of the result's intervals has not been validated. A staged coverage test at the two tunes, with the covariance rebuilt by the same procedure at each tune, ended without a coverage result: its first stage stopped under a fail-closed rule declared in advance. That rule stops the stage if any declared draw makes a cell of the drawn natures' composite denominator non-positive. In a cell, that denominator is the first drawn universe's denominator plus each later universe's departure from the central-value denominator, and a cell that is positive at the central value and non-positive in the sum is the failure. \lrval{stop-flagged} of the \lrval{stop-draws} declared draws failed, with at most \lrval{stop-maxcells} such cells in a draw. The record does not establish why those cells are non-positive. The stop concerns the declared composition of the natures, not the estimator's coverage, and says nothing about whether the intervals cover, in either direction.
