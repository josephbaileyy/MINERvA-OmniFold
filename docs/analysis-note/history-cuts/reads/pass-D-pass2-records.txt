\paragraph{Comparison with exact binned unfolding.} A diagnostic in simulation compares the boosted-tree residual with that of an exact binned iterative unfolding. The binned unfolding unfolds the same simulated events, with their exact efficiency and the response binned in the 10,499 occupied five-dimensional reconstructed cells, starting from the nominal prior. The comparison uses noise-free signal-only pseudo-data, the 109 supported fine joint cells (J cells) and the fixed historical truth deformations D1, W1, W3 and the $q_3$ stress, pooled with W2 (all defined in \S\ref{sec:gbdt-model-dependence}). The boosted-tree comparator for W2 includes background. The outcome is unchanged without it. A residual is eligible when its diagnosis is stable under the truth resolution: of the 376 J-cell and deformation combinations with residuals above \SI{2}{\percent}, 232 changed classification with the truth resolution or the response weighting and were excluded, leaving 144 (\SI{38}{\percent} of the 376). At five iterations the J-cell medians of the absolute residual agree: \SI{9.5}{\percent}, \SI{5.4}{\percent}, \SI{5.6}{\percent} and \SI{4.3}{\percent} for the boosted trees, against \SI{8.3}{\percent}, \SI{5.4}{\percent}, \SI{4.9}{\percent} and \SI{5.3}{\percent} for the exact binned unfolding, for D1, W1, W3 and the $q_3$ stress.

Of the 144 eligible residuals, pooled over the deformations, 15 are dominated by the tree approximation. Only 2 of these 15 lie in identified quantities, a share of 0.014 of the 144. An identified quantity has a relative Cram\'er--Rao width at or below \SI{2.5}{\percent} at the analysis exposure. A width between that bound and the \SI{10}{\percent} bound is neither. Of the 144, 121 lie in quantities that the binned response constrains weakly at the analysis exposure. Their Cram\'er--Rao widths exceed \SI{10}{\percent} at both truth resolutions tested (median \SI{31}{\percent} at the coarser resolution, that of the J cells themselves). Here the regularization trades a large variance for the bias.

Longer noise-free exact iteration removes much of the residual for D1 (J median \SI{3.3}{\percent} after 200 iterations and \SI{0.8}{\percent} after $10^{5}$) and part of it for W1 (\SI{3.3}{\percent} and \SI{2.8}{\percent}). The long runs do not converge (only 9--34 of the 109 J quantities converge for each traced deformation). These values are last iterates. The coarser muon projection retained only three cases and is uninformative.

Within this comparison, the residual is consistent with the regularization of the inverse problem at this binning and prior, and the tree approximation is not supported as its main cause. The baseline estimator's shifts, a median of 0.8 and at most 2.5, place this instability in the estimator family. The instability also explains the difference of at most \SI{1.2}{\percent} on data between the driver and the development pipeline. \subsection{Systematic universes}
\label{sec:uq-universes}
Detector and model systematics use the July universe sweep: the 44 error bands and 187 universes of the MINERvA Analysis Toolkit (MAT), identical to the standard systematics set of the published analysis. Each universe is re-unfolded with LightGBM at seed~42 and compared with the quoted 2D central value, unfolded with the same estimator and seed. The five lateral bands, which shift reconstructed kinematics rather than event weights, are taken from re-unfolds of re-selected universes (\S\ref{sec:uq-higherdim}) at the same seed. For a $\pm1\sigma$ pair the covariance is the outer product of the half-difference of the two shifts, excluding any common endpoint displacement from the central value (CV). The 100 PPFX hadron-production flux universes use the same mean-centered $1/N$ formula.
