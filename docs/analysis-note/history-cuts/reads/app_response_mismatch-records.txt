=== docs/analysis-note/app_guide.tex:6 (the appendix guide's sentence on App. D), at e6d8b402a
The appendices fall into three groups. The first gives methods and context for the body: statistical methods and the 2D comparison (App.~\ref{app:statmethods}) and those for the multidimensional results and inference (App.~\ref{app:statmethods-nd}); unbinned background subtraction (App.~\ref{app:negweight}); the protocol for a detector-response-mismatch closure, which rescales the reconstructed available energy of the pseudo-data while OmniFold keeps its nominal response (App.~\ref{app:response-mismatch}); and the survey of MINERvA measurement dimensionality (App.~\ref{app:landscape}). The second, on future work, covers point-cloud (PET) representations (App.~\ref{sec:pet}). The third describes the result package and what it reproduces (App.~\ref{app:release}) and the code layout (App.~\ref{app:codebase}), with the configurations and tests superseded or changed during the analysis (App.~\ref{app:devhistory}); it is reference material and is not needed to follow the physics results.
