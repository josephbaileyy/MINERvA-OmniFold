\subsection{Classifier-family cross-check (GBDT vs.\ NN)}
\label{sec:3d-classifier}
Our gradient-boosted decision trees differ from the neural-network classifiers used in published OmniFold analyses~\cite{Andreassen:2019cjw,Canelli:2025guide}. This cross-check uses the \estPurity{} GBDT unfold, with purity-based subtraction. The weight $w=p/(1-p)$ holds only if the GBDT output is a calibrated posterior. The step-1 reco classifier (data vs.\ MC reco) has a near-diagonal reliability curve, Brier \(\approx0.25\) at AUC \(\approx0.537\). Data and reco MC are nearly indistinguishable. The reweight is a modest correction. A full OmniFold cross-check with a Keras multilayer perceptron (MLP), the same weighted 3D inputs and the same five-iteration loop gives a total ratio of $1.0078$ to the GBDT result and median projection differences of \SIrange{0.7}{1.4}{\percent} (Fig.~\ref{fig:calibration}). In the populated bulk the \estPurity{} result therefore agrees between the two tested classifier families.
