\subsection{Closure for one injected available-energy deformation}
\label{sec:3d-closure}
The unfolding recovers a known $+\SI{30}{\percent}$ Gaussian bump injected into pseudo-data in \emph{truth} $\Eavail$ (center \SI{0.3}{GeV}, width \SI{0.15}{GeV}). The recovered step-2 mean weight is $1.048$ against the injected $1.0485$. Residuals against reweighted truth on the $\Eavail$-marginal have median $0.9999$ and standard deviation \textbf{\num{0.0006}} (\SI{0.06}{\percent}). The per-3D-bin residual standard deviation is \SI{3.2}{\percent} from MC statistics in sparse high-recoil corners.

Total normalization matches to better than \SI{1}{\percent}. In the metric of \S\ref{sec:validation} ($\Eavail$ projection) the residual is \SI{1.84}{\percent} against \SI{18.1}{\percent} injected (ratio $0.102$). No acceptance threshold set before the test is documented. The test has no pass or fail verdict.

This reweighting closure tests the 3D unfolding of configuration~(a) only and does not extend to (e). It covers one shape, this Gaussian, injected into the pseudo-data and unfolded \emph{with the same detector response that the unfolding uses}. For this injection the estimator neither removes the injected feature nor creates a spurious one. The closure's marginal scatter is far below the $\SI{3.7}{\percent}$ data-versus-2D scatter. The data comparison sets configuration (e) against configuration (f), a cross-configuration comparison. That comparison's scatter is therefore not interpreted as data--simulation structure resolved by the available-energy axis, and the ratio of the two scatters is not quoted. The test does \emph{not} exclude estimator bias for other deformations (\S\ref{sec:closure-map}). The hidden-variable closure exceeds its threshold at the largest amplitude (\S\ref{sec:validation}, App.~\ref{app:response-mismatch}).

\subsection{Classifier-family cross-check (GBDT vs.\ NN)}
\label{sec:3d-classifier}
Our gradient-boosted decision trees differ from the neural-network classifiers used in published OmniFold analyses~\cite{Andreassen:2019cjw,Canelli:2025guide}. This cross-check uses the configuration-(a) GBDT unfold, with purity-based subtraction. The weight $w=p/(1-p)$ holds only if the GBDT output is a calibrated posterior. The step-1 reco classifier (data vs.\ MC reco) has a near-diagonal reliability curve, Brier \(\approx0.25\) at AUC \(\approx0.537\). Data and reco MC are nearly indistinguishable. The reweight is a modest correction. A full OmniFold cross-check with a Keras multilayer perceptron (MLP), the same weighted 3D inputs and the same five-iteration loop gives a total ratio of $1.0078$ to the GBDT result and median projection differences of \SIrange{0.7}{1.4}{\percent} (Fig.~\ref{fig:calibration}). In the populated bulk the configuration-(a) result therefore agrees between the two tested classifier families.
