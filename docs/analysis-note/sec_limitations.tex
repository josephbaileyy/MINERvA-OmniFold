\label{sec:execsummary-limitations}The body states each limitation with the result it qualifies; the principal ones are these. $C_k$ denotes coverage within $\pm k$ standard deviations. The quoted 2D statistical block has had no coverage test; the fixed-truth coverage studies scored two earlier statistical bands, of which the first-built undercovers in its tails ($C_2=\covTwoDCTwo$, vs.\ $0.954$; \S\ref{sec:uq-boot}, \S\ref{sec:coverage}). No result's intervals have validated coverage (two dimensions: \S\ref{sec:uq-2d-scope}, where the procedure's covariance under-covers jointly at both held-out tunes, with \lrval{cov2d-ar23-joint} of \lrval{cov2d-ntoys} pseudo-experiments in the joint region at each, \S\ref{sec:coverage-tunes}; five dimensions: \S\ref{sec:coverage-5d}; the sixteen cells: \S\ref{sec:fived16-fd}, where a second, descriptive test flags joint under-coverage at both held-out tunes for a covariance without the response and run-period terms). The others are stated with their results: the generator comparisons (\S\S\ref{sec:3d}--\ref{sec:eavailw}); the absence of a 3D or 4D covariance (\S\ref{sec:3d}); the five-dimensional estimator's regularization bias (\S\ref{sec:val-departure}) and the purity-subtraction bias of configurations (a) and (c) (\S\ref{sec:val-negweight}); five-dimensional intervals and their coverage (\S\ref{sec:gbdt-model-dependence}, \S\ref{sec:coverage-5d}); the five-axis covariance of the configuration-(a) central value (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}); the calibration and nuisance model of the generator tests (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-frozen}, \S\ref{sec:joint5d-response}); tier~2 of the extended-fiducial study (App.~\ref{sec:fps}); the fake-data limitation of the sixteen-cell result (\S\ref{sec:fived16-fd}); the dependence of the sixteen cells on the unfolding algorithm (\S\ref{sec:fived16-ibu}); and point-cloud work (App.~\ref{sec:pet}).
