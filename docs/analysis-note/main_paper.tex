\begin{abstract}
We apply unbinned machine-learning unfolding, to our knowledge for the first
time on neutrino data, to MINERvA open data on medium-energy
$\nu_{\mu}$ charged-current inclusive interactions, first reproducing the
published cross section in muon transverse and longitudinal momentum.  Adding
available energy, three-momentum transfer and hadronic invariant mass gives
central values that pass marginal-normalization and injected-shape closure
tests, but the estimator's measured model dependence prevents a qualified
total uncertainty at useful precision: no five-dimensional cross section with
uncertainties is reported.  Instead, joint total and shape statistics,
calibrated with pseudo-experiments that rerun the full unfolding under a
declared nuisance model, reject all ten simple hypotheses for five
predictions (MINERvA Tune~v1, GENIE~2.12.10 with and without a
two-particle--two-hole component, NuWro~21.09, GiBUU~2019) at familywise level
0.05, with every submitted pseudo-experiment observed.  The rejections are
conditional on that model, which lacks a calorimetric recoil-response
uncertainty and whose fine-grid null is not converged.  An exploratory
$\pm4\%$ recoil-energy scaling of the data, not an uncertainty estimate,
removes no rejection.  The tests concern the specified predictions, not a
measured cross section, and claim no discrimination beyond matched coarse
projections.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data availability}
\label{sec:data-availability}

The input data and simulation are the MINERvA open-data AnaTuples, released under
CC0~\cite{MINERvA:OpenData}; this analysis used an earlier production of that
release, whose file names and sizes, but not checksums, are recorded with the
release described here.
A release of about \SI{16}{MB} lets a reader, with numpy, scipy and matplotlib
only, recompute every $p$ value, decision, robustness label and power figure of the
joint tests, the lost-seed resolution, the coarse-projection comparison and the
fine-grid $L_{2}$ ratios of condition~(i); regenerate Figs.~\ref{fig:validation}--\ref{fig:joint-nulls}; and recompute the
numbers quoted from Figs.~\ref{fig:validation}--\ref{fig:generator-context}.  It
contains the per-pseudo-experiment cell values after the declared nuisance terms,
their seeds, the predictions, the test domains, the variant shifts, the metric and
the observed data vector, the arrays behind Figs.~\ref{fig:validation}--\ref{fig:generator-context},
and the digest of every source product.  A replay of the packaged files from an
empty directory reproduces every recorded value and every checked figure number;
an earlier version of the release was also replayed by an independent reader.  The remaining quoted numbers (the
two-dimensional uncertainty and coverage figures, the closure and bias studies,
the low-recoil comparisons, the recoil-response sensitivity, the proxy
convergence rate of condition~(i) and the covariance-status measurements) are not
recomputable from this release.  The multiplier scan of Sec.~\ref{sec:joint} is
recomputable from the released sufficient inputs with the scan code of
Ref.~\cite{JointTestKappa2026}, but it is not part of the release's own
verification.
Extraction from the pseudo-experiment products, which are archived
(\SI{2.3}{GB}), and the event-level processing are not reproducible from it
either; they need the archived products and the open-data AnaTuples together
with the generator samples produced for this analysis.
