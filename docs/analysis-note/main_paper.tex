Applied to MINERvA open data for medium-energy $\nu_{\mu}$ charged-current
inclusive interactions, OmniFold reproduces the published muon-kinematic cross
section's integral within about 1\%, with a correlated shape difference.

transfer, and hadronic invariant mass), it yields central values only, whose
normalizations agree with lower-dimensional results.  Pseudo-experiments
reveal background-subtraction bias, incomplete recovery of generator-sized
truth changes, and statistical undercoverage at the tested truths; under the
specified envelope prescription, no tested reporting definition meets our
10\% precision criterion.

for five generator predictions all reject at the 5\% familywise level.

\section*{Data availability}
\label{sec:data-availability}

The input data and simulation are the MINERvA open-data AnaTuples, released under
CC0~\cite{MINERvA:OpenData}; this analysis used an earlier production of that
release, whose 2,374 input files are listed with their SHA256 checksums in the
records cited by the analysis note's appendix on the result package and fixed
identities~\cite{AnalysisNote2026}.  A candidate release package of about
\SI{16}{MB}, described there and prepared locally but not yet deposited, needs
only numpy, scipy, and matplotlib.
