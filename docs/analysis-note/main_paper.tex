Unbinned machine-learning unfolding can pass conventional closure tests and still fail more demanding ones. We show this for one OmniFold implementation that unfolds five observables simultaneously in MINERvA open data. A descriptive benchmark against the published two-observable measurement, made with binned iterative unfolding, agrees in normalization but differs in shape. On the same inputs, OmniFold also differs from binned iterative unfolding. When the simulated truth is changed, the five-observable estimator retains part of its prior.\fvgroup{prr}{\fvcase{prr-pr3-any}{not}{ Some regions move opposite to the change, and fake-data tests flag bias at two truths that no tested estimator setting removes.}\fvcase{prr-pr3-any}{clears}{ Some regions move opposite to the change, and fake-data tests flag bias at two truths, which a tested estimator setting removes.}} Rounding-level changes of the inputs violate its stability requirement, although its training is reproducible.\fvgroup{ll5d}{\fvcase{ll5d-campaign}{graded}{\fvcase{ll5d-alljoint}{all}{ Coverage tests of the two- and five-observable estimators' covariances, which omit the run-period and, for five observables, the response terms, find joint under-coverage at both held-out truths.}\fvcase{ll5d-alljoint}{notall}{ Coverage tests of the two- and five-observable estimators' covariances, which omit the run-period and, for five observables, the response terms, find joint under-coverage for the two-observable estimator at both held-out truths, and the five-observable test does not find it in every tested configuration.}\fvcase{ll5d-alljoint}{na}{}\fvcase{ll5d-route}{offset-proceeded}{ The five-observable coverage result is descriptive because its test continued past a check by a decision made after the check was seen.}\fvcase{ll5d-route}{repaired}{ The five-observable coverage result is descriptive because its repair was chosen after an earlier run stopped.}}\fvcase{ll5d-campaign}{gate-failed}{ A coverage test of the two-observable estimator's covariance, which omits the run-period term, finds joint under-coverage at both held-out truths. The second five-observable coverage test stopped at an entry condition fixed in advance and gives no coverage result.}\fvcase{ll5d-campaign}{offset-stopped}{ A coverage test of the two-observable estimator's covariance, which omits the run-period term, finds joint under-coverage at both held-out truths. The second five-observable coverage test was stopped by a decision made after a check was exceeded and gives no coverage result.}\fvcase{ll5d-campaign}{stop}{ A coverage test of the two-observable estimator's covariance, which omits the run-period term, finds joint under-coverage at both held-out truths. The second five-observable coverage test stopped at a check fixed in advance and gives no coverage result.}} No reported interval has validated coverage, and the five-observable output is presented only as an illustration. From these studies we give five checks to make before the intervals of an unbinned unfolding are interpreted.
\end{abstract}
