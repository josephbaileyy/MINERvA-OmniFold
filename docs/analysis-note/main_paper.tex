\begin{abstract}
We test the practical limits of multidimensional unbinned unfolding with
OmniFold on MINERvA open data for medium-energy $\nu_{\mu}$ charged-current
inclusive interactions.  The analysis reproduces the published
double-differential cross section in muon kinematics and extends to five
simultaneous observables by adding available energy, three-momentum transfer,
and hadronic invariant mass.  The higher-dimensional central values pass
marginal-normalization checks, and the three- and four-observable central values
pass injected-shape closure checks, but
background-inclusive tests and uncertainty studies expose background-treatment,
regularization, coverage, and estimator-seed limitations, and the measured model
dependence prevents a qualified five-dimensional measurement with total
uncertainties at useful precision.  Generator tests, calibrated with
pseudo-experiments that rerun the full analysis chain under a stated nuisance
model, reject all five tested predictions (MINERvA Tune~v1, GENIE~2.12.10 with
and without Valencia two-particle--two-hole contributions, NuWro~21.09, and
GiBUU~2019) in total and in shape at the 5\% familywise level.  These
conditional tests recover known discrepancies without demonstrated
discrimination from the tested joint information beyond matched coarse
projections, and their physical interpretation is limited by an omitted
calorimetric recoil-response uncertainty and unresolved fine-grid convergence.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data availability}
\label{sec:data-availability}

The input data and simulation are the MINERvA open-data AnaTuples, released under
CC0~\cite{MINERvA:OpenData}; this analysis used an earlier production of that
release, whose 2,374 inventoried input files are identified by SHA256
checksum~\cite{AnaTuplePreservation2026}.  A local release candidate, not yet deposited, of about
\SI{16}{MB}~\cite{ReleaseCandidate2026} lets a reader, with numpy, scipy, and
matplotlib only, recompute every $p$ value, decision, robustness label, and power
figure of the joint tests, the lost-seed resolution, the coarse-projection
comparison, and the fine-grid $L_{2}$ ratios of condition~(i); regenerate
Figs.~\ref{fig:joint}--\ref{fig:joint-nulls}; and recompute the numbers
quoted from Figs.~\ref{fig:joint} and~\ref{fig:generator-context}.  A replay
of the packaged files from an empty directory reproduces every recorded value
and every checked figure number.  The candidate predates the change of the
two-dimensional central value to LightGBM and carries the exact-split curve;
Fig.~\ref{fig:validation} and its numbers were instead regenerated with the
release's plotting code, extended to draw this analysis's band, from the
committed LightGBM values and covariance.  The other quoted numbers, apart from the
multiplier scan (recomputable from Ref.~\cite{JointTestKappa2026}), are not
recomputable from this release; they and the event-level processing require the
archived analysis products, the open-data AnaTuples, and the generator samples
produced for this analysis; the analysis note's release
appendix~\cite{AnalysisNote2026} states what each part of the release does and
does not reproduce.
