\title{Unbinned Five-Observable Unfolding of MINERvA Charged-Current
Neutrino Data and Calibrated Joint Tests of Generator Predictions}

\author{Joseph Bailey}
\email{josephrb@stanford.edu}
\affiliation{Department of Physics, Stanford University, Stanford, California 94305, USA}
\author{Benjamin Nachman}
\email{nachman@stanford.edu}
\affiliation{Department of Particle Physics and Astrophysics, Stanford University, Stanford, California 94305, USA}
\affiliation{SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, Menlo Park, California 94025, USA}

\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
We apply unbinned machine-learning unfolding to MINERvA medium-energy
$\nu_{\mu}$ charged-current inclusive data from the MINERvA open-data release.
To our knowledge this is the first unbinned unfolding of neutrino-scattering
data. A simultaneous two-observable unfold reproduces the published
$d^{2}\sigma/(d\pT\,d\pPar)$ measurement and its uncertainty scale; its
statistical band as first built undercovered a fixed truth in its tails, and the
rebuilt band has not been re-tested. We then
unfold up to five observables, adding available energy, three-momentum
transfer, and hadronic invariant mass, without an explicit five-dimensional
response tensor. The higher-dimensional central values pass marginal anchors
and injected-shape closure tests and recover the established low-recoil
discrepancy. The estimator's measured
model dependence prevents a qualified total uncertainty at useful precision,
so no five-dimensional cross-section measurement with uncertainties is
claimed. Instead, a joint test calibrated with pseudo-experiments that
run the full analysis chain compares the five-dimensional result with five
generator predictions under a declared nuisance model.
Frozen joint tests of five neutrino-interaction predictions (MINERvA Tune~v1,
GENIE~2.12.10 with and without MEC, NuWro~21.09, GiBUU~2019) against the
five-dimensional data, in total (rate and shape together) and in shape alone,
reject all ten simple hypotheses at familywise level 0.05 under the stated
conditions.  Every pseudo-experiment lost to scheduler limits was recovered in a
report-only rerun without changing a decision, and an exploratory $\pm4\%$ recoil-response
variation leaves every rejection in place.  These are statements about the
specified predictions, not a measured cross section.
\end{abstract}
