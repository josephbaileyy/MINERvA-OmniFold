We study OmniFold unfolding of MINERvA
open data for medium-energy $\nu_{\mu}$ charged-current inclusive
interactions.  It reproduces the published muon-kinematic cross section's integral
within about one percent, with a correlated shape difference.
% X5 G2 (M8): "reproduces the published ... double-differential cross section" narrowed to the integral.
Extended to five simultaneous observables (adding available energy,
three-momentum transfer, and hadronic invariant mass), it yields central values
only: normalizations agree with lower-dimensional ones, but
pseudo-experiments reveal background-subtraction bias and residual bias under
generator-sized truth changes,
statistical intervals miscover at the tested truths, and an envelope over five
tested truth deformations, a median of about 10--13\% per cell, keeps the
definitions studied from meeting our adopted 10\% criterion.

% Draft 6 (X6 G-prom, G-total; REVIEW-2 finding 5; M8): one sentence on the tests, as an illustration, with the
% NuWro clause (DECISION-20261020-joseph-nuwro-ch-claim.md, provisional; mp3b/REPORT.md:21-25).
Tests of hybrid null hypotheses for five generator predictions, calibrated in a
simulation without calorimetric recoil-response uncertainty and assuming
fine-grid convergence, all reject at the 5\% familywise level, without being
physical exclusions or gaining over matched coarse projections; NuWro's total
(rate-and-shape) test rejection is not robust to a hydrocarbon-target correction.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Data availability}
\label{sec:data-availability}

The input data and simulation are the MINERvA open-data AnaTuples, released under
CC0~\cite{MINERvA:OpenData}; this analysis used an earlier production of that
release, whose 2,374 input files are listed with their SHA256
checksums~\cite{AnaTuplePreservation2026}.  A candidate release package of
about \SI{16}{MB}~\cite{ReleaseCandidate2026}, prepared locally but not yet
deposited, needs only numpy, scipy, and matplotlib.
% plain-language pass, review fix R13 (DECISION-20261018-joseph-plain-language-pass)

recompute every $p$ value, decision, robustness result, and power figure of
the joint tests, together with the recovery of the lost pseudo-experiments,
the coarse-projection comparison, and the fine-grid $L_{2}$ ratios of
condition~(i); regenerate Figs.~\ref{fig:joint} and~\ref{fig:joint-nulls} and
the one-dimensional generator projections; and recompute the numbers
quoted from Fig.~\ref{fig:joint} and in Sec.~\ref{sec:central}.
