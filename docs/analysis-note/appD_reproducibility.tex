It recomputes the joint generator tests reported in this article (statistics, exceedance counts, $p$ values, decisions, and robustness and power summaries), with and without the recovered pseudo-experiments, together with the matched coarse-projection comparison and the fine-grid refinement ratios. It regenerates the $(E_{\mathrm{avail}},W)$ excess map (Fig.~\ref{fig:eavailWexcess}) exactly and checks the quoted $(E_{\mathrm{avail}},W)$ generator ratios against the arrays plotted in Fig.~\ref{fig:eavailWband}. It also recomputes the two-dimensional benchmark numbers of Sec.~\ref{sec:part-2d} for the \estTwoD{}, from the deposited central values and covariance and the published values and covariance. The replay package's README gives its file inventory, the figures it regenerates, and the verification tolerances.

\phantomsection\label{app:release-scope} The deposit also archives the \lrval{q-ncells}-cell 2D refined central values with $\CrepTwoD$ and its systematic, statistical, training-seed and run-period terms, and the 2D prior-shape term $V_{\mathrm{prior}}$, which $\CrepTwoD$ excludes; the sixteen refined cell integrals with $\CrepFiveD$ and its components, with $V_{\mathrm{prior}}$ listed separately; the generator predictions compared in this article on the sixteen cells and on the $(E_{\mathrm{avail}},W)$ grid; and the central values and support masks of the \resFiveAxis{}, with the projection matrix $P$ and the available-energy--mass projection covariance $V_{EW}$. The deposit also provides the unbinned central value: the per-event OmniFold weights of the two-dimensional central value on the simulated truth events, with each event's truth $p_T$ and $p_\parallel$, and the cross-section normalization. The weights cover the \num{32849103} simulated events that pass the truth selection. They are the same rows, in the same order, as those of the sixteen-cell weights below, and their truth $p_T$ and $p_\parallel$ agree exactly in single precision. Each event is keyed by its entry number in the input file, which the weights file identifies by its SHA-256 digest. It also carries its prior weight. The weights come from a rerun of the two-dimensional unfolding with unchanged code and inputs. Histogrammed on our analysis grid, they reproduce the deposited 2D refined central values in every bin to \num{9.2e-12} of their values. Histogrammed on MINERvA's published $p_T$ edges, they reproduce the unfolding on those edges that the central-value comparisons of Sec.~\ref{sec:results-xsec} use, to \num{5.1e-12}. Their completeness denominator is these same events, so the completeness is 1 in any binning, and the flux is a single integrated value. Readers can therefore choose any binning in $p_T$ and $p_\parallel$ inside the truth phase space of Sec.~\ref{sec:selection} [Eq.~\eqref{eq:muon-fiducial}]. Only these two grids were compared with full unfoldings. The weights give central values only, so they give no uncertainty for a new binning.

For the \resSixteen{}, the deposit also provides the per-event OmniFold weights on the \num{32849103} simulated events that pass the truth selection. Of these, \num{2801} lie outside the fine grid's $E_{\mathrm{avail}}$, $q_3$ or $W$ range and enter no cell integral. Each event is keyed by the SHA-256 digest of the input file and its entry number in that file. It carries its five truth variables and its prior and unfolded weights. Separate files give the cross-section normalization and the fine-grid edges, volumes, and completeness. The run that wrote the weights reproduced the reported cross section byte for byte. Summed with the normalization, the weights reproduce the sixteen cell integrals to double-precision rounding. The completeness denominator is the deposited events themselves, so the completeness is 1 in any binning. The flux enters as a single integrated value. Readers can therefore choose any binning inside the truth phase space of Sec.~\ref{sec:selection} [Eq.~\eqref{eq:muon-fiducial}], within the fine grid's range. The stored completeness differs from 1 in 32 fine cells, by at most \num{4.1e-4}. These cells form 16 adjacent pairs with equal and opposite differences. This pattern is consistent with 16 events that lie within single-precision rounding of a cell edge. Setting the stored completeness to 1 changes the sixteen cell integrals by at most \num{3.3e-6} of their values. The release provides central values only, so it gives no uncertainty for a new binning. No per-event weights are released for the \resFiveAxis{}.

\subsection{Code}
\label{app:analysis-code}
\label{app:codebase}

The \href{https://github.com/josephbaileyy/MINERvA-OmniFold}{MINERvA OmniFold repository} provides the MINERvA 101 event loop (\path{runEventLoopOmniFold.cpp}), the 2D and 3D drivers (\path{unfold_2d_omnifold_unbinned.py}, \path{unfold_3d_omnifold_unbinned.py}), scalar 4D and 5D production code (\path{nd-unfolding/}), and iterative reweighting (\path{unbinned_unfolding/python/omnifold.py}). The repository documents the upstream dependencies. The deposit identifies the code release and its manifests record the input digests for replay and the five-axis products.

\begin{sloppypar}
To replay the archived results, unpack the replay package from the deposit and run its verifier, \texttt{code/\allowbreak verify\_rc.py} (\texttt{publication/\allowbreak release/\allowbreak verify\_rc.py} in the repository), from its top directory with the pinned NumPy, SciPy, and Matplotlib requirements. A successful replay prints \verb|VERIFY: PASS|.
\end{sloppypar}

\subsection{Deposit notation}
\label{app:reproducibility-definitions}
\label{sec:front-definitions}
\label{app:devhistory-method}
\label{app:devhistory-seeds}
\label{app:devhistory-5d}
\label{app:devhistory-recovery-seeds}

Table~\ref{tab:estimators} gives the estimator settings and points to the joint-test variant. The low-capacity recovery test and the refined-estimator tests at three withheld truth shapes reuse seed sets from estimator development.

The matrices $\CrepTwoD$, $V_Z$, and $\CrepFiveD$ refer, respectively, to the \lrval{q-ncells}-bin muon result, the \resFiveAxis{}, and the sixteen refined cell integrals. Deposit file and array names and the analysis repository's records keep the earlier symbols: $Q_R$ is $\CrepTwoD$, $C_{\mathrm{rep}}$ is $\CrepFiveD$, ``the baseline covariance'' is $\CrepFiveDzero$, $C_e$ is $V_{\mathrm{stat+syst}}$, $C_e^{\mathrm{infl}}$ is $V_{\mathrm{stat+syst}}^{\mathrm{infl}}$, $C_{\mathrm{total}}$ is $\Csub$, $C_{\mathrm{model}}$ is $V_{\mathrm{prior}}$, $C_{\mathrm{resp}}$ is $V_{\mathrm{resp}}$, $C_{\mathrm{PL,5D}}$ is the five-dimensional $\Crun$, $C_Z$ is $V_Z$, and $C_{EW}$ is $V_{EW}$. Their constructions are given in Appendixes~\ref{app:covariance-construction} and \ref{app:multidimensional-statistics}.

For the exploratory $(E_{\mathrm{avail}},W)$ density projection, the deposit archives $V_{EW}=PV_ZP^{\mathsf T}$ and the matrix $P$, which sums over the three dropped axes with their bin widths, but not $V_Z$. The spectrum of $V_{EW}$ extends to the double-precision noise floor, making inversion sensitive to regularization. The projection applies the fine-grid mask and uses no additional acceptance factor at the destination binning; whether one is needed has not been decided. The deposit's README gives the array ordering and a worked projection check.
