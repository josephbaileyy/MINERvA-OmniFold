Table~\ref{tab:observables-impl} gives the implemented truth and reconstructed definitions of the five unfolded observables. $E_{\mu}$, $p_{\mu}$, and $\theta_{\mu}$ are the muon energy, momentum, and angle. $q_{0}$ is the calorimetric estimate of the energy transfer. $M$ is the struck-nucleon mass, $\bar M=(1.5M_{n}+M_{p})/2.5$.  Exact $Q^{2}$ is $-(k-k')^{2}$ from the generator's four-vectors. Truth $W$ instead uses the massless-muon $Q^{2}$ with $\theta_{\mu}$ from the beam axis, a difference whose effect on $W$ is given below.

Reconstructed $q_{3}$ and $W$ combine the muon kinematics with the calorimetric estimate $q_{0}$ of the energy transfer, which is separate from reconstructed $\Eavail$.  Since $W_{\mathrm{reco}}^{2}=(\bar M+q_{0})^{2}-q_{3,\mathrm{reco}}^{2}$ and $q_{3,\mathrm{reco}}$ increases with $q_{0}$ at fixed muon kinematics, reconstructed $W$ is an exact function of $(\pT,\pPar,q_{3})$, apart from the clamp at $W^{2}=0$, which applies to \SI{1.5}{\percent} of simulated events.

At truth level, $q_{3}$ is computed from the true neutrino and muon four-vectors, but $\theta_{\mu}$, and with it $\pT$ and $\pPar$, is measured from a fixed beam axis tilted by \SI{58.87}{mrad} from the detector axis rather than from the true neutrino direction.  Truth $W$ is therefore fixed by $(\pT,\pPar,q_{3})$ only up to that event-by-event angle, of about \SI{1}{mrad}, and the choice of $M$: the residual is within \SI{\pm2}{MeV} for 68\% of events and within $-49$ to $+67$~MeV for 99.9\%; \SI{0.25}{\percent} of events (weighted) change their coarse $W$ bin, and \SI{0.086}{\percent} (weighted) have no real solution. % D1 (crossdoc audit 2026-11-15): weighted, probe_omni.json:10 q3sq_minus_pt2_negative_wfrac 0.0008646 (the 0.15% was the unweighted 49754/32846302, :9 and :8); the Supplement prints m4w-noreal-pct, 0.086

The five reconstructed inputs therefore share information and do not, by themselves, provide five independent constraints: the result is five simultaneous observables, not five independent measurements. The five classifier inputs, the lower-dimensional support they populate, and the five reported observables are distinct. Only reconstructed $W$ is fixed by the other inputs; reconstructed $\Eavail$ comes from a separate calorimetric estimator and is not a function of them. The nearly degenerate correlation directions of the joint-test metric (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-design}) are consistent with this redundancy, but that origin has not been shown, and the information added by $W$ has not been measured.

The analysis excludes events with negative truth $W^{2}$, a weight fraction of $4.9\times10^{-5}$, from its five-observable truth sample and normalization; \S\ref{sec:eavailw-axis} gives the external predictions' convention. The generators measure $\pT$ and $\pPar$ about their own neutrino axis; their $\pT$ differs from the analysis truth $\pT$ by \SI{\pm4}{MeV} for 68\% of events. Over the 109 joint-test cells, this convention changes the analysis simulation's cell contents by factors between 0.9965 and 1.0035 (median 1.000008), at the level of its Monte Carlo precision, and shifts GENIE's central prediction by between $-0.47$ and $+0.53$ of each cell's null standard deviation. For GENIE with MEC, NuWro and GiBUU its effect is not reliably determined (Ref.~\cite{NoteRecords2026}, \recentry{mp5-truth-axis}).
