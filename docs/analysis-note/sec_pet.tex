\section{Recoil-point-cloud representation cross-check}
\label{sec:pet}

\paragraph{Status: diagnostic and method development, not a publication
uncertainty product.}
Everything in this section is a diagnostic and method-development result. No PET
covariance --- historical recoil-only or full-event --- is adopted as a publication
uncertainty product, and the full-event central/statistical pairing was declined on
2026-08-20 rather than resolved, with coverage the missing evidence rather than any
branch having been chosen. The publication uncertainty critical path runs through the
eventual adoption of a selection-complete scalar five-dimensional covariance
(\S\ref{sec:uq-combine}), not through this section. Routed record:
\texttt{OI-126} in \texttt{docs/OPEN\_ITEMS.md}.

\paragraph{What goes in.}
\label{sec:pet-inputs}
PET is the point-cloud transformer architecture used by OmniLearn~\cite{Mikuni:2024qsr},
here vendored inside the OmniFold implementation.  Table~\ref{tab:pet-inputs}
separates the historical recoil-only cross-check from the later full-event,
extended-fiducial-phase-space (extended-FPS) candidate.  Both are unbinned
classifiers over continuous event features; neither receives reporting-bin edges.

\paragraph{What comes out.}
\label{sec:pet-output-contract}
The classifier score is not the scientific result and PET does not emit a binned
network prediction.  Its probabilities (implemented through raw logits) are
intermediate density-ratio estimates.  OmniFold converts the final truth-step
logit to one push weight per simulated truth event,
$w_{\rm push}=\exp(\mathrm{logit})$, and those weights remain unbinned.  The
reported cross section is formed only afterward: apply the push weights to the
stored truth inventory, then histogram the requested observable and perform the
standard flux, target and bin-volume normalization~\cite{Andreassen:2019cjw}.
Consequently any supported observable that is a function of the stored truth
record or retained truth cloud can be projected without retraining.  This does
not create configurations absent from the generator support, recover constituents
lost above the cloud cap, or turn a recoil-only weight into a full-event weight.

\subsection{Shape cross-check against the scalar result}
\label{sec:pet-shape}
The first use of the recoil representation is a shape comparison against the production
scalar unfold, which tests the classifier family and the engineered-scalar versus
recoil-cloud input choice together. Here \texttt{cluster\_pos} is the measured
coordinate in one X/U/V scintillator view. Area-normalized against the frozen 4D GBDT result, the PET unfold
reproduces the scalar shape to a median \SIrange{2.3}{3.9}{\percent} per bin
($\pT$~\SI{3.9}{\percent}, $\pPar$~\SI{2.4}{\percent}, $\Eavail$~\SI{2.6}{\percent},
$q_{3}$~\SI{2.3}{\percent}; Fig.~\ref{fig:petgbdt}). In its role here this is a
recoil-representation sensitivity check that compares classifier families and
engineered-scalar versus recoil-cloud inputs together. Figure~\ref{fig:petdisplays}
visualizes representative events on a common energy--polar-angle plane and
shows the reconstructed and truth muon together with the recoil activity. The
PET classifier receives a variable-length set of single-view energy
deposits at reconstruction level and particles with full four-momenta at truth
level. Figure~\ref{fig:petcardinality} shows the
per-event cardinality of each cloud. The remainder of this section takes the same
representation from this shape check to an absolutely-normalized cross section.

Figure~\ref{fig:petcardinality} uses the coverage-complete point-cloud input:
only 174 of the \num{32849103} truth events have an empty cloud. Including the
GENIE residual-nucleus bookkeeping particles, as the PET input does, gives a
mean truth cardinality of $4.57$. The depleted $k=1$ bin is a generator-record
feature: a nominally single-hadron event, most often quasielastic, usually also
contains a residual-nucleus pseudo-particle and therefore enters at $k=2$.
Removing those pseudo-particles gives a mean physical-hadron multiplicity of
$4.43$ and a distribution that rises smoothly to its $k=2$ maximum. The
twelve-constituent cap is a compute/memory choice: PET cost grows with
cardinality, while only \SI{2.31}{\percent} of truth events reach the cap and
the twelfth constituent carries only \SI{0.09}{\percent} of the retained truth
energy on average.

\subsection{From shape check to absolute cross section}
\label{sec:pet-abs}
The PET network trains on a GPU on a 2M-event subsample (2 OmniFold
iterations); the learned step-2 truth reweight is then applied to the
\emph{full} 32.8M-event generator sample
(\texttt{MultiFold.reweight}), binned on the 4D grid, and pushed through the
standard extraction (\texttt{extract\_cross\_section\_nd} with the GBDT
run's completeness --- completeness is reweight-independent --- and the same
flux/POT/nucleon constants). Two gates validate the absolute path:
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Full-statistics reweight sanity}: the pushed weights over the
        32.8M generator events are finite with mean $1.028$;
  \item \textbf{Ordinary closure}: unfolding MC-reco-as-pseudo-data
        recovers MC truth to total $0.9884$ with uniform per-axis median
        residuals of $\sim\SI{1.1}{\percent}$ --- the absolute machinery is
        internally consistent. Because pseudo-data and prior come from the
        same simulation, this test cannot expose omitted muon dependence at
        fixed recoil.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{No current PET--GBDT level of agreement is quoted.}
The only such comparison that exists was taken with a two-iteration training,
against the campaign's pinned three-iteration policy (\texttt{CLM-010},
\texttt{CLM-012}, \textsc{frozen}); before the 2026-08-01 full-event schema change,
which makes a pre-08-01 PET total a different estimator rather than a
differently-trained one; and with unit measured weights and \emph{no} background
subtraction (audit finding J21), which at this analysis's
${\sim}\SI{3}{\percent}$ background scale biases PET high, so the underlying gap is
\emph{larger} than the withdrawn figure. It has not been re-extracted under the
pinned configuration, and the same three grounds withdraw the legacy closure level
and the higher-epoch retrain response. Routed record:
\texttt{docs/orchestration/INDEX-retracted-and-superseded-values.md}.

What survives independently of the withdrawn comparison is structural rather than
numerical: ordinary closure alone cannot assign a data-side gap uniquely to training
configuration, and the recoil-only feature contract is a further difference --- at
fixed recoil, the conditional muon distribution remains inherited from the prior.

\subsection{Statistical and training diagnostics of the recoil-only estimator}
\label{sec:pet-syst}
The PET statistical replicas independently
Poisson-fluctuate data and MC, retrain PET at a fixed estimator seed, and apply
the same coherent MC draw in the final truth binning and completeness.

The calculation uses the same background-subtracted five-dimensional
measured target as the scalar GBDT. Event-by-event data alignment is exact, the
MC arrays are byte-identical to the full-cloud input. The
nominal PET extraction, an identical-seed GPU-floor repeat, a
20-member coherent-Poisson retraining ensemble, and a 12-member crossed
subsample/estimator-seed ensemble have all passed their ordering, event-alignment, and
finite-output gates. The GPU nondeterminism is negligible relative to both
sampling and training variation. The recoil-only statistical block is a diagnostic
dispersion measurement built from 20 coherent replicas, and the pre-publication plan
to raise that to 100 has not been run. It is not an independently verified covariance,
it is not the statistical uncertainty of any PET result, and it is not paired with a
PET central value; a larger ensemble would sharpen the dispersion estimate and would
still not supply coverage evidence.

\emph{This block is not the object called} $C_{\rm stat}$ \emph{elsewhere in the
campaign.} That name denotes the full-event extended-fiducial 50-member Gate-5 family
(\texttt{VL132}), whose properties, limitations and declined pairing are the subject of
App.~\ref{app:cstatlimit}. The two are different constructions on different
populations --- 20 recoil-only replicas here against 50 full-event members there, and
two resampled inventories here against three there --- so neither object's measurements,
disclaimers or rulings transfer to the other.

The historical recoil-PET campaign produced vertical, retraining, ML,
statistical and detector blocks on a common nominal.  That assembly is now
quarantined rather than quoted as an uncertainty.  For a nuisance endpoint
$u$, the physical shift and the induced retraining response form one joint
shift,
\begin{equation}
  \delta_u=x_u^{\rm varied+retrained}-x_{\rm CV}.
\end{equation}
Writing this as a frozen-map piece plus a retraining increment is an algebraic
decomposition, but adding the two separate covariances omits
$\operatorname{Cov}(\delta_{\rm frozen},\delta_{\rm retrain})$ and its
transpose.  Positive semidefiniteness of that sum does not restore the missing
cross term.

The historical detector block is additionally CV-support-limited: it propagates
shifted weights and observables through the nominal cloud membership instead of
regenerating the selected cloud for every lateral endpoint.  A replacement
construction would need end-to-end joint nuisance/retraining shifts and the
selection-complete FPS lateral samples, and would belong to a full-event PET
estimator with its own statistical and ML ensembles.  This section therefore
retains the recoil-only central extraction and ordinary closure as historical
method-development diagnostics.  It quotes neither a current PET--GBDT agreement
level nor a current closure level, and no recoil-only covariance is paired with
a PET central value.

\subsection{Truth-cloud projection within the recoil cross-check}
\label{sec:pet-projection}

The truth side of the recoil-only PET unfold is \emph{already} a point cloud: the
generator-level final-state hadron set
(\texttt{part\_gen}, the top-12-by-energy truth hadrons with their PDG codes,
built by \texttt{CVUniverse::GetTruthFSHadrons}) is carried per event
alongside the learned step-2 push weight. Any truth observable that is
a function of those 4-vectors can therefore be obtained after the
unfold by re-binning the push-weighted truth events --- no re-training and no
re-unfolding. We demonstrate this \emph{reweight-then-project} capability on
three observables computed post-hoc from the stored cloud: available energy
$\Eavail$ (using the exact operational definition in
\S\ref{sec:eavail-definition}), the hadronic-system invariant mass
$W_{\text{had}}=\sqrt{(\sum E)^2-|\sum\vec p|^2}$, and the proton/charged-pion
multiplicities. The exercise also quantifies the two
limitations of the stored cloud.

\paragraph{Truncation is essentially lossless.}
Because $\Eavail$ recomputed from the cloud uses the same formula and
inputs as the value stored at dump time, the cloud-vs-stored residual isolates
the cost of keeping only the 12 highest-energy hadrons. Over the full
\num{32.8}~million truth events (every event now carries a cloud, below) the
residual has median
\SI{0}{MeV} and mean \SI{\pcEavailMean}{MeV}, with \SI{\pcEavailWithin}{\percent}
of events within \SI{10}{MeV} (Fig.~\ref{fig:pcvalid}). The entire deficit
lives in the \SI{\pcSaturated}{\percent} of events that saturate the 12-particle
cap (median deficit \SI{\pcSatDeficit}{MeV} there, exactly zero below it), and
because the ranking is by energy the dropped constituents are the lowest-energy
stored final-state hadrons --- a small, one-sided, bounded effect. The 12-particle cap therefore
does not limit $\Eavail$-scale observables and does not need to be raised.

\paragraph{Coverage: closing the acceptance gap.}
Originally the cloud was filled only in the reco-signal event loop, so the
truth-only ``miss'' events appended for the unfold (\SI{\pcMiss}{\percent} of
the truth sample) carried no cloud --- only \SI{\pcHasCloudBefore}{\percent} of
truth events had one, and cloud projections were correspondingly
acceptance-limited, landing exactly on the stored-$\Eavail$ projection
restricted to the has-cloud subset but sitting $1.5$--$2\times$ below
the full unfold per bin. This is a truth-side instance of the same
dangling-miss-row dump issue tracked for the systematic bank. We fixed it at
source by carrying the truth final-state hadrons through the
truth-denominator loop and filling \texttt{part\_gen} for the appended miss
rows (the reco cloud \texttt{part\_reco} correctly stays empty for a miss),
then re-dumping, rebuilding the inputs, and re-training. Coverage is now
complete: \SI{\pcHasCloud}{\percent} of truth events carry a cloud (only
\pcEmptyCloud{} residual empties out of \num{32.8}~million), and the
cloud-recomputed $d\sigma/d\Eavail$ now reproduces the full unfold to
$\lesssim\SI{\pcCloudFull}{\percent}$ in every $\Eavail$ bin
(Fig.~\ref{fig:pcxsec}, center, where the cloud, stored, and full-unfold
curves coincide). The projection is no longer acceptance-limited.

\paragraph{Exploratory projection yields new hadronic diagnostics.}
$W_{\text{had}}$ is not a lossy copy of the leptonic ``experimenter's''
$W$ stored at dump time --- the latter is reconstructed from muon kinematics
assuming a single struck nucleon at rest, while $W_{\text{had}}$ is the true
invariant mass of the hadron system. The two differ by a median
\SI{\pcWoffset}{GeV}, and the 2D correlation
(Fig.~\ref{fig:pcxsec}, left) makes the physics explicit: a sharp locus at
$W_{\text{had}}\approx\SI{0.94}{GeV}$ (single-baryon final states, whose
hadronic mass is just the nucleon rest mass irrespective of leptonic $W$) and
diagonal bands stepped above it by $\sim$one nucleon mass per extra final-state
baryon (multi-nucleon knockout, 2p2h), Fermi-smeared. Projecting the cloud
thus provides a physically interpretable hadron-cloud diagnostic from the
existing recoil-only unfold.

\paragraph{The projection and extrapolation limits.}
\label{sec:pet-projection-next}
Reweighting can only redistribute weight among truth configurations the
generator already produced; it cannot populate phase space the prior never
sampled --- the same extrapolation limit flagged for the extended-fiducial
study (\S\ref{sec:fps}). Within that limit, hadron-cloud diagnostics can be
projected over the stored truth spectrum. They do not
turn the recoil-derived weight into a full-event weight or remove the prior
conditional for omitted muon information.
The stored recoil-only result cannot retroactively acquire the muon dependence
of the later full-event estimator: those are separate estimators with the
distinct interfaces in Table~\ref{tab:pet-inputs}.  Nor can re-projection recover
the rare very-high-multiplicity constituents discarded above the 12-particle cap
(unnecessary at $\Eavail$ scale per the validation above). Full details and reproduction are in
\texttt{nd-unfolding/pet/POINTCLOUD\_PROJECTION.md}.

\subsection{Relation to the Gregor/minerva-ml representation}
\label{sec:pet-gregor}
Gregor Krzmanc's \texttt{minerva-ml} study starts from the same broad MINERvA
ME~FHC simulation but solves different supervised tasks: available-energy
regression and charged-current-pion classification on an OmniLearned/PET2
backbone~\cite{Krzmanc:2026transfer}.  At its pinned analysis commit, events are
represented by typed muon, photon, prong and calorimeter-blob tokens, with
collider-style $(\eta,\phi,\log p_T,\log E)$ coordinates, learned type embeddings,
$dE/dx$, position and timing; prongs or blobs above their separate caps are
combined into aggregate overflow tokens.\footnote{Implementation comparison:
\href{https://github.com/gregorkrz/minerva-ml/blob/af5d92ed2b3b448a09b6b7cf6b4f179e5757b4ed/DATASET.md}{\texttt{minerva-ml DATASET.md}}
and
\href{https://github.com/gregorkrz/minerva-ml/blob/af5d92ed2b3b448a09b6b7cf6b4f179e5757b4ed/MODELS.md}{\texttt{MODELS.md}},
commit \texttt{af5d92ed}.}
Our reco leg instead uses generic detector-geometry calorimeter clusters, has no
reconstructed-object type embedding, and discards tokens beyond the common
top-12-by-energy cap.  More importantly, our task is OmniFold density-ratio
estimation against real data with explicit backgrounds and native misses, not
supervised regression or classification on simulation.  Typed reconstructed
objects and aggregate overflow tokens are therefore prospective representation
improvements, not features of the present estimator.

\subsection{Full-event artifacts and present boundary}
\label{sec:pet-full-event-status}
Within its recoil-only contract, this study demonstrates paired reconstruction-
and truth-level point-cloud construction, GPU training, full-sample reweighting,
absolute cross-section extraction, coherent bootstrap replicas, and the response
studies above end to end. It does not establish simultaneous muon-plus-hadron
unfolding, because the muon was not a classifier input.

The later full-event extended-FPS work is not merely prospective: a nominal
artifact and a 50-member $C_{\rm stat}$ artifact exist.  The latter was
constructed by one builder and is not independently verified or adopted;
Joseph declined pairing it with the nominal on 2026-08-20.  The ruling does not
invalidate the measured dispersion and does not establish a mechanism; it leaves
a spatially coherent anomaly whose coverage has not been validated, and requires
estimator-equivalence plus coverage evidence before reconsideration.  No
$C_{\rm ML}$ and no full PET total covariance exist, and no PET covariance is
adopted as a publication uncertainty.  Gate~6 remains blocked under the exact
receipt prohibitions \texttt{do\_not\_select\_passing\_subset},
\texttt{do\_not\_construct\_C\_ML}, \texttt{do\_not\_move\_central},
\texttt{do\_not\_start\_leg\_2}, and \texttt{do\_not\_retry\_unchanged}.
The construction and its limitations are recorded in App.~\ref{app:cstatlimit};
none of these artifacts changes PET's diagnostic and method-development status.
