\label{sec:eavailw}

In central values, Valencia 2p2h closes about half of the low-recoil gap (\S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h}) but leaves a separate excess over the MINERvA Tune~v1 prior at high $\Eavail$. There 2p2h contributes nothing, and the sub-percent pion-FSI dials cannot reach the excess. The qualifications of the covariance are given in \S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}. The comparison with external generators depends on the generator. GiBUU is low throughout the $\Eavail$ tail: its $W$-integrated five-dimensional data/generator ratio, in configuration~(a), is $1.59$ at $\Eavail\in[0.8,1.5)$ and $1.37$ at $[1.5,3.0)$ ($E_\nu\approx\SI{20}{GeV}$ limit: \S\ref{sec:eavailw-band}). GENIE CV instead matches $[0.8,1.5)$ and is low in $[1.5,3.0)$, with a three-dimensional data/CV ratio, in configuration~(e), of $1.10$ there (\S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h}). Adding the hadronic invariant mass $W$ as the fifth OmniFold axis maps where in $W$ the high-$\Eavail$ excess lies. The validation of \S\ref{sec:val-fiveaxis} includes a model-dependence study, which establishes no bias--variance tradeoff and no new total uncertainty for this projection.

\subsection{$W$ as a fifth axis}
\label{sec:eavailw-axis}

Truth $W$ is the experimenter's invariant mass $W=\sqrt{M^{2}+2M(E_\nu-E_\mu)-Q^{2}}$, with struck-nucleon mass $M$ (\texttt{CVUniverse::GetTrueExperimentersW()}). Reconstructed $W$ combines the calorimetric energy transfer $q_{0}$ of the reconstructed-$q_{3}$ estimator and the muon-kinematic $Q^{2}$ with the average nucleon mass (\texttt{CVUniverse::RecoW()}). The external generator predictions instead compute truth $W$ with the neutron mass for every event and place events with $W^{2}\le0$ in the lowest $W$ bin, whereas the analysis uses the struck-nucleon mass and excludes such events from its five-observable truth sample. In the GENIE and NuWro samples this moves 0.14--0.15\% of the events into another $W$ bin. In GENIE CV and NuWro (without their 50--\SI{100}{GeV} samples) it changes the coarse-cell predictions by about \SI{0.1}{\percent} at the median and at most \SI{2.2}{\percent}. In any cell, that is at most about one Monte Carlo standard error of the prediction. The change was not evaluated at cell level for GENIE$+$MEC and cannot be evaluated for GiBUU.

Like $q_{3}$ (\S\ref{sec:4d}), and unlike $\Eavail$, $W$ changes under the lateral systematic shifts, so a shifted $W$ is stored for each lateral universe. The event-loop output files also store truth hadronic diagnostics: the proton and pion multiplicities and the leading-hadron angle. The $W$ edges $[0,1.1,1.4,1.8,2.2,3.0,100]\,\si{GeV}$ bracket the quasielastic peak, the $\Delta$, the transition region and the DIS tail. The configuration-(a) five-dimensional unfolding in $(\pT,\pPar,\Eavail,q_{3},W)$ passes the marginalization checks against the lower-dimensional results: integrating over $W$ recovers the configuration-(a) 4D normalization to \SI{\eavailMargin}{\percent} (5D/4D total $=1.0011$), with median differences of \SIrange{0.3}{1.5}{\percent} per axis, and $d\sigma/dW$ is finite and non-negative in all six bins.

Reconstructed $q_{3}$ and $W$ combine the muon kinematics with the calorimetric estimate $q_{0}$ of the energy transfer, which is separate from reconstructed $\Eavail$.  Since $W_{\mathrm{reco}}^{2}=(\bar M+q_{0})^{2}-q_{3,\mathrm{reco}}^{2}$ and $q_{3,\mathrm{reco}}$ increases with $q_{0}$ at fixed muon kinematics, reconstructed $W$ is an exact function of $(\pT,\pPar,q_{3})$, apart from the clamp at $W^{2}=0$, which applies to \SI{1.5}{\percent} of simulated events. At truth level, $q_{3}$ is computed from the true neutrino and muon four-vectors, but $\theta_{\mu}$, and with it $\pT$ and $\pPar$, is measured from a fixed beam axis tilted by \SI{58.87}{mrad} from the detector axis rather than from the true neutrino direction.  Truth $W$ is therefore fixed by $(\pT,\pPar,q_{3})$ only up to that event-by-event angle, of about \SI{1}{mrad}, and the choice of $M$: the residual is within \SI{\pm2}{MeV} for 68\% of events and within $-49$ to $+67$~MeV for 99.9\%; \SI{0.25}{\percent} of events (weighted) change their coarse $W$ bin, and \SI{0.15}{\percent} have no real solution. The five reconstructed inputs therefore share information and do not, by themselves, provide five independent constraints: the result is five simultaneous observables, not five independent measurements.  The article distinguishes the five classifier inputs, the lower-dimensional support they populate, and the five reported observables.  Only reconstructed $W$ is fixed by the other inputs; reconstructed $\Eavail$ comes from a separate calorimetric estimator and is not a function of them. The nearly degenerate correlation directions of the joint-test metric (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-design}) are consistent with this redundancy, but that origin has not been shown, and the information added by $W$ has not been measured. The analysis excludes events with negative truth $W^{2}$, a weight fraction of $4.9\times10^{-5}$, from its five-observable truth sample and normalization (the external predictions' convention is given in \S\ref{sec:eavailw-axis}). The generators measure $\pT$ and $\pPar$ about their own neutrino axis; their $\pT$ differs from the analysis truth $\pT$ by \SI{\pm4}{MeV} for 68\% of events. Over the 109 joint-test cells, this convention changes the analysis simulation's cell contents by factors between 0.9965 and 1.0035 (median 1.000008), at the level of its Monte Carlo precision, and shifts GENIE's central prediction by between $-0.47$ and $+0.53$ of each cell's null spread. For GENIE with MEC, NuWro and GiBUU its effect is not reliably determined (Ref.~\cite{NoteRecords2026}, \recentry{mp5-truth-axis}).

\subsection{Generator band: the corner against four external generators}
\label{sec:eavailw-band}

All four external generators fall below the data in the high-$\Eavail$, high-$W$ corner. Figure~\ref{fig:eavailWband} compares the spline-normalized $(\Eavail,W)$ predictions of GENIE~CV, GENIE$+$Valencia~2p2h/MEC, NuWro and GiBUU, with the GENIE and NuWro predictions flux-repaired as in \S\ref{sec:3d-models}. The 3D and $(\Eavail,W)$ comparisons are made from the same five-dimensional histogram of each generator. GENIE~CV totals \SI{2.76e-38}{cm^2/nucleon} in the measurement phase space in both; GENIE$+$MEC uses the normalization convention of the 3D comparison. In the corner ($\Eavail\ge\SI{0.8}{GeV}$, $W\ge\SI{1.8}{GeV}$), data/generator $=1.14$, $1.14$, $1.16$ and $1.61$, respectively; none reaches the data. For GENIE and NuWro this is close to their whole-plane shortfall (integrated ratios $1.11$, $1.07$ and $1.15$). Their corner ratios exceed their integrated ratios by \SIrange{1}{7}{\percent}, compared with \SI{16}{\percent} for GiBUU (integrated $1.39$; $\sigma=\SI{2.22e-38}{cm^2/nucleon}$ versus data \SI{3.07e-38}{}). GiBUU's larger contrast comes from its sample's energy limit: it was generated only up to $E_\nu\approx\SI{20}{GeV}$ (\S\ref{sec:3d-models}). In GENIE and NuWro, neutrinos above this energy carry \SIrange{14.3}{14.8}{\percent} of the corner cross section and \SIrange{6.8}{7.4}{\percent} overall. Removing them raises these generators' corner-to-integrated contrast to \SIrange{9}{16}{\percent}, bracketing GiBUU's. If GiBUU is instead supplied with the shares that GENIE and NuWro carry above this energy, data/GiBUU becomes about $1.37$ in the corner and $1.29$ integrated. With the flux repaired, the corner therefore has no distinct deficit for any of the four generators. GiBUU is lower than the others everywhere, and its energy limit accounts for the larger corner shortfall.

MINERvA shallow-inelastic neutrino and antineutrino measurements also report shape and normalization disagreements with generators \cite{MINERvA:2026sis}.
