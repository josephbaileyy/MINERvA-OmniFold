\subsection{Hadronic mass $W$ and the $(\Eavail,W)$ plane}
\label{sec:eavailw}

% Methods-paper sync fix round: aligned with paper_body.tex sec:central (descriptive central values; no mechanism). Register round n1 (R-N1): both are stated once, for this section too, at the sec:3d lead.
For the central values, Valencia 2p2h closes about half of the low-recoil gap (\S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h}) but leaves a separate excess over the MINERvA Tune~v1 prior at high $\Eavail$. There the predicted 2p2h contribution is zero, and the two tested pion final-state-interaction variations change the prediction by less than one percent, too little to explain the excess. See also \S\ref{sec:limitations}.

GiBUU is low throughout the $\Eavail$ tail. Its $W$-integrated five-dimensional data/generator ratio is $1.59$ at $\Eavail\in[0.8,1.5)$ and $1.37$ at $[1.5,3.0)$, in configuration~(a), the LightGBM unfolding with purity-based subtraction (\S\ref{sec:method-config}, Table~\ref{tab:config-key}); its sample stops at $E_\nu\approx\SI{20}{GeV}$ (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-band}). GENIE CV instead matches data at $[0.8,1.5)$ and is low at $[1.5,3.0)$, where its three-dimensional data/CV ratio is $1.10$ (\S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h}) in configuration~(e), the deterministic learner with refined negative-weight subtraction.

Adding the hadronic invariant mass $W$ as the fifth OmniFold axis maps where in $W$ the high-$\Eavail$ excess lies. The validation of \S\ref{sec:val-fiveaxis} includes a model-dependence study; it does not establish a bias--variance tradeoff across estimator settings and provides no new total uncertainty for this projection.

\subsubsection{$W$ as a fifth axis}
\label{sec:eavailw-axis}

Truth $W$ is the experimenter's invariant mass $W=\sqrt{M^{2}+2M(E_\nu-E_\mu)-Q^{2}}$, with struck-nucleon mass $M$. Reconstructed $W$ combines the calorimetric energy transfer $q_{0}$ of the reconstructed-$q_{3}$ estimator and the muon-kinematic $Q^{2}$ with the average nucleon mass. The external generator predictions instead compute truth $W$ with the neutron mass for every event and place events with $W^{2}\le0$ in the lowest $W$ bin, whereas the analysis uses the struck-nucleon mass and excludes such events from its five-observable truth sample. In the GENIE and NuWro samples this moves 0.14--0.15\% of the events into another $W$ bin. In GENIE CV and NuWro (without their 50--\SI{100}{GeV} samples) it changes the coarse-cell predictions by about \SI{0.1}{\percent} at the median and at most \SI{2.2}{\percent}. In any cell, that is at most about one Monte Carlo standard error of the prediction. The change was not evaluated at cell level for GENIE$+$MEC and cannot be evaluated for GiBUU.

Like $q_{3}$ (\S\ref{sec:4d}), and unlike $\Eavail$, $W$ changes under the lateral systematic shifts, so a shifted $W$ is stored for each lateral universe. The event-loop output files also store truth hadronic diagnostics: the proton and pion multiplicities and the polar angle of the summed momentum of all final-state particles other than muons and neutrinos. The $W$ edges $[0,1.1,1.4,1.8,2.2,3.0,100]\,\si{GeV}$ bracket the quasielastic peak, the $\Delta$, the transition region and the DIS tail.

The configuration-(a) five-dimensional unfolding in $(\pT,\pPar,\Eavail,q_{3},W)$ agrees under marginalization with the lower-dimensional results: integrating over $W$ reproduces the configuration-(a) 4D normalization to \SI{\eavailMargin}{\percent} (5D/4D total $=1.0011$) with median differences per axis of \SIrange{0.3}{1.5}{\percent}, and $d\sigma/dW$ is finite and non-negative in all six bins.

\subsubsection{Comparison with four external generators}
\label{sec:eavailw-band}

All four external generators fall below the data in the high-$\Eavail$, high-$W$ corner. Figure~\ref{fig:eavailWband} compares the $(\Eavail,W)$ predictions of GENIE~CV, GENIE$+$Valencia~2p2h/MEC, NuWro and GiBUU. As in \S\ref{sec:3d-models}, each is normalized by its flux-averaged total CC cross section per nucleon, and those of GENIE and NuWro are flux-corrected. The 3D and $(\Eavail,W)$ comparisons use the same five-dimensional histogram of each generator. GENIE~CV totals \SI{2.76e-38}{cm^2/nucleon} in the measurement phase space in both; GENIE$+$MEC uses the normalization convention of the 3D comparison.

In the corner ($\Eavail\ge\SI{0.8}{GeV}$, $W\ge\SI{1.8}{GeV}$), data/generator $=1.14$, $1.14$, $1.16$ and $1.61$, respectively. For GENIE and NuWro the corner ratios exceed their integrated ratios ($1.11$, $1.07$ and $1.15$) by \SIrange{1}{7}{\percent}, compared with \SI{16}{\percent} for GiBUU, whose integrated ratio is $1.39$ ($\sigma=\SI{2.22e-38}{cm^2/nucleon}$, against \SI{3.07e-38}{} for the data).

GiBUU's larger contrast comes from its sample's energy limit: it was generated only up to $E_\nu\approx\SI{20}{GeV}$ (\S\ref{sec:3d-models}). In GENIE and NuWro, neutrinos above this energy carry \SIrange{14.3}{14.8}{\percent} of the corner cross section and \SIrange{6.8}{7.4}{\percent} overall. Removing them raises these generators' corner-to-integrated contrast to \SIrange{9}{16}{\percent}, bracketing GiBUU's. If GiBUU is instead supplied with the shares that GENIE and NuWro carry above this energy, data/GiBUU becomes about $1.37$ in the corner and $1.29$ integrated. With the GENIE and NuWro fluxes corrected and GiBUU supplied with the shares above this energy, each generator's central-value corner ratio lies close to its integrated ratio. GiBUU is lower than the others everywhere.
