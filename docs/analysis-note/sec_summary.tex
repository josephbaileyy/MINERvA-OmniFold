\section{Summary and outlook}
\label{sec:summary}

\paragraph{Summary.}
Unbinned OmniFold with gradient-boosted trees reproduces the published
MINERvA ME FHC CC-inclusive $d^{2}\sigma/(d\pT\,d\pPar)$
and its uncertainty scale, with a correlated shape difference along the low-$\pT$
peak ridge ($\chi^{2}/\mathrm{ndf}=\chiPaper$ against the published covariance,
\S\ref{sec:results-tension}) (\S\ref{sec:execsummary}, \S\ref{sec:results}). The
result passes closure, iteration, pull, calibration, and classifier-family
checks, but the existing 2D toys do not furnish an independent-truth coverage
test. On that foundation we
performed \emph{unbinned} simultaneous three-, four- and five-observable
unfolds of MINERvA data ($+\Eavail$, $+q_{3}$, $+W$), each validated against its
lower-dimensional predecessor by projection anchors agreeing to $\approx$1--2\,\%. (Binned MINERvA multi-differential
measurements, up to triple-differential~\cite{MINERvA:2022qe,MINERvA:2026qeleme}, exist; the methodological distinction is the
unbinned, simultaneously-unfolded formulation.) The physics output:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item the low-$\Eavail$ excess over all four truth-level generators in the
        quasielastic--$\Delta$ dip recovers the established MINERvA low-recoil/2p2h deficit through an
        independent unfolding method, with small responses to the tested
        pion-FSI variations and an improved description when Valencia 2p2h is
        included. These tests do not exclude other transport effects or uniquely
        identify a nuclear mechanism (\S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h});
  \item the $(\Eavail,q_{3})$ plane resolves the deficit's momentum-transfer
        structure (\S\ref{sec:4d}); (the 4D systematic-budget composition is not
        quoted --- no corrected 4D covariance is adopted, \S\ref{sec:3d});
  \item the unified-throw machinery now tests the block sum with asymmetric
        endpoints, a fixed estimator seed, and mean-centered joint throws;
        the selection-complete lateral replacement has landed and one
        resulting 5D covariance, the CV-centered variant, is adopted as
        publication-under-exception,
        subject to the four measurements of \S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}; no
        corrected 4D covariance is reported
        (\S\ref{sec:uq-combine});
  \item the $(\Eavail,W)$ study localizes the remaining high-$\Eavail$
        excess over the MINERvA Tune~v1 prior to the deep-inelastic region.
        The four external generator predictions (GENIE and NuWro rebuilt on
        2026-09-27 after a flux-sampling defect) all lie below the central
        result there, each by about its overall normalization shortfall
        once the $E_\nu>\SI{20}{GeV}$ events missing from the GiBUU sample
        are accounted for (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-band}). A projected covariance for that plane
        is adopted and built (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-construction}); no
        significance is quoted because the estimator-seed sensitivity that
        travels with it (measured on two seed-varied sibling products) exceeds the limit declared for it before production
        (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted});
  \item the recoil-only PET point-cloud track provides a low-level-input
        representation cross-check, yielding an absolutely-normalized central
        value; its ordinary closure survives only as a legacy two-iteration
        self-consistency diagnostic, and no current closure level is quoted. Its historical five-component budget
        is quarantined because nuisance and retraining shifts require a joint
        covariance (or explicit cross term) and the detector sample was
        CV-support-limited (\S\ref{sec:pet}). Because the muon is omitted from both classifiers,
        these products do not establish a joint muon--hadron unfold and no
        covariance component transfers automatically to the full-event replacement.
        The whole track is diagnostic and method development: no PET covariance
        is adopted as a publication uncertainty product, and the full-event
        central/statistical pairing was declined rather than resolved.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Outlook.}
The scalar extended-fiducial study (\S\ref{sec:fps}) yields a preliminary
central value $\sigma_{\mathrm{ext}}=\SI{\fpsSigma}{cm^2/nucleon}$
(\SI{\fpsAbove}{\percent} above the restricted phase space), with an anchor,
closure tests and a three-prior envelope but without a corrected covariance
(\S\ref{sec:uq-combine}). About \SI{6}{\percent} of the rate is a newly
acceptance-supported extension, while about \SI{28}{\percent} is a separately
labeled prior-dominated extrapolation. The full-event PET candidate adds the
distinguished muon and declared event context; nominal and unverified
$C_{\rm stat}$ artifacts exist, but their pairing was declined and no PET
covariance is adopted. Its remaining closure and complete-UQ requirements are
method development and are not on the publication critical path. That path ran
through the adoption of a selection-complete scalar five-dimensional
covariance, which has happened under exception (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}); the path

\paragraph{Reusing the unbinned result.}
The public ATLAS charged-particle phase-space release
\cite{ATLAS:2026fpsdata} provides a concrete example of distributing unbinned
products. The emerging \texttt{omnifold\_publication} proposal
\cite{Bajpai:2026publication} emphasizes weight-family metadata, normalization,
row alignment, and checksums. A MINERvA release should likewise specify event
identity, estimator and uncertainty-family definitions, units, support,
normalization, and a reproducible projection example. That release is specified
for this analysis in App.~\ref{app:release}, which also records which of its
objects have fixed identities today and which do not exist yet; \textbf{no
release tag or release manifest exists}, so rebuilding the documents is not
reproduction of any result here. New projections require
their own applicability checks; nominal weights alone cannot certify arbitrary
downstream inference (App.~\ref{sec:unbinned-inference}). Neither this release
design nor a public example bears on the covariance adoption, which is settled
separately (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}).
