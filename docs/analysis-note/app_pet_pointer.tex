\subsection{Point-cloud representations: diagnostic method development}
\label{sec:pet}

Unfolding with point-cloud transformer (PET) representations of the full event, which could use low-level detector and particle information that the scalar observables of this note summarize, is diagnostic method development. Its design-selection studies are complete and selected no design. It is documented in a companion note~\cite{PETNote2026}. No central value, uncertainty or comparison from it is used or claimed in this note.
