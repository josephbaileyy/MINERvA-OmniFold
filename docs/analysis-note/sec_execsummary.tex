This note documents a cross-section measurement and its analysis. It will be released as a versioned public document with the Zenodo reproduction package (App.~\ref{app:release}). Superseded configurations, changed tests and consequential choices made after results were seen that bear on the released results are listed in App.~\ref{app:devhistory}, with what had been seen when each was made, where the note states it.

We apply \emph{unbinned machine-learning unfolding} (OmniFold~\cite{Andreassen:2019cjw, Andreassen:2021zzk}) to MINERvA's medium-energy neutrino data. Applied to the data, the five-observable estimator gives a cross section on the sixteen cells of \S\ref{sec:fived16} in $\pT$, $\pPar$, $\Eavail$, $q_{3}$ and $W$, as illustrative output, not for fitting or for constraining models. Its covariance gives a median per-cell uncertainty of \fvval{sigmed-reported}\%. As a descriptive comparison, with NuWro and GiBUU on carbon, three external generator predictions, compared on all sixteen cells, give $\chi^{2}$ between \fvval{g-ext-chi2int-min16} and \fvval{g-ext-chi2int-max16} for 16 degrees of freedom. GiBUU, compared only on the 8 cells its sample covers, gives $\chi^{2}=\fvval{g-gibuu-carbon-total-chi2int}$ on those cells (\S\ref{sec:fived16-gen}). Table~\ref{tab:result-status} summarizes the construction, checks and limits of each result component.

The unbinned result for $d^{2}\sigma/(d\pT\,d\pPar)$ reproduces the published integrated cross section~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq} within about one percent, with a correlated shape difference (\S\ref{sec:results}). Whether it is ready for publication as a measurement has not been established. The learner was chosen after the comparisons with the publication were known. The background-subtraction treatment was decided on a closure test before the rebuilt result's distance from the published result was computed. The rebuilt result was adopted after that distance was known (\S\ref{sec:method-config} and App.~\ref{app:devhistory}). The comparison with the publication is therefore not an independent test of the method.

Adding $\Eavail$, $q_{3}$ and $W$ gives simultaneous unfolds in three, four and five dimensions. The 3D, 4D and configuration-(a) five-dimensional unfolds (Table~\ref{tab:config-key}) are reported as central values. In configuration~(a), the purity-subtracted estimator of the five-dimensional central value, the 4D and 5D marginal normalizations agree with the lower-dimensional results (\S\ref{sec:4d}, \S\ref{sec:eavailw-axis}). The 3D marginal of configuration~(e), a deterministic estimator with refined negative-weight subtraction, agrees to \SI{0.141}{\percent} in total with the 2D result of configuration~(f), the nominal estimator with that subtraction, a cross-configuration comparison (\S\ref{sec:3d}). Reconstructed $W$ is fixed by $(\pT,\pPar,q_{3})$, apart from a clamp at $W^{2}=0$ (\SI{1.5}{\percent} of simulated events). The five are therefore simultaneous, not independent, observables (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-definitions}).

The generator comparisons of the central values are descriptive (\S\ref{sec:3d-models}, \S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h}).

The generator tests are an application that illustrates calibration within the implemented model, conditional on the assumptions of \S\ref{sec:joint5d}. They are not a measured rejection rate for null pseudo-data processed like the data. Calibrated joint five-dimensional tests of hybrid null hypotheses for five predictions, in total (rate and shape) and in shape, reject all ten under six stated conditions (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-conditions}). The rejections are not physical exclusions, and every test has $p<0.05$, without a multiplicity correction, in at least one of the two matched coarse projections. GENIE~2.12.10 is not rejected in the muon-only projection (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-projections}). The pseudo-experiments lost to time limits have been rerun, for reporting only, and every submitted draw has been observed. No recovered draw changes a decision (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-lost}). Correcting the carbon-only NuWro and GiBUU predictions to the CH target moves both toward the data on the joint cells. On the sixteen cells, GiBUU's total $\chi^{2}$ rises (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-ch}). The ten decisions, made with the carbon-only predictions, are unchanged. In the descriptive re-evaluation with corrected predictions, NuWro's total-test rejection is not robust to the correction (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-ch}).
