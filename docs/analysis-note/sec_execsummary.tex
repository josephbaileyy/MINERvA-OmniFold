We apply \emph{unbinned machine-learning unfolding} (OmniFold~\cite{Andreassen:2019cjw, Andreassen:2021zzk}) to MINERvA's medium-energy neutrino data. The principal result is the sixteen-cell cross section of \S\ref{sec:fived16}, with its covariance and generator comparison. The results hold for this implementation, one data set, and the configurations, observables and deformations tested (\S\ref{sec:intro}). The generator tests are an application that illustrates calibrated inference with a biased estimator.

\paragraph{Results:}

\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt, parsep=0pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Sixteen-cell five-observable cross section.} The cross section in $\pT$, $\pPar$, $\Eavail$, $q_{3}$ and $W$ is reported in sixteen cells with a covariance whose median per-cell uncertainty is \fvval{sigmed-C_total_resp}\%. Three external generator predictions compared on all sixteen cells give $\chi^{2}$ between \fvval{g-ext-chi2int-min16} and \fvval{g-ext-chi2int-max16} for 16 degrees of freedom, and GiBUU, on its 8 cells, \fvval{g-gibuu-total-chi2int} (\S\ref{sec:fived16-gen}).
  \item \textbf{Reproduction.} Unbinned $d^{2}\sigma/(d\pT\,d\pPar)$ reproduces the published integrated cross section~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq} within about one percent, with a correlated shape difference (\S\ref{sec:results}). Whether it is ready for publication as a measurement has not been established. LightGBM was chosen over the exact-split estimator, which is kept as a cross-check, after both comparisons with the publication were known (\S\ref{sec:method-config}).
  \item \textbf{Unbinned 3D/4D/5D central values.} Adding $\Eavail$, $q_{3}$ and $W$ gives simultaneous unfolds, and the 3D, 4D and configuration-(a) five-dimensional unfolds are reported as central values. In configuration (a), the 4D and 5D marginal-normalization checks against the lower-dimensional result pass to $\approx$1--2\,\%. The configuration-(e) 3D marginal agrees with the configuration-(f) 2D result to \SI{0.141}{\percent} in total, a comparison across configurations (\S\ref{sec:3d}). Reconstructed $W$ is fixed by $(\pT,\pPar,q_{3})$, so the five are simultaneous, not independent, observables (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-definitions}).
  \item \textbf{Descriptive comparisons of the central values.} All four truth-level generators, GENIE~\cite{Andreopoulos:2009rq}, MINERvA Tune~\cite{MINERvA:2016lowrecoil}, NuWro~\cite{Golan:2012wx} and GiBUU~\cite{Buss:2011mx}, under-predict the unfolded central value in the quasielastic--$\Delta$ dip ($0.1\le\Eavail<\SI{0.4}{GeV}$), the region of the established MINERvA low-recoil discrepancy~\cite{MINERvA:2016lowrecoil,MINERvA:2022incl} (\S\ref{sec:3d-models}, \S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h}). In cell-integrated terms, most of the positive difference from the Tune~v1 prior lies at $\Eavail\ge\SI{0.8}{GeV}$, much of it at $W\ge\SI{1.8}{GeV}$ (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-map}). The high-$\Eavail$, high-$W$ region carries the largest regularization bias measured in \S\ref{sec:5d}. The four external generators also fall below the data in this corner, each by about its overall shortfall once GiBUU's sample limit is allowed for (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-band}).
  \item \textbf{Application: joint five-dimensional generator tests.} Calibrated tests of hybrid null hypotheses for five predictions, in total (rate and shape) and in shape, reject all ten under six stated conditions (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-conditions}). The rejections are not physical exclusions, and matched coarse projections reject as well (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-projections}). The pseudo-experiments lost to time limits have been rerun, for reporting only, so every submitted draw has been observed. No recovered draw changes a decision (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-lost}). Correcting the carbon-only NuWro and GiBUU predictions to the CH target moves both toward the data. In this descriptive re-evaluation, NuWro's total-test rejection is not robust to the correction, and the ten decisions are unchanged (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-ch}).
  \item \textbf{A methodological finding.} The standard covariance sums single-source systematic matrices, each with all other sources nominal, and so omits nonlinear cross-source response terms. A corrected unified-throw test perturbs all reweightable sources together and stores the joint mean shift separately (\S\ref{sec:uq-combine}).
  \item \textbf{A preliminary extended-fiducial study.} Removing truth-muon cuts, with the fiducial volume and MINOS-based selection kept, puts about \SI{6}{\percent} of the scalar 2D rate in newly acceptance-supported cells (part of tier~1) and about \SI{28}{\percent} in low- or zero-efficiency cells (tier~2; \S\ref{sec:fps}).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Principal limitations:}\label{sec:execsummary-limitations} The body states each limitation with the result it qualifies; the principal ones are these. The quoted 2D statistical block has had no coverage test; the fixed-truth coverage studies scored two earlier statistical bands, of which the first-built undercovers in its tails ($C_2=\covTwoDCTwo$, vs.\ $0.954$; \S\ref{sec:uq-boot}, \S\ref{sec:coverage}). No result's intervals have validated coverage (two dimensions: \S\ref{sec:uq-2d-scope}; five dimensions: \S\ref{sec:coverage-5d}; the sixteen cells: \S\ref{sec:fived16-fd}). The others are stated with their results: the generator comparisons (\S\S\ref{sec:3d}--\ref{sec:eavailw}); the absence of a 3D or 4D covariance (\S\ref{sec:3d}); the five-dimensional estimator's regularization bias (\S\ref{sec:val-departure}) and the purity-subtraction bias of configurations (a) and (c) (\S\ref{sec:val-negweight}); five-dimensional intervals and their coverage (\S\ref{sec:gbdt-model-dependence}, \S\ref{sec:coverage-5d}); the five-axis covariance of the configuration-(a) central value (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}); the calibration and nuisance model of the generator tests (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-frozen}, \S\ref{sec:joint5d-response}); tier~2 of the extended-fiducial study (\S\ref{sec:fps}); the fake-data limitation of the sixteen-cell result (\S\ref{sec:fived16-fd}); and point-cloud work (App.~\ref{sec:pet}).
