\section*{Executive summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive summary}
\label{sec:execsummary}

We apply \emph{unbinned machine-learning unfolding}
(OmniFold~\cite{Andreassen:2019cjw, Andreassen:2021zzk}) to MINERvA's
medium-energy neutrino data. Instead of a migration matrix between histogram bins,
whose size multiplies with each added observable, OmniFold learns an event weight
that morphs simulation into data and transfers it to the pre-detector level through
the simulation's truth--reconstruction pairing. Reporting binning is imposed only at
the end, so a recorded observable can be added without constructing a new response
tensor, although training, support, and uncertainty propagation still become harder
with dimension. The analysis reproduces a published measurement and extends it to
simultaneous three-, four-, and five-observable scalar cross sections; it also studies
a recoil-only point-cloud representation and a preliminary extended fiducial domain
with relaxed truth-muon cuts. The specific scalar extensions studied here do not have
direct binned MINERvA counterparts. The fixed-dimension measurements use
gradient-boosted decision trees, validated with closure, iteration, pull, and
calibration diagnostics and neural-network cross-checks.

\paragraph{Results overview:}
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Reproduction.}
  Our unbinned $d^{2}\sigma/(d\pT\,d\pPar)$ matches the published measurement~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq}: total cross-section ratio $\ratioTot$, \SI{\binsTen}{\percent} of bins within \SI{10}{\percent}, per-bin standardized differences sub-$1\sigma$, and a separately reconstructed uncertainty budget whose median per-bin uncertainty (\SI{\uqMedian}{\percent}) matches the published \SI{\uqPaper}{\percent} (\S\ref{sec:results}, \S\ref{sec:systematics}, Fig.~\ref{fig:xsec}).

  \item \textbf{Unbinned 3D/4D/5D MINERvA unfolds.}
  Adding available energy ($\Eavail$), momentum transfer ($q_{3}$), and hadronic mass ($W$) produces unbinned simultaneous three-, four- and five-observable unfolds; every result passes its projection anchors against its lower-dimensional predecessor to $\approx$1--2\,\% (\S\ref{sec:3d}). Prior MINERvA multi-differential measurements, including a binned triple-differential one~\cite{MINERvA:2022qe}, used explicit binned unfolding; the distinction here is the unbinned, simultaneously-unfolded formulation, not multi-dimensionality itself.

  \item \textbf{Established low-recoil discrepancy recovered.}
  All four truth-level generators (GENIE~\cite{Andreopoulos:2009rq}, MINERvA Tune~\cite{MINERvA:2016lowrecoil}, NuWro~\cite{Golan:2012wx}, GiBUU~\cite{Buss:2011mx}) under-predict the data in the quasielastic--$\Delta$ dip ($0.1\le\Eavail<\SI{0.4}{GeV}$). The tested pion final-state-interaction dials move the spectrum by less than \SI{1}{\percent}, while adding Valencia 2p2h~\cite{Nieves:2011pp,Gran:2013kda} fills \SI{52}{\percent} of the data$-$CV gap in that dip (\SI{63}{\percent} of the integrated deficit; GENIE and NuWro are the flux-repaired predictions, \texttt{KNOWN\_ISSUES.md} row 83): the established MINERvA low-recoil/2p2h deficit~\cite{MINERvA:2016lowrecoil,MINERvA:2022incl}, re-found by an independent method (\S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h}, Fig.~\ref{fig:3dmodels}).

  \item \textbf{A new localization of the remaining excess.}
  The high-$\Eavail$ excess over the MINERvA Tune~v1 prior that 2p2h cannot explain concentrates in the deep-inelastic corner ($\Eavail\ge\SI{0.8}{GeV}$, $W\ge\SI{1.8}{GeV}$). The four external generators also lie below the data there, but on the flux-repaired predictions GENIE and NuWro fall short in the corner by about as much as overall (corner data/generator $1.14$--$1.16$ against $1.07$--$1.15$ integrated); only GiBUU is markedly more deficient there ($1.61$ against $1.39$ integrated). A covariance for this projection is adopted, as publication-under-exception (\zprojCells-cell projection \zprojDigest\ldots\ of the five-axis trunk \zcvDigest\ldots, paired to the figure's central values by digest identity), but the comparison is reported at central-value level: the covariance's estimator-seed sensitivity is \SI{\sprojMeasured}{\percent} against a \SI{\sprojBound}{\percent} limit fixed before production, so no compatibility metric computed from it would state a precision the input has (\S\ref{sec:eavailw}, Fig.~\ref{fig:eavailWband}; adoption and construction in \S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}, \S\ref{sec:eavailw-construction}).

  \item \textbf{A methodological finding.}
  The standard covariance adds matrices built for one systematic source at a time, with all other sources held nominal, so it contains no cross-source response terms. A corrected unified-throw test perturbs all reweightable sources simultaneously and reports the joint mean shift separately. One resulting 5D product is adopted as publication-under-exception; the other corrected candidates are not adopted (\S\ref{sec:uq-combine}, \S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}).

  \item \textbf{Recoil-point-cloud representation cross-check (diagnostic, method development).}
  A point-cloud transformer uses reconstructed recoil clusters at step~1 and truth final-state hadrons at step~2; muon kinematics enter selection and binning but neither classifier, so its central value and ordinary closure test a recoil-only estimator (\S\ref{sec:pet}, \S\ref{sec:pet-projection}).

  \item \textbf{A preliminary extended-fiducial study.}
  Re-running the scalar 2D unfold with the truth-muon kinematic cuts removed enlarges the signal definition, keeping the tracker fiducial interaction volume and the unchanged MINOS-based reconstructed selection. About \SI{6}{\percent} of the rate lies in a newly acceptance-supported extension (tier~1); about \SI{28}{\percent} lies in low- or zero-efficiency cells (tier~2) (\S\ref{sec:fps}).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{What is not claimed:}
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item \emph{Coverage.} No 2D coverage number is quoted; the independent-truth test remains open (\S\ref{sec:coverage}). No frequentist coverage is claimed for any five-dimensional interval: the 5D independent-truth test failed its predeclared futility criterion (\S\ref{sec:coverage-5d}).
% Source: docs/orchestration/OUTCOME-20260925-s5c-tier-s-futility-fail.md; VALIDATION_LEDGER VL150.
  \item \emph{Regularization bias.} The estimator does not follow a generator-sized change of the $\Eavail$ shape (up to \SI{74}{\percent} off in the highest-$W$ column), and this bias is in no quoted uncertainty. The unbinned negative-weight background treatment removes most of the purity method's bias at high $W$ but leaves up to about \SI{1}{\percent} in some joint cells (\S\ref{sec:val-negweight}).
% Source: docs/orchestration/OUTCOME-20260925-s5n-stage1-development-fail.md; VL151-VL152;
% KNOWN_ISSUES 75, 77.
  \item \emph{Unfolding-model dependence and estimator stability.} The GiBUU-to-GENIE departure is a deformation of fixed size, not a measured generator difference (its GENIE input was later found flux-defective, \texttt{KNOWN\_ISSUES.md} row 83); no tested remedy removes the shortfall, so it is carried as an unfolding-model dependence that is in no quoted covariance. No estimator qualifies: the refinement-capacity candidate's output on the real data moves by up to 2.6 statistical standard deviations under floating-point-level input changes, and whether any quoted uncertainty absorbs this is not established (\S\ref{sec:val-departure}).

  \item \emph{Mechanism.} The FSI statement concerns the tested pion dials only; it does not exclude other transport effects or uniquely identify the nuclear mechanism.
  \item \emph{Adopted covariance.} The adopted 5D product carries four measurements that qualify everything built from it (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}); the other corrected candidates are not adopted.
  \item \emph{PET.} No PET covariance is adopted as a publication uncertainty product; the historical uncertainty assembly is quarantined; a full-event estimator, trained jointly on the muon and recoil, would need a new nominal fit, omitted-muon stress closure, and uncertainty budget; and the full-event central/statistical pairing was declined rather than resolved, with coverage the missing evidence (\S\ref{sec:pet}).
  \item \emph{Extended fiducial domain.} Tier~2 is prior-dominated extrapolation, reported separately, and there is no corrected extended-fiducial covariance (\S\ref{sec:fps}).
\end{itemize}
