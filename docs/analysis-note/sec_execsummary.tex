\paragraph{Results overview:}
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt, parsep=0pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Reproduction.}
  Unbinned $d^{2}\sigma/(d\pT\,d\pPar)$ reproduces the published result~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq} bin by bin: total cross-section ratio $\ratioTot$, \SI{\binsTen}{\percent} of bins within \SI{10}{\percent}, and a separately reconstructed uncertainty comparable to the published one (per-bin median \SI{\uqMedian}{\percent} vs.\ \SI{\uqPaper}{\percent}, integral \SI{\uqIntegral}{\percent} vs.\ \SI{\uqPaperIntegral}{\percent}); its flux band carries MAT's $\nu$--$e$ and inverse-muon-decay constraint weights, which an earlier version omitted (\S\ref{sec:results-uq}). The central value and that uncertainty use the same LightGBM estimator; the quoted central value was changed to it from an exact-split estimator, now a cross-check, after both comparisons with the publication were known (\S\ref{sec:method-config}). Against the full published covariance $\chi^{2}/\mathrm{ndf}=\chiPaper$ (exact-split $\chiPaperExact$): a correlated shape difference (\S\ref{sec:results-tension}).

  \item \textbf{Established low-recoil discrepancy recovered.}
  All four truth-level generators (GENIE~\cite{Andreopoulos:2009rq}, MINERvA Tune~\cite{MINERvA:2016lowrecoil}, NuWro~\cite{Golan:2012wx}, GiBUU~\cite{Buss:2011mx}; GENIE and NuWro flux-repaired, \S\ref{sec:3d-models}) under-predict data in the quasielastic--$\Delta$ dip ($0.1\le\Eavail<\SI{0.4}{GeV}$). Tested pion-FSI dials shift the spectrum by less than \SI{1}{\percent} (same-sample ratio, pre-repair GENIE).   \item \textbf{High-$\Eavail$, high-$W$ excess (descriptive).}
  In cell-integrated terms, most of the positive difference from the Tune~v1 prior lies at $\Eavail\ge\SI{0.8}{GeV}$, much of it at $W\ge\SI{1.8}{GeV}$ in the deep-inelastic corner; a fifth of the positive difference sits in the single widest overflow cell, this region carries the largest regularization bias, and in ratio the excess also extends to low $W$. The four external generators also underpredict data in the corner, each by about its overall shortfall after allowing for GiBUU's $E_\nu\approx\SI{20}{GeV}$ sample limit. The projection's covariance is used under a documented exception, but the estimator-seed sensitivity measured on two seed-varied companion covariances exceeds the limit fixed before production, so the comparison uses central values only (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}).

  \item \textbf{Joint five-dimensional generator tests.}
  Calibrated tests of five predictions (Tune~v1, GENIE with and without MEC, NuWro, GiBUU), in total and in shape, reject all ten simple hypotheses at a family-wise error rate of 0.05 under six stated conditions. Each rejection also holds in a robustness variant, not used for the decisions, that assumes a larger residual below the finest tested grid; that residual is unmeasured. Pseudo-experiments lost to time limits were all rerun and observed afterwards, outside the decisions; none changes a decision. Correcting the carbon-only NuWro and GiBUU predictions to the CH target moves both toward the data, and NuWro's total-test rejection does not survive that correction robustly (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-ch}). These are hypothesis tests, not a measured uncertainty (\S\ref{sec:joint5d}).

  \item \textbf{A methodological finding.}
  The standard covariance sums single-source systematic matrices with all others nominal, omitting cross-source response terms; a corrected unified-throw test perturbs all reweightable sources together, reporting the joint mean shift separately (\S\ref{sec:uq-combine}).

  Removing truth-muon cuts (fiducial volume and MINOS-based selection kept) puts about \SI{6}{\percent} of the scalar 2D rate in newly acceptance-supported cells (part of tier~1) and about \SI{28}{\percent} in low- or zero-efficiency ones (tier~2) (\S\ref{sec:fps}).
