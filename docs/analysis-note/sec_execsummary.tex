\section*{Executive summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive summary}
\label{sec:execsummary}

We apply \emph{unbinned machine-learning unfolding}
(OmniFold~\cite{Andreassen:2019cjw, Andreassen:2021zzk}) to MINERvA's
medium-energy neutrino data.
% Cut (lane 1 skeleton; the migration-matrix/morphing mechanism): sec_method.tex:7-32, sec_3d.tex:4-11.
Reporting binning is imposed only at
the end, so a recorded observable can be added without constructing a new response
tensor, although training, support, and uncertainty propagation still become harder
with dimension. The analysis reproduces a published measurement and extends it to
simultaneous three-, four-, and five-observable scalar cross sections; it also studies
a preliminary extended fiducial domain with relaxed truth-muon cuts and a
recoil-only point-cloud representation.

\paragraph{Results overview:}
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Reproduction.}
  Our unbinned $d^{2}\sigma/(d\pT\,d\pPar)$ reproduces the published measurement~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq} bin by bin: total cross-section ratio $\ratioTot$, \SI{\binsTen}{\percent} of bins within \SI{10}{\percent}, per-bin standardized differences sub-$1\sigma$, and a separately reconstructed median per-bin uncertainty (\SI{\uqMedian}{\percent}) matching the published \SI{\uqPaper}{\percent} (\S\ref{sec:results}, \S\ref{sec:systematics}, Fig.~\ref{fig:xsec}). Against the full published covariance $\chi^{2}/\mathrm{ndf}=\chiPaper$: a correlated shape difference along the low-$\pT$ peak ridge, not a normalization one (\S\ref{sec:results-tension}).

  \item \textbf{Unbinned 3D/4D/5D MINERvA unfolds.}
  Adding available energy ($\Eavail$), momentum transfer ($q_{3}$), and hadronic mass ($W$) produces unbinned simultaneous three-, four- and five-observable unfolds; every result passes its projection anchors against its lower-dimensional predecessor to $\approx$1--2\,\% (\S\ref{sec:3d}). Binned triple-differential MINERvA measurements exist~\cite{MINERvA:2022qe,MINERvA:2026qeleme}; the distinction here is the unbinned, simultaneously-unfolded formulation, not multi-dimensionality itself.

  \item \textbf{Established low-recoil discrepancy recovered.}
  All four truth-level generators (GENIE~\cite{Andreopoulos:2009rq}, MINERvA Tune~\cite{MINERvA:2016lowrecoil}, NuWro~\cite{Golan:2012wx}, GiBUU~\cite{Buss:2011mx}; GENIE and NuWro flux-repaired, \S\ref{sec:3d-models}) under-predict the data in the quasielastic--$\Delta$ dip ($0.1\le\Eavail<\SI{0.4}{GeV}$). The tested pion final-state-interaction dials move the spectrum by less than \SI{1}{\percent} (a same-sample ratio on the pre-repair GENIE sample), while Valencia 2p2h~\cite{Nieves:2011pp,Gran:2013kda} fills \SI{52}{\percent} of the data$-$CV gap below $\Eavail=\SI{0.4}{GeV}$: the established MINERvA low-recoil/2p2h deficit~\cite{MINERvA:2016lowrecoil,MINERvA:2022incl}, re-found by an independent method (\S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h}, Fig.~\ref{fig:3dmodels}).

  % flux-repair scope and its ledger row: sec_3d.tex:245-270.

  \item \textbf{A new localization of the remaining excess.}
  The high-$\Eavail$ excess over the MINERvA Tune~v1 prior that 2p2h cannot explain concentrates in the deep-inelastic corner ($\Eavail\ge\SI{0.8}{GeV}$, $W\ge\SI{1.8}{GeV}$). The four external generators also lie below the data there; GENIE and NuWro fall short in the corner by about as much as overall (corner data/generator $1.14$--$1.16$ against $1.07$--$1.15$ integrated). GiBUU falls further short ($1.61$ against $1.39$ integrated), which its event sample's $E_\nu\approx\SI{20}{GeV}$ limit accounts for. A covariance for this projection is adopted under an exception, but the comparison is reported at central-value level: the estimator-seed sensitivity that travels with it, measured on two seed-varied companion products, is \SI{\sprojMeasured}{\percent} against a \SI{\sprojBound}{\percent} limit fixed before production, so no compatibility metric is quoted (\S\ref{sec:eavailw}, Fig.~\ref{fig:eavailWband}; adoption in \S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}).

  \item \textbf{A methodological finding.}
  The standard covariance adds matrices built for one systematic source at a time, with all other sources held nominal, so it contains no cross-source response terms. A corrected unified-throw test perturbs all reweightable sources simultaneously and reports the joint mean shift separately (\S\ref{sec:uq-combine}).

  \item \textbf{A preliminary extended-fiducial study.}
  Re-running the scalar 2D unfold without the truth-muon kinematic cuts, with the same tracker fiducial volume and MINOS-based reconstructed selection: about \SI{6}{\percent} of the rate lies in a newly acceptance-supported extension (part of tier~1); about \SI{28}{\percent} lies in low- or zero-efficiency cells (tier~2) (\S\ref{sec:fps}).

  \item \textbf{Recoil-point-cloud representation cross-check (diagnostic, method development).}
  A point-cloud transformer uses reconstructed recoil clusters at step~1 and truth final-state hadrons at step~2; muon kinematics enter selection and binning but neither classifier, so its central value and ordinary closure test a recoil-only estimator (\S\ref{sec:pet}, \S\ref{sec:pet-projection}).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{What is not claimed:}
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item \emph{Coverage.} No 2D coverage number is quoted; the independent-truth test remains open (\S\ref{sec:coverage}). No frequentist coverage is claimed for any five-dimensional interval; the one 5D independent-truth test, on bias-corrected statistical intervals of a deterministic variant of the estimator, failed its predeclared futility criterion (\S\ref{sec:coverage-5d}).
% Source: docs/orchestration/OUTCOME-20260925-s5c-tier-s-futility-fail.md; VALIDATION_LEDGER VL150.
  \item \emph{Regularization bias.} The estimator does not follow a generator-sized change of the $\Eavail$ shape (up to \SI{74}{\percent} off in the highest-$W$ column), and this bias is in no quoted uncertainty. The unbinned negative-weight background treatment removes most of the purity method's bias at high $W$ but leaves up to about \SI{1}{\percent} in some joint cells (\S\ref{sec:val-negweight}).
% Source: docs/orchestration/OUTCOME-20260925-s5n-stage1-development-fail.md; VL151-VL152;
% KNOWN_ISSUES 75, 77.
  \item \emph{Unfolding-model dependence and estimator stability.} The GiBUU-to-GENIE departure is a deformation of fixed size, not a measured generator difference; no tested remedy removes the shortfall, so it is the regularization bias above, in no quoted covariance. No estimator qualifies: the estimator's output on the real data (the refinement-capacity candidate and its baseline alike) moves by up to 2.6 statistical standard deviations under floating-point-level input changes. A later measurement places this rounding spread inside the refit-per-replica data bootstrap (about \SI{37}{\percent} of its variance), so it is not an unquantified extra uncertainty, but the central value is reproducible only to that size (\S\ref{sec:val-departure}).

  \item \emph{Adopted covariance.} The adopted 5D product carries four measurements that qualify everything built from it (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}); the other corrected candidates are not adopted.
  \item \emph{Extended fiducial domain.} Tier~2 is prior-dominated extrapolation, reported separately, and there is no corrected extended-fiducial covariance (\S\ref{sec:fps}).
  \item \emph{PET.} No PET covariance is adopted as a publication uncertainty product; a full-event estimator, trained jointly on the muon and recoil, has a nominal artifact and an unverified $C_{\rm stat}$ artifact but no uncertainty budget, and their central/statistical pairing was declined rather than resolved (\S\ref{sec:pet}).
