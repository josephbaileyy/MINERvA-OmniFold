We apply \emph{unbinned machine-learning unfolding} (OmniFold~\cite{Andreassen:2019cjw, Andreassen:2021zzk}) to MINERvA's medium-energy neutrino data. This note is the technical record of a methods and benchmark study of that unfolding. Applied to the data, the five-observable estimator gives a cross section on the sixteen cells of \S\ref{sec:fived16} in $\pT$, $\pPar$, $\Eavail$, $q_{3}$ and $W$, as illustrative output, not for fitting or for constraining models. Its covariance gives a median per-cell uncertainty of \fvval{sigmed-reported}\%. As a descriptive comparison, three external generator predictions, compared on all sixteen cells, give $\chi^{2}$ between \fvval{g-ext-chi2int-min16} and \fvval{g-ext-chi2int-max16} for 16 degrees of freedom. GiBUU, compared only on the 8 cells its sample covers, gives $\chi^{2}=\fvval{g-gibuu-total-chi2int}$ on those cells (\S\ref{sec:fived16-gen}). Table~\ref{tab:result-status} summarizes the status, validation checks, and citability of each result component.

The unbinned result for $d^{2}\sigma/(d\pT\,d\pPar)$ reproduces the published integrated cross section~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq} within about one percent, with a correlated shape difference (\S\ref{sec:results}). Whether it is ready for publication as a measurement has not been established. LightGBM was chosen over the exact-split estimator, which is kept as a cross-check, after both comparisons with the publication were known (\S\ref{sec:method-config}). The background-subtraction treatment of the 2D result was decided on a closure test before the rebuilt result's distance from the published result was computed, and the rebuilt result was adopted after that distance was known (\S\ref{sec:method-config}); the comparison with the publication is therefore not an independent test of the method.

Adding $\Eavail$, $q_{3}$ and $W$ gives simultaneous unfolds in three, four and five dimensions; the 3D, 4D and configuration-(a) five-dimensional unfolds are reported as central values. In configuration~(a), the purity-subtracted estimator of the five-dimensional central value, the 4D and 5D marginal-normalization checks against the lower-dimensional result pass (\S\ref{sec:eavailw}). The 3D marginal of configuration~(e), a deterministic estimator with refined negative-weight subtraction, agrees to \SI{0.141}{\percent} in total with the 2D result of configuration~(f), the nominal estimator with that subtraction, a cross-configuration comparison (\S\ref{sec:3d}). Reconstructed $W$ is fixed by $(\pT,\pPar,q_{3})$, so the five are simultaneous, not independent, observables (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-definitions}).

The generator comparisons of the central values are descriptive (\S\ref{sec:3d-models}, \S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h}).

The generator tests are an application that illustrates calibrated inference with a biased estimator. Calibrated joint five-dimensional tests of hybrid null hypotheses for five predictions, in total (rate and shape) and in shape, reject all ten under six stated conditions (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-conditions}). The rejections are not physical exclusions, and each matched coarse projection rejects in at least one of the two projections; GENIE~2.12.10 is not rejected in the muon-only projection (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-projections}). The pseudo-experiments lost to time limits have been rerun, for reporting only, so every submitted draw has been observed. No recovered draw changes a decision (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-lost}). Correcting the carbon-only NuWro and GiBUU predictions to the CH target moves both toward the data on the joint cells; on the sixteen cells GiBUU's total $\chi^{2}$ rises (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-ch}). The ten decisions, made with the carbon-only predictions, are unchanged. In the descriptive re-evaluation with corrected predictions, NuWro's total-test rejection is not robust to the correction (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-ch}).

A methodological finding concerns the standard covariance, which sums single-source systematic matrices, each with all other sources nominal, and so omits nonlinear cross-source response terms. No two-dimensional comparison of a joint throw with the block sum is reported. Where a five-dimensional construction stores the joint mean shift separately, that norm is not quoted (\S\ref{sec:uq-combine}).

A preliminary extended-fiducial study removes the truth-muon cuts and keeps the fiducial volume and MINOS-based selection. It puts about \SI{6}{\percent} of the scalar 2D rate in newly acceptance-supported cells (part of tier~1, the interpreted measurement) and about \SI{28}{\percent} in low- or zero-efficiency cells (tier~2, extrapolated from the prior; \S\ref{sec:fps}).
