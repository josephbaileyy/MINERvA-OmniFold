% Methods-paper sync (METHODS-IMPLEMENTATION.md sec. 4, framing; paper_body.tex sec:intro): validation study first, the generator tests as an application.
We apply \emph{unbinned machine-learning unfolding} (OmniFold~\cite{Andreassen:2019cjw, Andreassen:2021zzk}) to MINERvA's medium-energy neutrino data. The main results are the validation findings. They hold for this implementation, one data set, and the configurations, observables and deformations tested (\S\ref{sec:intro}). The generator tests are an application that illustrates calibrated inference with a biased estimator.

\paragraph{Results overview:}
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt, parsep=0pt, topsep=2pt]
  \item \textbf{Reproduction.} Unbinned $d^{2}\sigma/(d\pT\,d\pPar)$ reproduces the published integrated cross section~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq} within about one percent, with a correlated shape difference. The total cross-section ratio is $\ratioTot$, and \SI{\binsTen}{\percent} of bins are within \SI{10}{\percent}. A separately constructed uncertainty is comparable to the published one: its per-bin median is \SI{\uqMedian}{\percent} against \SI{\uqPaper}{\percent}, and its integral \SI{\uqIntegral}{\percent} against \SI{\uqPaperIntegral}{\percent}. Its flux band carries MAT's $\nu$--$e$ and inverse-muon-decay constraint weights (\S\ref{sec:results-uq}). A generator-shape model-dependence component, reported beside that uncertainty and absent from the published one, raises the per-bin median to \SI{\qrCmodelMedian}{\percent} (\S\ref{sec:uq-2d-scope}). The central value and that uncertainty share the LightGBM estimator, with refined negative-weight background subtraction. LightGBM was chosen over the exact-split estimator, which is kept as a cross-check, after both comparisons with the publication were known (\S\ref{sec:method-config}). Against the full published covariance, $\chi^{2}/\mathrm{ndf}=\chiPaper$ (exact-split: $\chiPaperExact$) is a descriptive distance. Its source has not been identified (\S\ref{sec:results-tension}).

  \item \textbf{Application: joint five-dimensional generator tests.} Calibrated tests of hybrid null hypotheses for five predictions, in total (rate and shape) and in shape, reject all ten under six stated conditions. The pseudo-experiments lost to time limits have been rerun, for reporting only, so every submitted draw has been observed. No recovered draw changes a decision (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-lost}). In this descriptive re-evaluation, NuWro's total-test rejection is not robust to the correction, and the ten decisions are unchanged (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-ch}).

  \item \textbf{A methodological finding.} The standard covariance sums single-source systematic matrices, each with all other sources nominal, and so omits nonlinear cross-source response terms. A corrected unified-throw test perturbs all reweightable sources together and stores the joint mean shift separately (\S\ref{sec:uq-combine}).

\paragraph{Principal limitations:} The body states each limitation with the result it qualifies; the principal ones are these. The quoted 2D statistical block has had no coverage test; the fixed-truth coverage studies scored two earlier statistical bands, of which the first-built undercovers in its tails ($C_2=\covTwoDCTwo$, vs.\ $0.954$; \S\ref{sec:uq-boot}, \S\ref{sec:coverage}). The generator comparisons of \S\S\ref{sec:3d}--\ref{sec:eavailw} are descriptive comparisons of central values that identify no nuclear or interaction mechanism. No 3D or 4D covariance is quoted (\S\ref{sec:3d}). The five-dimensional estimator recovers only part of a generator-sized truth change, with residuals of up to \SI{74}{\percent} in the highest-$W$ column. No quoted uncertainty includes this residual regularization bias (\S\ref{sec:val-departure}). Closure tests with backgrounds find a purity-subtraction bias of up to \SI{4}{\percent} in the highest-$W$ cells. The five-dimensional central value keeps that bias, and no quoted uncertainty includes it (\S\ref{sec:val-negweight}). Above two dimensions the results are central values without intervals of validated coverage. No five-dimensional measurement with total uncertainties was constructed (\S\ref{sec:gbdt-model-dependence}). The one five-dimensional coverage test, of bias-corrected statistical intervals of a deterministic estimator variant, miscovered at the tested truths. No frequentist coverage is therefore claimed for any five-dimensional interval (\S\ref{sec:coverage-5d}). The one five-dimensional covariance used in this note, with its $(\Eavail,W)$ projection, does not meet its specification's acceptance criteria. Four measurements limit what it supports (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}). The calibration of the generator tests is valid for the simulated null to the extent that the pseudo-experiment chain reproduces the analysis applied to the data (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-frozen}). Their nuisance model has no calorimetric recoil-response band (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-response}). Tier~2 of the extended-fiducial study is prior-dominated extrapolation (\S\ref{sec:fps}). Point-cloud (PET) unfolding is future work (\S\ref{sec:intro}).
