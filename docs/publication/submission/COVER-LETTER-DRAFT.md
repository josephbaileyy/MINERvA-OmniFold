Dear Editors,

We submit "Unbinned Five-Observable Unfolding of MINERvA Charged-Current Neutrino Data and Calibrated Joint Tests
of Generator Predictions" for consideration as a Regular Article in Physical Review D.

**Context.** Multi-differential neutrino cross-section measurements are central to constraining the interaction
models used by oscillation experiments. Measurements on data so far reach three dimensions, and they use binned
unfolding. Unbinned machine-learning unfolding has been applied to collider data and demonstrated on simulated
neutrino data. To our knowledge, this is its first application to neutrino-scattering data.

**Key findings.**
1. Using the MINERvA open-data release, a simultaneous two-observable unfold reproduces the published
   d²σ/(dp_T dp∥) measurement to 1.1% in total. Its statistical band, as first built, failed a pre-registered
   fixed-truth coverage test. We report the identified cause, the rebuilt band, and the fact that the rebuilt band
   has not yet been re-tested.
2. We extend the unfolding to five simultaneous observables (adding E_avail, q3 and W). The central values pass
   marginal anchors and closure tests and recover the known low-recoil discrepancy. We measure the estimator's
   regularization and background biases and state where they matter. Because its model dependence prevents a
   qualified total uncertainty at useful precision, we do not claim a five-dimensional cross-section measurement.
3. Instead, a joint test calibrated by pseudo-experiments through the full analysis chain, frozen before any
   observed statistic, rejects all ten simple hypotheses for five generator predictions at familywise level 0.05,
   under stated conditions. Every pseudo-experiment lost to scheduler limits was recovered in a report-only rerun
   without changing a decision. An independent recomputation agrees on every compared quantity. An exploratory ±4%
   recoil-response variation leaves every rejection in place. We state explicitly that the joint information does
   not add discrimination beyond matched coarse projections.
4. A 16 MB release lets readers replay every p-value and decision, and regenerate the figures, with standard
   numerical libraries.
