## What is decided

1. **The abstracts.** Joseph likes the proposed changes and asks two questions. The director answers them, and both go
   to the independent PRD review (item 2):
   - **Is a configuration label typical in an abstract?** It is not. PRD abstracts describe a method in words, not by
     internal labels such as "configuration (a)" or "(e)".
   - **Does the change make the abstract too long?** That is measured against the current abstract and against
     comparable PRD abstracts.
2. **Choices 2, 3, 4 and 5 go to the independent PRD-language sessions first.**
   - **Which sessions:** the paper rewrite lane's calibrated raters, on allowed accounts.
   - **What they rule on:** the abstract proposals, the further paper wording, the release-list scopings, the
     sensitivity variants and the qualifications. For each, they accept, deny or shorten, from the printed text alone.
   - **The truth check.** The lane checks every denial or shortening against the records. A qualifier stays where
     leaving it out would make a printed sentence false as read.
   - **Then Joseph sees one final draft.** Whether the paper ships a supplemental data file stays his release decision
     (Q8). The review advises only on its wording.
3. **Choice 6: the addendum is pushed.** That is `pvl/proj1d-addendum-20261027`, commit `9b8be28d`. The director
   pushes it and opens its PR, reviews it at its head, and merges it on the usual gates.
4. **Choice 7: pushing to the public GitHub repository is not publication.**
   - Branches, pull requests, records and merges on `github.com/josephbaileyy/MINERvA-OmniFold` are work in progress,
     and any session may push them on the usual gates without asking again.
   - **Publication** means arXiv, a journal submission, a data deposit or a release tag. Those remain Joseph's acts,
     and nothing here authorizes them.
   - Nothing secret is committed: no credentials, no private correspondence and no unpublished collaboration material
     beyond what the analysis already holds.
