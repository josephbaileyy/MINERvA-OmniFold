> ### 4. Hadronic-response interpretation
>
> Accepting PM-1 establishes how these bands are implemented and why they are outside the five-band kinematic replacement. It does not establish completeness of every physical detector uncertainty.

> Withdraw the inference:
>
> > "The bands are weight-only; therefore hadronic-response migration is absent from the covariance."

>
> Do not describe the treatment as negligible, conservative, complete, or deficient without evidence supporting that description. Do not claim that any limitation is largest at high (E_{\rm avail}) or high (W) merely because those observables involve hadronic energy.
