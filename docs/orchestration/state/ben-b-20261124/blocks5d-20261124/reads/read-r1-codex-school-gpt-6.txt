## BLOCKERS

- **The LL5D rebuild is a reference covariance for the raw errors, not their exact sampling covariance.** Subtracting each toy’s own clipped truth is correct for the declared error. But the universe blocks vary the unfolding MC on fixed mean pseudo-data, while the toys vary the clipped nature and unfold with nominal MC. Two universes can jointly push a row below zero even when neither clips it alone, creating a contribution to the error variance that the sum of single-band covariances cannot reproduce. The stat block also includes coordinate jitter absent from toys and bootstraps one nominal Poisson realization, while toys draw fresh Poisson counts at each nature’s rates. A trigger can therefore reflect these mismatches. Lateral and normalization draws are stored as metadata and applied only by LL5D’s scoring surrogate, so excluding them from the raw comparison is correct. ## MAJORS

- **The cal sets cannot isolate flux.** They draw uniform flux, interaction and detector universes, Poisson counts, and fresh half-MC splits. Their unweighted SD is compared with S1’s constraint-weighted flux-plus-stat prediction. COVFIX canonicalizes the tied space using ordered cell-axis projections and Gram–Schmidt. The existing sign test uses a generic SPD matrix. `b5d_build.py:65–74,166–173`, `test_b5d.py:61–65`, `B5D-DECLARATION.md:122–143`, `COVFIX-DECLARATION.md:953–958`
