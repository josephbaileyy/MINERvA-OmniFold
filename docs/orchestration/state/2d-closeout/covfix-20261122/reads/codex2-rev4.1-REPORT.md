## 2. Statistical audit

**Guards, labels, and carry — MAJOR, lines 143–189, 526–534, 554–561, 593–612.** The \(1/N\) split \(q_i=m_i^2+v_i\) is exact. The observed \(m_i^2\) includes noise in the estimated mean. The strict outer bounds and inclusive \([0.8,1.25]\) middle are internally consistent. The 2,000-resample, fixed-basis bootstrap with a \(t\) multiplier is a pointwise guard. It supplies no simultaneous confidence guarantee and needs calibration for the actual skewed or correlated toy law. As written, total PASS permits \(M\le1.25+h\), so a wider guard makes PASS easier. Affirmative evidence for a 1.25 upper tolerance would require an upper bound at or below 1.25. Specify whether unresolved bands are admissible and use identical P2 and PV bars if P2 is intended to screen for PV eligibility. P2 currently omits PV’s total-band condition. Bind the central vector to the CV nature and require finite, nonzero carry factors on aligned cells before using \(D_t^{-1}\). Check the size of the per-row rounding perturbation before relying on that vector.

**Pooled \( \hat f_R \) — MAJOR, lines 458–479.** The equal-weight four-ensemble formula and \(N_E/(N_E-1)\) correction are correct for a fixed carried basis and IID toys within each ensemble. Resampling whole toy vectors within each ensemble gives the appropriate bootstrap structure for its SE. The \(3\,\mathrm{SE}\) trigger is a construction rule. It has no stated error-rate guarantee. Disjoint seeds support independent random streams conditional on the shared bank. They do not establish independence over alternative banks. Define the factor when fewer than ten modes survive in an ensemble’s band. Bind the treatment of ties and cut-edge modes. The same rank band can represent different directions after carry, so report the post-carry fit per truth and band. If the factors scale all of \(F_A\), including the undrawn blocks, the grade must use that same accounting.

**\(C_b\), its PSD projection, and bias reporting — MAJOR, lines 481–494, 559–560, 662.** With fixed invertible \(D_t\), \(\Sigma_t/(N_t-1)\) is the correct subtraction under the draft’s \(1/N_t\) convention. The signed sum before projection is unbiased for the sum of mapped bias outer products. Taking the positive part gives a PSD matrix of rank at most four, because the signed sum is nonpositive on the complement of the four sample-mean vectors. Projection changes the expectation and can lose the undebiased sum’s member-wise bound. Call \(C_b\) a fitted bias allowance. The claims that it bounds the model class or necessarily over-covers smaller biases do not follow. Define the held-out debiased quadratic explicitly as
\[
\hat\beta_u^\top C_b^+\hat\beta_u-
\frac{\operatorname{tr}(C_b^+\Sigma_u)}{N_u-1}.
\]
Fix the pseudoinverse cutoff, the projection and metric for the outside-span fraction, zero-denominator handling, and uncertainty reporting. The inverse identity at line 488 holds for ordinary inverses with nonsingular \(D_t\). The grading pseudoinverses with separately truncated spectra require their own calculations in the two frames.

**Residual rule and \(K_C\) — MAJOR, lines 161–174, 230–240, 496–524, 599.** The \(10^{-12}\lambda_1(V_F)\) cutoff keeps the cutoff *scale* independent of the bias block for a fixed \(V_F\). Total eigenvectors and the kept span can still change. The \(10^{-6}\lambda_1(F_A)\) and 0.1 residual thresholds are policy choices. The regrade JSONs give aggregate cut diagnostics and cannot verify whether the 0.1 test fires among the well-determined modes. P0 needs the matrices, an explicit fluctuation reference for the old pair, and a specified evaluation frame for the new pair. Bind a span-level maximum principal angle if the intent is to protect every combination of well-determined modes. For \(K_C\), specify the order of carry and projection. Fixed reference-frame combinations and truth-transformed combinations are different estimands. Require both projected grading matrices to retain the declared rank before setting `ndf = k`. Pin the provenance and eligibility of the measurability subspace so its arrival cannot override the selection-data rule.

**The \(k\ge25\) floor — MAJOR, lines 517–522, 637–640.** The printed calculations reproduce for a Gaussian \(\chi^2\) mean centered at 1. At \(N=100\), \(\mathrm{SE}=0.02828\), \(h=0.05612\), and the low/high distances are \(5.09/6.85\) SE. At \(N=300\), they are \(0.01633\), \(0.03214\), and \(10.28/13.34\) SE. P-1 uses centered sample variance with divisor \(N\). Even for independent Gaussian modes with exactly the claimed fluctuation covariance, its mean is \((N-1)/N\) and its SE is \(\sqrt{2(N-1)/(N^2k)}\). The analogous distances are \(4.77/7.25\) and \(10.10/13.57\) SE before any undrawn contribution. Keep 25 as a reporting floor. Replace the five-sigma assurance with an illustration and calibrate the actual rule, including bands with as few as ten modes.

**Written null and false-FAIL simulation — BLOCKING, lines 545–587, 633–645.** The carried G2R shifts plus a Gaussian remainder define a candidate-matched *surrogate* if the remainder is PSD. Rebuilding `C_univ_new` to \(10^{-10}\) checks the bank arithmetic. It cannot establish that additive shifts in unfolded space reproduce weight-space composition, clipping, or passage through the nonlinear unfold. A regenerated universe block can also differ from the frozen G2R bank. The Gaussian remainder randomizes the bias allowance, \(C_{\rm PL}\), and the seed term, whereas PV has a fixed tune bias, no run-period draws, and seed 42. In particular, generating errors with covariance \(V_F+V_b\) and centering them for P-1 leaves \(V_b\) in their scatter. That can reject the very bias allowance that the design intends to test separately.

Bind a calibration law with fixed truth-specific bias vectors or a declared grid of bias alternatives, a centered fluctuation law with the drawn components, and an explicit scope for the Gaussian fallback. To estimate the 0.20 rate, generate independent **ensembles of \(N\) errors**, run the entire rule including the bootstrap guards, and specify simulation size, uncertainty, and the upper-bound decision. Twenty thousand single vectors estimate content probabilities. They do not define that experiment. Report both `FAILS` and `not PASS`, and apply the chosen rate criterion to the intended per-truth or two-truth event. Include the undrawn seed term in the expected P-1 scatter alongside \(C_{\rm PL}\), and distinguish the new \(\tau_F\) from REGRADE’s 0.0098 under its different matrix.

**\(K_J\), \(p_i\), and binomial size — MAJOR, lines 561–598, 633–642.** \(K_J\) is the correct in-ellipsoid count, and \(p_i\) is the marginal probability of \(|z_i|\le1\), distinct from \(q_i\). The strict binomial tail conventions work when the probabilities are known and the toys are IID. As a check, at \(N=300\) with the Gaussian fallback, \(q=0.6827\) gives joint cutoffs 188 and 221. At `ndf = 204`, the corresponding per-mode cutoffs are 174 and 234. Specify the no-cutoff sentinels and use analytic probabilities for the Gaussian fallback. For estimated probabilities, 20,000 replicates give an MC SE near 0.0033 at 0.68. Plug-in cutoffs do not guarantee the advertised sizes. Use conservative probability bounds in the appropriate direction and account for calibration error. With valid marginal cutoffs, the P-3 and P-4 contributions are each at most 0.025, regardless of mode dependence. The resulting 0.05 per truth and 0.10 over two truths apply to those checks. Extending them to `not VALIDATED` requires bounding P-1, P-2, the testability gate, and any relevant selection effects.

**False-PASS bounds — BLOCKING, lines 614–632.** At \(N=300\), `ndf = 204`, the unit Gaussian benchmark gives \(\mathrm{SE}=0.0057166\), \(h=0.0112499\), and critical \(M=1.26125\). The far point is 1.27840. With a fixed SE and normal sampling, its per-truth pass probability is 0.00135, giving \(1.82\times10^{-6}\) if **both** truths are that far outside tolerance, and \(5.47\times10^{-6}\) by a union bound over three candidates. At 1.26715 the corresponding figures are 0.151, 0.0228, and 0.0684. A candidate can be bad at just **one** truth. The corresponding union-bound examples are then about 0.00405 and 0.453 before crediting other checks. Even in the fixed-SE illustration, the one-sided rule passes about 97.5% of the time at the boundary and almost as often immediately above it. The SE also changes under alternatives. A Gaussian scale alternative alone multiplies the mean’s SE by its scale, and \(K_C\) can have just 25 directions. Label the printed figures as fixed-SE illustrations. Calibrate the frozen decision against explicit deficits at either truth, at the actual retained dimensions. Remove the inference that a validated true ratio is bounded within a few SE of 1.25.

## 3. Blindness and custody

- **BLOCKING, lines 99–101, 347–365, 403–433, 444–446, 539–547.** The prescribed path gives the builders ciphertext until after freeze. The execution boundary does not enforce that path. PG produces plaintext before encryption under a shared Unix identity, and the named tunes and public seeds make deterministic regeneration possible. An exclusive node limits scheduling access. It does not isolate a shared identity, filesystem, logs, or process access. Run PG and the PV writer/readout under director-controlled isolation, keep intermediates and logs private from the builders, and test the wrapper through a synthetic seal. If the experiment requires protection against independent regeneration, use director-held seeds committed by digest before PG. Otherwise state the cooperative no-regeneration assumption explicitly. Use authenticated encryption, including for the fallback, and verify the manifests before decrypting. Scan campaign-owned paths after both writer and readout, with cleanup on failure. Exact hashes alone detect exact copies. They cannot detect repacked or partial plaintext.
- **BLOCKING, lines 315–326, 358–365, 673–676.** The proposed spent-once rule is an auditable convention. A same-account user can delete the lock, and the draft does not specify atomic acquisition, authority over the consumption record, or crash semantics. Use a director-controlled, append-only consumption record keyed by seal and frozen digest. Acquire it atomically before first decrypt, require it for both secret-bearing PV roles, and define which event spends each role. Bind the treatment of crashes and partial output. Reconcile the executability exception with ST5 and forbid a second grading of exposed toys.
- **MAJOR, lines 374–399, 435–446, 510–516.** The probe is an allowed predeclared choice based on public configuration. The prose requires a model absent from both spent tunes, while the executable exclusion only rejects a complete pair match with either one.
