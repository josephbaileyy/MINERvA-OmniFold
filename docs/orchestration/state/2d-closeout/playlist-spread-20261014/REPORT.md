# Playlist spread of the yield proxy: does it matter for the 2D result? (2026-10-14)

**Answer: POSSIBLE EFFECT, SIZED.**

The production pools the 12 playlists with one global POT ratio. This is the known defect `KNOWN_ISSUES.md` J36.
Its effect on an efficiency-corrected rate is **+0.18 %** in total and at most **+0.32 %** in a marginal bin. It
is small.

The playlist spread itself is real. It is not a statistical fluctuation, and the code does not correct it. It comes
in two blocks:
- 1G–1M, low by about 3 %;
- 1N–1P, high by about 5 %.

In the data-POT-weighted sum the two nearly cancel. Suppose the low-intensity 1A–1E block were the right reference.
Then the full data set differs from it by:
- −0.03 % in total;
- −1.1 % to +1.9 % in the p_∥ marginal;
- −0.6 % to +1.1 % in the p_T marginal.

These are reco-level proxy numbers. These data cannot tell which block is right.

**CITABLE FOR:**
- how the production combines playlists (§1.1);
- the binned size of the J36 mix error (§1.2);
- the factor split of R per playlist (§2.1);
- the reco-level shape of the 1G–1M and 1N–1P deviations (§2.2);

- the AnaTuple-sample rates, as a sample (§3.3).

**NOT CITABLE FOR:**

- the cause of either block deviation. The §3.4 mechanisms are inferences;
- the §3.3 sample rates as full-playlist values.

## 1. Does it matter for the 2D result?

### 1.1 How playlists are combined

- **Event loop:** one playlist per job. It sets the playlist at `runEventLoopOmniFold.cpp:1695-1699` and writes that
  job's POT at `:1866-1869`. No event weight carries a POT factor.
- **Merge:** `sbatch_hadd_MEFHC.sh:61` and `uq/hadd_universes_full.py:25-40` concatenate the trees and sum
  `mcPOTUsed` and `dataPOTUsed`. No playlist label survives in the trees.
- **Producer:** `get_pot_scales` (`unfold_2d_omnifold_unbinned.py:117-126`) returns one `data_pot / mc_pot`. That one
  factor multiplies every MC weight (`:322,331,593,716-717`).
- **Flux:** this is the only part combined per playlist, weighted by data POT (`combine_flux_MEFHC.py:90-96`). The
  production passes it as `--mcfile` (`sbatch_unfold_2d_MEFHC_5iter_universes_full_CV.sh:26-27,45`).

**Measured:**
- The merged `_universes_full` file holds data POT 1.057394e21 and MC POT 4.978198e21. Both equal the per-playlist
  sums. The ratio is 0.212405.
- Its tree entry counts also equal the per-playlist sums (`outputs/potcheck.json`).
- The flux file's `DataPOTUsedForFluxWeighting` is 1.057394e21.

So the MC playlist mix follows MC POT, while the data mix follows data POT. Per-playlist POT_d/POT_mc runs from
0.1707 (1B) to 0.2371 (1D). Compared with the data mix, the pooled MC:
- over-weights 1M and 1B by about 1.2×;
- under-weights 1D, 1F and 1G by 8–11 %.

### 1.2 Size of the mix error on an efficiency-corrected rate (binned)

`scripts/mix_bias.py` unfolds the same pooled data twice on the triage 10 × 10 grid:
- once with the production mix: the global ratio applied to the summed MC;
- once with each playlist's MC scaled by its own POT_d/POT_mc.

MC statistics come from 20 event-level Poisson bootstrap toys, applied to both mixes at once.

| production mix / per-playlist mix − 1 | bin-by-bin | IBU, 5 iterations |
|---|---:|---:|
| total over the grid | +0.177 % (sd 0.002 %) | +0.175 % (sd 0.002 %) |
| p_∥ marginal, lowest bin → highest | +0.29 → +0.15 % | +0.32 → +0.15 % |
| p_T marginal | +0.04 to +0.19 % | −0.03 to +0.20 % |
| single bins with D ≥ 50 (88 bins) | −0.06 to +0.58 % | −0.29 to +0.36 % |

- **Where the error comes from.** The integrated efficiency is 0.61144 in the production mix and 0.61246 per
  playlist. The efficiency term alone gives +0.182 % on the in-grid rate. The background term gives −0.0045 %.
- **OmniFold.** It is inferred to move by about the IBU amount. No OmniFold run was made.
- **An earlier claim is slightly wrong.** `AUDIT-FINDINGS-20260731.md:694-699` says the total normalization "is not
  biased". That holds only if the MC rate per POT is the same in every playlist, and it is not. The bias is small,
  and of the same order as the pre-unfolding +0.12 % in the ledger (`VALIDATION_LEDGER.md:2264-2295`).

### 1.3 What the spread could do

`scripts/ref_shape.py` scales each playlist by its own POT and uses data-only errors.
- R's data-POT-weighted rms across playlists is 2.6 %, against 0.15–0.5 % statistical error per playlist.
- Against 1A–1E, the block totals are:
  - 1G–1M: 0.9739 ± 0.0012 (29 % of the data POT);
  - 1N–1P: 1.0502 ± 0.0016 (17 % of the data POT).
- The full data set against 1A–1E is:
  - 0.9997 in total;
  - 0.989–1.019 in the p_∥ marginal;
  - 0.994–1.011 in the p_T marginal.

  These errors are approximate, because the full set contains 1A–1E.

This is a choice of reference, not a measured bias.

## 2. Decompose the spread

### 2.1 Factors per playlist (`outputs/summary_tables.md` T1)

R = rel. (D − B)/POT ÷ (rel. eff × rel. truth/POT), each relative to the mean of 1A–1D.

| block | (D−B)/POT | eff S/T | truth/POT | R |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1A–1D | 0.997–1.002 | 0.997–1.003 | 1.000 | 0.998–1.003 |
| 1E | 0.984 | 0.996 | 0.999 | 0.989 |
| 1F | 0.955 | 0.962 | 1.000 | 0.993 |
| 1G / 1L / 1M | 0.971 / 0.922 / 0.924 | 0.975 / 0.928 / 0.929 | 1.024 | 0.972 |
| 1N / 1O / 1P | 0.966 / 0.919 / 0.917 | 0.917 / 0.870 / 0.870 | 1.011 / 0.998 / 1.000 | 1.043 / 1.059 / 1.055 |

- **Background is not the cause.** It rises from 1,090 to 1,192 per 10¹⁹ POT, which moves R by about 0.3 %.
- **1G–1M:** the MC truth rate per POT is 2.4 % high. The data rate and the MC efficiency fall together.
- **1N–1P:** the MC efficiency falls faster than the data rate.

### 2.2 Shape against 1A–1E

**1N–1P.** The excess is broad: it is not confined to high or low p_∥.
- In p_∥ it is:
  - 1.020 ± 0.012 at 1.5–2 GeV/c;
  - 1.03–1.05 at 2–5 GeV/c;
  - 1.075 at 6–10 GeV/c;
  - 1.05–1.07 above that.
- In p_T it is 1.042–1.076, flat to about 2 %.
- The shape χ² is 195 for 87 bins.
- In the MC, the efficiency loss is largest at low p_∥: 0.896 at 1.5–2 GeV/c, against 0.94 at 10–15 GeV/c. The data
  loss has the same shape but is smaller.

**1G–1M.** The block steps up at 8 GeV/c, which is what a difference in flux shape would produce (inferred).
- In p_∥ it is 0.954–0.989 below 8 GeV/c (0.9545 at 6–8 GeV/c), then 1.022–1.025 at 8–15 GeV/c.

  > **Correction 2026-10-14:** this line first read "0.955–0.989 below 6 GeV/c, then 1.022–1.025 at 6–10 GeV/c",
  > one bin off. The values are from `outputs/ref_shape.json` (`1G-1M_vs_1A-1E.by_pz` on edges 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8,
  > 10, 15, 20, 60 GeV/c), found by [`coverage/REPORT.md`](coverage/REPORT.md) §2.1 and re-read by the director.
- In p_T it is 0.963–1.000.

**1F** is within ±1.6 % in both marginals.

## 3. Detector and beam conditions

### 3.1 What the code applies per playlist

- **Flux.** `FluxAndCVReweighter.h:27-33` and MAT `FluxReweighter.cxx:1936-1976` map the playlists to three flux
  files:

  | playlists | flux file | measured integral (m⁻²/POT) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1A–1F | 1D | 0.0087219 |
  | 1G, 1L, 1M | 1M | 0.0088080 (+0.99 %) |
  | 1N–1P | 1N | 0.0086851 (−0.42 %) |

  The combined flux is 0.0087407.
- **MINOS efficiency.** For ME, `MINOSEfficiencyReweighter.h:35-38` applies an intensity-dependent correction
  (`MinosMuonEfficiencyCorrection.cxx:123-155`, with batch POT from `MinervaUniverse.cxx:441-483`). It uses no
  per-playlist table.
  - The "Inextinguishable" per-playlist tables (`MnvNormalization.h:689-809`) give 0.95 for low-p MINOS in 1A and
    0.92 in 1N–1P.
  - The ME path does not use them. The reweighter reaches them only in its non-ME branch (`:39-45`).
  - Applying them would lower the late MC further and raise R.
- **Dead time.** The cut is tdead ≤ 1 (`CCInclusiveCuts.h:102`, MAT `Cut.h:137`, `CVUniverse.h:131-133`). It is the
  same for Data and MC.
- **Target mass.** It is a single constant (`unfold_2d_omnifold_unbinned.py:1688-1698`). Nothing here is
  playlist-dependent.

### 3.2 The MC efficiency steps (T2)

- **The drop is in the reco selection,** which goes from 0.650 to 0.571. The fraction in the grid stays at 0.995.
- **The MINOS weight barely contributes.** It moves from 0.984 to 0.977 (−0.8 %).
- **Unweighted:**
  - rows with no reco-tree entry rise from 25.4 % to 31.5 %;
  - rows that are in the tree but fail the cuts rise from 9.8 % to 11.6 %.

### 3.3 AnaTuple sample (T3)

The sample is 3 Data files and 1 MC file per playlist, with a fiducial reco vertex.

- **Intensity.** Mean `numi_pot` agrees between Data and MC in every playlist. It is about 23 × 10¹² in 1A–1E, 28 in
  1F/1G, 38–39 in 1L/1M, 36 in 1N and 48.5 in 1O/1P. This matches the 6+0, 6+2 and 6+4 slip-stacking periods on
  [minerva.fnal.gov/minerva-playlists](https://minerva.fnal.gov/minerva-playlists/).
- **Selection fractions.** The Data/MC ratio of the fraction passing MINOS match, dead time and ν is 0.906–0.931 in
  every playlist. The match alone is 0.906–0.935; dead time is 0.989–1.003.
- **The step is in the reconstructed fiducial events per POT.** The Data/MC ratio is:
  - 0.975–1.011 for 1A–1M;
  - 1.034, 1.047 and 1.025 for 1N, 1O and 1P.

  In 1N the data rate (799 per 10¹⁷ POT) is back at the 1A–1D level (798–807), while the MC sits at 773. In 1O and
  1P the MC falls to 744–751.
- **The step marks a period, not an intensity.** 1L and 1M run at a similar intensity without the step. So it marks
  the 1N–1P period, not intensity alone.

### 3.4 Is this expected, and is it known? (inference)

- **The falling MC efficiency is expected.** It tracks intensity, and the MC overlays data gates
  ([`../stageD-inputs/REPORT.md`](../stageD-inputs/REPORT.md) §4.4).
- **In 1N–1P the MC loses more reconstructed events than the data.** The loss is upstream of the analysis cuts.
- **No public MINERvA statement about this was found.** The pages read were
  [playlists](https://minerva.fnal.gov/minerva-playlists/),
  [run periods](https://minerva.fnal.gov/data-run-periods/) and [get data](https://minerva.fnal.gov/getdata/). The
  playlists page warns of "some inconsistencies run-to-run" and of He-target states that differ between data and
  MC. Its playlist wiki is open to collaborators only.
- **Candidate causes for 1N–1P, none tested here:**
  - MC conditions that do not match the data;
  - POT accounting in the 1N–1P data;
  - a defect in the MC sample.
- **1G–1M** likely reflects the tilted-horn flux prediction (inferred).

## 5. Limits

- **The method is binned.** The proxy and both estimators use the triage grid. They are not OmniFold and not the
  analysis binning.
- **Statistics only.** §1.3 and §2.2 carry data-statistics errors only.
- **The AnaTuple sample is small.** File-to-file variation was not measured, and 1C Data has 445 fiducial events.
- **§3.3 uses a different population:** the fiducial reco tree, not the analysis selection.
- **The inputs match production only in their counts.** The May CV files match the `_universes_full` files in POT and
  entry counts only.
- **Not checked:**
  - whether the covariance covers the spread;
  - MINERvA's collaborator-only documentation;
  - POT accounting in the 1N–1P data.
- **Cluster-only files.** `intensity_v1.*` and `intensity_v2.*` are on the cluster only and are superseded.
