# 2310.06082 — MicroBooNE, generalized kinematic imbalance on argon (PRD 109, 092007)

Source read: src/2310.06082/x/main.tex (abstract; Observables; MicroBooNE Cross Section Measurement, which is the
whole extraction description; Conclusions) and src/2310.06082/x/supp.tex (Fake Data Studies section).

- **Where limitations live.** The abstract has none; its findings are stated as demonstrations. Q[2310.06082]: "These measurements allow us to demonstrate that the treatment of charged current quasielastic interactions in GENIE version 2 is inadequate to describe data." (main.tex:46, Abstract)
  The method's validation and its limits live in one paragraph of the measurement section. The details, with the
  one stated omission, live in the supplement's "Fake Data Studies" section. There is no limitations paragraph;
  the conclusion is all findings, with one hedged interpretation (see below).

- **Model dependence, regularization or unfolding bias.** The regularization is encoded in A_C, with a usage
  instruction and a one-clause magnitude statement ("even though its effect is small"). Q[2310.06082]: "The MATH matrix is included in the Supplemental Material and needs to be applied to all theory predictions in order to compare to the data measurements, even though its effect is small." (main.tex:687, MicroBooNE Cross Section Measurement)
  The prior or model dependence is tested with fake data and reported as a passed robustness test. Q[2310.06082]: "The robustness of the unfolding method was tested using fake data studies with alternative generator predictions, which are presented in the Supplemental Material." (main.tex:689, same section)
  Q[2310.06082]: "We found that the combination of these uncertainties covered the difference between the unfolded fake data prediction and the corresponding alternative-generator theory prediction." (main.tex:692, same section)
  The single-model unfolding is stated once, as a plain fact, without a caveat attached. Q[2310.06082]: "The unfolding is performed for each one of the observables of interest using the MATH model." (main.tex:685, same section)

- **Failed or partial validations.** None are reported. Every fake-data result is stated as a pass ("covered",
  "further supporting the robustness"). Q[2310.06082]: "Additionally, the aforementioned comparisons yielded MATH /ndof values below unity and p-values close to 1, further supporting the robustness of our unfolding procedure." (main.tex:693, same section)
  **States what was NOT done or validated?** Once, in the supplement, as a methodological choice with its reason:
  Q[2310.06082]: "In the case of all these variations, uncertainties due to beam exposure, number-of-targets, detector, fluxes, and re-interactions are irrelevant and were not included in the fake-data uncertainty for comparison." (supp.tex:304, Fake Data Studies)

- **How scope is stated.** The physics assumptions of the observables are stated once, in the definitions. Q[2310.06082]: "Under the assumption that FSI are weak or absent, the definitions given here produce a vector that aligns with the initial struck neutron momentum, consistent with the definitions used in previous work [N]." (main.tex:178, Observables)
  Results sentences carry no per-sentence scope. Interpretive hedges are single modal verbs on explanations, not on
  measurements. Q[2310.06082]: "Some of these differences might be originating from fact that neutrinos are scattering off heavy argon nuclei." (main.tex:1027, Conclusions)

- **What is deferred and how pointed to.** The A_C matrices, fake-data studies, the no-FSI results, the generator
  variants and a simultaneous double-differential extraction go to the Supplemental Material. Selection and
  systematic details go to a companion paper, through a one-line pointer. Q[2310.06082]: "More details on the systematic uncertainties and the cross-section extraction technique can be found in Ref. [N]." (main.tex:705, same section)
  Q[2310.06082]: "A simultaneous extraction of the double-differential results across all parts of the available phase-space is presented in the Supplemental Material." (main.tex:1018, same section)
  The supplement gives the purpose of the fake-data study in one sentence. Q[2310.06082]: "In order to ensure that the unfolded data results are not biased due to the choice of the original MC, we performed fake data studies and treated the signal reconstructed events from an alternative model as if they were real data." (supp.tex:299, Fake Data Studies)

- **Patterns worth copying.**
  1. One validation paragraph in the body (main.tex:689-693): what was tested, against which alternatives, the
     criterion (uncertainties cover the difference), and the outcome. Plots and details go to the supplement.
  2. Quantify a caveat in a clause ("even though its effect is small", main.tex:687) rather than with an open-ended
     disclaimer.
  3. Give a companion-paper pointer as one sentence at the end of the method paragraph (main.tex:705).
