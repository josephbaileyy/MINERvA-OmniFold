- **Model dependence, regularization or unfolding bias.** The bias is stated flatly, as a property of the class of
  methods, not hedged. Q[1705.03568]: "It's worth noting that the regularization method is effectively introducing an additional smearing to the unfolding results, which would lead to biases on the unfolded signal." (wiener.tex:225, Review of traditional regularization approach)
  The model dependence is conceded in the strongest possible word ("strictly"), and the mitigation is given in the
  next sentence. Q[1705.03568]: "The unfolded results in the Wiener-SVD method is strictly model dependent. Such dependence is reduced when the estimation of the true model takes into account the actual measurement as illustrated in Eq. N." (discussion.tex:50, Discussions and Recommendations)
  The remedy is a reporting prescription: publish the additional smearing matrix (later called A_C by MicroBooNE),
  so that the bias is carried by the comparison rather than the uncertainty. Q[1705.03568]: "We recommend to report this new smearing matrix MATH in the publication together with the unfolded results to enable a more direct comparison of expectations (e.g. from new theoretical calculations) with the unfolded results." (discussion.tex:12, Discussions and Recommendations)
  Q[1705.03568]: "Through reporting the new smearing matrix MATH , one can avoid including the bias due to unfolding in the final uncertainties." (discussion.tex:17, same section)

  That introduction sentence scopes the whole method's usefulness once: for hypothesis tests, folding is preferred.
