# 2102.03346 — T2K, first TKI measurement in CC1π⁺Xp (PRD 103, 112009)

Source read: src/2102.03346/x/ms.tex (abstract; Secs. Signal definition, Analysis method incl. "Cross section
extraction, error propagation and validation", Results incl. "Discussion", Conclusion; Appendix "More model
comparisons").

- **Where limitations live.** Limitations are spread over three places: the signal definition (model dependence of
  the efficiency), the validation subsection (mock-data studies), and the results discussion and conclusion
  (statistics, the phase-space restrictions). The abstract carries none: it states the result and the model
  preference. Q[2102.03346]: "Overall, the results show a preference for models which have a more realistic treatment of nuclear medium effects including the initial nuclear state and final-state interactions." (ms.tex:525, Abstract)
  The conclusion states the main limitation in one plain clause. Q[2102.03346]: "Our results are statistically limited and a large part of the model separation power comes from normalization differences." (ms.tex:1062, Conclusion)
  There is no dedicated limitations paragraph or section, and no footnotes or supplement carry caveats (inference,
  from reading the full source).

- **Model dependence, regularization or unfolding bias.** The bias source is named once, at the design step where it
  enters, together with the design choice that limits it. Q[2102.03346]: "In order to mitigate model dependence in the efficiency correction, phase-space restrictions are applied in the signal definition to restrict the measurement to the regions of kinematic phase space ND280 is sensitive to." (ms.tex:694, Data and event selection / Signal definition)
  Q[2102.03346]: "This leads to a potential source of bias from the input neutrino interaction model predictions." (ms.tex:694, Signal definition)
  The method is unregularized, and the paper words this as a positive design property. Q[2102.03346]: "An unregularized fit means that there is no prior constraint on the shape of TKI from the input signal model, thus reducing model bias on the fitted cross sections." (ms.tex:816, Analysis method / Binned likelihood fitting)
  The residual model dependence is absorbed into nuisance parameters, and is therefore part of the uncertainty
  rather than a caveat. Q[2102.03346]: "It is verified that with such comprehensive list of parameters, the fit can cover the bias in signal efficiency and background subtraction under extreme model variations as discussed in N." (ms.tex:865, Sources of systematic uncertainties)
  The mock-data (fake-data) studies are reported as a validation that passed, with summary statistics. Q[2102.03346]: "To ensure our results are not biased, a plethora of mock data studies with alternative neutrino event generators, nuclear ground state models, background models and altered flux models have been performed." (ms.tex:890, Cross section extraction, error propagation and validation)
  Q[2102.03346]: "It has been verified that even in the case of extreme deviations from the input MC model, such as doubling the signal/background interactions or completely turning off the FSI, the cross section extraction method employed can always recover the truth values to within a 1 MATH -uncertainty." (ms.tex:890, same subsection)

- **Failed or partial validations.** One partial result is reported: a post-fit tension in a few bins. It is given
  one sentence, then resolved by the mock-data studies, with no running caveat afterwards. Q[2102.03346]: "This indicates there might not be enough freedom in the shape of the signal particle kinematics." (ms.tex:950, Results)
  Q[2102.03346]: "However, from the mock data studies, it is concluded that the potential bias is much smaller than the statistical uncertainty and has little impact on this analysis." (ms.tex:950, Results)
  A known statistical weakness of the χ² comparison is flagged once, with a remedy (a shape-only χ²). Q[2102.03346]: "Therefore, the MATH statistics may suffer from "Peelle's pertinent puzzle" [N], in which the assumption in N that the variance is distributed as a multivariate Gaussian may not be valid for highly correlated results." (ms.tex:970, Comparisons to models)
  **States what was NOT done or validated?** Almost never. The only explicit "not" statements are about sensitivity:
  Q[2102.03346]: "The tight phase space restrictions used in this study reduces our sensitivity to FSI modelling." (ms.tex:1052, Discussion)
  Unvalidated components are not listed (inference).

- **How scope is stated.** Scope is set once, structurally, by the signal definition and its phase-space
  restrictions. Results sentences do not repeat it; it recurs only in the discussion, where it explains a weak
  sensitivity. One interpretive warning is given as advice to the reader, not as a hedge on every result:
  Q[2102.03346]: "Thus one should be careful in interpreting the model agreement when the difference between MATH and MATH is large." (ms.tex:1039, Discussion)

- **What is deferred and how pointed to.** The extra model comparisons go to an appendix, and the numbers to a
  data release cited once, at the end. Q[2102.03346]: "The data release for the results presented in this analysis is posted in Ref. [N]." (ms.tex:1070, Conclusion)
  Future work is stated as a plan, not as a gap in the present result. Q[2102.03346]: "Future analyses will aim to unfold cross sections in multiple TKI variables simultaneously and obtain their correlations which can then be used to separate effects due to the initial nuclear state and FSI." (ms.tex:1067, Conclusion)

- **Patterns worth copying.**
  1. Name the bias source once, where it enters, with the design choice that limits it (ms.tex:694 quotes above).
  2. Report the fake-data validation as a passed test with its criterion. A residual tension is turned into a
     bounded statement ("much smaller than the statistical uncertainty", ms.tex:950), not a running caveat.
  3. Give the limitation as a design fact with its consequence ("statistically limited", ms.tex:1062; "reduces our
     sensitivity to FSI modelling", ms.tex:1052).
  Not copyable: "exceptional care has been taken" is an assurance without content. Q[2102.03346]: "Due to the complex and multifaceted nature of this analysis, exceptional care has been taken in mitigating sources of potential model bias in the extracted results." (ms.tex:1065, Conclusion)
