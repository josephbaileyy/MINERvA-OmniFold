## Case 1

BEFORE (196 words):
We test OmniFold on MINERvA open data, unfolding five observables simultaneously, without binning. As a descriptive benchmark, a two-observable unfolding reproduces the published muon-kinematic cross-section integral within about 1%, but differs from the published shape more than a binned unfolding of the same inputs does. Under controlled truth changes, the result retains part of its prior, and some cells move opposite to them. Fake-data studies at a GiBUU-like truth and a synthetic momentum-transfer tilt find residual bias beyond uncertainty-based criteria fixed before grading. On noise-free pseudo-data at both truths, binned iterative unfolding overshoots the truth along the prior-to-truth direction. The five-observable estimator's results are sensitive to rounding-level changes of its inputs, beyond a requirement fixed in advance. The procedure's two-dimensional covariance, rebuilt at each of two held-out generator tunes, under-covers at both. The reported five-observable intervals have no validated coverage. A corresponding five-observable test of the procedure's covariance at those tunes stopped at an entry condition fixed in advance, before its pseudo-experiments, and gives no coverage result. On data, the sixteen reported cells depend on the unfolding algorithm: binned iterative unfolding differs from them. We therefore present the five-observable output on data only as an illustration.
