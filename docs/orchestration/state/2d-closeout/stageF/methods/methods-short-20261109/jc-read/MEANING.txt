## Answers
- **A.** "goes with" is an association. The records show it at K = 10: fixed inputs, 1.03 vs 2.27, and T > max. "therefore"
  asserts an inference that the pre-declared read left unresolved, and the closeness depends on K (2.16 at K = 20). Anchor it at
  the published ten iterations and drop "therefore", or record Joseph's ruling over DECLARATION §1.5.
- **B.** No changed sentence ranks reliability or names inputs as a cause. None claims input agreement outright. The
  "therefore" step implicitly leans on it, against the kept "near one does not by itself indicate reproduction".
- **C.** Only half true under the paragraph's own definition of "differs": OmniFold differs from IBU by the test, and lies
  farther from the publication only descriptively.
- **D.** "near" is fair descriptively for 1.03 against 2.27 at the published count; it is unanchored in K. "binned result" is
  correct for the publication.
