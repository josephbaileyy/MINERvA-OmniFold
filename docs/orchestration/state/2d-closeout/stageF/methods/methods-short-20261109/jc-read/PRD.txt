## Verdicts
1. IBU framing: ACCEPT WITH EDITS. "Goes with" is correlational and defensible: the paired test shows OmniFold and
   IBU differ on identical inputs, and IBU sits near the published result. "Therefore" holds only at the published
   iteration count. At K=20 the IBU distance (2.16) is close to OmniFold's (2.27), and the body hides that behind
   "(other iteration counts: S7)". The HEAD caveat on input differences was deleted without a replacement. Table S1
   lists five inputs as "not compared", and an outside reader will ask whether they act differently on a method that
   uses within-bin information. Scope "close" to the published K, put the K range in the body, and say the
   input-by-method question is untested. That wording neither blames the inputs nor ranks the methods.
2. Ranking/blame: ACCEPT WITH EDITS. No sentence blames the inputs. Table 1 still says "IBU lies closer ...;
   mechanism not resolved", which is out of step with the new framing. Nothing says that being close to the published
   result is not an accuracy criterion. Without that, "differs from both" reads as OmniFold being the outlier, so the
   paper should say it once.
3. Abstract sentence: REVISE. On a first read, "published binned result" sounds like a result reported in bins, and
   both results are reported in 205 bins. "The same inputs" has no antecedent (same as the publication's?). "Its"
   refers back to "benchmark".
4. Dropping c: ACCEPT, with one required consistency fix. supplemental_body.tex:265 still divides "by the
   completeness", so the supplement now contradicts eq. (1). It is optional to make U say that unmatched truth
   events are included, which puts the no-efficiency logic next to the equation.
5. Missing items: ACCEPT WITH EDITS. The published K is in the body ("the published count", sec. 4.2) and its origin is
   in S6 (chosen in a fake-data study), which is enough. Why K=10: it is the published count and the declared primary
   setting, also stated, which is fine. Missing: the K range (edit E3). Not raised in the body: on paired replicas
   the IBU/OmniFold distance ratio is 0.70-1.13, against 0.45 on the data (S7 says why "was not tested"). A careful
   referee will ask whether "close" survives resampling. The descriptive sentence ending sec. 4.2 covers this only
   partly. I do not require an edit, but expect the question in a response letter.
