You are an independent referee checking seven editorial corrections to a JINST methods paper (OmniFold unfolding of MINERvA open data) and its Supplement for MEANING changes. They were approved as editorial only: no claim may change, no qualifier may be lost, nothing may be strengthened. Read in full: diff.txt (the whole change, with context), new_supplement.tex and new_paper.tex (the whole sources after the change; \fvval{key} prints a value filled elsewhere), and ref_g1_decl_44_50.txt (the record for item 6).

The corrections:
1. Supplement table tab:prr-levels (Table S13), caption: "or, for the observables, by a column mask on the inputs of all three" becomes "... on the inputs of the learners named in the ``Applies to'' column". A referee noted that the table's first observable row ("no W in step 1") changes only the step-1 classifier, so "all three" contradicted it.
2. Supplement, the binning paragraphs: the q3 edges' unit changes from GeV to GeV/c, as in the paper and the cells table. "Hadronic axes span [0,100] GeV" becomes "Hadronic axes span [0,100], in GeV (q3 in GeV/c)".
3. Supplement table tab:cov-design (Table S14): a new row, "Excluded from C_T", reads "the run-period block" for the two-dimensional test and "C_resp, C_PL,5D" for the second five-observable test. Its sources are the paper's section 6.2: "The run-period block was neither drawn nor included in the graded covariance." for the two-dimensional test, and "The graded covariance includes the model term, ... and leaves out the response and run-period terms." for the five-observable test.
4. Supplement table S11, test 10's criterion: "as 8, on the sixteen cells, with alpha/16 per cell" becomes "as 8, on the sixteen cells: alpha/16 per cell, alpha jointly". Test 8's row reads "alpha/205 per cell, alpha jointly".
5. Paper table tab:outcomes caption: "``Descriptive'' has two uses here." becomes "Two rows use ``Descriptive'' in a specific sense." A referee noted that the benchmark rows also call a distance descriptive, so "two uses" was false. The two following sentences are unchanged.
6. Supplement S7, the sixteen-cell IBU comparison: "its truth grid is the coarse grid whose unions are the cells R00--R15" becomes "its truth bins are the boxes from which the sixteen cells are formed (section~\ref{supp:cells})". In the paper "coarse grid" names another (3^5) grid. The paper's section 4.5 now says "its truth bins are the boxes from which the sixteen cells are formed". The record says the truth grid is "the H2 coarse cells ... empty cells dropped".
7. Paper section 7: "a check that plausible changes of the unfolding and detector response cannot produce the pattern" becomes "... cannot produce such contrasts". "The pattern" had no antecedent. The preceding sentence asks for "contrasts within fixed muon-kinematic regions".

For each item answer:
