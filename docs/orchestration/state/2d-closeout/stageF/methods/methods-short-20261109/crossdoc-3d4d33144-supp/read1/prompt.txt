You are an independent referee checking two corrections to the Supplement of a JINST methods paper (OmniFold unfolding of MINERvA open data) for MEANING. They were approved as corrections of factual mismatches with the companion analysis note. No claim may be strengthened. Read in full: diff.txt (the change), new_supplement.tex (the whole Supplement source after the change), note_sec_fived16_190_199.tex (the note's run-period-block passage and its source comment, at the note's release revision), audit_declaration_excerpt.txt (the governing record, with original line numbers), and note_sec_eavailw.tex (the note's section 8; its subsections are 8.1 sec:eavailw-axis, 8.2 sec:eavailw-definitions, 8.3 sec:eavailw-generators, in that order).

Correction 1, a timing disclosure. Old: "The rule for adding it was fixed before the block's numbers were read." New: "The rule for adding it was fixed after the block's reported numbers had been read, but before any of the measurements that decide it had been computed, and its second and third requirements were expected to hold." The note says the conditions "were fixed before any of the measurements that decide them was computed" and "were set after the block's reported numbers had been read, and the second and third were not expected to fire". The note frames them as conditions under which the block would NOT be added. The Supplement frames the same three as requirements that held, so "not expected to fire" becomes "expected to hold".
(a) Quote the declaration lines that establish each of the following: the reported numbers were read before the stop condition was written; no audit measurement had been computed; clauses N and R (the second and third) were not expected to fire.
(b) Is every part of the new sentence supported? Is the mapping "not expected to fire" -> "expected to hold" exact for the Supplement's positive framing, and is the order of the three requirements the same in both documents?
(c) Does the sentence keep "The run-period block was added after the period differences had been seen"? Does anything now read as more prospective than the record allows?

Correction 2, a pointer. The Supplement's sentence "For GENIE with MEC, NuWro and GiBUU its effect is not reliably determined (ref., section N-sec:eavailw-axis)" now points to N-sec:eavailw-definitions. Check in note_sec_eavailw.tex that the beam-axis convention's effects (the factors, the median, the GENIE shifts, "not reliably determined") are in subsection 8.2 and not in 8.1. Report a ranked list of problems, each with a concrete fix, then a final line "VERDICT: PASS" or "VERDICT: FAIL".
