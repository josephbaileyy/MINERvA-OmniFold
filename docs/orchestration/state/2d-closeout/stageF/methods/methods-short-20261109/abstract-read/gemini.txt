### Overall Verdict

Both abstracts honestly state the primary diagnostic conclusions: unbinned unfolding retains part of its prior, exhibits numerical instability under rounding, and lacks validated coverage. 

However, **LONGEST is unacceptable for publication** due to Sentence 6, which is an incoherent 76-word run-on riddled with accidental repetition and unexplained jargon. **SHORTEST is far more cohesive**, though both versions need minor revisions to distinguish 2D benchmark choices from 5D results, clarify the coverage test transitions, and mention data-level run-period and algorithmic discrepancies.
