We test unbinned unfolding with OmniFold on MINERvA open data in five observables simultaneously. As a benchmark, the two-observable unfolding reproduces the published muon-kinematic cross-section integral within about 1%, but differs in shape for an unidentified reason. In controlled studies the result retains part of its prior: fake-data studies at a GiBUU-like truth and at a synthetic momentum-transfer tilt find residual bias beyond uncertainty-based criteria fixed before grading; binned iterative unfolding of noise-free pseudo-data at five iterations retains less of the prior than the five-observable estimator at both.                 7.1.   Limitations

1. No reported interval has validated coverage. The
   fake-data tests assess only bias. The first coverage
   test of the five-observable covariance did not run
   to a result. The second test of section 6.3 grades
   the procedure’s covariance at two truths. The pro-
   cedure’s two-dimensional covariance under-covers
   at both held-out tunes (section 6.2). Neither the
   coverage of Cmodel at truths held out of its con-
   struction nor the size of either run-period block is
   independently validated (supplementary material,
   section S5, and section S6). The per-cell cover-
   age readings cited are pointwise, for each reported
   quantity separately, not the stronger simultaneous
   coverage of all of them. The joint readings grade
   the fraction of pseudo-experiments whose χ2 lies in
   a region fixed in advance.
2. Four of the sixteen cells, R02, R06, R10 and R15,
   have a diagonal fraction (section 2) of only 0.16–
   0.25.
