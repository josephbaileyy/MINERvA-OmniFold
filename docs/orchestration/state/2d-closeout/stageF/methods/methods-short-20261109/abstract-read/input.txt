As a benchmark, the unfolding reproduces the published muon-kinematic cross-section integral within about 1%, but differs in shape for an unidentified reason. In controlled studies the result retains part of its prior: fake-data studies at a GiBUU-like truth and at a synthetic momentum-transfer tilt find residual bias beyond uncertainty-based criteria fixed before grading; at both, the share of the prior retained by binned iterative unfolding is undefined. The five-observable estimator is numerically unstable: rounding-level input changes shift results by up to 2.6 statistical standard deviations, beyond a requirement fixed in advance; an average of 96 fits meets it but has greater bias at one of 17 truths. The procedure's two-dimensional covariance, rebuilt at each of two held-out generator tunes, under-covers at both; coverage of the reported five-observable intervals is not established. The same coverage test of the procedure's five-observable covariance stopped at a check fixed in advance, before its pseudo-experiments, and gives no coverage result.
