**3. Clarity.**

The following sentences need revision; replacements preserve their intended claims.

- Abstract, “A corresponding test … where the fake-data bias is not flagged …”:  
  “A descriptive coverage test evaluates the five-observable covariance at the two held-out tunes. Separate fake-data bias tests did not flag those tunes.”  
  Identify those separate tests: the coverage readout attributes AR23’s R07 flag to bias in both variants (`digest`:118–126, 76–84).

- Abstract, “For its two tunes, it finds …”:  
  “Both variants flag joint under-coverage at both tunes; AR23 also flags under-coverage in cell R07.”

- §6.2, “each drawn uncertainty group’s pseudo-data are built as its covariance block is built …”:  
  “For each sampled uncertainty group, the pseudo-data use the construction used for its covariance block. Lateral effects are represented by a surrogate; model, response and run-period variations are not sampled.”

- §6.2, “A run continued or repaired after a check was seen is descriptive”:  
  “Under the reporting policy, continuing after a failed check or repairing the test after a STOP makes the resulting readings descriptive.”

- §6.2, “an entry check that matches each simulated truth event … found no event matched”:  
  “At both tunes, the event-matching entry check reported every row unmatched because it compared weights stored at different precisions.”

- §6.2, “Each cell flag is pointwise, with the false-flag rate given above”:  
  “Each cell flag concerns that cell’s marginal interval. The thresholds limit the probability of any false cell flag to 0.025 within one tune and variant.”  
  Distinguish this threshold calibration from the route’s descriptive evidential status.

- §6.2, “both matrices have 16 degrees of freedom”:  
  “The joint reading carries over only if both covariance matrices have rank 16.”

- S9’s clipping-offset definition:  
  “Here \(C_T\) is the covariance rebuilt at the tune and \(\sigma_c=\sqrt{(C_T)_{cc}}\). The diagnostic offset \(o_c\) measures the response to the mean mass added by clipping, minus the corresponding truth shift, in units of \(\sigma_c\).”  
  This reflects declaration:432–438 more precisely than suggesting an exact ensemble-average unfolding bias.

- S9, “Its readings are therefore descriptive,” immediately after successful rerun checks:  
  “Because the repair was chosen after the STOP was observed, the coverage readings remain descriptive.”
