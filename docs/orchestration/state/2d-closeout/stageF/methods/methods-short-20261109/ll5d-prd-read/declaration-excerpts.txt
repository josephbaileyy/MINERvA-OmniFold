   430	### 5.3 clipcheck: the clipping offset up front (the 11-02 redesign's addition (ii), per row)
   431	
   432	- **The clipmean,** per truth and per reading: one unfold of d_T plus the mean over the 300 declared draws of each
   433	  row's |λ⁻|. That is the response to the clipped data mass around d_T.
   434	- **The truth side,** at no unfold: τ_c is the mean over the 300 draws of U·(T_D(clipped) − T_D(unclipped tdw and wt,
   435	  with T kept only where the unclipped denominator is > 0)), over σ_c.

   438	  used it. The final readout recomputes o_c with C_T.
