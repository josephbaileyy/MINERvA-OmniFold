=== ABSTRACT ===
We benchmark an unbinned five-observable OmniFold implementation on MINERvA open data, testing its truth recovery, numerical stability and uncertainty calibration. A descriptive twodimensional benchmark reproduces the published integral within about 1% but shows a shape difference. On our inputs, OmniFold differs from binned iterative unfolding, the publication’s method, beyond paired noise. Under controlled truth changes, the five-observable result retains part of its prior, and some cells move opposite to them. Fake-data studies at a GiBUU hybrid truth and a synthetic momentum-transfer tilt find residual bias beyond uncertainty-based criteria. On noise-free pseudo-data at both truths, binned iterative unfolding overshoots the truth along the prior-to-truth direction. The five-observable estimator’s results are sensitive to rounding-level input changes, beyond its stability requirement. The two-observable estimator’s covariance, rebuilt at two held-out generator tunes, under-covers at both. The reported five-observable intervals lack validated coverage. A corresponding test of the five-observable estimator’s covariance at those tunes, where the fake-data bias is not flagged, uses pseudo-data that omit the model, response and run-period variations. For its two tunes, it finds under-coverage at both in each variant, with and without the detector’s neutron and proton interaction bands. The test ran with a repair chosen after an earlier run stopped, and is descriptive. On data, the sixteen reported cells depend on the unfolding algorithm: binned iterative unfolding differs from them. We therefore present this five-observable output only as an illustration. 1.

=== SECTION 6.2 (the coverage tests), the five-observable test ===
A second test carries the design of the two-dimensional test to the sixteen cells. At each of the same two tunes it draws 300 pseudoexperiments in each variant, with the same random draws in both: each drawn uncertainty group’s pseudo-data are built as its covariance block is built, the lateral uncertainties are carried by a surrogate, and the model, response and run-period terms are not drawn. The covariance is rebuilt once per tune by the reported procedure. The graded covariance includes the model term, whose role is to cover each tune’s departure from the simulation, and leaves out the response and run-period terms. The test has two correlated variants: a primary one without the GEANT4 neutron and proton bands, and one with them. The primary variant leaves out the two bands whose pseudo-experiment and simulation-side blocks disagreed in the first test’s checks, from its pseudo-data and 16 from its covariance alike. The pseudo-experiment and simulation-side constructions match in each operation checked, and what separates their blocks for these bands lies inside the estimator and is unclassified (supplementary material, section S8.6). Excluding the two bands defines a restricted test, which cannot validate their treatment. A run continued or repaired after a check was seen is descriptive. The test uses the reported estimator. The construction of the pseudo-data, and the checks made on it before the main run, are given in supplementary material, section S8. Each tune and each variant is graded separately, per cell and jointly, against thresholds set from the test’s null distribution. A pass means that no under-coverage is detected at these thresholds, whose resolution is about 0.10 in coverage per cell and 0.08 jointly. In one variant at one tune, the cell tests raise a false flag with probability at most 0.025, and so does the joint test. Because the response and run-period terms are left out of the graded covariance, a cell that passes at a tune also passes under the procedure’s full covariance there, and the joint reading, the fraction with χ2 below the test’s cut, carries over only if both matrices have 16 degrees of freedom. An earlier run of this test stopped at a check fixed in advance, an entry check that matches each simulated truth event across two of the test’s inputs, which found no event matched. The test then ran with a repair chosen after that stop was seen: the correction of an implementation defect in that check, which compared event weights stored at two precisions. The repair changed no rule, threshold, bound or cell. The results below are therefore descriptive (supplementary material, section S8). Without the two bands, the test at G18 flags under-coverage jointly (12 of 300 in the joint region, flagged at 195 or fewer, mean χ2 /ndf = 3.07 ± 0.08), with no cell flagged, and at AR23 it flags under-coverage jointly (1 of 300 in the joint region, flagged at 195 or fewer, mean χ2 /ndf = 3.70 ± 0.10) and in cell R07 (163 of 300 within its interval, flagged at 178 or fewer). With the two bands, at G18 it flags undercoverage jointly (14 of 300 in the joint region, flagged at 195 or fewer, mean χ2 /ndf = 3.04 ± 0.08), with no cell flagged, and at AR23 it flags under-coverage jointly (4 of 300 in the joint region, flagged at 195 or fewer, mean χ2 /ndf = 3.69 ± 0.10) and in cell R07 (167 of 300 within its interval, flagged at 178 or fewer). Without the two bands at G18, it over-covers in cells R02, R03, R05, R06 and R09. With the two bands at G18, it over-covers in cells R02, R03, R06 and R09. Without the two bands at AR23, it over-covers in cells R02, R03, R06, R09, R11 and R15. With the two bands at AR23, it over-covers in cells R02, R03, R05, R06, R09, R11 and R15. Each cell flag is pointwise, with the false-flag rate given above. c.

=== SECTION 7.1 Limitations, Uncertainty calibration item ===
Uncertainty calibration. No reported interval has validated coverage. The fake-data tests grade bias only (section 3.3). The first coverage test of the fiveobservable covariance did not run to a result. The second test of section 6.2 grades the procedure’s covariance at two truths. The procedure’s twodimensional covariance under-covers at both heldout tunes (section 6.2). Neither the coverage of Cmodel at truths held out of its construction nor the size of either run-period block is independently validated (supplementary material, section S5, and section S6). The per-cell coverage readings cited are pointwise, for each reported quantity separately. Simultaneous coverage of all of them is not graded. The joint readings grade the fraction of pseudoexperiments whose χ2 lies in the test’s joint region. The covariances are estimated from finite ensembles, and their sampling precision is not quantified. 6. The systematic-universe unfoldings of the twodimensional covariance approximate the weighting of events that fail reconstruction. The approximation is not corrected. Tested in two universes, its effect is excluded only above twice the spread of an earlier training-seed block (section 3.2 and ref. [14], section 4.6). 7.

=== SUPPLEMENT S9 (the second test's study) ===
The second five-observable test’s design is the shared one of section S8.3 (table S13). Its primary variant leaves out the GEANT4 neutron and proton bands, the two bands whose pseudo-experiment and simulation-side blocks disagreed in the checks of the stopped first test (section S8.6, below), for a reason not identified. The second variant includes them and is reported beside it. Negative expected counts in its pseudo-data are clipped to zero, and checks test the effect of the clipping before the main run, on a sample whose first 300 draws are the coverage pseudo-experiments’ draws, at the same seeds. Each cell’s clipping offset oc is the shift that the clipping causes in its unfolded result, net of the clipping’s shift of its truth, in units of σc under CT . A flagged cell with |oc | ≥ δ ∗ = 0.667, a bound fixed in advance at the offset that lowers a Gaussian interval’s content by the per-cell resolution of 0.10, is read as a possible clipping artefact, a reading that cannot remove the flag. The clipping checks gave the following: no cell reads “passes, weakened by clipping”, no flagged cell is read as a possible clipping artefact, and the largest clipping offset is |oc | = 0.048 under CT , in R05 at AR23 in the variant with the two bands, against δ ∗ = 0.667. An earlier run stopped at a check fixed in advance, and the test then ran with a repair chosen after that stop was seen: at both tunes, an entry check that matches each simulated truth event across two of the test’s inputs found every event unmatched, and the run stopped. The entry checks had used the coverage pseudo-experiments’ draws (as above), but none had been unfolded or graded. Every other entry check had passed, and their readings were visible when the repair was chosen. A diagnosis classed the failure as an implementation defect: the check compared event weights stored at two floating-point precisions, so no event could match. The repair compares them at one precision and adds a check of the matching on the real inputs, which passed. It changed no rule, threshold, bound or cell. The entry checks 32 were then rerun at both tunes and passed. Its readings are therefore descriptive. S8.6
