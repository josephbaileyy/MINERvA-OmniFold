## 1. IBU framing. Verdict: fair, with no ranking or attribution stated; two requested changes.
(a) "identical inputs" (abstract, §1, §4.1, §7) is ambiguous. A reader can take it as "identical to the publication's inputs", which §4.2's untested-input-differences caveat and §5.4 ("They show neither whether those inputs equal the publication's...") contradict. §5.4 already says "this analysis's inputs". Use that.
(b) §4.1 joins "published result is itself a binned unfolding" to "OmniFold and binned IBU differ" with "and", right before "origin ... not identified". Read together, that reads as an implied attribution, and §4.1 does not carry §4.2's input caveat. The published-is-IBU fact also appears in §1, §4.2, §7 and Table 4, so §4.1 can drop it.

- §1 old: "A comparison using identical inputs establishes a difference between OmniFold and binned IBU beyond paired replica noise, and its origin remains unidentified."
  new: "On this analysis's inputs, a comparison detects a difference between OmniFold and binned IBU beyond paired replica noise, and its origin remains unidentified."
  ("detects" matches Table 4's "Difference detected" and §4.2's definition of "differs".)
- §4.1 old: "The published result is itself a binned unfolding, and on identical inputs OmniFold and binned IBU differ beyond paired replica noise (section~\ref{sec:paired2d})."
  new: "On this analysis's inputs, OmniFold and binned IBU differ beyond paired replica noise (section~\ref{sec:paired2d})."
- §7 old: "On identical inputs, OmniFold and binned IBU, the publication's method, differ beyond paired replica noise,"
  new: "On this analysis's inputs, OmniFold and binned IBU, the publication's method, differ beyond paired replica noise,"

- old: "On identical inputs, it differs from binned iterative unfolding, the publication's method, beyond paired noise."
  new: "On our inputs, OmniFold differs from binned iterative unfolding, the publication's method, beyond paired noise."
- Optional, net zero words: cutting "muon-kinematic cross-section" leaves "the published integral" without saying what was published.

## 3. Four contrast sentences. Verdict: the regressor and caption sentences read well and keep their information. The 135 MeV and lateral sentences each lose one fact.

  old: "which subtracts a defined constant of \SI{135}{MeV}, below the charged-pion mass, in the charged-pion term."
  new: "which subtracts a defined constant of \SI{135}{MeV} in place of the charged-pion mass in the charged-pion term."

- Regressor ("...which therefore estimates the unweighted mean weight at fixed truth"): keep. Caption ("Each point represents a distinct quantity, from the same two perturbations of the data."): keep.
