== supplemental_body.tex: the shared null and flag rule (unchanged)
992:A cell under-covers when its count of the \fvval{null-ntoys} errors inside the interval is at most the largest $k$ with $P(\mathrm{Bin}(\fvval{null-ntoys},p_c)\leq k)<\fvval{null-alpha}/N_{\mathrm{cells}}$, and the joint region under-covers when its count is at most the largest $k$ with $P(\mathrm{Bin}(\fvval{null-ntoys},q)\leq k)<\fvval{null-alpha}$. The rule is one-sided: over-coverage is reported and is not a failure. The test size per tune (and, in the second five-observable test, per variant) is at most \fvval{null-size}. A cell whose clipping narrows the drawn spread below \fvval{cov-weak} of its variance reads ``passes, weakened by clipping'' when it is not flagged.
990:With $e_c$ a pseudo-experiment's error in cell $c$, the nominal content of each per-cell interval $|e_c|\leq\sigma_c$ is $P_0=\fvval{null-p0}$ for a Gaussian error. Because the band draws are two-point or discrete, each test replaces $P_0$ with a draw-matched null content. The null error is the sum of one draw from each drawn band's centred discrete law at the tune (its universes' shifts with their draw probabilities) and one Gaussian with the remaining blocks' summed covariance, including $C_{\mathrm{model}}$. The components must reproduce $C_T$ to $\fvval{null-tol}$ of its scale. From $\fvval{null-nmc}$ simulated null errors, the null gives each cell's content $p_c$, the probability that $|e_c|\leq\sigma_c$, and the joint content $q$, the probability that $\chi^2=e^{\top}C_T^{+}e\leq F^{-1}_{\chi^2_{n}}(P_0)$, with $C_T^{+}$ the inverse on $C_T$'s non-null subspace and $n$ its dimension. The joint region is one-sided: it has an upper boundary only. Each content is lowered by \fvval{null-se-word} Monte Carlo standard errors before use.
