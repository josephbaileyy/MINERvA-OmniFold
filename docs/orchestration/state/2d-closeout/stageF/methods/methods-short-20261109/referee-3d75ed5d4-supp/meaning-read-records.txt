   125	## 4. The quantities and the noise, per pair (c, t)
   126	
   127	**The point values:**
   128	- r = H1 `bias[c]` and ν = H1 `noise_sigma[c]`, the standard error of r. ν is 0 for the noise-free closure rows.
   129	- d = H2's B0 `d[c]` at split 991000.
   130	- b = r (1 + d), which is §4.1's (X̂ − X)/P, because X/P = 1 + d.
   131	- **Applies** ⇔ |d| ≥ 0.01, by the point value.
   132	- **The margin** is m = 0.5 |d| − |b|. The pair passes by the point value iff m ≥ 0.
   133	
   134	**The noise band:**
   135	- σ_d(c) is the sample s.d. (ddof 1) of H2's B0 `d[c]` at A7_gibuu over the five splits 991000 and 994001–994004.
   136	  They are the only measured points of d's noise. σ_d(c) is applied to every truth in cell c, and the readout prints it.
