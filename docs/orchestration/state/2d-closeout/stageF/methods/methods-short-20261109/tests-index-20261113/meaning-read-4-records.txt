## The Supplement paragraph (supplemental_body.tex:1028), new
A first coverage test of the five-observable covariance at the two tunes gave no coverage result. Its staged redesign declared \fvval{fd1b-ntoys} pseudo-experiments at each tune. Each pseudo-experiment draws every uncertainty group (statistical, flux, interaction, detector, lateral and normalization), with the simulation and background template resampled. It is unfolded with the estimator and the nominal simulation, and graded against a covariance remade at the tune by the reported procedure. The per-cell and joint in-interval counts are read against a draw-matched null, at $\fvval{fd1b-alpha}/16$ per cell and \fvval{fd1b-alpha} jointly. The design allowed an optional plug-in truth, which was not used. One of its preliminary checks stopped it before any coverage pseudo-experiment ran. That check compared pseudo-experiments that move only the detector response at fixed truth with blocks built from the prior and the response together. A staged redesign then stopped at its first stage, under its stopping rules. The check required, in every cell, the ratio of the pseudo-experiments' standard deviation to that of the reported block to lie in the window \fvval{cov1b-win-lo}--\fvval{cov1b-win-hi}. The diagnostic study of that check gave the following results. No set of \fvval{cov1b-kmax-word} or fewer of the \fvval{cov1b-nbands} drawn bands brings every cell inside the window. In R08, the cell with the largest ratio, the interaction bands MaRES, MaCCQE and MvRES carry most of the excess. Estimator noise is rejected as the explanation for every failing cell.
