\section{Discussion and conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

This implementation performs simultaneous unbinned unfolding in five observables, without a separate binned response tensor for each projection. The five-observable result agrees with the two-dimensional result in each muon-kinematic quadrant, within the tested family of cross-covariances. The output's limitations are listed in section~\ref{sec:limitations}.

The studies of sections~\ref{sec:method}--\ref{sec:stability} and of the companion note give five checks to make before the intervals of an unbinned unfolding are interpreted. The checks are general. The findings stated with them apply to this implementation.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Closure should include the backgrounds (section~\ref{sec:closure}).
\item Generator-sized truth changes should be recovered, and the retained part of the prior measured (sections~\ref{sec:departure} and~\ref{sec:prr}).
\item Coverage should be tested at changed truths, jointly as well as per cell, with the joint statistic read in the covariance's eigenbasis (section~\ref{sec:calib}).
\item Robustness should be tested against rounding-level input changes as well as training seeds (section~\ref{sec:cov5d}).
\item Run periods should be compared before and after unfolding (section~\ref{sec:closure2d}).
\end{enumerate}
In this implementation, background-inclusive closure meets the bias criteria on the sixteen cells, and each of the other four checks finds a problem: part of the prior is retained, the two-dimensional covariance under-covers for a matrix without the run-period term\fvgroupalt{ll5d}{}{\fvgroup{ll5d}{\fvcase{ll5d-alljoint}{all}{ (the second five-observable test also flags joint under-coverage\fvcase{ll5d-status}{descriptive}{\fvcase{ll5d-route}{offset-proceeded}{, reported descriptively,}\fvcase{ll5d-route}{repaired}{, reported descriptively after an entry-check repair,}}\fvcase{ll5d-status}{graded}{}\fvcase{ll5d-status}{invalid}{}\fvcase{ll5d-status}{stopped}{} for a covariance without the response and run-period terms)}\fvcase{ll5d-alljoint}{notall}{}\fvcase{ll5d-alljoint}{na}{}}}, the result is not stable under rounding, and a run-period step survives unfolding.

\label{sec:limits}
The benchmark shows agreement in normalization and a correlated shape difference in two dimensions. On this analysis's inputs, OmniFold and binned IBU, the publication's method, differ beyond paired replica noise, and the origin of the difference remains unidentified. The benchmark does not validate the five-observable distribution. The bias-screening rules grade the estimator's bias under simulated truths. The rules do not identify where the detector model is inadequate. Whether the two-dimensional shape difference arises in the hadronic observables is open. Testing it needs contrasts within fixed muon-kinematic regions, with their joint uncertainty, and a check that plausible changes of the unfolding and detector response cannot produce the pattern. That test is a next step.


\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Estimator bias.} Recovery within the reported uncertainty, as graded on the sixteen reported cells, is not achieved for every generator-sized truth change tested. The unfolded result retains part of its prior, and some $(\Eavail,W)$ cells move opposite to a truth change (section~\ref{sec:prior}).
\item Four of the sixteen cells, \fvval{h2-lowstab-cells}, have a low diagonal fraction (table~\ref{tab:cells}).
\item The step-1 weight of truth events without a reconstructed partner comes from a regression fitted with equal event weights (section~\ref{sec:unfolding}). The effect of using a regression weighted with each event's weight at that iteration was tested in the data unfolding and in noise-free, signal-only closures on the full simulation. Its effect on the flags from half-sample pseudo-data with background and noise was not tested. % absence-ok: scopes what RSN's WR tested (DECISION-20261112-joseph-consult-d1-d7.md D3 and its addendum (Joseph: "i approve the draft")); the half-sample flags' attribution is T3's MW reading, after LL5D (RESPONSE.md pre-submission checklist item 1)
\item The reported cells depend on the unfolding algorithm, and the reported covariance has no term for that dependence: on data, binned IBU differs from them by up to \fvval{ibu16-dsig-max} times a cell's reported standard deviation (section~\ref{sec:ibu}).
\item \emph{Numerical behaviour.} The estimator fails its data-stability requirement under rounding-level changes of its inputs. An ensemble-averaged variant that meets it on the pseudo-data used to test stability is worse at one truth and is not adopted. The instability of the data result itself is therefore not mitigated (section~\ref{sec:cov5d}).
\item \emph{Uncertainty calibration.} No reported interval has validated coverage. The fake-data tests grade bias only (section~\ref{sec:strategy}). The first coverage test of the five-observable covariance did not run to a result. The second test of section~\ref{sec:ll5d} \fvgroupalt{ll5d}{applies pre-specified criteria to the procedure's covariance at two truths}{\fvgroup{ll5d}{\fvcase{ll5d-campaign}{graded}{applies pre-specified criteria to the procedure's covariance at two truths\fvcase{ll5d-alljoint}{all}{ and flags joint under-coverage at both in both variants, with the response and run-period terms omitted}\fvcase{ll5d-alljoint}{notall}{}\fvcase{ll5d-alljoint}{na}{}\fvcase{ll5d-status}{descriptive}{\fvcase{ll5d-route}{offset-proceeded}{; it is reported descriptively}\fvcase{ll5d-route}{repaired}{; it is reported descriptively under the rule for repairs chosen after a stop}}\fvcase{ll5d-status}{graded}{}\fvcase{ll5d-status}{invalid}{}\fvcase{ll5d-status}{stopped}{}}\fvcase{ll5d-campaign}{gate-failed}{stopped before it gave a coverage result}\fvcase{ll5d-campaign}{offset-stopped}{stopped before it gave a coverage result}\fvcase{ll5d-campaign}{stop}{stopped before it gave a coverage result}}}. The procedure's two-dimensional covariance under-covers at both held-out tunes (section~\ref{sec:calib}). Neither the coverage of $C_{\mathrm{model}}$ at truths held out of its construction nor the size of either run-period block is independently validated (\ssec{S5}{supp:cov16}, and \ssecc{S6}{supp:cov2d}). The per-cell coverage readings cited are pointwise, for each reported quantity separately. Simultaneous coverage of all of them is not graded. The joint readings grade the fraction of pseudo-experiments whose $\chi^2$ lies in the test's joint region. The covariances are estimated from finite ensembles, and their sampling precision is not quantified. % absence-ok: the director's G3.3 ruling (2026-11-12): a scope sentence; an unpriced analysis is on the follow-up list
\item \emph{Data and response.} The data show a run-period inconsistency, present before unfolding, of unknown cause (section~\ref{sec:closure2d}).
\item The simulated detector response is not varied in the closure, deformation and fake-data tests (sections~\ref{sec:strategy} and~\ref{sec:fd16}), beyond the descriptive closures with a shifted recoil response of section~\ref{sec:fd16}. The coverage tests vary it only through the detector-systematic uncertainties they draw (section~\ref{sec:calib}). Because the closure, deformation, fake-data and coverage tests all use simulated pseudo-data, none tests the detector model against data.
\end{enumerate}
