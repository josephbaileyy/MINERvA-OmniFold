The reported covariance

The reported covariance is Ctotal + Cresp , with Ctotal = Ce + Cmodel . Ce sums the statistical bootstrap
of configuration (e), the flux universes weighted by the neutrino–electron and inverse-muon-decay
constraint, the interaction and detector bands, the lateral bands, the normalization and the trainingseed block. The training-seed block was measured and is zero in every cell: with full-sample binning
and deterministic training, the unfolds at the other seeds are bitwise equal to the central one.
Table 12 gives each group’s per-cell relative σ.
Cmodel is built from the change in the unfolded data when the prior is reweighted to each of three
generators, GENIE + MEC, NuWro and GiBUU: each vertex is that relative shift of the data result,
and Cmodel is the sum of their outer products, unscaled. It is generator-shape model dependence,
not a calibrated bound. Its coverage at truths held out of its construction has not been validated.
GiBUU’s shape is applied only below p∥ = 10 GeV/c, above which the prior is unchanged; between
p∥ = 6 and 10 GeV/c GiBUU’s sample lacks the part above Eν = 20 GeV, 4.9–5.3% of the cross
section there. GiBUU’s vertex carries 0.890 of Cmodel ’s trace, NuWro’s 0.095 and GENIE + MEC’s
0.014. In every cell, 77–100% of the prior weight lies beside a step in the reweighting ratio larger
than 0.1 drawn by the noise gate or a cut, and the shifts enter as measured.
Cresp is the response of configuration (e)’s data result to a one-standard-deviation scale of the
simulation’s reconstructed recoil energy, for protons, mesons, electromagnetic deposits and other
deposits separately, summed over the four. It does not cover neutron or muon deposits, the null
distributions of the joint tests, the configuration-(a) products or the two-, three- and four-dimensional
results. Its median per-cell relative σ is 2.14%, and adding it raises the total’s σ by a factor of
1.011–1.220 (median 1.048). Of the trace of (Ctotal + Cresp )/f f T , Ctotal carries 0.9262 and the four
response species 0.0168, 0.0436, 0.0071 and 0.0062.

Fake-data tests of the estimator’s bias

The fake-data tests ask how the estimator’s bias at a truth different from the simulation compares
with the reported covariance. Pseudo-data are generated at 12 graded truths and unfolded with
configuration (e), and the mean unfolded result is compared with each truth on the 16 cells, through
χ2 with 16 degrees of freedom and the per-cell pull z. The truths are the simulation’s prior; the

shapes W1, W2 and W3 of §9.4; a GiBUU-to-GENIE Eavail shape; a noise-free closure at the NuWro
shape; and a synthetic tilt of the q3 distribution at fixed (Eavail , W ). A truth from a generator
that contributes a vertex to Cmodel is compared with the covariance without that vertex, so that no
truth is compared with a covariance that contains its own generator’s shift. A truth is listed when
p < 0.01, or when a cell has |z| > 2. Table 13 gives each truth under the reported covariance and,
