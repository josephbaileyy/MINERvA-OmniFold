\subsection{A sixteen-cell five-observable measurement}
\label{sec:result16}
A five-observable cross section with total uncertainties is reported separately from those reporting definitions: configuration~(e)'s data result on 16 cells, R00--R15. They are the cells of the coarser joint grid of the study above after a purity merge: each of the \fvval{h2-nboxes} boxes formed by one interior edge per axis whose purity is below \fvval{h2-tau} is merged into a neighbour, \fvval{h2-nunreported} boxes remain unreported, and every reported cell passes the study's support rule. The reported covariance is $C_{\mathrm{total}}+C_{\mathrm{resp}}$. $C_{\mathrm{total}}$ sums the statistical, flux, interaction, detector, lateral and normalization uncertainties of configuration~(e), a training-seed term measured to be zero, and $C_{\mathrm{model}}$. $C_{\mathrm{model}}$ is built from the change in the unfolded data when the prior is reweighted to GENIE + MEC, NuWro and GiBUU in turn; it is generator-shape model dependence, unscaled, and its coverage at truths held out of its construction has not been validated. $C_{\mathrm{resp}}$ is the response of the result to a one-standard-deviation scale of the simulated recoil energy of protons, mesons, electromagnetic and other deposits; it does not cover neutron or muon deposits. The median per-cell relative uncertainty is \fvval{sigmed-C_total_resp}\% (\fvval{sigmed-C_total}\% without $C_{\mathrm{resp}}$).
