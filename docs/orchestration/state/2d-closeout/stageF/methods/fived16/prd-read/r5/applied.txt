A new study unfolded pseudo-data at two GENIE v3 tunes that no part of the analysis used, to test the estimator's bias against the reported covariance; it is a bias test, not a coverage test. H1 (paper). OLD: (none)
NEW: Two GENIE v3 tunes, G18_10a_02_11b and AR23_20i_00_000, were generated for this test and held out of the analysis. Each reweights the simulated truth to the tune's shape using the C_model procedure; the detector response is the simulation's and is not probed. For each tune, 20 Poisson pseudo-experiments and a noise-free closure are compared with the truth using the full reported covariance. Both tunes satisfy both bias criteria: the pseudo-experiment means give χ²=11.84 (p=0.76) and 16.86 (p=0.39), respectively, with |z|≤0.65 in every cell of the means and closures. Both are GENIE tunes and share their quasielastic model; G18_10a_02_11b also uses a Valencia-family MEC model, as does the GENIE + MEC component of C_model. They provide partial evidence and are not independent tests. Omitting that component gives χ²=15.15 (p=0.51) and 24.60 (p=0.077), respectively, as a descriptive comparison. These tests assess bias, not coverage: only event counts fluctuate, without variation of systematic parameters, so interval widths cannot be tested.

H2 (note). OLD: (none)
NEW: Two truths are held out of the analysis: the GENIE v3 tunes G18_10a_02_11b and AR23_20i_00_000, generated for this test. Each enters as its gated, normalized shape ratio to GENIE CV on the fine grid, built as the C_model vertices are, pooled over a coarser cell or set to one where the tune's sample fails the ratio's statistical gate, and applied to the simulated truth under the simulation's detector response. At each, 20 Poisson pseudo-experiments on half the simulation and a noise-free closure are graded against the full reported covariance, as truths without a vertex, in a group of their own that is never pooled with the groups above. The bias passes both criteria in all four records. The mean of the pseudo-experiments gives χ²=11.84 (p=0.76; largest |z| 0.62, in R11) at G18_10a_02_11b and 16.86 (p=0.39; 0.64, in R07) at AR23_20i_00_000; the closures give 11.44 (p=0.78; 0.61, in R11) and 16.05 (p=0.45; 0.60, in R07). In every cell of the four records, the bias is below 0.65 of the cell's reported σ. The group has 0 of 64 cells with |z|>2 (Gaussian reference 2.91) and 0 of 4 records with p<0.01 (reference 0.04). The references count the four records as independent, but they are two truths, each counted by its pseudo-experiments and by its closure, so they are context only. The two tunes share their quasielastic model, and the MEC model of G18_10a_02_11b is of the Valencia family used in the GENIE + MEC vertex. Both are GENIE tunes, so they are partial evidence and not independent tests. Descriptively, without the GENIE + MEC vertex the pseudo-experiments give χ²=15.15 (p=0.51) and 24.60 (p=0.077), and the closures 14.35 (p=0.57) and 22.43 (p=0.13). The model-only χ², defined as in Table 15, are 817–922 in the four records, with R00 beyond 2σ at AR23_20i_00_000 in both its records. The grading, rerun with the two truths, reproduces every record of the fake-data truths above exactly. This is a bias test, not a coverage test: Poisson pseudo-experiments with no draws of the systematic parameters cannot test the width of the intervals, and the detector response is not probed.
