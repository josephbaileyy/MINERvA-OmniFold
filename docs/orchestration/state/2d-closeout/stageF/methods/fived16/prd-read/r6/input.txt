BACKGROUND. The paper reports a sixteen-cell, five-observable neutrino cross section with a covariance. Its intervals' coverage has not been validated. A new study unfolded pseudo-data at two GENIE v3 tunes that no part of the analysis used, to test the estimator's bias against the reported covariance; it is a bias test, not a coverage test. A1 and A2 are the paper's and the note's abstract sentence, and H1 a new paragraph of the paper's fake-data section (OLD: none). P02 is a sentence of the paper's introduction.

A1 (paper abstract). OLD: The coverage of the intervals has not been validated.
NEW: The coverage of the intervals has not been validated; a bias test at two held-out GENIE v3 truths finds the estimator's bias below the reported uncertainty in every cell but does not test interval widths.

A2 (note abstract). OLD: The coverage of the result's intervals has not been validated.
NEW: The coverage of the result's intervals has not been validated; a bias test at two held-out GENIE v3 truths finds the estimator's bias below the reported uncertainty in every cell but does not test interval widths.

H1 (paper). OLD: (none)
NEW: Two GENIE v3 tunes, G18_10a_02_11b and AR23_20i_00_000, were generated for this test and held out of the analysis. Each reweights the simulated truth by the tune's shape ratio to GENIE CV, built as for the C_model components; the detector response is the simulation's and is not probed. For each tune, 20 Poisson pseudo-experiments and a noise-free closure are compared with the truth using the full reported covariance. Both tunes satisfy both bias criteria: the pseudo-experiment means give χ²=11.84 (p=0.76) and 16.86 (p=0.39), respectively, with |z|≤0.65 in every cell of the means and closures. Both are GENIE tunes and use the same quasielastic cross-section model; G18_10a_02_11b also uses a Valencia-family MEC model, as does the GENIE + MEC component of C_model. They provide partial evidence and are not independent tests. Omitting that component gives χ²=15.15 (p=0.51) and 24.60 (p=0.077), respectively, as a descriptive comparison. These tests assess bias, not coverage: only event counts fluctuate, without variation of systematic parameters, so interval widths cannot be tested.

P02 (paper). NEW: The validation studies assess the result through the two-dimensional reproduction, background-subtraction tests, recovery of generator-sized truth changes, a coverage test and a fake-data study of estimator bias. They examine whether the unfolding gives reliable multidimensional cross sections when the simulation mismodels the truth by generator-sized amounts.
