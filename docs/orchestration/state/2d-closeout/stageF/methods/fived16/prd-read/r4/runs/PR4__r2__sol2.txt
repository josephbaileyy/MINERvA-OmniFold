SHORTER-FORM: Weakening only the flux correlations by 10% lowers the χ² of the 16-cell predictions by 24–48%. The same change to the detector or lateral correlations lowers it by at most 18%. With the correlations of the interaction, flux, detector and lateral groups removed, every 16-cell prediction keeps χ² ≥ 45 for 16 degrees of freedom. The flux correlations are estimated from 100 hadron-production (PPFX) flux universes [16], weighted by the neutrino–electron and inverse-muon-decay constraint. Bootstrapping these universes gives 16th–84th percentile bands for each 16-cell χ², with edges within 1.5–6.2% of the reported value and a median at most 1.0% above it. These bands describe scatter from estimating the correlations with these universes; weakening probes sensitivity to incorrect correlations. The bands are not intervals for χ² under the true correlations.
REASON: The addition usefully distinguishes estimation scatter from correlation sensitivity, but tighter wording better suits the measurement paper.
