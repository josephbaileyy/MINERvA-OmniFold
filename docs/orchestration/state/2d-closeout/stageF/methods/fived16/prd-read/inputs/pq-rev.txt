CONTEXT (not to be judged). The two paragraphs of the paper's subsection V E that precede Part 1:

To include model dependence, a dedicated study defines the envelope of a cell as the largest shift of its data result when the simulation's prior is reweighted to each of five generator-sized changes of the truth, and adds it linearly to the probabilistic half-width. The envelope is a sensitivity diagnostic that tracks the regularization bias, not an interval. Our precision target is a 10% median half-width for the total 68% interval. With the envelope added, none of the reporting definitions of a five-observable measurement tested in the study meets this target. With the joint-test estimator, the envelope alone exceeds it for the 109 coarse joint cells and for the fallback definition, the 39 supported (E_avail,W) cells. For a coarser, non-nested partition of all five observables into 27 cells, the same estimator's envelope alone does not exceed it, but the 1.4% normalization contained in every probabilistic half-width lifts the median above it. The study is described in Ref. [14], Sec. 9.4.

Over these definitions, the envelope has a median of about 10–13% per cell, comparable to the generator differences that such a measurement would need to resolve. It motivates the different route to inference of Sec. VII: after a descriptive comparison of the central values, the generator predictions are tested as hypotheses.

PART 1. Two subsections of the paper (tables omitted).

V F. A sixteen-cell five-observable measurement

A five-observable cross section with total uncertainties is reported separately from those reporting definitions: configuration (e)'s data result on 16 cells, R00–R15. They come from the 32 boxes formed by one interior edge per axis (p_T 0.7, p_∥ 4.5 GeV/c, E_avail 0.8, q_3 2, W 1.8 GeV), of which the study's 27 coarser cells are the supported ones; each supported box with purity below 0.1 is merged into a neighbour, 4 boxes remain unreported, and every reported cell passes the support rule. Its model term differs from the study's envelope: there, the largest shift of a cell over five generator-sized truth changes, added linearly to the half-width; here, a covariance built from three generator shifts. The reported covariance is C_total+C_resp. C_total sums the statistical, flux, interaction, detector, lateral and normalization uncertainties of configuration (e), a training-seed term measured to be zero, and C_model. C_model is built from the change in the unfolded data when the prior is reweighted to GENIE + MEC, NuWro and GiBUU in turn; it is generator-shape model dependence, unscaled and not a calibrated bound, and its coverage at truths held out of its construction has not been validated. C_resp is the response of the result to a one-standard-deviation scale of the reconstructed recoil energy in the simulation, for protons, mesons, electromagnetic and other deposits; it does not cover neutron or muon deposits, nor species, energy-dependence or resolution effects beyond a scale, and it does not enter the generator tests of Sec. VII or the paper's other results. The median per-cell relative uncertainty is 7.29% (6.74% without C_resp). The note gives the cells, the central values and the per-group uncertainties.

To compare the estimator's bias with the reported covariance when the truth differs from the simulation, we unfold pseudo-data at 12 truths and compare the mean unfolded result with each truth on the 16 cells through χ² with 16 degrees of freedom. The truths are the simulation's prior; the five hybrid truths of the generator tests of Sec. VII, each a prediction on the fine grid; NuWro shape changes in E_avail and in (p_T,p_∥,E_avail); a GENIE + MEC shape change in (E_avail,W); a GiBUU-to-GENIE E_avail ratio; a noise-free closure at the NuWro shape; and a synthetic tilt of the q_3 distribution at fixed (E_avail,W). A truth from a generator that contributes to C_model is compared with the covariance without that generator's component, so that no such truth is compared with a covariance that includes its own generator's component; 6 further truths, whose offset correction failed its control, are ungraded and given in the note. A truth is listed when p<0.01 or when a cell has |z|>2. Table IV gives the results under the reported covariance and under C_total alone.

Under the reported covariance, without the GiBUU component for the two GiBUU truths, the bias is listed at the GiBUU E_avail shape (cell R06, z=2.01), at the GiBUU hybrid truth (p=8.0 × 10^-29; cells R02, R06, R07) and at the synthetic q_3 tilt (p=6.4 × 10^-6): the reported uncertainty does not cover the fake-data bias at these GiBUU-like truths or at the q_3 tilt. At the other 9 truths the bias stays within the reported uncertainty by both criteria: no cell beyond 2σ and p ≥ 0.01.

VII B. Comparison on the sixteen reported cells

Table V compares the sixteen-cell result of Sec. V F with five predictions through χ² against the reported covariance plus each prediction's finite-sample (diagonal) covariance V_g, with shape-only χ² beside it; no diagonal-only χ² is formed. GiBUU's sample stops at E_ν=20 GeV, so it is compared on the 8 cells with p_∥<4.5 GeV/c only. The reported uncertainty does not cover the fake-data bias at the GiBUU-like truths (Sec. V F). NuWro and GiBUU are on carbon, with no correction to CH, and MINERvA Tune v1 is the simulation's prior. Because C_model is built from shifts toward GENIE + MEC, NuWro and GiBUU, each relative to GENIE CV, those comparisons use a covariance built in part from the compared generator, and the GENIE CV comparison does through that reference.

Weakening only the flux correlations by 10 % lowers the χ² of the 16-cell predictions by 24–48 %. The same change to the detector or lateral correlations lowers it by at most 18 %. With the correlations of the interaction, flux, detector and lateral groups removed, every 16-cell prediction keeps χ² ≥ 45 for 16 degrees of freedom.
