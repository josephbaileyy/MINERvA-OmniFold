For each tune, 20 Poisson pseudo-experiments and a noise-free closure are compared with the truth using the full reported covariance. Both tunes satisfy the bias criteria: the pseudo-experiment means give χ²=11.84 (p=0.76) and 16.86 (p=0.39), respectively, with |z|≤0.65 in every cell of the means and closures. The tunes share a quasielastic cross-section model; G18_10a_02_11b and the GENIE + MEC component of C_model also use Valencia-family MEC models. These tests provide partial evidence and are not independent. Omitting that C_model component gives χ²=15.15 (p=0.51) and 24.60 (p=0.077), respectively, as a descriptive comparison. The tests assess bias, not coverage: only event counts fluctuate, with systematic parameters fixed, so interval widths cannot be tested.
