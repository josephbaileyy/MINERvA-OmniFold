Q1.
BEFORE (the paper abstract, before d2 (accepted as correct)): We report a simultaneous measurement of the ν_μ charged-current inclusive cross section in five observables, the muon transverse and longitudinal momenta, available hadronic energy, three-momentum transfer and hadronic invariant mass, unfolded without binning with OmniFold from MINERvA medium-energy open data and reported in sixteen cells with a covariance whose median per-cell uncertainty is 7.29%. Three external generator predictions compared on all sixteen cells give χ² of 109–272 for 16 degrees of freedom, and GiBUU, on 8 cells, 58. The coverage of the intervals has not been validated; a bias test at two held-out GENIE v3 truths finds the estimator's bias below the reported uncertainty in every cell but does not test interval widths. In a fake-data study, the reported uncertainty does not cover the estimator's bias at GiBUU-like truths or at a synthetic q3 tilt. The sixteen-cell χ² values fall by 24–48% when only the flux correlations are weakened by 10%. The same unfolding reproduces the integral of the published muon-kinematic cross section within about 1%, with a correlated shape difference. Pseudo-experiments measure a background-subtraction bias that negative-weight subtraction largely removes, a partial recovery of generator-sized truth changes, and statistical undercoverage of a deterministic purity-subtracted variant at the tested truths. As an illustration, simulation-calibrated tests of hybrid null hypotheses for five generator predictions all reject at the 5% familywise level, with no evidence of gain over matched coarse projections.
AFTER: We report a simultaneous measurement of the ν_μ charged-current inclusive cross section in five observables, the muon transverse and longitudinal momenta, available hadronic energy, three-momentum transfer and hadronic invariant mass, unfolded without binning with OmniFold from MINERvA medium-energy open data and reported in sixteen cells with a validated covariance whose median per-cell uncertainty is 7.29%. BEFORE (the note abstract, before d2 (accepted as correct)): This note documents an unbinned, simultaneous unfolding of MINERvA medium-energy ν_μ charged-current inclusive open data with OmniFold, and its validation. Its principal result is a cross section in five observables, muon transverse and longitudinal momentum, available energy (E_avail), three-momentum transfer (q3) and hadronic invariant mass (W), in sixteen cells, with a covariance of statistical, flux, interaction, detector, lateral, normalization, generator-shape and calorimetric-response terms; its median per-cell uncertainty is 7.29%. Three external generator predictions compared on all sixteen cells give χ² between 109 and 272 for 16 degrees of freedom, and GiBUU, on its 8 cells, 58. The coverage of the result's intervals has not been validated; a bias test at two held-out GENIE v3 truths finds the estimator's bias below the reported uncertainty in every cell but does not test interval widths. In a fake-data study, the reported uncertainty does not cover the estimator's bias at GiBUU-like truths or at a synthetic q3 tilt. The sixteen-cell χ² values fall by 24–48% when only the flux correlations are weakened by 10%. The note also gives the reproduction of the published double-differential cross section in muon momentum, whose integral agrees within about one percent, with a correlated shape difference and a covariance that has not been independently validated; three- and four-observable central values; and an earlier purity-subtracted five-observable central value on a finer grid, whose covariance does not meet its specification's acceptance criteria and whose quoted uncertainties omit that estimator's background bias of up to 4% at high W and its regularization bias. The validation studies show that negative-weight background subtraction removes most of the purity method's bias, that generator-sized truth changes are recovered only in part, a residual interpreted as regularization bias, that statistical intervals of a deterministic purity-subtracted variant miscover at the tested truths, and that no tested reporting definition meets a predeclared 10% precision criterion. Joint tests calibrated in simulation reject hybrid null hypotheses for five generator predictions at a 5% family-wise error rate; they are not physical exclusions, show no evidence of discrimination beyond matched coarse projections, omit an uncertainty on the calorimetric recoil response and assume an unshown convergence of the fine grid on which the null hypotheses are defined, and NuWro's total-test rejection is not robust to a hydrocarbon-target correction.
AFTER: We report a simultaneous measurement of the ν_μ charged-current inclusive cross section in five observables, the muon transverse and longitudinal momenta, available hadronic energy, three-momentum transfer and hadronic invariant mass, unfolded without binning with OmniFold from MINERvA medium-energy open data and reported in sixteen cells with a covariance whose median per-cell uncertainty is 7.29%. Three external generator predictions compared on all sixteen cells give χ² between 109 and 272 for 16 degrees of freedom, and GiBUU, on its 8 cells, 58. The coverage of the result's intervals has been validated; a bias test at two held-out GENIE v3 truths finds the estimator's bias below the reported uncertainty in every cell but does not test interval widths. In a fake-data study, the reported uncertainty does not cover the estimator's bias at GiBUU-like truths or at a synthetic q3 tilt.
