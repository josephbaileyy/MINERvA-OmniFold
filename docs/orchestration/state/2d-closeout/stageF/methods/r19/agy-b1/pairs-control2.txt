The generator tests are conditional tests of simulated hybrid null hypotheses, under which the cross section equals a prediction on every cell of a fine five-observable grid, with the MINERvA Tune v1 composition below that grid, as defined in Sec. VI A. They are calibrated within the implemented simulation model, whose detector model has no calorimetric recoil-response uncertainty. Under the assumptions of Sec. VI B, their decision rule controls the familywise error rate of the ten decisions at 5%, on average over the nuisance priors. The rejections are not physical exclusions of the generators and provide no measurement uncertainty.

Q5.
BEFORE: The GiBUU sample stops at Eν ≈ 20 GeV. The predictions compute W with the neutron mass for every event. In each cell where this choice was evaluated, it changes their coarse-cell predictions by at most about one Monte Carlo standard error. Its effect cannot be evaluated for GiBUU. The predictions also measure pT about their own neutrino axis, which differs from the analysis truth pT by ±4 MeV for 68% of events. Hydrogen has no quasielastic, 2p2h, or coherent νµ charged-current channel, so normalizing per nucleon does not remove the difference between the targets. In GENIE’s own CH sample, the carbon-only cross section per nucleon exceeds that of CH by 8.3 % for W < 1.1 GeV. The NuWro and GiBUU comparisons contain a target difference of this kind, which the tests of Sec. VI do not correct. These comparisons of the predictions with the analysis definitions are given in Ref. [14], Secs. 8.1 and 8.3.

BEFORE: A five-observable cross section with total uncertainties is reported separately from those reporting definitions: the joint-test estimator’s data result on 16 cells, R00–R15. They come from the 32 boxes formed by one interior edge per axis (pT 0.7, p∥ 4.5 GeV/c, Eavail 0.8, q3 2, W 1.8 GeV): each supported box with purity below 0.1 is merged into a neighbour, 4 boxes remain unreported, and every reported cell passes the support rule. Unlike the envelope of Sec. III A, its model term is a covariance built from three generator shifts. The reported covariance is Ctotal + Cresp . Ctotal sums the statistical, flux, interaction, detector, lateral and normalization uncertainties of the joint-test estimator and Cmodel . Cmodel is built from the change in the unfolded data when the prior is reweighted to GENIE + MEC, NuWro and GiBUU in turn; it represents unscaled generator-shape dependence, not a calibrated bound, and its coverage at truths held out of its construction has not been validated. Cresp is the response of the result to a one-standard-deviation scale of the reconstructed recoil energy in the simulation, for protons, mesons, electromagnetic and other deposits; it does not cover neutron or muon deposits, nor species, energy-dependence or resolution effects beyond a scale, and it enters only this result, its fake-data tests and its comparison in Sec. V A. The median per-cell relative uncertainty is 7.29% (6.74% without Cresp ). The note gives the cells, the central values, the per-group uncertainties and the correlation matrix of the reported covariance.
