\subsection{Comparison on the sixteen reported cells}
\label{sec:gen16}

Unfolded results can be compared with any prediction at truth level without simulating each generator through the
detector.
This makes the two-dimensional result reusable, the higher-dimensional central values usable for qualitative
comparison, and the sixteen-cell result usable for the comparison that follows.
Table~\ref{tab:gen16} compares the sixteen-cell result of Sec.~\ref{sec:result16} with five predictions through a
$\chi^2$ against the reported covariance plus each prediction's finite-sample (diagonal) covariance $V_g$, with a
shape-only $\chi^2$ beside it.
Figure~\ref{fig:ratio16} shows the data over each prediction, cell by cell.
GiBUU's sample stops at $E_\nu=\fvval{cmodel-gibuu-enu}$~GeV, so GiBUU is compared on the 8 cells with
$\pPar<\fvval{h2-mid-pz}$~GeV/$c$ only.
NuWro and GiBUU are on carbon, with no correction to CH, and MINERvA Tune~v1 is the simulation's prior.
The GENIE + MEC, NuWro and GiBUU comparisons use covariance contributions derived from those generators, and
GENIE CV enters as their common reference.

Weakening only the flux correlations by \fvval{sens-alpha-pct}\,\% lowers the $\chi^2$ of the 16-cell predictions by
\fvval{sens-flux-fall-lo}--\fvval{sens-flux-fall-hi}\,\%.
The same change to the detector or lateral correlations lowers it by at most \fvval{sens-detlat-fall-max}\,\%.
With the correlations of the interaction, flux, detector and lateral groups removed, every 16-cell prediction keeps
$\chi^2\geq\fvval{sens-joint-chi2-min}$ for 16 degrees of freedom.
The flux correlations are estimated from \fvval{fb-universes} hadron-production (PPFX) flux
universes~\cite{MINERvA:2016iqn}, weighted by the neutrino--electron and inverse-muon-decay constraint.
Bootstrapping these universes, with the flux variances kept, gives \fvval{fb-plo}th--\fvval{fb-phi}th percentile
bands for each 16-cell $\chi^2$, with edges within \fvval{fb-edge-max}\,\% of the reported value and a median at
most \fvval{fb-offset-relmax}\,\% above it.
These bands describe the scatter of the $\chi^2$ from estimating the correlations with these universes, not its
sensitivity to incorrect correlations, which the weakening probes.
In particular, they are not intervals for the $\chi^2$ under the true correlations.

\subsection{Descriptive comparison of the central values}
\label{sec:central}

These comparisons use the three- and four-observable central values of the joint-test estimator, without
uncertainties.
GENIE, the MINERvA Tune~v1, NuWro, and GiBUU all underpredict the unfolded central value at low $\Eavail$, between
the quasielastic and $\Delta$ peaks, in the region of the established low-recoil
discrepancy~\cite{MINERvA:2016lowrecoil,MINERvA:2022incl}.
Comparisons with pion final-state-interaction variations, GENIE's Valencia 2p2h component, and the published
measurement are given in Ref.~\cite{AnalysisNote2026}, Secs.~\ref*{N-sec:3d-2p2h} and~\ref*{N-sec:4d}.

The five-observable $(\Eavail,W)$ map of the earlier purity-subtracted central value, and its comparisons with these
generators, are given in Ref.~\cite{AnalysisNote2026}, Secs.~\ref*{N-sec:eavailw-map} and~\ref*{N-sec:eavailw-band}.
