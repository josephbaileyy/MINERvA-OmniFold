**(D) Recommendation**

**Major revision.** The presentation round did not change the recommendation. It improved readability and disclosure, but the unvalidated coverage, the failed covariance consistency check, and the prior dependence remain.

Top three remaining issues:
1. The covariance is unvalidated and failed its own pseudo-experiment consistency check. The training-seed block is zero while rounding-level input changes move results by up to 2.6 σ_stat.
2. Evidence for prior retention and regularization (IV E) comes from a non-reported development variant in a noise-free setting. The fake-data failures (GiBUU hybrid, q₃ tilt) are shown for the reported estimator, with no link between the two.
3. The methodological choices were made with the published result in view: learner, 2D background treatment and iteration count. The paper discloses part of this. It also contains a "fixed in advance" claim unsupported in the body, and a Table I flagging statement inconsistent with its own table.
