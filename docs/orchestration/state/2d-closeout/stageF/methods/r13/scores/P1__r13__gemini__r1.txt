RATING: 3
JUSTIFICATION: Although grammatically meticulous and scientifically precise, the manuscript frequently lapses into the register of an internal analysis note or defensive audit log, detailing chronological development history, discarded intermediate methods, and post-hoc procedural justifications rather than presenting direct, journal-ready conclusions.

1. QUOTE: "The test design was fixed before any observed statistic was computed, although its reviewers had already seen descriptive per-cell residuals of the data. The calibration’s computing budget was later extended by a bounded amount with observed p values visible; the error control above permits this, because it holds for any stopping rule (Ref. [14], Sec. 10.3). The pseudo-experiments that computing time limits interrupted or prevented (224 for calibration, 53 for power) were rerun; none reaches the observed statistic in any test or shifted null, so no assignment of the lost outcomes changes any decision (Ref. [14], Sec. 10.6)."
   REWRITE: The test protocol and rejection thresholds were defined prior to unblinding the test statistics. Because the decision rule controls the familywise error rate under arbitrary sequential stopping, extending the pseudo-experiment sample and completing interrupted batches does not inflate the 5% error rate.

2. QUOTE: "Two earlier versions of the statistical band, distinct from the quoted block, were scored on 100 closure pseudo-experiments at a fixed truth (Ref. [14], Sec. 5.2). The first undercovered in its tails: pooled over experiments and bins, 91.2 % of standardized residuals fell within two standard deviations, against 95.4 % nominal. Rescoring the same pseudo-experiments against the rebuilt band removes the tail undercoverage: 97.7 % fall within two standard deviations and 78.1 % within one. This rescoring is not an independent coverage test, because the simulated part of the rebuilt band reuses the resampling mechanism of those pseudo-experiments."
   REWRITE: In 100 closure pseudo-experiments evaluated at nominal truth, 97.7% of standardized residuals fall within two standard deviations and 78.1% within one; however, because the covariance model and pseudo-experiments share the same resampling procedure, this check does not constitute an independent test of coverage.

3. QUOTE: "This learner was chosen after both comparisons were known, so the benchmark is a reproduction rather than an independent validation. The two learners differ by a per-bin median of 0.98 % (maximum 12.2 %), a dependence included in no term of the covariance."
   REWRITE: Because the LightGBM implementation was selected after comparison with data, this benchmark serves as a cross-check rather than an independent validation; differences between the LightGBM and exact-split implementations exhibit a per-bin median of 0.98% (up to 12.2%), which is not included in the systematic covariance.
