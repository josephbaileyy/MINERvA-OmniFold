Q2. [paper_body.tex PB123]
BEFORE: The median per-cell relative uncertainty is \fvval{sigmed-C_total_resp}\%, and \fvval{sigmed-C_total}\% without $C_{\mathrm{resp}}$. The note gives the cells, the central values, the per-group uncertainties and the correlation matrix of the reported covariance.
AFTER: The run-period block $C_{\mathrm{PL,5D}}$ accounts for an inconsistency among the three run-period groups, whose unfolded results, each relative to its own simulation, differ by more than their bootstrap scatter. The cause is known. As for the two-dimensional result (Sec.~\ref{sec:uq2d}), the block is built from the double ratios among the groups, with no reference period; it has rank \fvval{pl-rank}, and with three groups its size is poorly determined. It was added after these differences were seen, following an audit whose stopping criterion was fixed in advance. Passing that audit does not validate the block: its size and coverage have not been independently validated. The median per-cell relative uncertainty is \fvval{sigmed-reported}\%, \fvval{sigmed-reported-nopl}\% without $C_{\mathrm{PL,5D}}$, and \fvval{sigmed-C_total}\% without $C_{\mathrm{PL,5D}}$ or $C_{\mathrm{resp}}$. The block raises the median by about \fvval{pl-medrise}\,\% and one cell's uncertainty, \fvval{pl-cellrise-maxcell}'s, by \fvval{pl-cellrise-max}\,\%. The fake-data listing (Sec.~\ref{sec:fd16}) and the generator comparison (Sec.~\ref{sec:gen16}) are given both with and without it; the note gives the per-cell uncertainties both ways too, with the block's construction and audit (\nsec{sec:fived16-pl}). Every $\chi^2$ of this result inverts the full covariance of the cells compared, $16\times16$ or $8\times8$, by Cholesky decomposition and without truncation, so its number of degrees of freedom is the number of cells (one fewer for the shape-only $\chi^2$). Of the blocks, the statistical block is the unbiased sample covariance of \fvval{s1-stat-replicas} bootstrap replicas, and the flux block a covariance of \fvval{fb-universes} weighted universes (effective number \fvval{s1-flux-neff}), normalized by the sum of the weights. The others are a 2p2h band, two-point shifts, chosen models, the normalization and the training-seed block, which is zero. No finite-ensemble correction is applied. The note gives each block's ensemble and normalization, the cells, the central values, the per-group uncertainties and the correlation matrix of the reported covariance (\nsec{sec:fived16-cov}).
