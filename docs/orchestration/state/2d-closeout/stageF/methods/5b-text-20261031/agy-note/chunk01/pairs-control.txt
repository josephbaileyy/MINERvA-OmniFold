The other bands and the lateral shifts are two-point shifts, the normalization is one outer product, $C_{\mathrm{model}}$ sums three chosen generator shifts, $C_{\mathrm{resp}}$ four $\pm1\sigma$ shifts, and $C_{\mathrm{PL,5D}}$ is built from three exposure-weighted periods; the training-seed block is zero. Each comparison with a prediction adds $V_g$, its diagonal sum of squared event weights, and each fake-data comparison the variance of the mean over its pseudo-experiments. No finite-ensemble correction is applied to any block. The standard correction is derived for a single unweighted sample covariance, which none of these sums is; the flux bootstrap below measures the scatter of the $\chi^2$ from estimating the flux block, and is a correction. \subsection{The run-period block} \label{sec:fived16-pl} Data from the three run-period groups (playlists 1A--1F, 1G--1M and 1N--1P) are not mutually consistent on the sixteen cells within the other uncertainties: each group's unfolded result over its own simulated truth (the double ratio) differs from the others by more than the scatter of their bootstrap replicas. The cause is not known, and these data cannot establish which period, if any, is right. As for the two-dimensional result (\S\ref{sec:uq-combine}, Table~\ref{tab:pl-added}), the block $C_{\mathrm{PL,5D}}$ is built from these double ratios with no reference period: each group's offset from the exposure-weighted mean of the three, applied to the central value, with a reliability-weight factor for the weighted spread. It has rank \fvval{pl-rank} and is not debiased for the replica noise it contains. With three groups its own size is poorly determined: the 68\,\% interval on the ratio of its true to its estimated $\sigma$ is \fvval{pl-sigratio-lo}--\fvval{pl-sigratio-hi}, and in cells \fvval{pl-noise-cells} it is dominated by that noise. The block was chosen after the period differences had been seen. Before it was added, an audit tested its overlap with the existing blocks, especially the flux and detector-response ones; its noise treatment, with ten bootstrap replicas per period; and the stability of its size under resampling. The audit's stopping condition was fixed before any of its numbers were read, and it did not fire. The addition therefore followed the data, and passing the audit does not validate the block: built from the observed differences, it contains them by construction, and its size and coverage have not been independently validated. The per-cell uncertainties, the fake-data tests and the generator comparison are given here with and without it. Added to $C_{\mathrm{total}}+C_{\mathrm{resp}}$, the block raises the median per-cell relative $\sigma$ from \fvval{sigmed-C_total_resp}\,\% to \fvval{sigmed-reported}\,\%, by about \fvval{pl-medrise}\,\%. The median of the cells' own increases is \fvval{pl-cellrise-med}\,\%, and the largest is \fvval{pl-cellrise-max}\,\%, in \fvval{pl-cellrise-maxcell}. The block's median per-cell relative $\sigma$ is \fvval{sigmed-C_PL5D}\,\%, at most \fvval{sigmax-C_PL5D}\,\%, and its share of the reported variance has a median of \fvval{pl-share-med}\,\% over the cells and a maximum of \fvval{pl-share-max}\,\%. With the block, each prediction's total $\chi^2$ is lower by \fvval{pl-chi2fall-total-lo}--\fvval{pl-chi2fall-total-hi}\,\% of its value without it, and each shape-only $\chi^2$ by \fvval{pl-chi2fall-shape_only-lo}--\fvval{pl-chi2fall-shape_only-hi}\,\% (Table~\ref{tab:fived16-gen}). These falls are larger than the change in any cell's $\sigma$; we infer that the predictions' residuals have components along the directions the rank-\fvval{pl-rank} block adds, an interpretation the comparison does not test. Under the reported covariance one fake-data truth fewer is listed (\S\ref{sec:fived16-fd}), with the bias unchanged. \subsection{Fake-data tests of the estimator's bias} \label{sec:fived16-fd} The fake-data tests compare the estimator's bias at truths other than the simulation with the reported covariance.
