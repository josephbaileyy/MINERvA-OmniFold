BEFORE:
\paragraph{Ruterbories 2021, Section V.} Three uncertainty categories --- flux, detector response, neutrino-interaction model --- evaluated by ``re-extracting the cross section using modified simulations'', i.e.\ the universe method, but with \emph{no covariance formula given}. Specifics quoted: flux below $4\%$ across most of phase space thanks to the $\nu$-$e$-scattering constraint (Valencia, arXiv:1906.00111); $1.4\%$ target-nucleon normalization; in-situ test-beam hadron response with per-particle GEANT inelastic variations; muon energy scale $1\%$ constrained by the low-recoil fit of Bashyal (JINST 16 P08068), with the explicit statement that \emph{``the fit causes the flux and muon energy scale uncertainties to be correlated and that correlation is propagated to the final result''} --- the only non-block-diagonal piece the paper admits. The only covariance in its equations is $V$ in the model-test $\chi^{2}$ (Eq.~1, ``the measurement covariance matrix''), with $205$ degrees of freedom (Table I), plus a log-normal $\chi^{2}$ variant motivated by the highly-correlated flux normalization (Peelle's Pertinent Puzzle). \emph{What the paper does not say.} It gives no per-band covariance construction, no $\pm1\sigma$ pair formula and no band-independence or block-sum statement. It says nothing about the rank, condition number or regularization of $V$, and it reports no bootstrap or coverage validation (it uses D'Agostini iterative unfolding). All of this is left to MAT or unstated.

\paragraph{MAT per band and in total.} \texttt{MnvVertErrorBand::CalcCovMx} implements Eq.~\eqref{eq:matcov}, referenced to the universe mean $\langle x\rangle_{u}$ rather than $X_{\CV}$, normalized by biased $1/N$ rather than Bessel's $1/(N-1)$ (a $1\%$ offset for PPFX, $N=100$), with no special case for $N=2$. The folk ``MINERvA-101 $\pm1\sigma$ pair'' formula $\tfrac12(\boldsymbol{\Delta}_{+}\boldsymbol{\Delta}_{+}\T + \boldsymbol{\Delta}_{-}\boldsymbol{\Delta}_{-}\T)$ agrees only for CV-symmetric shifts. The quoted universe block, filled into a \texttt{PlotUtils::MnvH1D} with MAT's universe weights on Flux, agrees with \texttt{MnvH1D::GetTotalErrorMatrix} to $1.3\times10^{-16}$ of its largest element; the quoted Flux band carries MAT's $\nu$--$e$ and inverse-muon-decay constraint weights. An earlier check compared \texttt{MnvH1D::GetTotalErrorMatrix} with our band sum without the normalization band (\texttt{-{}-add-norm 0}), over $44$ bands / $187$ universes, and gave a maximum difference of $5.5\times10^{-17}$ of the largest element (machine $\varepsilon$) and $\sqrt{\Tr\mathbf{C}}$ matching to seven digits. That check filled the \texttt{MnvH1D} without MAT's per-universe weights (\texttt{SetUnivWgts}), so both sides were unweighted. It also ran on an older sweep from before the flux fix, not on a quoted product. It therefore could not detect that the Flux band omitted those constraint weights.

\paragraph{Bootstrap/paper-stat double-count.} The combined distance (\S\ref{sec:chisq}) adds $\mathbf{C}^{\boot}$ to \texttt{TotalCov}, double-counting the paper's included statistical block. Subtracting that block uses $\texttt{TotalCov}-\texttt{StatOnlyCov}$ as baseline before our additions, changing $\chi^{2}/\mathrm{ndf}$ $\chiCombined\to \chiCombinedSubStat$. It over-corrects: our $\sqrt{\Tr\mathbf{C}^{\boot}}=1.886\times10^{-40}$ is $0.407$ of the paper's $\sqrt{\Tr\,\texttt{StatOnlyCov}}=4.639\times10^{-40}$, leaving no comparable replacement. We retain double-counted $\chiCombined$ as the well-defined ``what does OmniFold UQ add on top of the published $V$'' distance; the subtraction result remains a secondary diagnostic.

\paragraph{Remaining refinements.} The known band-independence exception, the Bashyal flux\,$\leftrightarrow$\,Muon\_Energy\_MINOS joint block, has been rederived ($\rho=\rhoFluxMuon$). It preserves systematic rank because its directions already lie in the universe covariance's span (\S\ref{sec:uq-universes}). The quoted 2D covariance therefore remains the block sum, with the cross term as a diagnostic. A fitted $(\Phi_{\text{norm}},\Phi_{\text{shape}},\text{MuonE}_{\text{scale}})$ posterior from MAT-MINERvA could supply a paper-independent $\rho$. The collaboration uses the same truncated-spectral pseudo-inverse as the ours-only $\chi^{2}$ (private communication), so the convention is collaboration practice, not an invention of this analysis. No published reference for it has been identified.

BEFORE:
\paragraph{A methodological component (estimator and iteration).} Changing the GBDT backend on the same data and normalization ($\Sigma\sigma$ within $0.1\%$) shifts paper-cov $\chi^{2}$ by $\sim1$ unit. The integrated agreement does not mean the bins agree: the LightGBM seed-1 and exact-split central values differ by a median of \SI{0.97}{\percent} per bin, $1.3$ statistical standard deviations (seed~42: \SI{0.98}{\percent}, 1.32; \S\ref{sec:method-config}). Iteration-count effects are negligible, \begin{center} \begin{tabular}{@{}lcc@{}} \toprule OmniFold configuration & paper-cov $\chi^{2}/\mathrm{ndf}$ & pull RMS \\ \midrule exact-split, 5 iter (cross-check; earlier central value) & $3.66$ & $0.598$ \\ HistGBT, 5 iter (10-seed mean) & $2.70\pm0.04$ & $0.56$ \\ LightGBM, 5 iter, seed 42, refined subtraction (quoted) & $2.270$ & $0.535$ \\ LightGBM, 5 iter, seed 42 & $2.654$ & $0.564$ \\ LightGBM, 5 iter, seed 1 & $2.65$ & $0.563$ \\ LightGBM, 10 iter, seed 1 & $2.66$ & $0.565$ \\ \bottomrule \end{tabular} \end{center} All rows except the quoted one use the purity-based subtraction. (i) \textbf{Iteration count is negligible}: LightGBM $5\to10$ iters moves the $\chi^{2}$ by $+0.01$ ($2.645\to2.657$), so OmniFold is well-converged at $5$ iters and the tension is \emph{not} an under-iteration artifact. (ii) \textbf{The GBDT estimator carries a $\sim1$-unit band}: the exact-split cross-check ($3.66$) is the outlier, while both histogram-binned backends agree at $2.65$--$2.70$ (HistGBT is a $10$-seed mean, range $2.63$--$2.74$; the $\pm0.04$ ML-seed jitter is $\sim25\times$ smaller than the $0.96$ exact$\to$hist gap, so that gap is genuinely the estimator, not seed noise). The exact-gradient trees fit finer shape structure (less implicit smoothing) and so deviate more from the paper's IBU-regularized result. This is direct evidence that $\sim1$ $\chi^{2}$-unit of the tension is OmniFold's implicit regularization rather than a physical data--MC disagreement.
