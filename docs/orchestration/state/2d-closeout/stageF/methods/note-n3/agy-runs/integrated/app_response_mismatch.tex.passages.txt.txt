=== PASSAGE AD-1 (app_response_mismatch.tex) ===
BEFORE:
\paragraph{Reconstruction-only test implemented in the pipeline.} The first-stage diagnostic uses the ordinary five-dimensional closure sample. After copying pseudo-data arrays, before background mixing or classifier training, it changes only the reconstructed pseudo-data record \begin{equation} \Eavail^{\mathrm{pseudo}} \longrightarrow (1+\epsilon)\Eavail^{\mathrm{pseudo}} . \end{equation} The nominal reconstructed simulation, all truth coordinates, event weights, and event membership stay fixed. The pseudo-data therefore have a deliberately incompatible conditional response; OmniFold still uses the nominal response. The protocol requires both signs of $\epsilon$, at an amplitude fixed before the results are examined. A documented hadronic-response variation or a quantified assumption should justify that amplitude; the inherited factor in reconstructed $\Eavail$ is not enough on its own.

Outputs include the full five-dimensional unfolded/reference residual, one-dimensional $\Eavail$ and $W$ projections, and an $(\Eavail,W)$ ratio map. The primary observable is signed bias versus $W$, reporting $W<\SI{1.1}{GeV}$ separately from the high-$W$ tail. The global integral is a control rather than the decision statistic. The implementation runs only as a closure on pseudo-data and only on the five observables, and it cannot be combined with truth-reweight injection or bootstrapping. It labels every output as a diagnostic that must not be quoted.

\paragraph{Physically correlated follow-up.} The reconstruction-only arm is intentionally cheap and adversarial; it is not a detector model. A more faithful follow-up would select quasielastic events, shift leading-nucleon energy up and down, and recompute reconstructed $\Eavail$, $q_{3}$, $W$, and any affected selection from the altered low-level record. The current scalar event-loop outputs lack the information for that recomputation, so this arm needs dedicated diagnostic output from the event loop. It is deferred unless the reconstruction-only test shows material bias or the collaboration asks for the stronger response model.
