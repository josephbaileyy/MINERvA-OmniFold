\subsection{Closure: the one injected $\Eavail$ deformation is recovered} \label{sec:3d-closure} A reweighting closure tests the 3D unfolding on one injected deformation. It tests configuration~(a) only. The configuration-(a) unfolding recovers a known $+\SI{30}{\percent}$ Gaussian bump injected into pseudo-data in \emph{truth} $\Eavail$ (center \SI{0.3}{GeV}, width \SI{0.15}{GeV}). The recovered step-2 mean weight is $1.048$ against the injected $1.0485$. Residuals against reweighted truth on the $\Eavail$-marginal have median $0.9999$ and standard deviation \textbf{\num{0.0006}} (\SI{0.06}{\percent}); the per-3D-bin residual standard deviation is \SI{3.2}{\percent} from MC statistics in sparse high-recoil corners. The closure's marginal scatter is far below the $\SI{3.7}{\percent}$ data-versus-2D scatter. The two belong to different configurations, however: the closure to (a), the data comparison to (e) against (f). Their ratio is therefore not quoted. Total normalization matches to better than \SI{1}{\percent}. In the metric of \S\ref{sec:validation} ($\Eavail$ projection) the residual is \SI{1.84}{\percent} vs \SI{18.1}{\percent} injected (ratio $0.102$). This residual is not graded, because no acceptance threshold fixed before the test is on record.

\paragraph{Flux repair of the GENIE and NuWro predictions.} Except in three studies that keep the unrepaired samples, the GENIE and NuWro predictions in \S\S\ref{sec:3d}--\ref{sec:eavailw} are repaired by exact per-event reweighting to the analysis PPFX central-value flux on 0--\SI{100}{GeV}, with correctly sampled 50--\SI{100}{GeV} supplements. The three studies, each of which says so where it appears, are the FSI-dial studies, the departure ratio of \S\ref{sec:val-negweight} and the NuWro-shaped prior of \S\ref{sec:fps}. The repair corrects the flux from which the GENIE (CV and $+$Valencia~2p2h) and NuWro samples were generated. That flux had no PPFX weights, was capped at \SI{50}{GeV}, and stored flux \emph{densities} in variable-width bins that were sampled and normalized as \emph{contents}, which under-weighted every wide high-energy bin and suppressed the high-$\pPar$ predictions and the integrated rates (Ref.~\cite{NoteRecords2026}, \recentry{flux-repair}). GiBUU reads its own flux and receives an energy-dependent per-event reweight to the same convention, which changes its integrated cross section by a factor $0.9966$. Its sample stops at $E_\nu\approx\SI{20}{GeV}$, so it is empty above $\pPar\approx\SI{20}{GeV/c}$. The comparisons here and in \S\ref{sec:eavailw-band} are made with unchanged figure code from the repaired inputs. The analysis simulation, MINERvA Tune~v1, is not affected.

BEFORE:
\paragraph{No 4D uncertainty is reported.} No 4D uncertainty magnitude, and no ratio of the unified-throw to the block covariance, is reported. A corrected 4D covariance would use the actual minus and plus variation endpoints; MAT's mean-centered covariance blocks, normalized by $1/N$; one fixed estimator seed; and mean-centered joint throws, with the mean shift reported separately. It would not subtract a scalar jitter floor. Because the single-pass shifted observables exist only for events selected at the central value, re-selected event loops (\S\ref{sec:uq-higherdim}) would be needed to bound migration effects.
