\subsection{ML/optimization uncertainty: training-seed variation} \label{sec:uq-ml} The ML block is the sample covariance, over five training-seed settings, of the mean of 20 bootstrap replicas at each setting. Each setting is a triplet $(s,s{+}1,s{+}2)$ of seeds for the step-1 classifier, step-2 classifier and miss regressor, with $s\in\{42,45,48,51,54\}$; the block has rank~4 (\S\ref{sec:seedscan}). It is the smallest block, with median \SI{0.048}{\percent} per bin. The block measures LightGBM seed variation, with the bootstrap\,$\times$\,seed interaction at its $1/20$ share. With four degrees of freedom, its variance has a Gaussian relative standard error of about \SI{71}{\percent}. Its per-bin $\sigma$ is a median 0.27 of that of the earlier ten-seed block. The reason is that the bootstrap\,$\times$\,seed interaction, which dominated the earlier scan, is already inside the fixed-seed statistical block; this block carries it only at its $1/20$ share, a median 0.763 of its diagonal. The interaction itself (a median 0.072 of the statistical variance) and the universe\,$\times$\,retraining term (\S\ref{sec:uq-2d-scope}) are reported and not added.

No block contains the dependence on the learner. The central value and the exact-split cross-check differ by a per-bin median of \SI{1.03}{\percent} (maximum \SI{17.6}{\percent}; \S\ref{sec:method-config}); they differ in background treatment as well as in learner. For comparison, the mean of the earlier ten-seed LightGBM scan (Ref.~\cite{NoteRecords2026}, superseded values: retired material) differs from the cross-check by a median of \SI{0.99}{\percent} per bin. One reseeded exact-split run differs from the unseeded one by a per-bin median of \SI{0.18}{\percent} (maximum \SI{1.6}{\percent}). The spread of the exact-split estimator over seeds is unmeasured (\S\ref{sec:method-config}).

\subsection{Further checks of the 2D covariance} \label{sec:uq-2d-construction} The quoted universe block uses the July sweep. Seven of its universes are reproduced to $\le1.4\times10^{-11}$ by the committed driver; the others are accepted by inference, because the same code produced them (Ref.~\cite{NoteRecords2026}, \recentry{july-sweep}).

The run-period block $C_{\mathrm{PL}}$ is an uncertainty assigned for an observed inconsistency between run periods whose cause is unknown; it is not a measured systematic uncertainty from an identified source. Subsets of the data taken in different run periods are not mutually consistent within the other uncertainties: after unfolding, the double ratio (data over simulated truth) of playlists 1G--1M to the earliest periods, 1A--1F, has a $\pPar$ step of $(+6.89\pm0.46)\%$ at \SI{8}{GeV/c}. Diluted into the combined result, the raw shift of those playlists is small bin by bin, with every marginal $z$ below 0.7, but its $\pPar$ $\chi^{2}$ against the earlier budget is 34.4, where noise would give 2.0. The cause is not known, and these data cannot establish which period, if any, is correct.

\paragraph{Seed-related terms reported, not added.} Two seed-related terms are reported and not added to the covariance. The bootstrap\,$\times$\,seed interaction is a median 0.072 of the statistical variance; the training-seed block includes it at its $1/20$ share. For the universe\,$\times$\,retraining term, in the six non-null universes tested (of 187), the seed standard deviation of each universe's shift is a median 1.08--1.36 times the $\sigma$ of the earlier ten-seed block, with four degrees of freedom each. In those universes the universe block, built at one training seed, therefore carries single-seed training noise of about the earlier seed block's size; it is part of the block as built and is not separated from the physical variation. In two of them the noise is comparable to or larger than the shift: in \texttt{2p2h:0} the shift is \SI{0.097}{\percent} against a seed standard deviation of \SI{0.192}{\percent}, and in \texttt{MaCCQE:0} \SI{0.239}{\percent} against \SI{0.210}{\percent} (per-bin medians). In expectation such noise adds variance to a band rather than removing it (inferred), and these two bands are largely training noise (inferred from the medians). The universe\,$\times$\,retraining values and the band-centering numbers were not recomputed independently: they cannot be recomputed from the saved covariance products, and the verification compared only the checksums of their inputs.
