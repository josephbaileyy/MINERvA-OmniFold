BEFORE:
In central values, Valencia 2p2h closes about half of the low-recoil gap (\S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h}) but leaves a separate excess over the MINERvA Tune~v1 prior at high $\Eavail$. There 2p2h contributes nothing, and the sub-percent pion-FSI dials cannot reach the excess. The qualifications of the covariance are given in \S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}. The comparison with external generators depends on the generator. GiBUU is low throughout the $\Eavail$ tail: its $W$-integrated five-dimensional data/generator ratio, in configuration~(a), is $1.59$ at $\Eavail\in[0.8,1.5)$ and $1.37$ at $[1.5,3.0)$. Section~\ref{sec:eavailw-band} discusses its $E_\nu\approx\SI{20}{GeV}$ limit. GENIE CV instead matches $[0.8,1.5)$ and is low in $[1.5,3.0)$, with a three-dimensional data/CV ratio, in configuration~(e), of $1.10$ there (\S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h}). Adding the hadronic invariant mass $W$ as the fifth OmniFold axis maps where in $W$ the high-$\Eavail$ excess lies. The validation of \S\ref{sec:val-fiveaxis} includes a model-dependence study, which establishes no bias--variance tradeoff and no new total uncertainty for this projection.

=== PASSAGE EW-2 (sec_eavailw.tex) ===
BEFORE:
Like $q_{3}$ (\S\ref{sec:4d}), and unlike $\Eavail$, $W$ changes under the lateral systematic shifts, so a shifted $W$ is stored for each lateral universe. The event-loop output files also store truth hadronic diagnostics: the proton and pion multiplicities and the leading-hadron angle. The $W$ edges $[0,1.1,1.4,1.8,2.2,3.0,100]\,\si{GeV}$ bracket the quasielastic peak, the $\Delta$, the transition region and the DIS tail. The five-dimensional unfolding in $(\pT,\pPar,\Eavail,q_{3},W)$ passes the marginalization checks against the lower-dimensional results: integrating over $W$ recovers the configuration-(a) 4D normalization to \SI{\eavailMargin}{\percent} (5D/4D total $=1.0011$), with median differences of \SIrange{0.3}{1.5}{\percent} per axis, and $d\sigma/dW$ is finite and non-negative in all six bins.
