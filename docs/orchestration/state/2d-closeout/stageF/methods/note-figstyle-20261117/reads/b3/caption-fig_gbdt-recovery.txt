Noise-free 5D recovery: historical truths, fixed simulated response. Solid/dashed OmniFold curves: 100 trees/8 leaves and 400/31. Iteration maxima: D1/W1 40, $q_3$ 30, W3 200, capacity probes 10. Some baseline traces end at their last saved checkpoint. Top: median $|\hat f/f_{\rm true}-1|$ over supported cells. Bottom: residual proxy $\operatorname{median}(|\hat f/f_{\rm true}-1|/|f_{\rm true}/f_0-1|)$ for departures above 1\%, dotted bound 0.25. Different denominators prevent reading one minus this proxy as an exact recovered fraction. The $q_3$ stress has no eligible EW departure. These traces measure no repeat variance.
