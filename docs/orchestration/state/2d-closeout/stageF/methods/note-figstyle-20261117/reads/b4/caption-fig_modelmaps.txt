Our result and the published data show the same tune deficit, with ratios above unity over most of the plane and below it at the lowest $\pT$. The maps are close because both results depart from the tune in the same places by similar amounts. Shown: ratios to MINERvA Tune~v1 on the $(\pT,\pPar)$ grid, (a, left)~our result and (b, right)~published data, on a common color scale of $0.7$--$1.3$. The direct comparison is the median bin ratio of Table~\ref{tab:compare} and the residual map of Fig.~\ref{fig:pull}.
