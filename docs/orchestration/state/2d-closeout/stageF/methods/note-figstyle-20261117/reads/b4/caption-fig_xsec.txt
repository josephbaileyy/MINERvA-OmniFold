The unfolded 2D cross section and its projections have the shapes expected for few-GeV CC-inclusive scattering. Bars: weight statistics only, not the covariance. Unfolded-point bars show reweighted-MC weight statistics: square root of summed squared event weights, combined in quadrature with the propagated completeness-ratio bin error (ratio one by construction). These represent neither the data's statistical uncertainty nor the reported covariance. Top: the 2D cross section, in the format of Fig.~13 of Ref.~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq}; bottom: the $\pT$ and $\pPar$ projections. Published-data bars (top) show square roots of the statistical-covariance diagonal. Horizontal bars span bins.
