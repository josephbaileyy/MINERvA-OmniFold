The unfolded cross section compared with the prior, GENIE 2.12.6 $+$ MINERvA Tune~v1 simulated truth; no published measurement is used. Bars: weight statistics only, not the covariance (Fig.~\ref{fig:xsec}); they alone enter the descriptive panel $\chi^{2}$, and they represent neither the data's statistical uncertainty nor the reported covariance. Shown: the unfolded cross section (points) and the simulated truth (histogram), sliced in $\pT$ versus $\pPar$. The analysis-MC truth denominator uses the unfolded result's flux, nucleon-count and efficiency normalization. Only the panel format follows Fig.~13 of Ref.~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq}. Published-data comparisons are in Figs.~\ref{fig:pull} and~\ref{fig:model} and Table~\ref{tab:compare}.
