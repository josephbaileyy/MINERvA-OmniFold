Historical W3 at five iterations. Horizontal gives minus the noise-free relative truth residual; vertical gives the relative shift in real-data $R$ when its MC prior changes to W3, normalized to CV-prior results. Points are supported cells, not independent repeated experiments. The diagonal marks equality. Strong correlation supplies no per-cell bound; shifts do not measure unknown real-data bias.
