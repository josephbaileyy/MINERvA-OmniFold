fig:gbdt-bias-spread (sec_5d.tex)
Estimator $R$ at five iterations. Each supported J or H2 cell gives the mean and SD of $(\hat f-f_{\rm true})/f_{\rm true}$ in the same background-inclusive ensemble: 40 nominal experiments, 20 each for W2 and W3. Bars give pointwise 95\% Student-$t$ intervals for means and normal-model intervals for SDs, conditional on saved simulation. They supply neither simultaneous bounds nor total uncertainties. Truth panels and reporting cells form no frontier across estimator settings.
