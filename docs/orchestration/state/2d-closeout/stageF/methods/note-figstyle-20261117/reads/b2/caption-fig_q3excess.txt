4D data/Tune~v1 ratio (``data/GENIE'' in the figure) projected onto $(\Eavail,q_{3})$ (left; blank cells are kinematically empty), with $q_{3}$ (center) and $\Eavail$ (right) marginals. The $\Eavail$ marginal reproduces the 3D low-recoil excess. In the displayed joint plane the data/Tune~v1 ratio rises with $q_{3}$ across the quasielastic--$\Delta$ dip. Configuration~(a), with purity-based subtraction (\S\ref{sec:method-config}). Uncertainties are not drawn because no 4D covariance is quoted (\S\ref{sec:3d}).
