JUSTIFICATION: The prose is technically fluent, but repeated scope disclaimers, stacked qualifications, and procedural bookkeeping give several passages the register of an internal validation report rather than a well-edited PRD paper.
1. QUOTE: "The three- to five-observable outputs are central values, outputs of the estimators of Sec. III under the implemented observable definitions, simulated detector response, background treatment, and regularization; their intervals have no validated coverage, and the paper does not deliver a validated five-observable cross-section measurement."
   REWRITE: The three- to five-observable results are central estimates obtained with the observable definitions, detector simulation, background treatment, and regularization specified in Secs. II and III. Their interval coverage has not been validated, so they do not constitute a validated five-observable cross-section measurement.
2. QUOTE: "The pseudo-experiments that computing time limits interrupted or prevented (224 for calibration, 53 for power) were rerun; none reaches the observed statistic in any test or shifted null, so no assignment of the lost outcomes changes any decision (Ref. [14], Sec. 10.6)."
   REWRITE: Computing limits initially affected 224 calibration and 53 power pseudo-experiments. All were subsequently completed, with no exceedances of the observed statistics for any test or shifted null; any assignment of outcomes to these trials leaves the decisions unchanged (Ref. [14], Sec. 10.6).
3. QUOTE: "These target-corrected evaluations are descriptive and do not re-grade the decisions of Table IV, which concern each prediction on the target it was generated for and stand (Ref. [14], Sec. 10.9)."
   REWRITE: Table IV reports tests of predictions for their original targets. The target-corrected comparisons are descriptive and do not revise those test results (Ref. [14], Sec. 10.9).
