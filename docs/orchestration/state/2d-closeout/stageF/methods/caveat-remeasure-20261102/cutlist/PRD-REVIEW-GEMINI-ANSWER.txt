### 5. Overall Target (~6 caveats / 1,000 words)
**Yes, ~6 per 1,000 words is an appropriate, realistic target.** While published PRD neutrino measurements average 1.4–4.5, this paper presents a pioneering, high-dimensional machine-learning unfolding with significant known pathologies (regularization bias, run-period tensions, and unvalidated coverage). A higher caveat density than standard analyses is necessary to maintain experimental integrity; ~6/1k strikes the right balance between transparent peer-reviewed reporting and readable physics narrative.
