                Applied to MINERvA open data for medium-energy νµ charged-current inclusive interactions,
              OmniFold reproduces the published muon-kinematic cross section’s integral within about 1%, with
              a correlated shape difference. With five simultaneous observables (adding available energy, three-
              momentum transfer, and hadronic invariant mass), it yields central values only, whose normalizations
              agree with lower-dimensional results. Pseudo-experiments measure a background-subtraction bias, a
              partial recovery of generator-sized truth changes, and statistical undercoverage at the tested truths.
              With a model-dependence envelope added linearly to the uncertainty, no tested reporting definition
              meets our 10% precision criterion. As an illustration, simulation-calibrated tests of hybrid null
              hypotheses for five generator predictions all reject at the 5% familywise level, with no evidence of
              gain over matched coarse projections.
