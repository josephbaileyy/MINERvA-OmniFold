**3. Caveats and limitations**

Several qualifications must survive the reduction:

- **The new and old 5D products must be distinguished explicitly.** The current paper says no five-observable measurement with total uncertainties was constructed and no quoted uncertainty includes regularization bias. The addition introduces a different reporting product with model-dependence and hadronic-response terms. Those blanket statements need to become product-specific. Conversely, adding those terms does not establish validated coverage. The supplied addition does not identify its estimator configuration, so neither the old purity bias nor its removal can automatically be assigned to it.

- **The new fake-data failures belong beside the new inference.** The GiBUU-like failure must accompany the GiBUU comparison, as planned. The synthetic \(q_3\)-tilt failure also needs an explicit statement in the main uncertainty discussion. It is not adequately conveyed by a GiBUU-specific footnote. “Strongly disfavoured under the reported covariance” must remain distinguishable from a calibrated physical exclusion.

- **A covariance width, a bias envelope and empirical coverage are different quantities.** Approximately 7.3% median uncertainty in 16 cells does not contradict failure of an earlier envelope-plus-half-width criterion in other partitions. It also does not demonstrate that the earlier coverage problem has been solved.

- **The 2D benchmark remains a reproduction.** The data are shared with the publication, the uncertainty bands are correlated, the learner was selected after comparisons were known, and the current uncertainty has no independent coverage validation. Keep the unexplained shape discrepancy and period inconsistency.

- **The methodological failures have a defined scope.** State which estimator, truth deformation and reporting quantities each test concerns. The exact-binned diagnosis is signal-only, noise-free, response-fixed and restricted to a subset of residuals. It supports a diagnosis within that scope; it does not prove that unbinned information cannot help.

- **Definitions and comparison domains remain necessary.** Keep the non-independence of reconstructed \(W\), the operational available-energy definition, flux matching, target composition and GiBUU’s restricted support. A generator comparison needs matching observables and domains even when its detailed preparation studies move.

- **Do not retain stronger claims from relocated studies than their qualifications permit.** If the older excess map is mentioned, retain its descriptive status and unresolved bias/response limitations. If the all-ten-reject claim is retained despite my recommendation, its hybrid-null definition, missing response uncertainty, unproven grid adequacy and target sensitivity must remain too. That qualification burden is a principal reason to move the whole application.

Comparable papers do not generally establish coverage for every conceivable truth. The issue here is that relevant failures have actually been observed. Their consequences cannot be delegated entirely to a companion note.

**5. Estimated length**

**Approximately 7,000–8,000 words of substantive text and captions, including the 5D addition**, is a reasonable target.

Using the stated approximately 9,000-word baseline, I estimate removing or compressing roughly 3,000–3,500 words, chiefly from §VII and supporting diagnostics, and adding roughly 1,500–2,000 words for a self-contained 16-cell result, uncertainty construction, validation summary and captions. These are editorial estimates, not a counted rewrite. Bibliography and acknowledgments would be additional.

That leaves enough space for a substantial PRD methods paper whose main result includes both a useful data product and a clear account of where its inference remains limited.
