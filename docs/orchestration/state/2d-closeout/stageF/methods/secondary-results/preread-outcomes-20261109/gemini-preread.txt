### Separate Inquiries

#### A. Scoping When Coverage Passes
Even if the rebuilt covariance achieves nominal coverage at both tunes in both variants (AB1 / SC1), the scoping remains objectionable for four reasons:
1. **Incomplete Error Budget in Pseudo-Data:** Section 6.3 explicitly reveals that the like-for-like coverage pseudo-experiments do *not* draw the recoil-energy scale response term ($C_{\text{resp}}$) or the run-period term ($C_{\text{PL,5D}}$), and lateral uncertainties are handled via a linear surrogate. Testing coverage of a covariance against pseudo-data generated without major systematic variations does not validate the procedure's covariance.
2. **Static Covariance Rebuilding:** The procedure covariance is rebuilt once per truth rather than independently for each pseudo-experiment. This omits the sample variance of the covariance estimator itself.
3. **Lack of Model Independence:** Both test truths are GENIE v3 tunes sharing identical quasielastic models with each other and with Tune v1, and G18 shares its 2p2h model with $C_{\text{model}}$. Neither tune embodies the severe kinematic distortions where the estimator failed bias tests (GiBUU and $q_3$ tilt).
4. **2D vs. 5D Incoherence:** The manuscript establishes that the 2D procedure under-covers catastrophically at both tunes ($\chi^2/\text{ndf} = 16.55$ and $24.17$; 0 of 300 pseudo-experiments in the joint region). A passing 5D coverage test on identical tunes implies that coarse 16-cell binning or massive uncalibrated $C_{\text{model}}$ inflation is masking underlying miscoverage.

The text must explicitly state that the passing test validates only a restricted subset of uncertainties against closely related generator tunes.

#### B. Disclosure of Stoppages, Overrides, and Repairs in the Abstract
The Abstract **must** disclose when a test stopped, was repaired, or proceeded via an unblinded post-check override.
* Assigning Case AB1 to SC14 (where a pre-registered clipping-offset bound was exceeded and overridden post hoc) and SC15 (where the test stopped and was repaired post hoc) is unacceptable. It presents an unblinded, non-pre-registered protocol modification as a clean, pre-registered pass.
* In methods papers establishing validation protocols, the boundary between automated pre-registered gates and post-hoc human intervention is a headline finding. Presenting a repaired or overridden pass as an unqualified "covers" in the Abstract misleads the majority of readers who do not inspect the technical narrative of Section 6.3.

#### C. Mutual Consistency Across Abstract, Main Text, and Table 1
Several direct contradictions and reporting asymmetries exist among the cases:
1. **AB1 vs. SC14 / SC15:** SC14 and SC15 describe protocol violations (post-hoc override and post-hoc repair), yet the paper maps both to AB1, which asserts an unqualified coverage pass without mentioning any override or repair.
2. **Table 1 Row (AB2 / SC3) vs. SC3 / AB2:** In SC3 and AB2, the variant with detector hadron bands under-covers at one tune. However, Table 1 assigns this outcome the headline status **"Covers"**, directly contradicting the presence of cell-level under-coverage.
3. **Suppression of Secondary Variant in AB6, AB7, AB8, AB9:** While the main text (SC7–SC12) and Table 1 report outcomes for both variants (with and without hadron bands), Abstract cases AB6–AB9 omit the secondary variant entirely.
4. **Abstract Omission of the $q_3$ Tilt in C1–C4:** While Section 5.3 reports prior retention for both flagged truths (GiBUU hybrid and synthetic $q_3$ tilt), all Abstract cases (C1–C4) mention only the GiBUU-like truth.

#### D. Expected Outcomes Lacking Pre-Written Cases
A reader or referee would expect cases covering the following plausible outcomes:
1. **Primary Variant Under-Covers, Secondary Variant Covers:** Detector hadron-interaction bands add substantial variance. Adding them could easily elevate an under-covering cell or joint statistic into nominal coverage. No case exists for this standard statistical scenario.
2. **Mixed Outcomes Between Flagged Truths in C1–C4:** Cases C1–C4 assume identical behavior at both flagged truths. A split outcome—for example, implementation-specific retention at the GiBUU hybrid truth but an information limit at the synthetic $q_3$ tilt—has no pre-written text.
3. **Tune-Asymmetric Variant Invalidation:** A scenario where the primary variant is invalidated by controls at one tune while the secondary variant is valid (or vice versa) is completely omitted.
4. **Algorithmic Numerical Failure:** Execution halts during pseudo-experiment unfolding due to non-convergence, unbounded tree leaf weights, or NaN density ratios in LightGBM, as opposed to pre-set diagnostic gate stops.
