# Referee report on the pre-written cases

I judged only from the manuscript, Supplement and cases file, and I did not comment on bracketed placeholders.

## Shared points (cited as G1–G5 below)

- **G1. Meaning of "covers".** A pass can only mean that no under-coverage was detected at the test's threshold. The manuscript says "Agree" and "Differ" mean only "at this noise" for the IBU comparisons, but gives no such statement for coverage. No case says what degree of under-coverage this test could detect, or that a positive control was run. "Covers" should be worded as "no under-coverage detected".
- **G2. What was drawn.** The test does not draw the response (Cresp) or run-period terms, and uses a surrogate for the lateral terms. The pseudo-data for each group are built "as its covariance block is built". A pass therefore certifies that the propagation and estimator are consistent with the assumed block sizes, not that the sizes are right. No case says whether the undrawn terms are inside the covariance being tested. If they are, they inflate it and can hide under-coverage in the drawn terms.
- **G3. Per-cell flags.** These are pointwise, and there are 16 cells × 2 tunes × 2 variants. Section 6.2 gave a false-flag rate per tune, and the new cases give none. Cases that report cell flags also omit the joint reading.
- **G4. Scope clause.** The "not the reported intervals at the data" clause is attached to passes but missing from several failures (AB6, AB7, AB9, AB12). A failure at the tunes equally does not show that the reported intervals fail.
- **G5. Weak stress at the tunes.** Section 5.2 shows the bias at both tunes is below 0.62σ in every cell. The truths where bias exceeds uncertainty (GiBUU hybrid, q3 tilt) are not the ones tested. A pass here cannot speak to coverage where the estimator is known to be biased.

## A. If the coverage test passes, do I accept the scoping?

Not as written. The clause "not the reported intervals at the data" is necessary but not sufficient. I would still object that:
- G2 applies: undrawn terms, a surrogate, and nuisance draws built from the covariance's own blocks.
- G5 applies: a pass at near-unbiased truths says nothing at the flagged truths.
- Only two truths are tested, and they are not independent of Cmodel (they share a QE model, and G18 shares a 2p2h family).
- Power is not stated.
- The Discussion says "Until the coverage… resolved". It should say explicitly that a pass would not lift the illustration status.

## B. Should the abstract also say it?

- **SC14 and SC15: yes.** These claim a result that depends on a decision made after seeing the check. The abstract prints AB1, which is silent. The abstract already stresses "fixed before the tests were graded", so a reader will assume this test was too.
- **SC17: one clause, preferably.** No result is claimed, but SC14 shows the same exceedance led to a different discretionary choice. Say "by a decision made after the check".

## C. Consistency between abstract, main text and Table 1

- The abstract-to-case mapping is stated only for SC7–10, 14 and 15, so I inferred the rest. Please state it fully.
- Several abstracts lose a main-text qualifier:
  - AB2/AB3 lose "in cells".
  - AB6 loses the joint-only versus cell-only distinction.
  - AB1 loses the weakened passes and over-coverage of SC2.
  - The SC14, SC15 and SC17 disclosures are absent from the abstract, and AB12 is absent from its Table 1 row.
- Main-text failures carry the scope clause, but AB6, AB7, AB9 and AB12 do not.
- The Table 1 "Covers" status for AB3/SC4 contradicts a with-bands failure at both tunes.
- "GiBUU-like" means the GiBUU hybrid truth in the abstract and the GiBUU Eavail shape in the Discussion. The two should be disambiguated.

## D. Outcomes with no case

- **Variants differing in mixed ways.** The primary fails but the bands variant covers. Cell-only or joint-only failures that differ by tune or variant. Invalid in one variant only. A catch-all per-tune, per-variant sentence is needed.
- **Post-hoc modifiers on non-pass outcomes.** The proceeded-after-check and repaired-test prefixes exist only for all-covers, so a post-hoc proceed or repair that ends in a failure prints no disclosure. They should be modifiers applicable to every outcome.
- **Joint over-coverage.** Conservative intervals overall, with mean χ²/ndf well below 1.
- **Weakened passes with the bands or at the other tune.**
- **Pseudo-experiment count short of plan**, or a result in an indeterminate zone.
- **A failed positive control.**
- **IBU unresolved class.** The IBU difference straddles its bar, as in the 2D IBU comparison. Also the two truths giving different classes, and IBU non-convergence.
