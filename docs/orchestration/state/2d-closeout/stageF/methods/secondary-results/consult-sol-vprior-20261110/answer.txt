The direction is not universally inflation or deflation. With \(P\) the unchanged CH prior, write \(r=g_C/g_{CH}\). Then
\[

Because the spread is an RMS **about the prior**, raising a prediction can bring it nearer the prior or move it farther away. The relevant quantity is its squared departure, not merely the size of carbon/CH−1.
