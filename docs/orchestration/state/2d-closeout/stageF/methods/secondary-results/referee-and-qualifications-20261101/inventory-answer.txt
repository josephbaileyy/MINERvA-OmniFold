## 5. The honest answer, by topic

### 5.1 Interval coverage: why 1B stopped, and whether any validation is feasible

**What 1B was.** A nominal control plus two held-out GENIE v3 truths, funded at 44.5–145 node-h, with the control gating everything and "Its failure is a STOP" (`DEC-20261030-joseph-fund-1b-with-4a.md:25-30`).

**Why it stopped.**
- Gate D compares the toys' spread with S1's covariance blocks per cell and must lie in [0.9, 1.1]. ρ_c ran 0.696–2.296, with 2 cells below and 9 above, so 11 of 16 were outside (`FD-1B-STAGE2-REPORT.md:142`).
- By group the interaction median was 1.659 (range 0.418–6.521), flux 0.996 and detector 0.973 (`:146-150`).
- The interaction ratio is above 1 in 13 of 16 cells, opposite to the declaration's anticipated gap. "No other mechanism is offered" (`:178-185`).
- The free diagnosis failed both its tests. No set of ≤ 3 bands fixes R08 and R07 (`FD-1B-DIAG-REPORT.md:38-57`), and the uncarried alternative is in the window in only 4 of 16 cells (`:59-84`). So 1B stops (`:10-15`), as the pre-set fork required (`DEC-20261031-joseph-three-covariance…:46-56`).
- The stage 2 run cost 5.489 node-h against a cap of 23.00 (`FD-1B-STAGE2-REPORT.md:38`), about 4–12% of the funded range (arithmetic). A separate half-MC defect was found and repaired, and does not explain gate D (`:61-105`).

**What is and is not feasible.**
- **At the GiBUU-like and q3-tilt truths it is impossible by construction.** If a cell's bias exceeds σ, coverage is at most 0.5 for any symmetric error (`LP:115-122`). The plan lists R02, R06, R07, R10 and R15 for A7 GiBUU with mean 0.502 (`LP:135`). This was computed before the run-period block, so it is indicative only.
- **At the GENIE v3 truths a test is possible in principle.** The bias there is at most 0.644σ (`FD-F1-REPORT.md:35-40`), so coverage turns on spread against width (`LPA:66-68`).
- **The machinery is the obstacle.**
  - The 5D lateral dumps no longer exist, so that block is a surrogate (`FD-1B-DECLARATION.md:99`).
  - C_model and C_resp are not drawn (`:102-103`), and the control tests only 71.0% of C_e (`:106-108`).
  - No repair is named (`FD-1B-DIAG-REPORT.md:12`).
- **The only recorded independent design is nested.** It costs 6.04 node-h per experiment, about 1,800 node-h per arm (`LP:227-228`). That is about 87% of the 2,074.5 left and 1.7 times the half-of-remaining rule (arithmetic), so it needs the lead's decision.
- **A pass would be weak.**
  - Its fixed meaning is "no undercoverage detected at the study's sensitivity", "not a validation of coverage" (`FD-1B-DECLARATION.md:184-187`).
  - The per-cell standard error is 0.0269 (`:126`).
  - The test arms are expected to over-cover at zero bias, with mean 1σ coverage 0.791, because C_model is in the reported matrix but not in the toys (`LP:198-200`).
- **It would test the wrong matrix.** 1B tests the covariance without the run-period block (`FD-1B-STAGE1-REPORT.md:17-19`; `DEC-20261030-joseph-4b…:53-54`), but the reported one now includes it (`PB:80`).
- **The cheap stand-ins are descriptive only.** F0's offset control failed at χ²₁₆ = 245.74 and A7's draws differ from S1's (`LPA:339-342`).

**Conclusion.** "The coverage … has not been validated" holds in every outcome (`DEC-20261031-joseph-three-covariance…:67-69`). The best case is a narrowed statement at GENIE-family truths, for 1,800 node-h or more and weeks of development. It would still not cover GiBUU-like truths, the run-period block or the detector response. I rate a clean "validated" at essentially zero (my judgement).
