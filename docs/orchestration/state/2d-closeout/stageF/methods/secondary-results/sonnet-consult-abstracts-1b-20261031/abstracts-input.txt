PAPER ABSTRACT, OLD:
We report a simultaneous measurement of the νµ charged-current inclusive cross section in five observables, the muon transverse and longitudinal momenta, available hadronic energy, three-momentum transfer and hadronic invariant mass, unfolded without binning with OmniFold from MINERvA medium-energy open data and reported in sixteen cells with a covariance whose median per-cell uncertainty is 7.29%. Three external generator predictions, compared on all sixteen cells, give χ2 between 109 and 272 for 16 degrees of freedom. GiBUU, compared only on the 8 cells its sample covers, gives χ2 = 58 on those cells. In a fake-data study, the reported uncertainty does not cover the estimator's bias at GiBUU-like truths or at a synthetic q3 tilt. The same unfolding reproduces the integral of the published muon-kinematic cross section within about 1%, with a correlated shape difference.

NOTE ABSTRACT, NEW:
We report a simultaneous measurement of the νµ charged-current inclusive cross section in five observables: the muon transverse and longitudinal momenta, available hadronic energy, three-momentum transfer and hadronic invariant mass. The cross section is unfolded without binning with OmniFold from MINERvA medium-energy open data. It is reported in sixteen cells, with a covariance whose median per-cell uncertainty is 7.86%. Three external generator predictions, compared on all sixteen cells, give χ2 between 105 and 224 for 16 degrees of freedom. GiBUU, compared only on the 8 cells its sample covers, gives χ2 = 45 on those cells. The coverage of the result's intervals has not been validated; a bias test at two held-out GENIE v3 truths finds the estimator's bias below the reported uncertainty in every cell but does not test interval widths. In a fake-data study, the estimator's bias exceeds the thresholds set against the reported uncertainty at a GiBUU-like truth and at a synthetic q3 tilt.
