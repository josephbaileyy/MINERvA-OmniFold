We benchmark simultaneous unbinned unfolding in five observables with OmniFold on MINERvA open data. A descriptive two-observable benchmark agrees with the published normalization but differs in shape. OmniFold differs from binned iterative unfolding on shared inputs in both the benchmark and five-observable results. The five-observable estimator retains part of its prior.
