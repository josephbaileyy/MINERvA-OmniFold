Abstract
This note documents an unbinned, simultaneous unfolding of MINERvA medium-energy νµ
charged-current inclusive open data with OmniFold, and its validation. Its principal result is a
cross section in five observables, muon transverse and longitudinal momentum, available energy
(Eavail ), three-momentum transfer (q3 ) and hadronic invariant mass (W ), in sixteen cells, with a
covariance of statistical, flux, interaction, detector, lateral, normalization, generator-shape and
calorimetric-response terms; its median per-cell uncertainty is 7.29%. Three external generator
predictions compared on all sixteen cells give χ2 between 109 and 272 for 16 degrees of freedom,
and GiBUU, on its 8 cells, 58. The coverage of the result’s intervals has not been validated. In
a fake-data study, the reported uncertainty does not cover the estimator’s bias at GiBUU-like
truths or at a synthetic q3 tilt. The sixteen-cell χ2 values fall by 24–48% when only the flux
correlations are weakened by 10%. The note also gives the reproduction of the published doubledifferential cross section in muon momentum, whose integral agrees within about one percent,
with a correlated shape difference and a covariance that has not been independently validated;
three- and four-observable central values; and an earlier purity-subtracted five-observable central
value on a finer grid, whose covariance does not meet its specification’s acceptance criteria and
whose quoted uncertainties omit that estimator’s background bias of up to 4% at high W and
its regularization bias. The validation studies show that negative-weight background subtraction
removes most of the purity method’s bias, that generator-sized truth changes are recovered only
in part, a residual interpreted as regularization bias, that statistical intervals of a deterministic
purity-subtracted variant miscover at the tested truths, and that no tested reporting definition
meets a predeclared 10% precision criterion. Joint tests calibrated in simulation reject hybrid
null hypotheses for five generator predictions at a 5% family-wise error rate; they are not physical
exclusions, show no evidence of discrimination beyond matched coarse projections, omit an
uncertainty on the calorimetric recoil response and assume an unshown convergence of the fine
grid on which the null hypotheses are defined, and NuWro’s total-test rejection is not robust to
a hydrocarbon-target correction.

Executive summary
We apply unbinned machine-learning unfolding (OmniFold [1, 2]) to MINERvA’s medium-energy
neutrino data. The main results are the validation findings. They hold for this implementation, one
data set, and the configurations, observables and deformations tested (§1). The generator tests are
an application that illustrates calibrated inference with a biased estimator.
Results overview:
• Reproduction. Unbinned d2 σ/(dpT dp∥ ) reproduces the published integrated cross section [3]
within about one percent, with a correlated shape difference. The total cross-section ratio is
1.010, and 95.1 % of bins are within 10 %. A separately constructed uncertainty is comparable
to the published one: its per-bin median is 6.41 % against 6.85 %, and its integral 4.76 %
against 4.61 %. Its flux band carries MAT’s ν–e and inverse-muon-decay constraint weights
(§6.3). A generator-shape model-dependence component, reported beside that uncertainty and
absent from the published one, raises the per-bin median to 7.90 % (§4.6). The central value
and that uncertainty share the LightGBM estimator, with refined negative-weight background
subtraction. LightGBM was chosen over the exact-split estimator, which is kept as a cross-check,
after both comparisons with the publication were known (§3.2). Against the full published
covariance, χ2 /ndf = 2.27 (exact-split: 3.66) is a descriptive distance. Its source has not been
identified (§6.4).
• Unbinned 3D/4D/5D MINERvA unfolds. Adding Eavail , q3 and W gives simultaneous
unfolds. In configuration (a), the 4D and 5D marginal-normalization checks against the
lower-dimensional result pass to ≈1–2 %. The configuration-(e) 3D marginal agrees with the
configuration-(f) 2D result to 0.141 % in total, a comparison across configurations (§7). Binned
MINERvA measurements already reach triple-differential [4, 5]. The 3D and 4D injected-bump
closures, of configuration (a), leave 0.10 of the injected Eavail change and close q3 to 1.1 %.
Neither closure is graded (§7.4, Table 5). Reconstructed W is fixed by (pT , p∥ , q3 ), so the five
are simultaneous, not independent, observables (§8.2). Eavail keeps the inherited 135 MeV
charged-pion constant (§7.1).
• Low-Eavail central values (descriptive). All four truth-level generators, GENIE [6], MINERvA Tune [7], NuWro [8] and GiBUU [9], under-predict the unfolded central value in the
quasielastic–∆ dip (0.1 ≤ Eavail < 0.4 GeV), the region of the established MINERvA lowrecoil discrepancy [7, 10]. The GENIE and NuWro predictions are flux-repaired (§7.6). The
comparisons with the tested pion-FSI dials and with Valencia 2p2h [11, 12] are in §7.7 (Fig. 19).
• High-Eavail , high-W excess (descriptive). In cell-integrated terms, most of the positive
difference from the Tune v1 prior lies at Eavail ≥ 0.8 GeV, much of it at W ≥ 1.8 GeV. A fifth
of the positive difference sits in the single widest overflow cell. The high-Eavail , high-W region
carries the largest regularization bias measured in §9. In ratio, the excess also extends to low
W . The four external generators also fall below the data in this corner, each by about its
overall shortfall once GiBUU’s sample limit is allowed for (§8.5). The NuWro and GiBUU
ratios contain an uncorrected carbon-versus-CH target difference (§8.3).
• Application: joint five-dimensional generator tests. Calibrated tests of hybrid null
hypotheses for five predictions, in total (rate and shape) and in shape, reject all ten under six
stated conditions. The five predictions are Tune v1, GENIE with and without MEC, NuWro
and GiBUU; each null uses the prediction on the fine grid, with the Tune v1 composition below
it. The family-wise error rate is controlled at 5% under the stated calibration assumptions,
and the size is averaged over the nuisance priors (§B.5). The closest decisions, the NuWro and
2

GENIE CV shape tests, lie 1.11 and 1.30 null standard deviations beyond their boundary, with
the Holm order held fixed (§11.4). The rejections are not physical exclusions, and matched
coarse projections reject as well (§11.7). Each rejection also holds in a robustness variant,
not used for the decisions, that assumes a larger residual below the finest tested grid (§11.4).
The pseudo-experiments lost to time limits have been rerun, for reporting only, so every
submitted draw has been observed. No recovered draw changes a decision (§11.6). Correcting
the carbon-only NuWro and GiBUU predictions to the CH target moves both toward the data.
In this descriptive re-evaluation, NuWro’s total-test rejection is not robust to the correction,
and the ten decisions are unchanged (§11.9).
• A methodological finding. The standard covariance sums single-source systematic matrices,
each with all other sources nominal, and so omits nonlinear cross-source response terms. A
corrected unified-throw test perturbs all reweightable sources together and stores the joint
mean shift separately (§4.5).
• A preliminary extended-fiducial study. Removing truth-muon cuts, with the fiducial
volume and MINOS-based selection kept, puts about 6 % of the scalar 2D rate in newly
acceptance-supported cells, which are part of tier 1. It puts about 28 % in low- or zeroefficiency cells, which form tier 2 (§12).
