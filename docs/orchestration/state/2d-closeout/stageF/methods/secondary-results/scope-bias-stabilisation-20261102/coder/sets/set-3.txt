Every result is the mean of eight unfoldings whose inputs are independently perturbed at the level of floating-point rounding. A single unfolding moves by up to 2.6 statistical standard deviations under such perturbations; the eight-member mean meets the data-stability requirement of at most 0.3 (median) and 1.0 (maximum) statistical standard deviations.
