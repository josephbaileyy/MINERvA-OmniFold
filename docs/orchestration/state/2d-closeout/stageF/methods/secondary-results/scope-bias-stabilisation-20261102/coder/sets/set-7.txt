The joint-test estimator is unstable under perturbations of its inputs within floating-point rounding. Rounding-level changes of its inputs move its results by up to 2.6 statistical standard deviations, and those of the lower-capacity development variant by up to 2.5, so the estimator fails a data-stability requirement of at most 0.3 (median) and 1.0 (maximum) statistical standard deviations. Every bootstrap replica refits the estimator and carries this spread at the same size (variance ratio 1.02), so it makes up about 37% of the statistical block's variance and less than a tenth of any total uncertainty. It limits how exactly the central values can be reproduced.
