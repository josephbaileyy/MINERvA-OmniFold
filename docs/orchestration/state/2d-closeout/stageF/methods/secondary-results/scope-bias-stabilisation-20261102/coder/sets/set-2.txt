Each classifier is trained with two-fold cross-fitting: it sets the weights of the half of the sample it was not trained on. It is trained without early stopping, and the two classes keep their physical weights.

The others are a 2p2h band, two-point shifts, chosen models, the normalization, the run-period block, built from three exposure-weighted periods, and the training-seed block, which is zero.

Rounding-level changes of the inputs move the results by up to 0.9 statistical standard deviations (median 0.2), within the data-stability requirement of at most 0.3 (median) and 1.0 (maximum) statistical standard deviations.
