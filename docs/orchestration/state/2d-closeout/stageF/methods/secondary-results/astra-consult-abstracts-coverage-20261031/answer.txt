1. **Diagnosis — editorial judgments/inferences.**

The paper’s opening sentence overloads the measurement with the observable list, method, dataset, reporting cells and uncertainty. “With a covariance whose median per-cell uncertainty” describes bookkeeping; “median relative uncertainty per cell” describes the result. “Exceeds the thresholds set against the reported uncertainty” is cumbersome but protects a real distinction: these are bias-reporting criteria, not coverage tests. The paper underreports that distinction by omitting the explicit coverage statement. Its numerical generator comparisons need a brief indication that they are not calibrated exclusions. (`P/main_paper.tex:46`; `P/paper_body.tex:264`)

The note’s shorter opening sentences work better. “The coverage of the result’s intervals has not been validated” is necessary, not excessive hedging. The adjoining “but does not test interval widths” repeats that qualification and interrupts the result. Keep the positive held-out bias finding, then state the coverage limitation once. (`P/main_note.tex:72`)

The previous paper wording, “the reported uncertainty does not cover the estimator’s bias,” was liable to be mistaken for a coverage measurement. The revision improves scientific precision. Neither abstract should imply that adding the run-period block reduced bias: only the covariance changed. (`origin/main:P/main_paper.tex:46`; `P/paper_body.tex:193`)

**Paper:**

> We measure the inclusive muon-neutrino charged-current cross section simultaneously in five observables: muon transverse and longitudinal momenta, available hadronic energy, three-momentum transfer and hadronic invariant mass. MINERvA medium-energy open data are unfolded with OmniFold without binning and reported in sixteen cells. The median relative uncertainty per cell is 7.86%, including a run-period contribution estimated after differences between periods were observed. Three external generator predictions give χ² values of 105–224 for 16 degrees of freedom; GiBUU gives 45 on the eight cells covered by its sample. Fake-data studies find residual bias exceeding predeclared uncertainty-based thresholds at a GiBUU-like truth and a synthetic momentum-transfer tilt. The coverage of the reported uncertainty intervals has not been validated.

3. **Coverage and feasible next steps.**

**Inference about practice:** PRD cross-section papers commonly describe closure, alternative-model tests and assigned systematic uncertainties without claiming universal frequentist coverage or disclaiming its absence in the abstract. The repository’s literature review supports that presentation pattern; it does not establish universal journal practice. Its MINERvA reference-paper review explicitly identifies no coverage discussion. (`R/REPORT.md:100–146`; `R/notes/2106.16210.md:41–42`)

**Recommendation/inference:** this result warrants the short abstract statement above because there is affirmative evidence of substantial residual bias at alternative truths, not merely an omitted validation exercise. Nevertheless, “coverage failed” would be false for these reported intervals. Gate D stopped the test before coverage toys ran; even the older matrix without the run-period block was not tested. (`1a4815a8a:F/FD-1B-STAGE2-REPORT.md:11–24`)

Nor would a successful restart establish general validity: the protocol defines PASS as **“no undercoverage detected at the study’s sensitivity”**, at the tested truth under nominal response. (`F/FD-1B-DECLARATION.md:179–197`)

**Recommended next investigation—inference:** diagnose the existing derivation products before commissioning another ensemble. Their predicted-to-reference scatter ratios span **0.696–2.296**; the interaction contribution is the largest discrepancy. Per-band decomposition is possible from the existing **176** products, but the cause remains unmeasured. A reader-only diagnosis should require no new unfolding jobs—my estimate, not a costed declaration. (`1a4815a8a:F/FD-1B-STAGE2-REPORT.md:142–183`)

After establishing a defensible ensemble, a newly declared limited coverage study could narrow the gap. The historical estimate was **44.5–145 CPU node-hours**, including development and verification; it is not a current remaining-cost estimate. At **300 toys**, the per-cell standard error near nominal coverage is **0.027**. (`F/LIMITATION-PLAN-ADDENDUM-20261028.md:108–131`)

**Inference:** enlarging uncertainties after seeing failures cannot independently validate them. The run-period addition itself explicitly supports no coverage claim. No general coverage validation is possible from the finite truth set and restricted detector-response model; broader claims require new assumptions and independent tests. (`F/FD-CT-5B-ADDITION-REPORT.md:10–31`; `F/FD-1B-DECLARATION.md:34–47`)
