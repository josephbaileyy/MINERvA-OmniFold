GENIE~CV totals \SI{2.76e-38}{cm^2/nucleon} in the measurement phase space in both; GENIE$+$MEC uses the normalization convention of the 3D comparison. In the corner ($\Eavail\ge\SI{0.8}{GeV}$, $W\ge\SI{1.8}{GeV}$), data/generator $=1.14$, $1.14$, $1.16$ and $1.61$, respectively; none reaches the data. With the flux repaired, the corner therefore has no distinct deficit for any of the four generators. GiBUU is lower than the others everywhere, and its energy limit accounts for the larger corner shortfall.
