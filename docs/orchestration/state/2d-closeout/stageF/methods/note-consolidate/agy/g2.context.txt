ROW R09b (passage sec_3d:91): fact: The 3D closure does not test response mismatch
  home sec_validation.tex: "Every closure test here therefore keeps the simulated MINERvA response"
    in its paragraph: Only the MINERvA simulation supplies a detector response, the conditional distribution of reconstructed events given truth. Other generators (GiBUU, NuWro, GENIE variants) supply truth only. Every closure test here therefore keeps the simulated MINERvA response and changes the truth distribution, by reweighting the simulated events, or changes the analysis chain around it. Reweighting to another generator's shape is a valid known-truth test of the unfolding; it is not a test of the detector model. A closure test is only as informative as what it changes: an ordinary closure, whose pseudo-data are the simulation itself, is an identity check that a constant estimator would pass. Table~\ref{tab:closure-map} collects the tests by what they change.

    in its paragraph: All four external generators fall below the data in the high-$\Eavail$, high-$W$ corner. Figure~\ref{fig:eavailWband} compares the spline-normalized $(\Eavail,W)$ predictions of GENIE~CV, GENIE$+$Valencia~2p2h/MEC, NuWro and GiBUU, with the GENIE and NuWro predictions flux-repaired as in \S\ref{sec:3d-models}. The 3D and $(\Eavail,W)$ comparisons are made from the same five-dimensional histogram of each generator. GENIE~CV totals \SI{2.76e-38}{cm^2/nucleon} in the measurement phase space in both; GENIE$+$MEC uses the normalization convention of the 3D comparison. In the corner ($\Eavail\ge\SI{0.8}{GeV}$, $W\ge\SI{1.8}{GeV}$), data/generator $=1.14$, $1.14$, $1.16$ and $1.61$, respectively; none reaches the data. For GENIE and NuWro this is close to their whole-plane shortfall (integrated ratios $1.11$, $1.07$ and $1.15$). Their corner ratios exceed their integrated ratios by \SIrange{1}{7}{\percent}, compared with \SI{16}{\percent} for GiBUU (integrated $1.39$; $\sigma=\SI{2.22e-38}{cm^2/nucleon}$ versus data \SI{3.07e-38}{}). GiBUU's larger contrast comes from its sample's energy limit: it was generated only up to $E_\nu\approx\SI{20}{GeV}$ (\S\ref{sec:3d-models}). In GENIE and NuWro, neutrinos above this energy carry \SIrange{14.3}{14.8}{\percent} of the corner cross section and \SIrange{6.8}{7.4}{\percent} overall. Removing them raises these generators' corner-to-integrated contrast to \SIrange{9}{16}{\percent}, bracketing GiBUU's. If GiBUU is instead supplied with the shares that GENIE and NuWro carry above this energy, data/GiBUU becomes about $1.37$ in the corner and $1.29$ integrated. With the flux repaired, the corner therefore has no distinct deficit for any of the four generators. GiBUU is lower than the others everywhere, and its energy limit accounts for the larger corner shortfall.
  home sec_3d.tex: "Its sample stops at $E_\nu\approx\SI{20}{GeV}$, so it is empty above $\pPar\approx\SI{20}{GeV/c}$."
    in its paragraph: \paragraph{Flux repair of the GENIE and NuWro predictions.} Except in three studies that keep the unrepaired samples, the GENIE and NuWro predictions in \S\S\ref{sec:3d}--\ref{sec:eavailw} are repaired by exact per-event reweighting to the analysis PPFX central-value flux on 0--\SI{100}{GeV}, with correctly sampled 50--\SI{100}{GeV} supplements.
