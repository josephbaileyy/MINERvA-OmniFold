\paragraph{Bin-identical comparison to the published low-recoil measurement.} Supplemental data from Ref.~\cite{MINERvA:2022incl} (44-cell $d^{2}\sigma/(d\Eavail\,dq_{3})$ with its full covariance, distributed inside the public arXiv source package) permit a direct quantitative cross-check. Both measurements are merged onto their maximal common grid, the edges present in \emph{both} binnings. Our $(\pT,\pPar)$ marginalization is restricted to $\pPar<\SI{20}{GeV/c}$ to mirror the Ascencio muon selection ($\theta_\mu<20^{\circ}$, $1.5<p_\mu<\SI{20}{GeV}$). Together, these give two super-cells, $\Eavail<\SI{0.4}{GeV}$ in $q_{3}\in[0.4,0.6)$ and $[0.6,1.2)\,\si{GeV}$: the low-recoil 2p2h region. Our central result lies \SI{9.2}{\percent} and \SI{6.0}{\percent} above Ascencio. No pulls or full-covariance $\chi^{2}$ are reported (no corrected 4D covariance, above; Fig.~\ref{fig:ascencio}). Both share MINERvA flux/detector systematics, treated here as independent. The $p_\mu$ and $\pPar$ cuts differ by $\lesssim\SI{6}{\percent}$ kinematic smearing at the \SI{20}{GeV} boundary. The two analyses also define true $\Eavail$ differently (\S\ref{sec:eavail-definition}): in this analysis's simulation, Ascencio's broader definition moves \SI{11.0}{\percent} of the population of our lowest true-$\Eavail$ bin ($\Eavail<\SI{0.1}{GeV}$) out of it. That shift has the sign and location of the residual. The two common cells, however, span our three lowest bins ($\Eavail<\SI{0.4}{GeV}$), and the aggregate shift over those bins has not been computed. The definition difference therefore remains an alternative explanation that is not excluded. At the \SI{10}{\percent} scale of the residual, the comparison neither refutes nor validates the result. A finer comparison on all 44 cells remains future work; it requires re-unfolding on the Ascencio edges, which is straightforward for the unbinned method, \emph{and} a systematic sweep on that binning.
