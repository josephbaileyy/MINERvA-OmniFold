=== PASSAGE EW-2 (sec_eavailw.tex) ===
BEFORE:
Truth $W$ is the experimenter's invariant mass $W=\sqrt{M^{2}+2M(E_\nu-E_\mu)-Q^{2}}$, with struck-nucleon mass $M$ (\texttt{CVUniverse::GetTrueExperimentersW()}). Reconstructed $W$ combines the calorimetric energy transfer $q_{0}$ of the reconstructed-$q_{3}$ estimator and the muon-kinematic $Q^{2}$ with the average nucleon mass (\texttt{CVUniverse::RecoW()}). The external generator predictions instead compute truth $W$ with the neutron mass for every event and place events with $W^{2}\le0$ in the lowest $W$ bin, whereas the analysis uses the struck-nucleon mass and excludes such events from its five-observable truth sample. In the GENIE and NuWro samples this moves 0.14--0.15\% of the events into another $W$ bin. In GENIE CV and NuWro (without their 50--\SI{100}{GeV} samples) it changes the coarse-cell predictions by about \SI{0.1}{\percent} at the median and at most \SI{2.2}{\percent}, at most about one Monte Carlo standard error of the prediction in any cell; it was not evaluated at cell level for GENIE$+$MEC and cannot be evaluated for GiBUU.

In GENIE's own CH sample, the carbon-only cross section per nucleon exceeds that of CH by \SI{8.3}{\percent} for $W<\SI{1.1}{GeV}$ and is \SI{2.2}{\percent} below it for $1.1\le W<\SI{1.4}{GeV}$ (\SI{+5.6}{\percent} and \SI{-4.6}{\percent} with the overall normalization removed). The NuWro and GiBUU comparisons contain a target difference of this kind, which the tests of \S\ref{sec:joint5d}, as run, do not correct; \S\ref{sec:joint5d-ch} re-evaluates them with the correction.

=== PASSAGE EW-5 (sec_eavailw.tex) ===
BEFORE:
All four external generators fall below the data in the high-$\Eavail$, high-$W$ corner. Figure~\ref{fig:eavailWband} compares the spline-normalized $(\Eavail,W)$ predictions of GENIE~CV, GENIE$+$Valencia~2p2h/MEC, NuWro and GiBUU, with the GENIE and NuWro predictions flux-repaired as in \S\ref{sec:3d-models}. The 3D and $(\Eavail,W)$ comparisons are made from the same five-dimensional histogram of each generator; GENIE~CV totals \SI{2.76e-38}{cm^2/nucleon} in the measurement phase space in both; GENIE$+$MEC uses the normalization convention of the 3D comparison. In the corner ($\Eavail\ge\SI{0.8}{GeV}$, $W\ge\SI{1.8}{GeV}$), data/generator $=1.14$, $1.14$, $1.16$ and $1.61$, respectively; none reaches the data. For GENIE and NuWro this is close to their whole-plane shortfall (integrated ratios $1.11$, $1.07$ and $1.15$). Their corner ratios exceed their integrated ratios by \SIrange{1}{7}{\percent}, compared with \SI{16}{\percent} for GiBUU (integrated $1.39$; $\sigma=\SI{2.22e-38}{cm^2/nucleon}$ versus data \SI{3.07e-38}{}). GiBUU's larger contrast comes from its sample's energy limit: it was generated only up to $E_\nu\approx\SI{20}{GeV}$ (\S\ref{sec:3d-models}). In GENIE and NuWro, neutrinos above this energy carry \SIrange{14.3}{14.8}{\percent} of the corner cross section and \SIrange{6.8}{7.4}{\percent} overall. Removing them raises these generators' corner-to-integrated contrast to \SIrange{9}{16}{\percent}, bracketing GiBUU's. Supplying GiBUU with their share instead gives about $1.37$ in the corner and $1.29$ integrated. With the flux repaired, the corner therefore has no distinct deficit for any of the four generators. GiBUU is lower than the others everywhere, and its energy limit accounts for the larger corner shortfall.
