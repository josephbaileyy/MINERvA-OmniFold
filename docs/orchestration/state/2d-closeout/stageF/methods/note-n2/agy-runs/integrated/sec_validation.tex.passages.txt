\paragraph{What the remaining validation needs.} The follow-up assessment~\cite{TwoDFollowup2026} distinguishes a proposed split-sample width check from repeated-sampling coverage. With the MC sample, binning maps and background subtraction held fixed, such a check could establish only the precision conditional on them, at its tested exposure, and nonlinear unfolding does not guarantee a simple scaling from half to full exposure. It cannot supply the variation across independent MC populations. Validated total uncertainties for all 205 bins, the integral and the projections therefore cannot be obtained from the current inputs. They would require independent populations or a validated generative law, a continuous nuisance generator for tests of total intervals, and an untouched generator-truth domain for bias checks (\S\ref{sec:uq-2d-scope}). This is a limit of the available inputs, not a general impossibility result for 2D unfolding.

\paragraph{Unbinned diagnostic (C2ST).} In the classifier two-sample test (C2ST), a classifier is trained to separate the data from the (reweighted) simulation in the full unbinned feature space, and its held-out AUC or accuracy is compared with the chance value $0.5$. The AUC drops from $\approx0.535$ before unfolding to $\approx0.501$ after, indicating removal of the reconstructed data\,$\leftrightarrow$\,MC mismatch. This binning-free check is descriptive, and no calibrated $p$-value is quoted: as run, the C2ST trains and evaluates on the same sample without cross-fitting, and a valid null would require re-running the full cross-fitted OmniFold pipeline per pseudoexperiment. Calibrated unbinned goodness-of-fit remains open~\cite{Canelli:2025guide}.

In reported bin $i$ with $\bar X^{\text{truth}}_{i}>0$, define the across-toy standard deviation $\sigma_{i}$ (ddof $=1$) and standardized residual $r^{(t)}_{i}=(U^{(t)}_{i}-\bar X^{\text{truth}}_{i})/\sigma_{i}$. Compare the per-bin fraction $c_{i}$ of toys with $|r^{(t)}_{i}|\le1$ to the unit-normal $\Phi(1)-\Phi(-1)=\mathrm{erf}(1/\sqrt 2)\approx0.6827$ and the mean absolute pull $\langle|r|\rangle$ to $\sqrt{2/\pi}$. The fraction with $|r|\le1$ is \SI{\pullFrac}{\percent} (Gaussian reference $68.27\%$; $\Delta = +0.44\%$; median $68.50\%$); $\langle|r|\rangle=\pullMeanAbs$ vs $\sqrt{2/\pi}=\pullGauss$ (within $0.5\%$); the signed residual mean is $+0.006 \pm 0.082\,\sigma$ (no bias); and \SI{97.6}{\percent} of bins have a within-bin fraction above \SI{65}{\percent} (only $5$ of $205$ fall below). These show that the standardized toy residuals are approximately normal.
