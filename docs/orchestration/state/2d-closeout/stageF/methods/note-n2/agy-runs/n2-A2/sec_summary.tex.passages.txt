\paragraph{Summary.} The executive summary (p.~\pageref{sec:execsummary}) lists each result with its scope; this section states the conclusions they support. The main results are the validation findings, within the scope stated in \S\ref{sec:intro}; the generator tests are an application that illustrates calibrated inference with a biased estimator. \begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt] \item Unbinned OmniFold with gradient-boosted trees reproduces the integral of the published MINERvA ME FHC CC-inclusive $d^{2}\sigma/(d\pT\,d\pPar)$ within about one percent and its uncertainty scale (\S\ref{sec:results}), with a correlated shape difference ($\chi^{2}/\mathrm{ndf}=\chiPaper$, \S\ref{sec:results-tension}) whose size depends on the estimator (exact-split cross-check $\chiPaperExact$). A generator-shape model-dependence component, reported beside the uncertainty, raises its per-bin median to \SI{\qrCmodelMedian}{\percent} (\S\ref{sec:uq-2d-scope}). The central value and its uncertainty share the LightGBM estimator, which was chosen after both comparisons were known (\S\ref{sec:method-config}). \item Simultaneous unbinned three-, four- and five-observable unfolds ($+\Eavail$, $+q_{3}$, $+W$) pass marginal-normalization checks against the lower-dimensional results to $\approx$1--2\,\% in configuration (a) (4D and 5D), and the configuration-(e) 3D marginal agrees with the configuration-(f) 2D result to \SI{0.141}{\percent} in total, across configurations; the 3D and 4D injected-bump closures, of configuration (a), leave 0.10 of the injected $\Eavail$ change and close $q_{3}$ to \SI{1.1}{\percent}, with no acceptance threshold recorded in advance (\S\ref{sec:3d}). The central values lie above all four truth-level generators of \S\ref{sec:3d-models} in the quasielastic--$\Delta$ dip, the region of the established low-recoil discrepancy (\S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h}, \S\ref{sec:4d}). They lie above the Tune~v1 prior mostly at high $\Eavail$ and high $W$. A fifth of that positive difference sits in the single widest overflow cell, in ratio the excess also extends to low $W$, and the regularization bias is largest in this region (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-map}). There all four external generators also lie below the data, each by about its overall shortfall once the $E_\nu>\SI{20}{GeV}$ events missing from GiBUU are accounted for (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-band}), and the NuWro and GiBUU ratios contain an uncorrected carbon-versus-CH target difference. \item Pseudo-experiments with backgrounds reveal a purity-subtraction bias of up to \SI{4}{\percent} in the highest-$W$ cells, which the quoted five-dimensional central value keeps and no quoted uncertainty includes; the refined negative-weight subtraction largely removes it. In a two-dimensional closure test with backgrounds, the purity and refined estimates differ by more than one statistical standard deviation in 14 of the 205 cells, where the purity estimate lies up to $5.9\,\sigma_{\mathrm{stat}}$ from the truth and the refined one within $0.44\,\sigma_{\mathrm{stat}}$, and on this basis the two-dimensional result was moved to the refined estimator (App.~\ref{app:negweight}). \item As an application, joint tests whose design was fixed before any production pseudo-experiment or observed statistic, with two data-informed elements (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-frozen}), calibrated through the full analysis chain, reject all ten hybrid generator hypotheses under six stated conditions, with the family-wise error rate controlled at 5\% under the stated calibration assumptions and the size averaged over the nuisance priors. They are not physical exclusions, recover established discrepancies, and show no evidence of discrimination beyond matched coarse projections. In a descriptive re-evaluation, NuWro's total (rate-and-shape) rejection is not robust to correcting its carbon prediction to CH, and the ten decisions are unchanged (\S\ref{sec:joint5d}, \S\ref{sec:joint5d-ch}). \end{itemize}

\paragraph{Principal limitations.} The principal limitations, each stated in the body with the result it qualifies, are these. The quoted two-dimensional statistical block has had no coverage test (\S\ref{sec:uq-boot}, \S\ref{sec:coverage}). The generator comparisons of \S\S\ref{sec:3d}--\ref{sec:eavailw} are descriptive comparisons of central values that identify no nuclear or interaction mechanism, and no 3D or 4D covariance is quoted (\S\ref{sec:3d}). The five-dimensional estimator recovers only part of truth changes of about the size of generator differences, and no quoted uncertainty includes this residual bias, interpreted as regularization bias (\S\ref{sec:val-departure}). Above two dimensions the results are central values without intervals of validated coverage: no five-dimensional measurement with total uncertainties was constructed (\S\ref{sec:gbdt-model-dependence}), and bias-corrected statistical intervals of a deterministic estimator variant miscover at the tested truths (\S\ref{sec:coverage-5d}). For the one five-dimensional covariance used in this note, which does not meet its specification's acceptance criteria, two companion covariances built at different estimator seeds show a seed sensitivity above the limit set before production, so no covariance-based significance is quoted (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}). The calibration of the generator tests is valid for the simulated null to the extent that the pseudo-experiment chain reproduces the analysis applied to the data (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-frozen}), and their nuisance model has no calorimetric recoil-response band (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-response}).

The main results are the validation findings, within the scope stated in \S\ref{sec:intro}. The generator tests are an application that illustrates calibrated inference with a biased estimator. \begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt] \item Unbinned OmniFold with gradient-boosted trees reproduces the integral of the published MINERvA ME FHC CC-inclusive $d^{2}\sigma/(d\pT\,d\pPar)$ within about one percent and its uncertainty scale (\S\ref{sec:results}).
