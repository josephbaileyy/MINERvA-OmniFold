BEFORE:
The 2D justification in \S\ref{sec:chisq} does not transfer to N-D. An earlier 5D covariance from the standard production chain, distinct from $C_Z$ and built before $C_Z$ was chosen for use, has $10\,694$ reported bins, effective positive rank $\mathbf{263}$ and infinite numerical condition number. Its $4825$-bin 5D$\to$4D projection retains rank $263$ exactly. About ${\sim}45$ two-endpoint MAT bands plus statistical and ML blocks cannot span $10\,694$ directions, so the rank limit is a consequence of the construction, not a defect. Quoting $\chi^{2}/n_{\text{reported}}$ would understate tension by roughly $40\times$ in 5D or $18\times$ in 4D.

BEFORE:
\paragraph{Finite-ensemble bias of the inverse, and why no correction is prescribed.} Without declaration (v), the ensemble sizes of the blocks would go unrecorded, and an uncorrected finite-ensemble bias could enter unnoticed. The inverse of a noisy sample covariance is biased \emph{upward}: for an unbiased $1/(N-1)$ estimator over $N$ independent Gaussian realizations the standard debiasing factor is $(N-p-2)/(N-1)$, and for this analysis's biased $1/N$ production convention (universe-mean centered, biased $1/N$) it is $(N-p-2)/N$. The superseded unified-throw 5D covariance has $N=160$, truncated $p=100$, giving $58/160\simeq0.363$: uncorrected precision is too large by $\simeq2.76$. For fixed residuals this inflates $\chi^{2}$ and overstates tension; confidence regions from the same over-tight covariance become over-optimistic.
