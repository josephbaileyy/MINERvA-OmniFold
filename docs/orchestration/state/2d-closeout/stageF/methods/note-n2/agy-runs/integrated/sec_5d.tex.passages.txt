BEFORE:
\paragraph{Comparison with exact binned unfolding.} A diagnostic in simulation compares the boosted-tree residual with that of an exact binned iterative unfolding. It uses the 109 J cells, noise-free signal-only pseudo-data, and the fixed historical truth deformations D1, W1, W3 and the $q_3$ stress (labels as in \S\ref{sec:gbdt-model-dependence}), pooled with W2; W2's boosted-tree comparator includes background, and the outcome is unchanged without it. The binned unfolding unfolds the same simulated events, with their exact efficiency and the response binned in the 10,499 occupied five-dimensional reconstructed cells, starting from the nominal prior. At five iterations the J-cell medians of the absolute residual agree: \SI{9.5}{\percent}, \SI{5.4}{\percent}, \SI{5.6}{\percent} and \SI{4.3}{\percent} for the boosted trees, against \SI{8.3}{\percent}, \SI{5.4}{\percent}, \SI{4.9}{\percent} and \SI{5.3}{\percent} for the exact binned unfolding, for D1, W1, W3 and the $q_3$ stress. Of the 144 eligible residuals, pooled over the deformations, 15 are dominated by the tree approximation, 2 of them in identified quantities, a share of 0.014. Most of the 144 (121) lie in quantities that the binned response constrains weakly at the analysis exposure: their Cram\'er--Rao widths exceed \SI{10}{\percent} at both truth resolutions tested (median \SI{31}{\percent} at the coarser, the J cells themselves), so the regularization trades a large variance for the bias. Longer noise-free exact iteration removes much of the residual for D1 (J median \SI{3.3}{\percent} after 200 iterations and \SI{0.8}{\percent} after $10^{5}$) and part of it for W1 (\SI{3.3}{\percent} and \SI{2.8}{\percent}), but not for W3 and the $q_3$ stress (\SI{4.4}{\percent} and \SI{6.6}{\percent} after 200 iterations, \SI{8.9}{\percent} and \SI{6.7}{\percent} after $10^{5}$), whose within-cell binning bias grows with the iteration number. The long runs do not converge (only 9--34 of the 109 J quantities converge for each traced deformation), so these values are last iterates. The 144 eligible residuals are those whose diagnosis is stable under the truth resolution: of 376 J-cell and deformation combinations with residuals above \SI{2}{\percent}, 232 changed classification with the truth resolution or the response weighting and were excluded, leaving the 144 (\SI{38}{\percent}). The coarser muon projection retained only three cases and is uninformative. Within this comparison (binned, signal-only and noise-free, with the detector response fixed at the simulation's, on the development truths, and over these 144 residuals only), the residual is consistent with the regularization of the inverse problem at this binning and prior, and the tree approximation is not supported as its main cause. What unbinned or biased estimators could recover, the coverage failure of \S\ref{sec:coverage-5d} and the validity of any interval lie outside the diagnostic's scope; it establishes no impossibility, and the precision thresholds it proposes are not acceptance criteria for any result in this note. The exclusions, definitions and numerical verification are in the binned response diagnostic record (Ref.~\cite{NoteRecords2026}, \texttt{response-diagnostic}).
