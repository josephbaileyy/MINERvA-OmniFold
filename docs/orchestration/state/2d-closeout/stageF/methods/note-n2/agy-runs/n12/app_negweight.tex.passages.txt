=== PASSAGE AC-1 (app_negweight.tex, App. C, what the quoted extended-fiducial number used) ===
BEFORE:
\paragraph{What the quoted extended-fiducial number used.} The $\sigma_{\mathrm{ext}}=\SI{\fpsSigma}{cm^2/nucleon}$ in \S\ref{sec:fps} was not produced with negative-weight injection. It comes from the extended-fiducial ME~FHC battery of 2026-06-10, run before the driver supported negative-weight subtraction. Its ten endpoint unfolds used the driver's purity default and are recorded as purity controls, not publication inputs (Ref.~\cite{NoteRecords2026}, \texttt{fps-battery}). The central value of that battery, \SI{\fpsSigma}{cm^2/nucleon}, is recorded in the validation ledger with its passed published-region check against the control. No extended-fiducial production with refined negative-weight subtraction has been run, so negative-weight injection, the intended extended-fiducial baseline, did not produce the quoted number. 

AFTER:
\paragraph{What the quoted extended-fiducial number used.} The $\sigma_{\mathrm{ext}}=\SI{\fpsSigma}{cm^2/nucleon}$ in \S\ref{sec:fps} was not produced with negative-weight injection. It comes from the extended-fiducial ME~FHC battery of 2026-06-10, run before the driver supported negative-weight subtraction. The extended-fiducial central value was therefore produced with the binned purity correction, not with refined negative-weight subtraction, and negative-weight injection, the intended extended-fiducial baseline, did not produce the quoted number.
