BEFORE:
We apply \emph{unbinned machine-learning unfolding} (OmniFold~\cite{Andreassen:2019cjw, Andreassen:2021zzk}) to MINERvA's medium-energy neutrino data. The main results are the validation findings; they hold for this implementation, one data set, and the configurations, observables and deformations tested (\S\ref{sec:intro}). The generator tests are an application that illustrates calibrated inference with a biased estimator.

\paragraph{Results overview:} \begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt, parsep=0pt, topsep=2pt] \item \textbf{Reproduction.} Unbinned $d^{2}\sigma/(d\pT\,d\pPar)$ reproduces the published integrated cross section~\cite{MINERvA:2021owq} within about one percent, with a correlated shape difference: total cross-section ratio $\ratioTot$, \SI{\binsTen}{\percent} of bins within \SI{10}{\percent}, and a separately constructed uncertainty comparable to the published one (per-bin median \SI{\uqMedian}{\percent} vs.\ \SI{\uqPaper}{\percent}, integral \SI{\uqIntegral}{\percent} vs.\ \SI{\uqPaperIntegral}{\percent}); its flux band carries MAT's $\nu$--$e$ and inverse-muon-decay constraint weights (\S\ref{sec:results-uq}). A generator-shape model-dependence component, reported beside that uncertainty and absent from the published one, raises the per-bin median to \SI{\qrCmodelMedian}{\percent} (\S\ref{sec:uq-2d-scope}). The central value and that uncertainty share the LightGBM estimator, with refined negative-weight background subtraction; LightGBM was chosen over the exact-split estimator, now a cross-check, after both comparisons with the publication were known (\S\ref{sec:method-config}). Against the full published covariance, $\chi^{2}/\mathrm{ndf}=\chiPaper$ (exact-split: $\chiPaperExact$) is a descriptive distance whose source has not been identified (\S\ref{sec:results-tension}).

\item \textbf{Unbinned 3D/4D/5D MINERvA unfolds.} Adding $\Eavail$, $q_{3}$ and $W$ gives simultaneous unfolds. In configuration (a), the 4D and 5D marginal-normalization checks against the lower-dimensional result pass to $\approx$1--2\,\%; the configuration-(e) 3D marginal agrees with the configuration-(f) 2D result to \SI{0.141}{\percent} in total, a cross-configuration comparison (\S\ref{sec:3d}); binned MINERvA measurements already reach triple-differential~\cite{MINERvA:2022qelike3d,MINERvA:2026qeleme}. The 3D and 4D injected-bump closures, of configuration (a), leave 0.10 of the injected $\Eavail$ change and close $q_{3}$ to \SI{1.1}{\percent}; neither has an acceptance threshold recorded in advance (\S\ref{sec:3d-closure}, \S\ref{sec:4d}). Reconstructed $W$ is fixed by $(\pT,\pPar,q_{3})$, so the five are simultaneous, not independent, observables (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-definitions}). $\Eavail$ keeps the inherited \SI{135}{MeV} charged-pion constant (\S\ref{sec:eavail-definition}).

\item \textbf{Low-$\Eavail$ central values (descriptive).} All four truth-level generators (GENIE~\cite{Andreopoulos:2009rq}, MINERvA Tune~\cite{MINERvA:2016lowrecoil}, NuWro~\cite{Golan:2012wx}, GiBUU~\cite{Buss:2011mx}; GENIE and NuWro flux-repaired, \S\ref{sec:3d-models}) under-predict the unfolded central value in the quasielastic--$\Delta$ dip ($0.1\le\Eavail<\SI{0.4}{GeV}$), the region of the established MINERvA low-recoil discrepancy~\cite{MINERvA:2016lowrecoil,MINERvA:2022incl}. The comparisons with the tested pion-FSI dials and with Valencia 2p2h~\cite{Nieves:2011pp,Gran:2013kda} are in \S\ref{sec:3d-2p2h} (Fig.~\ref{fig:3dmodels}).

\item \textbf{High-$\Eavail$, high-$W$ excess (descriptive).} In cell-integrated terms, most of the positive difference from the Tune~v1 prior lies at $\Eavail\ge\SI{0.8}{GeV}$, much of it at $W\ge\SI{1.8}{GeV}$. A fifth of the positive difference sits in the single widest overflow cell, this region carries the largest regularization bias, and in ratio the excess also extends to low $W$. The four external generators also fall below the data in this corner, each by about its overall shortfall once GiBUU's $E_\nu\approx\SI{20}{GeV}$ sample limit is allowed for, and the NuWro and GiBUU ratios contain an uncorrected carbon-versus-CH target difference (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-generators}).

\item \textbf{Application: joint five-dimensional generator tests.} Calibrated tests of hybrid null hypotheses for five predictions (Tune~v1, GENIE with and without MEC, NuWro, GiBUU; each prediction on the fine grid, with the Tune~v1 composition below it), in total (rate and shape) and in shape, reject all ten under six stated conditions, with the family-wise error rate controlled at 5\% under the stated calibration assumptions and the size averaged over the nuisance priors (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-fwer}). The closest decisions, the NuWro and GENIE~CV shape tests, lie 1.11 and 1.30 null standard deviations beyond their boundary (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-conditions}). The rejections are not physical exclusions, and matched coarse projections reject as well (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-projections}). Each rejection also holds in a robustness variant, not used for the decisions, that assumes a larger residual below the finest tested grid; that residual is unmeasured. The pseudo-experiments lost to time limits were rerun afterwards, for reporting only, so every submitted draw has been observed, and no recovered draw changes a decision (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-lost}). Correcting the carbon-only NuWro and GiBUU predictions to the CH target moves both toward the data; in this descriptive re-evaluation, NuWro's total-test rejection is not robust to the correction, and the ten decisions are unchanged (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-ch}).

\item \textbf{A methodological finding.} The standard covariance sums single-source systematic matrices, each with all other sources nominal, and so omits nonlinear cross-source response terms; a corrected unified-throw test perturbs all reweightable sources together and stores the joint mean shift separately (\S\ref{sec:uq-combine}).

\item \textbf{A preliminary extended-fiducial study.} Removing truth-muon cuts (fiducial volume and MINOS-based selection kept) puts about \SI{6}{\percent} of the scalar 2D rate in newly acceptance-supported cells (part of tier~1) and about \SI{28}{\percent} in low- or zero-efficiency cells (tier~2) (\S\ref{sec:fps}).

\paragraph{Principal limitations:} The body states each limitation with the result it qualifies; the principal ones are these. The quoted 2D statistical block has had no coverage test; the fixed-truth coverage studies scored two earlier statistical bands, of which the first-built undercovers in its tails ($C_2=\covTwoDCTwo$, vs.\ $0.954$; \S\ref{sec:uq-boot}, \S\ref{sec:coverage}). The generator comparisons of \S\S\ref{sec:3d}--\ref{sec:eavailw} are descriptive comparisons of central values that identify no nuclear or interaction mechanism, and no 3D or 4D covariance is quoted (\S\ref{sec:3d}). No quoted uncertainty includes the residual regularization bias of the five-dimensional estimator, which recovers only part of a generator-sized truth change, with residuals of up to \SI{74}{\percent} in the highest-$W$ column (\S\ref{sec:val-departure}), or the purity-subtraction bias found in closure tests with backgrounds, up to \SI{4}{\percent} in the highest-$W$ cells, which the five-dimensional central value keeps (\S\ref{sec:val-negweight}). Above two dimensions the results are central values without intervals of validated coverage: no five-dimensional measurement with total uncertainties was constructed (\S\ref{sec:gbdt-model-dependence}), and no frequentist coverage is claimed for any five-dimensional interval, the one coverage test, of bias-corrected statistical intervals of a deterministic estimator variant, having miscovered at the tested truths (\S\ref{sec:coverage-5d}). The one five-dimensional covariance used in this note, with its $(\Eavail,W)$ projection, does not meet its specification's acceptance criteria, and four measurements limit what it supports (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}). The calibration of the generator tests is valid for the simulated null to the extent that the pseudo-experiment chain reproduces the analysis applied to the data (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-frozen}), and their nuisance model has no calorimetric recoil-response band (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-response}). Tier~2 of the extended-fiducial study is prior-dominated extrapolation (\S\ref{sec:fps}). Point-cloud (PET) unfolding is future work (\S\ref{sec:intro}).

\paragraph{Principal limitations:} The body states each limitation with the result it qualifies; the principal ones are these. The quoted 2D statistical block has had no coverage test; the fixed-truth coverage studies scored two earlier statistical bands, of which the first-built undercovers in its tails ($C_2=\covTwoDCTwo$, vs.\ $0.954$; \S\ref{sec:uq-boot}, \S\ref{sec:coverage}). The generator comparisons of \S\S\ref{sec:3d}--\ref{sec:eavailw} are descriptive comparisons of central values that identify no nuclear or interaction mechanism. No 3D or 4D covariance is quoted (\S\ref{sec:3d}). The five-dimensional estimator recovers only part of a generator-sized truth change, with residuals of up to \SI{74}{\percent} in the highest-$W$ column. No quoted uncertainty includes this residual regularization bias (\S\ref{sec:val-departure}). Closure tests with backgrounds find a purity-subtraction bias of up to \SI{4}{\percent} in the highest-$W$ cells. The five-dimensional central value keeps that bias, and no quoted uncertainty includes it (\S\ref{sec:val-negweight}). Above two dimensions the results are central values without intervals of validated coverage. No five-dimensional measurement with total uncertainties was constructed (\S\ref{sec:gbdt-model-dependence}). The one coverage test, of bias-corrected statistical intervals of a deterministic estimator variant, miscovered at the tested truths. No frequentist coverage is therefore claimed for any five-dimensional interval (\S\ref{sec:coverage-5d}). The one five-dimensional covariance used in this note, with its $(\Eavail,W)$ projection, does not meet its specification's acceptance criteria. Four measurements limit what it supports (\S\ref{sec:eavailw-adopted}). The calibration of the generator tests is valid for the simulated null to the extent that the pseudo-experiment chain reproduces the analysis applied to the data (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-frozen}). Their nuisance model has no calorimetric recoil-response band (\S\ref{sec:joint5d-response}). Tier~2 of the extended-fiducial study is prior-dominated extrapolation (\S\ref{sec:fps}). Point-cloud (PET) unfolding is future work (\S\ref{sec:intro}).
