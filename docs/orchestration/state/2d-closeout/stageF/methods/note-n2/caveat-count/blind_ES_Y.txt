Executive summary
We apply unbinned machine-learning unfolding (OmniFold [1, 2]) to MINERvA’s medium-energy
neutrino data. The main results are the validation findings; they hold for this implementation, one
data set, and the configurations, observables and deformations tested (§1). The generator tests are
an application that illustrates calibrated inference with a biased estimator.
Results overview:
• Reproduction. Unbinned d2 σ/(dpT dp∥ ) reproduces the published integrated cross section [3]
within about one percent, with a correlated shape difference: total cross-section ratio 1.010,
95.1 % of bins within 10 %, and a separately constructed uncertainty comparable to the
published one (per-bin median 6.41 % vs. 6.85 %, integral 4.76 % vs. 4.61 %); its flux band
carries MAT’s ν–e and inverse-muon-decay constraint weights (§6.3). A generator-shape
model-dependence component, reported beside that uncertainty and absent from the published
one, raises the per-bin median to 7.90 % (§4.6). The central value and that uncertainty share
the LightGBM estimator, with refined negative-weight background subtraction; LightGBM
was chosen over the exact-split estimator, now a cross-check, after both comparisons with
the publication were known (§3.2). Against the full published covariance, χ2 /ndf = 2.27
(exact-split: 3.66) is a descriptive distance whose source has not been identified (§6.4).
• Unbinned 3D/4D/5D MINERvA unfolds. Adding Eavail , q3 and W gives simultaneous
unfolds. In configuration (a), the 4D and 5D marginal-normalization checks against the
lower-dimensional result pass to ≈1–2 %; the configuration-(e) 3D marginal agrees with the
configuration-(f) 2D result to 0.141 % in total, a cross-configuration comparison (§7); binned
MINERvA measurements already reach triple-differential [4, 5]. The 3D and 4D injected-bump
closures, of configuration (a), leave 0.10 of the injected Eavail change and close q3 to 1.1 %;
neither has an acceptance threshold recorded in advance (§7.4, §7.8). Reconstructed W is fixed
by (pT , p∥ , q3 ), so the five are simultaneous, not independent, observables (§8.2). Eavail keeps
the inherited 135 MeV charged-pion constant (§7.1).
• Low-Eavail central values (descriptive). All four truth-level generators (GENIE [6],
MINERvA Tune [7], NuWro [8], GiBUU [9]; GENIE and NuWro flux-repaired, §7.6) underpredict the unfolded central value in the quasielastic–∆ dip (0.1 ≤ Eavail < 0.4 GeV), the



region of the established MINERvA low-recoil discrepancy [7, 10]. The comparisons with the
tested pion-FSI dials and with Valencia 2p2h [11, 12] are in §7.7 (Fig. 19).
• High-Eavail , high-W excess (descriptive). In cell-integrated terms, most of the positive
difference from the Tune v1 prior lies at Eavail ≥ 0.8 GeV, much of it at W ≥ 1.8 GeV. A
fifth of the positive difference sits in the single widest overflow cell, this region carries the
largest regularization bias, and in ratio the excess also extends to low W . The four external
generators also fall below the data in this corner, each by about its overall shortfall once
GiBUU’s Eν ≈ 20 GeV sample limit is allowed for, and the NuWro and GiBUU ratios contain
an uncorrected carbon-versus-CH target difference (§8.3).
• Application: joint five-dimensional generator tests. Calibrated tests of hybrid null
hypotheses for five predictions (Tune v1, GENIE with and without MEC, NuWro, GiBUU;
each prediction on the fine grid, with the Tune v1 composition below it), in total (rate and
shape) and in shape, reject all ten under six stated conditions, with the family-wise error
rate controlled at 5% under the stated calibration assumptions and the size averaged over
the nuisance priors (§B.5). The closest decisions, the NuWro and GENIE CV shape tests, lie
1.11 and 1.30 null standard deviations beyond their boundary (§10.4). The rejections are not
physical exclusions, and matched coarse projections reject as well (§10.7). Each rejection also
holds in a robustness variant, not used for the decisions, that assumes a larger residual below
the finest tested grid; that residual is unmeasured. The pseudo-experiments lost to time limits
were rerun afterwards, for reporting only, so every submitted draw has been observed, and no
recovered draw changes a decision (§10.6). Correcting the carbon-only NuWro and GiBUU
predictions to the CH target moves both toward the data; in this descriptive re-evaluation,
NuWro’s total-test rejection is not robust to the correction, and the ten decisions are unchanged
(§10.9).
• A methodological finding. The standard covariance sums single-source systematic matrices,
each with all other sources nominal, and so omits nonlinear cross-source response terms; a
corrected unified-throw test perturbs all reweightable sources together and stores the joint
mean shift separately (§4.5).
• A preliminary extended-fiducial study. Removing truth-muon cuts (fiducial volume
and MINOS-based selection kept) puts about 6 % of the scalar 2D rate in newly acceptancesupported cells (part of tier 1) and about 28 % in low- or zero-efficiency cells (tier 2) (§11).

Principal limitations: The body states each limitation with the result it qualifies; the principal
ones are these. The quoted 2D statistical block has had no coverage test; the fixed-truth coverage
studies scored two earlier statistical bands, of which the first-built undercovers in its tails (C2 = 0.912,
vs. 0.954; §4.2, §5.2). The generator comparisons of §§7–8 are descriptive comparisons of central
values that identify no nuclear or interaction mechanism, and no 3D or 4D covariance is quoted (§7).
No quoted uncertainty includes the residual regularization bias of the five-dimensional estimator,
which recovers only part of a generator-sized truth change, with residuals of up to 74 % in the highestW column (§9.3), or the purity-subtraction bias found in closure tests with backgrounds, up to 4 % in
the highest-W cells, which the five-dimensional central value keeps (§9.2). Above two dimensions the
results are central values without intervals of validated coverage: no five-dimensional measurement
with total uncertainties was constructed (§9.4), and no frequentist coverage is claimed for any fivedimensional interval, the one coverage test, of bias-corrected statistical intervals of a deterministic
estimator variant, having miscovered at the tested truths (§9.5). The one five-dimensional covariance
used in this note, with its (Eavail , W ) projection, does not meet its specification’s acceptance criteria,
and four measurements limit what it supports (§9.6). The calibration of the generator tests is valid
for the simulated null to the extent that the pseudo-experiment chain reproduces the analysis applied
to the data (§10.3), and their nuisance model has no calorimetric recoil-response band (§10.8). Tier 2
of the extended-fiducial study is prior-dominated extrapolation (§11). Point-cloud (PET) unfolding
is future work (§1).
