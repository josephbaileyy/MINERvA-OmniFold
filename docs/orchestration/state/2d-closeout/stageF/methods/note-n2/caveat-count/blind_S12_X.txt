Summary and outlook

Summary. The executive summary (p. 2) lists each result with its scope. This section states the
conclusions they support. The main results are the validation findings, within the scope stated in §1.
The generator tests are an application that illustrates calibrated inference with a biased estimator.
• Unbinned OmniFold with gradient-boosted trees reproduces the integral of the published
MINERvA ME FHC CC-inclusive d2 σ/(dpT dp∥ ) within about one percent and its uncertainty
scale (§6). The reproduction has a correlated shape difference (χ2 /ndf = 2.27, §6.4), whose size
depends on the estimator (exact-split cross-check 3.66). A generator-shape model-dependence
component, reported beside the uncertainty, raises its per-bin median to 7.90 % (§4.6). The
central value and its uncertainty share the LightGBM estimator, which was chosen after both
comparisons were known (§3.2).
• The simultaneous unbinned three-, four- and five-observable unfolds add Eavail , q3 and W in
turn. In configuration (a), the 4D and 5D unfolds pass marginal-normalization checks against
the lower-dimensional results to ≈1–2 %. The configuration-(e) 3D marginal agrees with the
configuration-(f) 2D result to 0.141 % in total, a comparison across configurations. The 3D
and 4D injected-bump closures, of configuration (a), leave 0.10 of the injected Eavail change
and close q3 to 1.1 %. No acceptance threshold fixed before either closure is on record (§7).
The central values lie above all four truth-level generators of §7.6 in the quasielastic–∆ dip, the
region of the established low-recoil discrepancy (§7.7, §7.8). They lie above the Tune v1 prior
mostly at high Eavail and high W . A fifth of that positive difference sits in the single widest
overflow cell. In ratio, the excess also extends to low W . The regularization bias is largest
in the high-Eavail , high-W region (§8.4). There all four external generators also lie below the
data, each by about its overall shortfall once the Eν > 20 GeV events missing from GiBUU are
accounted for (§8.5). The NuWro and GiBUU ratios contain an uncorrected carbon-versus-CH
target difference.
• Pseudo-experiments with backgrounds reveal a purity-subtraction bias of up to 4 % in the
highest-W cells. The quoted five-dimensional central value keeps this bias, and no quoted
uncertainty includes it. The refined negative-weight subtraction largely removes it. In a

two-dimensional closure test with backgrounds, the purity and refined estimates differ by more
than one statistical standard deviation in 14 of the 205 cells. In those cells the purity estimate
lies up to 5.9 σstat from the truth, and the refined one within 0.44 σstat . On this basis, the
two-dimensional result uses the refined estimator (App. C).
• As an application, joint tests calibrated through the full analysis chain reject all ten hybrid
generator hypotheses under six stated conditions. Their design was fixed before any production
pseudo-experiment or observed statistic, with two data-informed elements (§10.3). The familywise error rate is controlled at 5% under the stated calibration assumptions, and the size is
averaged over the nuisance priors. The tests are not physical exclusions, recover established
discrepancies, and show no evidence of discrimination beyond matched coarse projections. In a
descriptive re-evaluation, NuWro’s total (rate-and-shape) rejection is not robust to correcting
its carbon prediction to CH, and the ten decisions are unchanged (§10, §10.9).
Principal limitations. The principal limitations, each stated in the body with the result it
qualifies, are these. The quoted two-dimensional statistical block has had no coverage test (§4.2,
§5.2). The generator comparisons of §§7–8 are descriptive comparisons of central values that identify
no nuclear or interaction mechanism. No 3D or 4D covariance is quoted (§7). The five-dimensional
estimator recovers only part of truth changes of about the size of generator differences. No quoted
uncertainty includes this residual bias, which is interpreted as regularization bias (§9.3). Above two
dimensions the results are central values without intervals of validated coverage. No five-dimensional
measurement with total uncertainties was constructed (§9.4). Bias-corrected statistical intervals
of a deterministic five-dimensional estimator variant miscover at the tested truths (§9.5). The one
five-dimensional covariance used in this note does not meet its specification’s acceptance criteria.
Two companion covariances, built at different estimator seeds, show a seed sensitivity above the
limit set before production, so no covariance-based significance is quoted (§9.6). The calibration of
the generator tests is valid for the simulated null to the extent that the pseudo-experiment chain
reproduces the analysis applied to the data (§10.3). Their nuisance model has no calorimetric
recoil-response band (§10.8).
Outlook. The scalar extended-fiducial study (§11) yields a preliminary central value σext =
4.502 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon, against 3.073 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon for its control unfold in the restricted
phase space. New acceptance-supported cells contribute about 6 % of the rate; separately labeled
prior-dominated extrapolation contributes about 28 %. Further refinements are a fifth external
generator, NEUT, and a finer 44-cell comparison with the Ascencio et al. measurement (the
maximal-common-grid comparison is in §7.8). A flux–muon-energy-scale posterior derived from
MAT-MINERvA, independent of the published analysis, would let the correlation ρ between the
leading flux mode and the muon-energy-scale band (§4) be derived entirely within this analysis.
Reusing the unbinned result. The public ATLAS charged-particle phase-space release [62]
exemplifies unbinned distribution. The emerging omnifold_publication proposal [63] emphasizes
weight-family metadata, normalization, row alignment, and checksums. A MINERvA release
should likewise specify event identity, estimator and uncertainty-family definitions, units, support,
normalization, and a reproducible projection example. App. G specifies this release for our analysis,
including which of its objects already have fixed identities and which are still to be produced. A
local release candidate, with checksums and a verifier that recomputes and checks its results, exists,
but no release has been tagged or deposited; rebuilding this note’s PDFs assembles documents
and reproduces no scientific result (App. G). New projections require their own applicability checks;

nominal weights alone cannot certify arbitrary downstream inference (App. B.4). The release design
and its public example have no bearing on the choice of covariance (§9.6).
