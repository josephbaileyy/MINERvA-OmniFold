Executive summary
We apply unbinned machine-learning unfolding (OmniFold [1, 2]) to MINERvA’s medium-energy
neutrino data. The main results are the validation findings. They hold for this implementation, one
data set, and the configurations, observables and deformations tested (§1). The generator tests are
an application that illustrates calibrated inference with a biased estimator.
Results overview:
• Reproduction. Unbinned d2 σ/(dpT dp∥ ) reproduces the published integrated cross section [3]
within about one percent, with a correlated shape difference. The total cross-section ratio is
1.010, and 95.1 % of bins are within 10 %. A separately constructed uncertainty is comparable
to the published one: its per-bin median is 6.41 % against 6.85 %, and its integral 4.76 %
against 4.61 %. Its flux band carries MAT’s ν–e and inverse-muon-decay constraint weights
(§6.3). A generator-shape model-dependence component, reported beside that uncertainty and
absent from the published one, raises the per-bin median to 7.90 % (§4.6). The central value
and that uncertainty share the LightGBM estimator, with refined negative-weight background
subtraction. LightGBM was chosen over the exact-split estimator, which is kept as a cross-check,
after both comparisons with the publication were known (§3.2). Against the full published
covariance, χ2 /ndf = 2.27 (exact-split: 3.66) is a descriptive distance. Its source has not been
identified (§6.4).
• Unbinned 3D/4D/5D MINERvA unfolds. Adding Eavail , q3 and W gives simultaneous
unfolds. In configuration (a), the 4D and 5D marginal-normalization checks against the
lower-dimensional result pass to ≈1–2 %. The configuration-(e) 3D marginal agrees with the
configuration-(f) 2D result to 0.141 % in total, a comparison across configurations (§7). Binned
MINERvA measurements already reach triple-differential [4, 5]. The 3D and 4D injected-bump
closures, of configuration (a), leave 0.10 of the injected Eavail change and close q3 to 1.1 %. No
acceptance threshold fixed before either closure is on record (§7.4, §7.8). Reconstructed W is
fixed by (pT , p∥ , q3 ), so the five are simultaneous, not independent, observables (§8.2). Eavail
keeps the inherited 135 MeV charged-pion constant (§7.1).
• Low-Eavail central values (descriptive). All four truth-level generators, GENIE [6], MINERvA Tune [7], NuWro [8] and GiBUU [9], under-predict the unfolded central value in the



quasielastic–∆ dip (0.1 ≤ Eavail < 0.4 GeV), the region of the established MINERvA lowrecoil discrepancy [7, 10]. The GENIE and NuWro predictions are flux-repaired (§7.6). The
comparisons with the tested pion-FSI dials and with Valencia 2p2h [11, 12] are in §7.7 (Fig. 19).
• High-Eavail , high-W excess (descriptive). In cell-integrated terms, most of the positive
difference from the Tune v1 prior lies at Eavail ≥ 0.8 GeV, much of it at W ≥ 1.8 GeV. A fifth
of the positive difference sits in the single widest overflow cell. The high-Eavail , high-W region
carries the largest regularization bias. In ratio, the excess also extends to low W . The four
external generators also fall below the data in this corner, each by about its overall shortfall
once GiBUU’s Eν ≈ 20 GeV sample limit is allowed for. The NuWro and GiBUU ratios contain
an uncorrected carbon-versus-CH target difference (§8.3).
• Application: joint five-dimensional generator tests. Calibrated tests of hybrid null
hypotheses for five predictions, in total (rate and shape) and in shape, reject all ten under six
stated conditions. The five predictions are Tune v1, GENIE with and without MEC, NuWro
and GiBUU; each null uses the prediction on the fine grid, with the Tune v1 composition below
it. The family-wise error rate is controlled at 5% under the stated calibration assumptions,
and the size is averaged over the nuisance priors (§B.5). The closest decisions, the NuWro and
GENIE CV shape tests, lie 1.11 and 1.30 null standard deviations beyond their boundary, with
the Holm order held fixed (§10.4). The rejections are not physical exclusions, and matched
coarse projections reject as well (§10.7). Each rejection also holds in a robustness variant, not
used for the decisions, that assumes a larger residual below the finest tested grid. That residual
is unmeasured. The pseudo-experiments lost to time limits have been rerun, for reporting only,
so every submitted draw has been observed. No recovered draw changes a decision (§10.6).
Correcting the carbon-only NuWro and GiBUU predictions to the CH target moves both
toward the data. In this descriptive re-evaluation, NuWro’s total-test rejection is not robust to
the correction, and the ten decisions are unchanged (§10.9).
• A methodological finding. The standard covariance sums single-source systematic matrices,
each with all other sources nominal, and so omits nonlinear cross-source response terms. A
corrected unified-throw test perturbs all reweightable sources together and stores the joint
mean shift separately (§4.5).
• A preliminary extended-fiducial study. Removing truth-muon cuts, with the fiducial
volume and MINOS-based selection kept, puts about 6 % of the scalar 2D rate in newly
acceptance-supported cells, which are part of tier 1. It puts about 28 % in low- or zeroefficiency cells, which form tier 2 (§11).

Principal limitations: The body states each limitation with the result it qualifies; the principal
ones are these. The quoted 2D statistical block has had no coverage test; the fixed-truth coverage
studies scored two earlier statistical bands, of which the first-built undercovers in its tails (C2 = 0.912,
vs. 0.954; §4.2, §5.2). The generator comparisons of §§7–8 are descriptive comparisons of central
values that identify no nuclear or interaction mechanism. No 3D or 4D covariance is quoted (§7).
The five-dimensional estimator recovers only part of a generator-sized truth change, with residuals of
up to 74 % in the highest-W column. No quoted uncertainty includes this residual regularization bias
(§9.3). Closure tests with backgrounds find a purity-subtraction bias of up to 4 % in the highest-W
cells. The five-dimensional central value keeps that bias, and no quoted uncertainty includes it
(§9.2). Above two dimensions the results are central values without intervals of validated coverage.
No five-dimensional measurement with total uncertainties was constructed (§9.4). The one fivedimensional coverage test, of bias-corrected statistical intervals of a deterministic estimator variant,
miscovered at the tested truths. No frequentist coverage is therefore claimed for any five-dimensional
interval (§9.5). The one five-dimensional covariance used in this note, with its (Eavail , W ) projection,
does not meet its specification’s acceptance criteria. Four measurements limit what it supports
(§9.6). The calibration of the generator tests is valid for the simulated null to the extent that
the pseudo-experiment chain reproduces the analysis applied to the data (§10.3). Their nuisance
model has no calorimetric recoil-response band (§10.8). Tier 2 of the extended-fiducial study is
prior-dominated extrapolation (§11). Point-cloud (PET) unfolding is future work (§1).
