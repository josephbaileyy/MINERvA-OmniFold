   REWRITE: The error bars on the unfolded points and projections in Fig. 7 are the stored histogram bin uncertainties; they are not derived from the covariance matrix described in §4.

quote the per-bin pulls and the truncated result together, with a regularization scan made for the

   REWRITE: We report the per-bin standardized differences alongside the truncated χ2 as descriptive measures (App. A.4). The regularization scan used an earlier covariance estimate for the exact-split cross-check and was not repeated for the covariance reported here.
