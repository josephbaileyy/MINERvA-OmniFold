3. QUOTE: "No 4D uncertainty is reported. Nor is any ratio of the unified-throw to the block covariance.
A corrected 4D covariance would use the actual minus and plus variation endpoints; MAT’s meancentered covariance blocks, normalized by 1/N ; one fixed estimator seed; and mean-centered joint
throws, with the mean shift reported separately. It would not subtract a scalar jitter floor. Because
the single-pass shifted observables exist only for events selected at the central value, re-selected event
loops (§4.7) would be needed to bound migration effects."
   REWRITE: We report neither four-dimensional uncertainties nor the ratio of the unified-throw covariance to the block covariance. Evaluating these quantities would require the negative and positive variation endpoints, MAT covariance blocks centered on their means and normalized by 1/N, and joint throws centered on their mean and evaluated with a fixed estimator seed. The mean shift would be reported separately, without subtracting a scalar jitter floor. Because shifted observables are available only for events passing the nominal selection, additional event loops with the selection reapplied would be needed to bound migration effects (§4.7).
