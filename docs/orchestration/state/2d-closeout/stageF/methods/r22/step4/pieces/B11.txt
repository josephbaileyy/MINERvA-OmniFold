Extended fiducial phase-space study

This section gives a preliminary scalar central value for an extended fiducial domain, with a threeprior dependence envelope, a hidden-variable closure and a 200-pseudo-experiment containment
diagnostic for the extension. The signal remains νµ CC in the tracker interaction fiducial volume. The
truth-muon kinematic cuts are removed, but the reconstructed MINOS-based selection is unchanged,
so the domain is an extended fiducial one, not unrestricted neutrino phase space. The result consists
only of central values and these validation tests, without a quoted covariance.

12.1

Acceptance and the extended domain

The published cuts (θµ < 20◦ , 1.5 < p∥ < 60 GeV/c, pT < 4.5 GeV/c) exclude a third of the signal.
Of the weighted true νµ CC rate in the tracker fiducial volume, 66.4 % lies inside the cuts, 22.3 % at
p∥ < 1.5 GeV/c, 11.3 % at θµ > 20◦ , and the high-pT /p∥ tails are negligible (Fig. 32). OmniFold’s

treatment of events that fail reconstruction already corrects for acceptance, so removing the truthlevel kinematic cuts is a change of configuration only: the truth denominator grows from 32.8 million
to 49 million events. In regions without acceptance the unfolding extrapolates through the step-1
regressor for missed events; the validation tests below quantify the resulting prior dependence.

12.2

Validation tests

Four tests characterize the extension (Fig. 33):
1. Published-region check. The control unfolds the standard 2D input on the published
binning, with settings identical to the extended-domain unfolding’s. In the published sub-block,
the extended-domain unfolding reproduces it with integral ratio 0.9994, median cell ratio
1.0013 and median absolute relative deviation from the control 0.57 % over all 185 reported
cells. The control’s total, 3.073 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon, agrees at the printed precision with the
purity-subtracted 2D totals (exact-split and LightGBM). The quoted 2D result, which uses
refined negative-weight subtraction, is 3.069 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon, 0.14 % lower, so the control
does not reproduce it.
2. Closure. Extended-grid self-closure using simulated reconstruction as pseudo-data returns
recovered/truth = 1.0000 in every cell, including the extension, with no event-accounting or
normalization bias from the enlarged denominator. In hidden-variable closure, a Gaussian
truth bump is injected in true Eavail , unseen by the (pT , p∥ ) unfolding. The per-cell absolute
relative deviation of the recovered cross section from the bump-reweighted (pT , p∥ ) truth has
a median of 0.17 % (90th percentile 0.65 %) in the published cells and a median of 0.77 %
(maximum 4.05 %) in the extension, both well inside the three-prior envelope.
3. Three-prior envelope. The total cross section varies by ±1.5 % across three priors (MINERvA
Tune v1, bare GENIE and NuWro-shaped). The NuWro-shaped prior reweights the truth
(pT , p∥ ) shape to two million raw NuWro events without phase-space cuts. The sample’s
mis-sampled flux (§7.6) suppresses its high-p∥ shape, and this prior was not rebuilt after the
flux repair. The per-cell half-spread of the three results, divided by their mean, has a median


of 2.9 % in the published region and 7.9 % (90th percentile 62 %) in the extension, concentrated
in zero-acceptance cells (Fig. 34).
4. Containment diagnostic. With a fixed estimator seed and one disjoint 100/100 split into
calibration and evaluation sets, widths learned from the calibration set contain the fixed truth
in 68.67 % of evaluation (bin, pseudo-experiment) pairs, compared with the 68.27 % Gaussian
nominal. Bin correlations and estimated widths preclude a global binomial error or a calibrated
test, and the closure pseudo-experiments fluctuate statistically only, so a full uncertainty band
is not tested. The statistical-band, pull and regional fractions are in App. B.3; none is used to
certify a covariance.

12.3

Central value and two-tier reporting

The preliminary extended-fiducial CC-inclusive central value on the tracker fiducial volume is
σext = 4.502 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon (central value),
compared with 3.073 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon for the control unfolding in the restricted phase space.
The extended-fiducial central value was produced with the binned purity correction, not with refined
negative-weight subtraction, the treatment designated for the extended-fiducial analysis (App. C).
We adopt two-tier reporting. Tier 1 comprises the acceptance-supported cells (ϵ ≳ 2%): the
published region and the measurable part of the extension, which carries ∼ 6 % of the rate, chiefly
in the θ > 20◦ wedge and the p∥ ∈ [0.75, 1.5] GeV/c edge. Tier 2, prior-extrapolated, comprises the
low-acceptance cells (∼ 28 % of the rate) and is flagged separately with an explicit additional threeprior envelope band. Both tiers enter the sum; only tier 1 is interpreted as an acceptance-supported
measurement. No uncertainty magnitude is assigned beyond the three-prior envelope, because no
extended-domain covariance has been constructed with re-selected lateral bands under the procedure
of §4.5.

13

Summary and outlook

Summary. Beyond the results stated with their scopes in the executive summary (p. 1), the body
supports two further conclusions.
• The refined negative-weight subtraction, which the sixteen-cell result uses (§10), largely removes
the bias that purity-based background subtraction leaves in the highest-W cells (§9.2). In a
two-dimensional closure test with backgrounds, the purity and refined estimates differ by more
than one statistical standard deviation in 14 of the 205 cells. In those cells the purity estimate

lies up to 5.9 σstat from the truth, and the refined one within 0.44 σstat . On this basis, the
two-dimensional result uses the refined estimator (App. C).
• The joint tests’ design was fixed before any production pseudo-experiment or observed statistic,
with two data-informed elements (§11.3). The tests recover established discrepancies and show
no evidence of discrimination beyond matched coarse projections (§11).
Principal limitations. The executive summary lists the principal limitations (p. 2), each with
the section that states it. The quoted two-dimensional statistical block has had no coverage test
(§4.2, §5.2).
Outlook. The scalar extended-fiducial study (§12) yields a preliminary central value σext =
4.502 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon, against 3.073 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon for its control unfolding in the
restricted phase space. New acceptance-supported cells contribute about 6 % of the rate; separately
labeled prior-dominated extrapolation contributes about 28 %. Further refinements are a fifth
external generator, NEUT, and a finer 44-cell comparison with the Ascencio et al. measurement
(the maximal-common-grid comparison is in §7.8). A flux and muon-energy-scale posterior derived
with MAT-MINERvA, independently of the published analysis, would determine the correlation ρ
between the leading flux mode and the muon-energy-scale band (§4) entirely within this analysis.
Reusing the unbinned result. The public ATLAS charged-particle phase-space release [61]
exemplifies unbinned distribution. The emerging omnifold_publication proposal [62] emphasizes
weight-family metadata, normalization, row alignment, and checksums. A MINERvA release
should likewise specify event identity, estimator and uncertainty-family definitions, units, support,
normalization, and a reproducible projection example. App. G specifies this release for our analysis,
including which of its objects already have fixed identities and which are still to be produced. A
local release candidate exists, with checksums and a verifier that recomputes and checks its results;
no release has been tagged or deposited. Rebuilding this note’s PDFs assembles documents
and reproduces no scientific result (App. G). New projections require their own applicability checks;
nominal weights alone cannot certify arbitrary downstream inference (App. B.4). The release design
and its public example have no bearing on the choice of the five-axis covariance of the configuration-(a)
central value (§9.6).
