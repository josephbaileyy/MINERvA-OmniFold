## 4. The adoption rule, fixed before any score

**Note, per piece.** NEW is the piece re-split from NEW's PDF; the base is §2's piece. A piece is adopted if, and only
if, all three hold:
1. **The pairwise.** NEW beats the base in at least 3 of the 4 blind P2N runs: sol2 and gemini, each in both orders,
   with `note-rating/P2N.prompt.txt` unchanged. A run that fails both attempts counts as a loss for NEW.
2. **No sol2 order flip.** sol2 picks the same version in both orders.
3. **P1N does not fall.** On the 3 sol2 P1N runs, NEW's mean is at least the base's mean and NEW's lowest run is at
   least the base's lowest run.

A piece that fails any of the three keeps its base text. The adopted pieces are assembled, rebuilt and re-gated.

**Paper.** r21's rule, unchanged: NEW beats the base in at least 3 of 4 blind P2 runs (sol2 and gemini, both orders), sol2
does not flip, and on the 3 sol2 P1 runs NEW's mean and lowest run are each at least the base's.

## 5. Measurement and the stopping rule

- **Measured after each round:**
  - P1 sol2 ×3 on the paper;
  - P1N sol2 ×3 on every note piece. The note's score is the mean over its pieces.
- **Targets:** the anchors' re-measured 3.667 for the paper and 3.875 for the note.
- **The decision's stopping rule:** stop when P1's mean on each document reaches its target, or after three rounds with
  no movement in P1.
- **"Movement"** (the director's definition, fixed here before any score): a rise in the document's sol2 P1 mean over
  its three runs above the previous round's mean. At P1's resolution, that means at least one run up by 0.5. "No
  movement" means the mean is equal or lower. For the note, the mean is P1N's piece mean.
- **Limit:** P1N's resolution in the 3 band is low (34 of 45 base runs are exactly 3.0), so that limit is reported with
  every round's score.
