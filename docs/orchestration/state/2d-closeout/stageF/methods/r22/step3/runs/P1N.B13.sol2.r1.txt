1. QUOTE: "The reductions keep the per-cell values, the identity of each
input and the exclusions, and distinguish complete outputs from checkpoints that carry no metadata.

   REWRITE: The reductions retain per-cell values, input identifiers, and exclusions. For checkpoints lacking metadata, only iterations that reproduce the recorded values are included. The reductions were checked numerically.

The appropriate background treatment for the configuration-(a) five-dimensional central value remains under study.
3. QUOTE: "Background treatment and choice of covariance. The choice of background treatment is
independent of the choice of covariance, and this appendix selects no five-dimensional covariance
for use. The lateral-band component from re-selected event loops is built, with five bands and ten
endpoint unfolds whose distinct checksums are listed in the record of the five-axis covariance of
the configuration-(a) central value. The status of that five-axis covariance and the scope of its use
are stated in §9.6. The choice of background treatment settles one of the conditions for using a
component; every other condition still applies."
   REWRITE: Background treatment and covariance selection are separate choices. The lateral-band covariance component for configuration (a) comprises five bands evaluated with ten endpoint unfolds from re-selected event loops; their checksums are recorded with the five-axis covariance. Section 9.6 specifies the validation status and permitted use of that covariance. Consistent background treatment satisfies one requirement for including a component; the remaining validation requirements must also be met.
