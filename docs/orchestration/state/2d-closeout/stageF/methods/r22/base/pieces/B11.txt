Extended fiducial phase-space study

This section gives a preliminary scalar central value for an extended fiducial domain, with a threeprior dependence envelope, a hidden-variable closure and a 200-toy containment diagnostic for the
extension. The signal remains νµ CC in the tracker interaction fiducial volume. The truth-muon
kinematic cuts are removed, but the reconstructed MINOS-based selection is unchanged, so the
domain is an extended fiducial one, not unrestricted neutrino phase space. No covariance is quoted
for it, so the result consists of central values and these validation tests.

12.1

Acceptance and the extended domain

The published cuts (θµ < 20◦ , 1.5 < p∥ < 60 GeV/c, pT < 4.5 GeV/c) exclude a third of the
signal. Of the weighted true νµ CC rate in the tracker fiducial volume, 66.4 % lies inside the cuts,
22.3 % at p∥ < 1.5 GeV/c, 11.3 % at θµ > 20◦ , and the high-pT /p∥ tails are negligible (Fig. 32).
OmniFold’s treatment of events that fail reconstruction already corrects for acceptance, so removing
the truth-level kinematic cuts is a change of configuration only: the truth denominator grows from
32.8 million to 49 million events, and the reconstruction selection, including the MINOS match, is

unchanged. In regions without acceptance the unfold extrapolates through the step-1 regressor for
missed events; the validation tests below quantify the resulting prior dependence.

12.2

Validation battery

Four tests characterize the extension (Fig. 33):
1. Published-region check. In the published sub-block, the extended unfold reproduces
the control (standard 2D input, paper grid, identical settings) with integral ratio 0.9994,
median cell ratio 1.0013, and median deviation 0.57 % across all 185 reported cells: in the
published region the extended unfold agrees with the control at this level. The control’s total,
3.073 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon, equals at the printed precision that of the purity-subtracted 2D
central values (exact-split and LightGBM). The quoted 2D result, with refined negative-weight
subtraction, is 3.069 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon, 0.14 % lower, so the control no longer reproduces it.
2. Closure. Extended-grid self-closure using simulated reconstruction as pseudo-data returns
recovered/truth = 1.0000 in every cell, including the extension, with no event-accounting or
normalization bias from the enlarged denominator. In hidden-variable closure, a Gaussian
truth bump is injected in true Eavail , unseen by the (pT , p∥ ) unfold. The recovered cross section
reproduces the bump-reweighted (pT , p∥ ) truth to median 0.17 % (90th percentile 0.65 %) in
the published cells and median 0.77 % (max 4.05 %) in the extension, both well inside the prior
envelope.
3. Three-prior envelope. Repeating the unfold with three priors (MINERvA Tune v1, bare
GENIE, and NuWro-shaped) spans the total cross section by only ±1.5 %. The NuWro-shaped
prior reweights the truth (pT , p∥ ) shape to two million raw NuWro events without phase-space
cuts. The sample’s mis-sampled flux (§7.6) suppresses its high-p∥ shape, and this prior was
not rebuilt after the flux repair. Per-cell half-spread/mean has median 2.9 % in the published
region and 7.9 % (90th percentile 62 %) in the extension, concentrated in zero-acceptance cells
(Fig. 34).
4. Containment diagnostic. With a fixed estimator seed and one 100/100 calibration–evaluation
split, widths learned from the disjoint calibration half contain the fixed truth in 68.67 % of

evaluation bin–toy cells, compared with the 68.27 % Gaussian nominal. Bin correlations and
estimated widths preclude a global binomial error or a calibrated test. The diagnostic probes
the statistical closure-toy stream, not a full uncertainty band. The statistical-band, pull and
regional fractions are in App. B.3; none is used as a covariance-certification test.

12.3

Central value and two-tier reporting

The preliminary extended-fiducial CC-inclusive central value on the tracker fiducial volume is
σext = 4.502 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon (central value),

compared with 3.073 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon for the control unfold in the restricted phase space. The
extended-fiducial central value was produced with the binned purity correction (the battery of 2026-0610), not with refined negative-weight subtraction, the treatment designated for the extended-fiducial
analysis (App. C). We adopt two-tier reporting. Tier 1 comprises the acceptance-supported cells
(ϵ ≳ 2%): the published region and the measurable part of the extension, which carries ∼ 6 % of the
rate, chiefly in the θ > 20◦ wedge and the p∥ ∈ [0.75, 1.5] GeV/c edge. Tier 2, prior-extrapolated,
comprises the dead cells (∼ 28 % of the rate) and is flagged separately with an explicit additional
three-prior envelope band. Both tiers enter the sum; only tier 1 is interpreted as an acceptancesupported measurement. No uncertainty magnitude is assigned beyond the three-prior envelope,
because no extended-domain covariance has been constructed with re-selected lateral bands under
the procedure of §4.5.

13

Summary and outlook

Summary. The executive summary (p. 1) gives each result with its scope and lists the principal
limitations. Two further conclusions follow from the body.
• The refined negative-weight subtraction, which the sixteen-cell result uses (§10), largely removes
the bias that purity-based background subtraction leaves in the highest-W cells (§9.2). In a
two-dimensional closure test with backgrounds, the purity and refined estimates differ by more
than one statistical standard deviation in 14 of the 205 cells. In those cells the purity estimate
lies up to 5.9 σstat from the truth, and the refined one within 0.44 σstat . On this basis, the
two-dimensional result uses the refined estimator (App. C).
• The joint tests’ design was fixed before any production pseudo-experiment or observed statistic,
with two data-informed elements (§11.3). The tests recover established discrepancies and show
no evidence of discrimination beyond matched coarse projections (§11).
Principal limitations. The executive summary lists the principal limitations (p. 2), each with
the section that states it. The quoted two-dimensional statistical block has had no coverage test
(§4.2, §5.2).
Outlook. The scalar extended-fiducial study (§12) yields a preliminary central value σext =
4.502 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon, against 3.073 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon for its control unfold in the restricted
phase space. New acceptance-supported cells contribute about 6 % of the rate; separately labeled
prior-dominated extrapolation contributes about 28 %. Further refinements are a fifth external
generator, NEUT, and a finer 44-cell comparison with the Ascencio et al. measurement (the
maximal-common-grid comparison is in §7.8). A flux–muon-energy-scale posterior derived from
MAT-MINERvA, independent of the published analysis, would let the correlation ρ between the
leading flux mode and the muon-energy-scale band (§4) be derived entirely within this analysis.
Reusing the unbinned result. The public ATLAS charged-particle phase-space release [61]
exemplifies unbinned distribution. The emerging omnifold_publication proposal [62] emphasizes
weight-family metadata, normalization, row alignment, and checksums. A MINERvA release
should likewise specify event identity, estimator and uncertainty-family definitions, units, support,
normalization, and a reproducible projection example. App. G specifies this release for our analysis,
including which of its objects already have fixed identities and which are still to be produced. A
local release candidate, with checksums and a verifier that recomputes and checks its results, exists,

but no release has been tagged or deposited; rebuilding this note’s PDFs assembles documents
and reproduces no scientific result (App. G). New projections require their own applicability checks;
nominal weights alone cannot certify arbitrary downstream inference (App. B.4). The release design
and its public example have no bearing on the choice of the five-axis covariance of the configuration-(a)
central value (§9.6).
