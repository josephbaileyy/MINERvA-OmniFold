The sixteen-cell five-observable measurement

This section gives a five-dimensional cross-section result with total uncertainties: the data result of
configuration (e) (§3.2) on 16 cells, R00–R15, with the reported covariance Ctotal + Cresp . It is a
separate product from the configuration-(a) five-dimensional central value of Secs. 8 and 9 and from
the five-axis covariance used with that central value (§9.6). Statements in those sections about that
central value and that covariance do not describe this result, and statements here do not describe

them. The section gives the cells and the result (§10.1), the covariance and what it is built from
(§10.2), the fake-data tests of the estimator’s bias against the covariance (§10.3) and the comparison
with generator predictions (§10.4).

10.2

The reported covariance

The reported covariance is Ctotal + Cresp , with Ctotal = Ce + Cmodel . Ce sums the statistical bootstrap
of configuration (e), the flux universes weighted by the neutrino–electron and inverse-muon-decay
constraint, the interaction and detector bands, the lateral bands, the normalization and the trainingseed block. The training-seed block was measured and is zero in every cell: with full-sample binning
and deterministic training, the unfolds at the other seeds are bitwise equal to the central one.
Table 11 gives each group’s per-cell relative σ.
Cmodel is built from the change in the unfolded data when the prior is reweighted to each of three
generators, GENIE + MEC, NuWro and GiBUU: each vertex is that relative shift of the data result,
and Cmodel is the sum of their outer products, unscaled. It is generator-shape model dependence,
not a calibrated bound. Its coverage at truths held out of its construction has not been validated.
GiBUU’s shape is applied only below p∥ = 10 GeV/c, above which the prior is unchanged; between

p∥ = 6 and 10 GeV/c GiBUU’s sample lacks the part above Eν = 20 GeV: at least 4.9–5.3% of the
cross section there, from GENIE CV and NuWro samples that stop at Eν = 50 GeV. GiBUU’s vertex
carries 0.890 of Cmodel ’s trace, NuWro’s 0.095 and GENIE + MEC’s 0.014. In every cell, 77–100%
of the prior weight lies beside a step in the reweighting ratio larger than 0.1 drawn by the noise gate
or a cut, and the shifts enter as measured.
Cresp is the response of configuration (e)’s data result to a one-standard-deviation scale of the
simulation’s reconstructed recoil energy, for protons, mesons, electromagnetic deposits and other
deposits separately, summed over the four. It does not cover neutron or muon deposits, the effects of
particle species, energy dependence and resolution beyond a scale, the null distributions of the joint
tests, the configuration-(a) products or the two-, three- and four-dimensional results. A cross-talk
term, reported beside Cresp and not summed into it, has a median per-cell relative σ of 0.36%. Its
median per-cell relative σ is 2.14%, and adding it raises the total’s σ by a factor of 1.011–1.220
(median 1.048). Of the trace of (Ctotal + Cresp )/f f T , Ctotal carries 0.9262 and the four response
species 0.0168, 0.0436, 0.0071 and 0.0062.

Two truths are held out of the analysis: the GENIE v3 tunes G18_10a_02_11b and AR23_20i_00_000, generated for this test. Each enters as its gated, normalized shape ratio to GENIE CV
on the fine grid, built as the reweighting ratios of the Cmodel vertices are, pooled over a coarser cell
or set to one where the tune’s sample fails the ratio’s statistical gate, and applied to the simulated
truth under the simulation’s detector response. At each, 20 Poisson pseudo-experiments on half the
simulation and a noise-free closure are graded against the full reported covariance, as truths without
a vertex, in a group of their own that is never pooled with the groups above. The bias passes both
criteria in all four records. The mean of the pseudo-experiments gives χ2 = 11.84 (p = 0.76; largest
|z| 0.62, in R11) at G18_10a_02_11b and 16.86 (p = 0.39; 0.64, in R07) at AR23_20i_00_000; the

four records, the bias is below 0.65 of the cell’s reported σ. The group has 0 of 64 cells with |z| > 2
(Gaussian reference 2.91) and 0 of 4 records with p < 0.01 (reference 0.04). The references count the
four records as independent, but they are two truths, each counted by its pseudo-experiments and
by its closure, so they are context only.
The two tunes use the same quasielastic cross-section model, GENIE’s NievesQELCCPXSec,
AR23_20i_00_000 with a z-expansion form factor, and the MEC model of G18_10a_02_11b is
of the Valencia family used in the GENIE + MEC vertex. Both are GENIE tunes, so they are
partial evidence and not independent tests. Descriptively, without the GENIE + MEC vertex
the pseudo-experiments give χ2 = 15.15 (p = 0.51) and 24.60 (p = 0.077), and the closures 14.35
(p = 0.57) and 22.43 (p = 0.13). The model-only χ2 , defined as in Table 14, are 817–922 in the four
records, with R00 beyond 2σ at AR23_20i_00_000 in both its records. The grading, rerun with
the two truths, reproduces every record of the fake-data truths above exactly. This is a bias test,
not a coverage test: Poisson pseudo-experiments with no draws of the systematic parameters cannot
test the width of the intervals, and the detector response is not probed. The coverage of the result’s

intervals has not been validated.

Weakening only the flux correlations by 10 % lowers the χ2 of the 16-cell predictions by 24–
48 %. The same change to the detector or lateral correlations lowers it by at most 18 %. With the
correlations of the interaction, flux, detector and lateral groups removed, every 16-cell prediction
keeps χ2 ≥ 45 for 16 degrees of freedom. Table 17 gives each group’s sensitivity, GiBUU’s eight-cell
values included. These are descriptive sensitivities of the reported χ2 , not alternative results: one
group’s correlations are shrunk toward the diagonal by a fraction α of the way, with its variances kept.
The flux correlations are themselves estimated from the 100 PPFX flux universes, weighted by the
neutrino–electron and inverse-muon-decay constraint (§10.2). Rebuilding the flux block from 2000
bootstrap replicates of those universes, with its variances kept, gives 16–84 % percentile bands of the
total χ2 : GENIE 2.12.10 CV 106–112 around 109, GENIE 2.12.10 + Valencia MEC 262–283 around
272, NuWro 21.09 185–191 around 188, MINERvA Tune v1 315–350 around 330, and GiBUU 2019,
on its 8 cells, 57–59 around 58. In each, the bootstrap median lies above the reported value, by
0.25, 1.22, 0.75, 3.12 and 0.16 in the same order. These bands measure the estimation scatter of the
reported χ2 given these universes, not how much it would change if the flux correlations were wrong,
which the weakening above probes; they are not intervals for the χ2 under the true correlations.
