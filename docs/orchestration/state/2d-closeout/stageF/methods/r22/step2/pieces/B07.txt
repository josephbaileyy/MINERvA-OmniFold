Fifth axis: W and the (Eavail , W ) plane

For the central values, Valencia 2p2h closes about half of the low-recoil gap (§7.7) but leaves a separate
excess over the MINERvA Tune v1 prior at high Eavail . There the predicted 2p2h contribution is
zero, and the two tested pion final-state-interaction variations change the prediction by less than
one percent, too little to explain the excess.
GiBUU is low throughout the Eavail tail. Its W -integrated five-dimensional data/generator ratio
is 1.59 at Eavail ∈ [0.8, 1.5) and 1.37 at [1.5, 3.0), in configuration (a), the LightGBM unfolding
with purity-based subtraction (§3.2); its sample stops at Eν ≈ 20 GeV (§8.5). GENIE CV instead
matches data at [0.8, 1.5) and is low at [1.5, 3.0), where its three-dimensional data/CV ratio is 1.10
(§7.7) in configuration (e), the deterministic learner with refined negative-weight subtraction.
Adding the hadronic invariant mass W as the fifth OmniFold axis maps where in W the high49

Eavail excess lies. The validation of §9.1 includes a model-dependence study; it does not establish
a bias–variance tradeoff across estimator settings and provides no new total uncertainty for this
projection.

Truth W is the experimenter’s invariant mass W = M 2 + 2M (Eν − Eµ ) − Q2 , with struck-nucleon
mass M . Reconstructed W combines the calorimetric energy transfer q0 of the reconstructed-q3
estimator and the muon-kinematic Q2 with the average nucleon mass. The external generator
predictions instead compute truth W with the neutron mass for every event and place events with
W 2 ≤ 0 in the lowest W bin, whereas the analysis uses the struck-nucleon mass and excludes
such events from its five-observable truth sample. In the GENIE and NuWro samples this moves
0.14–0.15% of the events into another W bin. In GENIE CV and NuWro (without their 50–100 GeV
samples) it changes the coarse-cell predictions by about 0.1 % at the median and at most 2.2 %. In
any cell, that is at most about one Monte Carlo standard error of the prediction. The change was
not evaluated at cell level for GENIE+MEC and cannot be evaluated for GiBUU.
Like q3 (§7.8), and unlike Eavail , W changes under the lateral systematic shifts, so a shifted
W is stored for each lateral universe. The event-loop output files also store truth hadronic
diagnostics: the proton and pion multiplicities and the leading-hadron angle. The W edges
[0, 1.1, 1.4, 1.8, 2.2, 3.0, 100] GeV bracket the quasielastic peak, the ∆, the transition region and
the DIS tail.
The configuration-(a) five-dimensional unfolding in (pT , p∥ , Eavail , q3 , W ) agrees under marginalization with the lower-dimensional results: integrating over W reproduces the configuration-(a) 4D
normalization to 0.11 % (5D/4D total = 1.0011) with median differences per axis of 0.3 % to 1.5 %,
and dσ/dW is finite and non-negative in all six bins.

8.4

Distribution of the excess in the (Eavail , W ) plane

Figure 24 shows the unfolded five-dimensional result projected onto (Eavail , W ), with the MINERvA
Tune v1 prediction subtracted. That prediction is the OmniFold prior, the simulation’s truth weighted
by its full tune weight; the untuned GENIE CV of §8.5 is a separate prediction. In cell-integrated
terms, the positive excess lies mostly at high Eavail and high W . The two highest-Eavail bands
carry 57 % of it, and the region Eavail ≥ 0.8 GeV carries 67 %, with 83 % of that at W ≥ 1.8 GeV.
The largest cell, the corner Eavail > 3 GeV, W > 3 GeV, holds 22 %. A secondary excess lies at
W < 1.1 GeV, with a small deficit at W ≃ 1.4–1.8 GeV.
These statements hold under the nominal detector response, and the regularization bias of Sec. 9
is largest at high Eavail and high W . Estimator-seed shifts of central values are a small fraction of
the projected uncertainties (§9.6). The seed sensitivity of the shares, which are ratios of central-value
differences, has not been measured separately. The response-mismatch closure of Appendix D is
designed to test whether an incorrect hadronic response can move strength into or out of the low-W
component. It has not yet produced a result.

Comparison with four external generators

All four external generators fall below the data in the high-Eavail , high-W corner. Figure 25 compares
the (Eavail , W ) predictions of GENIE CV, GENIE+Valencia 2p2h/MEC, NuWro and GiBUU. As in
§7.6, each is normalized by its flux-averaged total CC cross section per nucleon, and those of GENIE

and NuWro are flux-corrected. The 3D and (Eavail , W ) comparisons use the same five-dimensional
histogram of each generator. GENIE CV totals 2.76 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon in the measurement phase
space in both; GENIE+MEC uses the normalization convention of the 3D comparison.
In the corner (Eavail ≥ 0.8 GeV, W ≥ 1.8 GeV), data/generator = 1.14, 1.14, 1.16 and 1.61,
respectively. For GENIE and NuWro the corner ratios exceed their integrated ratios (1.11, 1.07
and 1.15) by 1 % to 7 %, compared with 16 % for GiBUU, whose integrated ratio is 1.39 (σ =
2.22 × 10−38 cm2 /nucleon, against 3.07 × 10−38 for the data).
GiBUU’s larger contrast comes from its sample’s energy limit: it was generated only up to
Eν ≈ 20 GeV (§7.6). In GENIE and NuWro, neutrinos above this energy carry 14.3 % to 14.8 %
of the corner cross section and 6.8 % to 7.4 % overall. Removing them raises these generators’
corner-to-integrated contrast to 9 % to 16 %, bracketing GiBUU’s. If GiBUU is instead supplied with
the shares that GENIE and NuWro carry above this energy, data/GiBUU becomes about 1.37 in the
corner and 1.29 integrated. With the GENIE and NuWro fluxes corrected and GiBUU supplied with
the shares above this energy, each generator’s central-value corner ratio lies close to its integrated
ratio. GiBUU is lower than the others everywhere.
Valencia 2p2h raises the GENIE+MEC integrated prediction while leaving the corner essentially
unchanged (1.142 → 1.139), since it populates low W . At W ∈ [2.2, 3.0) GeV, data/generator = 1.12,
1.11, 1.13 and 1.34, again close to the integrated ratios. At 1.4 ≤ W < 1.8 GeV, instead, both
GENIE predictions lie 9 % to 12 % above the data. These are central-value ratios. No compatibility
metric based on the configuration-(a) five-axis covariance is quoted, because the estimator-seed
sensitivity of its construction, measured with two companion covariances, exceeds the limit set before
