   REWRITE: Statistical uncertainties were evaluated using a 300-replica bootstrap ensemble. In closure tests at nominal truth, 97.7% of standardized residuals fall within two standard deviations, though formal frequentist coverage on an independent simulated sample has not been evaluated.
