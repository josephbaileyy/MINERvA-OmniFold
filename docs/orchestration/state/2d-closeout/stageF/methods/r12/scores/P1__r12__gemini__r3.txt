RATING: 2.5
JUSTIFICATION: While the manuscript is grammatically sound and technically meticulous, its prose frequently departs from the archival journal register of Physical Review D into that of an internal technical report, collaboration audit memo, or software QA document characterized by development-history narratives, batch-job accounting, and defensive record-keeping.

1. QUOTE: "The test design was fixed before any observed statistic was computed; its reviewers had seen descriptive per-cell residuals of the data before it was fixed, and a bounded extension of the calibration’s computing budget was decided with observed p values visible, which the result above covers, since it holds for any stopping rule (Ref. [14], Sec. 10.3). Computing time limits interrupted or prevented 224 calibration and 53 power pseudo-experiments; when rerun, none of them reaches the observed statistic in any test or shifted null, so no assignment of the lost outcomes changes any decision (Ref. [14], Sec. 10.6)."
   REWRITE: The hypothesis-testing protocol and its stopping rule were defined prior to the calculation of test statistics to ensure familywise error-rate control. While computational timeouts affected an initial set of 224 calibration and 53 power pseudo-experiments, reevaluating these instances produced no exceedances of the observed statistics and left all test decisions unchanged.

2. QUOTE: "In 100 closure pseudo-experiments at a fixed truth, an earlier version of the statistical band undercovered in its tails: pooled over experiments and bins, 91.2 % of standardized residuals fell within two standard deviations, against 95.4 % nominal. Rescoring the same pseudo-experiments against the rebuilt band removes the tail undercoverage: 97.7 % fall within two standard deviations and 78.1 % within one. Because the simulated part of the rebuilt band reuses the resampling mechanism of those pseudo-experiments, this rescoring is not an independent coverage test (Ref. [14], Sec. 5.2). The rebuilt band is not the quoted block, which is a separate 300-replica ensemble; neither has been tested on independent pseudo-experiments."
   REWRITE: In 100 closure pseudo-experiments evaluated at nominal truth, 78.1% and 97.7% of standardized residuals fell within one and two standard deviations of the revised statistical band, respectively. However, because the resampled background templates share the pseudo-experiment generation mechanism, this test does not constitute an independent assessment of coverage.

3. QUOTE: "The estimators are also unstable at the level of rounding. Rounding-level input changes move the results of the higher-capacity refinement by up to 2.6 statistical standard deviations, as they move those of the baseline estimator (2.5), so the refinement fails a data-stability requirement of at most 0.3 (median) and 1.0 (maximum) statistical standard deviations."
   REWRITE: The estimators also display numerical sensitivity to input precision: rounding-level variations displace the unfolded cross sections by up to 2.6 statistical standard deviations for the refined estimator and 2.5 for the baseline model, exceeding the intended stability tolerance.
