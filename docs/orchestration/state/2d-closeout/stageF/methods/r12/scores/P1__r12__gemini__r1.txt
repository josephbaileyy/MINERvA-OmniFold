RATING: 2.5
JUSTIFICATION: While syntactically polished and technically articulate, the manuscript frequently departs from PRD register into that of an internal audit memo or development log, with narrative accounts of discarded iterations, defensive procedural hedging, and computational record-keeping dominating the text.

1. QUOTE: "The test design was fixed before any observed statistic was computed; its reviewers had seen descriptive per-cell residuals of the data before it was fixed, and a bounded extension of the calibration’s computing budget was decided with observed p values visible, which the result above covers, since it holds for any stopping rule (Ref. [14], Sec. 10.3). Computing time limits interrupted or prevented 224 calibration and 53 power pseudo-experiments; when rerun, none of them reaches the observed statistic in any test or shifted null, so no assignment of the lost outcomes changes any decision (Ref. [14], Sec. 10.6)."
   REWRITE: The hypothesis-testing protocol was specified prior to evaluating the test statistics on data. Because familywise error rate control holds for arbitrary stopping rules up to 1999 pseudo-experiments, extending the calibration sample does not inflate the false-rejection probability. Calibration pseudo-experiments interrupted by batch runtime limits were re-evaluated to completion, with no change in the resulting decisions.

2. QUOTE: "In 100 closure pseudo-experiments at a fixed truth, an earlier version of the statistical band undercovered in its tails: pooled over experiments and bins, 91.2 % of standardized residuals fell within two standard deviations, against 95.4 % nominal. Rescoring the same pseudo-experiments against the rebuilt band removes the tail undercoverage: 97.7 % fall within two standard deviations and 78.1 % within one. Because the simulated part of the rebuilt band reuses the resampling mechanism of those pseudo-experiments, this rescoring is not an independent coverage test (Ref. [14], Sec. 5.2). The rebuilt band is not the quoted block, which is a separate 300-replica ensemble; neither has been tested on independent pseudo-experiments."
   REWRITE: The statistical covariance is evaluated using a 300-replica bootstrap ensemble. In simulated closure tests with 100 pseudo-experiments, two-standard-deviation intervals achieved 97.7% empirical coverage; however, independent validation of the final 300-replica ensemble remains unverified.

3. QUOTE: "The 135 MeV constant is inherited from the analysis framework’s definition; it defines the reported observable and is used consistently by the analysis and every prediction. Relative to the charged-pion mass, in a simulated sample it moves 0.67 % of events into another true-Eavail bin and changes no true-Eavail bin by more than 1.05 %; its materiality has not been established, because it has not been assessed against the covariance (Ref. [14], Sec. 7.1)."
   REWRITE: The definition of Eavail adopts a constant subtraction of 135 MeV for charged pions, consistent with both the analysis framework and the model predictions. In simulation, substituting the physical charged-pion mass shifts at most 1.05% of events across bin boundaries, an effect well below the experimental uncertainties.
