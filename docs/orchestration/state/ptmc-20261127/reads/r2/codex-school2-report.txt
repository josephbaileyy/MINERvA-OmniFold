## Part B — Findings before binding

1. **BLOCKER — §2.2 and C-COMP, establish that the resampled denominator belongs to the CV estimator.** On positive support, the proposed algebra is correct **if** the canonical denominator rows share the corresponding original-signal draws and have equal or cellwise proportional event weights. Writing \(A_j\) for the fixed flux, exposure, target, area and unit conversion, both methods extract \(x_j^*=A_jD_j^*(U_j^*/T_j^*)\). For IBU, this gives OOFVC’s \(A_js_{K,j}^*D_j^*/\sum_i M_{ij}^*\). For OmniFold, \(U_j^*/T_j^*\) is the resampled truth-weighted mean of its step-2 ratios. The driver, however, loads the canonical denominator and signal separately with different filtering. `hasTruthOnlyMisses` selects the frozen production branch and supplies no event-level verification. Equal CV sums alone cannot establish the coupling. With no moved weight, the proposed formula equals \(T_{\mathrm{orig,CV}}/D_{\mathrm{CV}}\) algebraically on positive support. Multiply–divide arithmetic need not recover the frozen ROOT value bit for bit. C-COMP also permits checking a recorded \(c^*\) without checking the histogram actually consumed. **Fix:** bind and verify the denominator-to-signal event mapping and weights, or resample matched canonical denominator rows directly with the same factors. Assert the Phase-17 precondition, bind the actual numerator, denominator and completeness passed to both extractions, and require an all-one R fixture to reproduce R CV. Reuse the frozen production histogram explicitly for the no-migration bitwise regression. State which denominator histograms are CV diagnostics and which drive replica extraction. `re2_driver_at_M.py:644,774,1230,1322,1792,2303,2408,2431,2479`, `OOFVC-DECLARATION.md:182,235`, `PTMC-DECLARATION.md:127,247`.

2. **BLOCKER — §2.2 and §7, define support and the consequences of a failed draw.** \(T_{\mathrm{orig,CV}}=0\) is not the driver’s zero-denominator condition. ROOT divides by the canonical denominator, and extraction emits zero when completeness is nonpositive. A cell with \(D_{\mathrm{CV}}=T_{\mathrm{orig,CV}}=0\) can have moved truth weight and a nonzero unfolded count that extraction suppresses. A zero on only one side of the original-signal/denominator comparison invalidates the proposed coupling. With positive CV support, \(T_{\mathrm{orig}}^*=0\) is a possible Poisson draw. With moved weight it gives positive-over-zero, and without moved weight it gives \(0/0\). The draft stops both without saying how this interacts with retries or the fixed ensemble. **Fix:** specify a cell table covering structural zeros, one-sided mismatches, replica loss of support, and nonfinite or invalid operands. Preflight the support on all 224 cells and every fixed seed. If retaining the STOP policy, abort the read on a deterministic support failure and do not substitute a seed. Report any cells where the central extraction suppresses moved support. With a verified coupling and common CV/replica functional, evaluating completeness on resampled inputs does not itself alter \(\delta_R\) or introduce an extraction mismatch. It changes \(C_{\delta,R}\) relative to a frozen-\(c\) bootstrap through the replica-specific scaling shared by the two methods. Nonlinear bootstrap centering and any conditioning on accepted support can still affect the empirical null. `PTMC-DECLARATION.md:140,247,262,361`, `re2_driver_at_M.py:1239,1300,1322`, `ref_c2c1_read.py:128`.

3. **BLOCKER — C-PROTO and §5, remove the inference licensed by the five-seed bound.** Marginal standard deviations cannot bound errors in retained eigenmodes. For example, with unit marginal variances and a retained eigenvalue \(10^{-10}\) in the \((1,-1)\) direction, an error vector \((0.001,-0.001)\) passes the coordinatewise limit and has squared whitened norm 20,000. Protocol errors can also rotate the retained subspace or move an individual leave-one-out maximum. Five seeds miss 195 paired differences, and the pilot does not bound the effect on the central difference in the statistic’s metric. Using \(C_B\) is legitimate for a descriptive scale and creates no computational loop. As a qualification, it is self-referential because the separate-refit variation at issue can inflate the scale used to approve it. **Fix:** keep the five-seed check as a diagnostic, state the different protocols whenever printing both triples, and remove “all else held fixed” and the convention-only implication of “stay.” To license that comparison, bind a shared-refinement production arm at CV and all 200 seeds with its own budget. Use explicit per-K finite/zero rules for the diagnostic. `PTMC-DECLARATION.md:282,329,335`, `REF-C2C1-DECLARATION.md:98,127`, `ref_c2c1_read.py:115,128`.

4. **BLOCKER — C-DRAW-R, verify signed weights before refinement.** The driver applies the data multiplicities, applies the background multiplicities to `inj_w`, concatenates signed weights, and replaces them with `w_ref`. At OmniFold entry, the stated exact equality to CV weights times the draws is valid for the MC truth/reco inputs, but generally false for the measured-side rows. Checking `b_bkg` against an injection list produced by the same path can also miss a shared entry-order error. Sums alone cannot certify historical row pairing. **Fix:** independently reconstruct ordered entry IDs, coordinates and signed weights from the original loaders and check them at `refine_stay_positive` entry. At OmniFold entry, verify the actual refinement output and ordered MC weights. Compare the historical stream flags, offsets, lengths and all three full-stream sums; describe those records as aggregate checks. Bind fixtures for row permutations, zero draws, upper endpoints and fake refusal. `PTMC-DECLARATION.md:236`, `re2_driver_at_M.py:343,387,2147,2187,2244,2279`, `oofvc_driver.diff:20`.

The literal “skipped and appended” sets therefore overlap by 536,518. **Fix:** require the background skip set to equal the rows file. Partition that set into appended and reco-outside entries, require those two subsets to be disjoint, and require the full skip set to be disjoint from the actual injection IDs. Keep the in-grid histogram removal check and separately audit exact upper endpoints, which production can inject even though ROOT bins them as overflow.
