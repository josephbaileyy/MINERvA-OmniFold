2. **[BLOCKER] `C-DRAW-R` checks unrefined Poisson factors on weights *reaching `ohf.omnifold`*, which fails by construction after `refine_stay_positive`**
   - **Location:** [PTMC-DECLARATION.md](file:///Users/josephbailey/ptmc-reads/decl-r2-agy/inputs/PTMC-DECLARATION.md#L238-L243) (§3.1 `C-DRAW-R`).
   - **Evidence:** `C-DRAW-R` states that row by row, `"the weights reaching the OmniFold call equal the CV weights times the expected factors"` for `"data rows: the data draw"` and `"injected rows: b_bkg at re2_injected_entries"`. In [re2_driver_at_M.py](file:///Users/josephbailey/ptmc-reads/decl-r2-agy/inputs/re2_driver_at_M.py#L2197-L2210) and [re2_driver_at_M.py](file:///Users/josephbailey/ptmc-reads/decl-r2-agy/inputs/re2_driver_at_M.py#L2286), `negweight-refined` forms `sgn_w = np.concatenate([measured_weights, -inj_w])` and passes it to `refine_stay_positive` (`:476-519`), so the `measured_weights` reaching `ohf.omnifold` are the non-negative refined weights `w_ref = |sgn_w|·max(2g − 1, 0)`, not `1 * b_data` or `-inj_w_CV * b_bkg`. Also verify `n.bkg` and `sum.bkg` in `p1Streams` ([re2_driver_at_M.py](file:///Users/josephbailey/ptmc-reads/decl-r2-agy/inputs/re2_driver_at_M.py#L2248-L2250)) against `Q_R S1_b`, and note the appended list is empty on `p_off_b{1..5}`.

   - **Location:** [PTMC-DECLARATION.md](file:///Users/josephbailey/ptmc-reads/decl-r2-agy/inputs/PTMC-DECLARATION.md#L234-L235) (§3.1 `C-PAIR`).
