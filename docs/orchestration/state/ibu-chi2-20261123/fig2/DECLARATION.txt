# Fig. 2 test: do our selected data and our selected MC match MINERvA's in the two regions? (declared before computing)

Question. The fold test finds our reco data above R t_pub at reco p|| 40-60 (S_COL = +0.100) and below it at reco
pT 0-0.07 (S_ROW = -0.064). Separate:
  DATA  our selected data already differ from MINERvA's selected data in that region (selection, exposure mix, or reco
        bin assignment), from
  MC    our data agree but our total selected MC differs from theirs (MC production, weighting, reconstruction), from
  NEITHER  both agree, which points to signal/background classification, conditional migration, efficiency or extraction.

Inputs.
- MINERvA: arXiv 2106.16210v4 PDF (sha256 39b6b8f1e6a59007ce31f4da4ab1af50dd07b491f537a3bbd898d57e2271ef69), page 4,
  Fig. 2: black points (data) and red line (MINERvA Tune v1 total), Events (x1e-5) per (GeV/c)^2, divided by the printed
  panel scale factor, times the cell area, gives counts D_r and Q_r. Extracted from the vector paths by a worker that
  does not see our numbers.
- Ours: evloop/results/ibuchi_products.npz (sha f66c0e1b...), BASE: N_r = BASE_N, P_r = BASE_M.sum(axis=1) + BASE_B
  (MC already POT-scaled to data by ana.py).
- Cell index g = ipt*16 + ipz, pT edges 0,.07,.15,.25,.33,.40,.47,.55,.70,.85,1.00,1.25,1.50,2.50,4.50;
  p|| edges 1.5,2,2.5,3,3.5,4,4.5,5,6,7,8,9,10,15,20,40,60.

Controls on the extraction (any failure: STOP, no reading).
- C-DUP: every cell appears in a top (fixed pT) and a bottom (fixed p||) panel. On cells with D > 100, the median
  |top/bottom - 1| <= 1 % for data and for MC. delta_reg = |region-sum top/bottom - 1| for each region, data and MC.
- C-ERR: where the data error bar is resolvable, N implied by the bar half-length (sqrt(N) statistics) agrees with the
  point's N: median ratio within 2 % of 1.
- C-TOT: sum of D over all cells within 1 % of the paper's 4,105,696 selected events (page 3).

Statistics (regions COL = ipz 15; ROW = ipt 0; ALL = cells extracted in both):
  rho_data(reg) = [sum_reg N / sum_reg D] / [sum_ALL N / sum_ALL D] - 1
  rho_MC(reg)   = [sum_reg P / sum_reg Q] / [sum_ALL P / sum_ALL Q] - 1
  Thresholds: tau_COL = 0.05, tau_ROW = 0.032 (half of |S|). delta = max(delta_reg(data), delta_reg(MC)).

Readings, per region:
  UNRESOLVED  3*delta >= tau.
  DATA        |rho_data| >= tau.
  MC          |rho_data| < tau and |rho_MC| >= tau.
  NEITHER     |rho_data| < tau and |rho_MC| < tau.
Expected before computing: the truth test found shipped Tune v1 truth 16-20 % above ours at truth p|| 40-60. A COL MC
reading with their MC higher than ours is therefore anticipated from that result and is not by itself new; its size is
reported. Descriptive only: absolute ratios sum N / sum D and sum P / sum Q, per-cell ratios, and their data/MC in
each region.
