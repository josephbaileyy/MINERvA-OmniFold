### Q3. Readings of "PARTIAL if reduction >= 30 % within |d| <= 3 %"

#### Alternative Readings:
The declaration text ([lines 42–43](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josephbailey-local-research-MINERvA-OmniFold/8f21a498-e586-4293-aaeb-c5560401abaa/scratchpad/evloop/agyscore/files/DECLARATION.txt#L42-L43)):
> `"COL, for CURV, RANGE: EXPLAINS if the best d has |d| <= 3 %, |S_COL(d)| <= |S_COL,BASE|/3, and no column with p|| < 20 moves by more than 1 percentage point; PARTIAL if reduction >= 30 % within |d| <= 3 %; else NONE."`

While the EXPLAINS clause explicitly specifies `"the best d"`, the PARTIAL clause shifts phrasing to `"reduction >= 30 % within |d| <= 3 %"`. This admits two primary readings:
1. **The Fitted $\hat{d}$ Reading (Scorer's implementation):**
   Evaluate reduction strictly at the global best-fit parameter $\hat{d} = \text{argmin}(\chi^2)$:
   $$\text{reduction}(\hat{d}) = 1 - \frac{|S_{\text{COL}}(\hat{d})|}{|S_{\text{COL,BASE}}|} \ge 0.30$$
2. **The Profile/Existential Reading (Alternative):**
   Evaluate whether *any* tested parameter within the scan window $|d| \le 3\%$ achieves a $30\%$ reduction:
   $$\max_{|d| \le 0.03} \left(1 - \frac{|S_{\text{COL}}(d)|}{|S_{\text{COL,BASE}}|}\right) \ge 0.30$$
3. **The Confidence-Interval Reading (Alternative):**
   Evaluate whether any $d$ inside the declared $1\sigma$ uncertainty interval ($\Delta \chi^2 \le 1$ around $\hat{d}$) achieves $\ge 30\%$ reduction.

#### Conservatism of the Scorer's Choice:
The scorer's choice (Reading 1) is strictly the **more conservative** one:
- **Prevents cherry-picking:** In an unconstrained scan over 61 points in $d \in [-3\%, +3\%]$, a shift might fortuitously pull down $S_{\text{COL}}$ while drastically degrading the fit across the rest of the 223 cells (leading to a high $\chi^2$ that is statistically ruled out).
- **Physical consistency:** It demands that the single parameter value favored by the complete dataset $\hat{d}$ must itself resolve at least $30\%$ of the discrepancy.
- **Reporting transparency:** The scorer evaluates the formal classification `COL` at $\hat{d}$, but also computes and outputs `best_COL_reduction_any_d` descriptively ([line 57](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josephbailey-local-research-MINERvA-OmniFold/8f21a498-e586-4293-aaeb-c5560401abaa/scratchpad/evloop/agyscore/files/score_evloop.py#L57)), providing full visibility into Alternative 2 without compromising the conservative classification bar.
