### Q1. Correctness and Completeness of the MAD Guard

**Yes**, the guard `sp & (sig["mu_mad_p"] > 0)` (along with `bkg["mu_mad_p"] > 0` and `dat["mu_mad_p"] > 0`) is **correct and complete**.

In [ana_new.py](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josephbailey-local-research-MINERvA-OmniFold/8f21a498-e586-4293-aaeb-c5560401abaa/scratchpad/evloop/agyconfirm/files/ana_new.py#L48):


- **Completeness**: On truth-only miss rows, `sig["sim_pass"] != 0` is set, but all `mu_*` branches (including `mu_mad_p` and `mu_theta`) are initialized to `-9999.0` ([runEventLoopOmniFold.cpp#L202](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josephbailey-local-research-MINERvA-OmniFold/8f21a498-e586-4293-aaeb-c5560401abaa/scratchpad/evloop/agyconfirm/files/runEventLoopOmniFold.cpp#L202)). Without the guard, `kin(-9999, -9999)` could trigonometrically wrap into the fiducial grid ($p_T \in [0, 4.5]$ GeV, $p_z \in [1.5, 60]$ GeV). Requiring `sig["mu_mad_p"] > 0` strictly eliminates miss rows.
- **No valid reco muon excluded**: In MINERvA / MasterAnaDev, muon momentum $p$ is the magnitude of the 3-momentum ($|\vec{p}|$), which is strictly positive ($p > 0$, and $\ge 1500\text{ MeV}$ for fiducial phase space). Non-positive values ($\le 0$) occur only as missing/sentinel entries (`-9999.0`). Thus, no physical reconstructed muon can have `mu_mad_p <= 0`.
