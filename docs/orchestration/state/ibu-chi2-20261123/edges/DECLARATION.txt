# Edge test: are MINERvA's pT edges 0.075 / 0.325 / 0.475, and does that remove our low-pT deficit against Fig. 2? (declared before computing)

Evidence so far.
- The blind extraction of Figs. 4 and 5 measured drawn pT edges at 0.0760/0.0747, 0.3255/0.3241 and 0.4757/0.4764,
  not 0.07, 0.33 and 0.47.
- The release's bin_mapping.txt prints 0.07, 0.33 and 0.47. Printing the binary doubles 0.075, 0.325 and 0.475 with %.2f
  gives exactly those strings.
- Our analysis uses 0.07, 0.33 and 0.47.

Hypothesis E. MINERvA's pT edges are 0, .075, .15, .25, .325, .40, .475, .55, .70, .85, 1.00, 1.25, 1.50, 2.50, 4.50.

Test. Rebin our selected data (evloop merged output, tree `data`, branches measured/measured_pz, read-only on login02)
twice: with our edges, and with the E edges. Compare each with the Fig. 2 bottom-block extraction (fig2_points.csv,
data). Their counts are rebuilt as displayed density / factor x 1e5 x area, using the *matching* edges for the area. For
each p|| slice s and pT row i = 0..6:
- f_ours(s, i) = N(s, i) / N(s, all rows); f_theirs is the same.
- X = sum of (f_ours - f_theirs)^2 / var, with var as in axis/DECLARATION.txt.
- rho_i = pooled ratio over slices, weighted by their slice totals.

Readings:
- EDGES-CONFIRMED  with E edges, |rho_i - 1| <= 0.015 for i = 0, 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, and X_E <= 0.5 X_ours.
- EDGES-PARTIAL    X_E < X_ours, and |rho_0 - 1| at least halves, but not CONFIRMED.
- EDGES-NO         otherwise.

Control C-REP: with our edges, the rebinned counts equal BASE_N exactly.
