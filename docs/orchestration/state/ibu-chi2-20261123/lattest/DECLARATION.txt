# Lateral-response test: does a muon-momentum or beam-angle shift of our response close the fold-test misfit? (declared before computing)

Question: the published result folded through our response misses our data at reco p|| 40-60 (data high) and reco
pT 0-0.07 (data low). Do the standard lateral shifts of our response move or close those gaps?

Compute: one Slurm job, account m3246, CPU, qos interactive, 1 node, limit 60 min (Joseph's go "use whatever you need").
Inputs (read-only; atimes move): CV = the verified copy oflow-split-20261123/cv.root (43f8cc16...); laterals =
cov2d-inputs-20261104/laterals/runEventLoopOmniFold_5D_MEFHC_active_<BAND>_<0|1>.root for BAND in Muon_Energy_MINOS,
Muon_Energy_MINERvA, MuonResolution, BeamAngleX, BeamAngleY. Index 0/1 are the two 1-sigma laterals (sign not assumed).

Builder (each file; driver conventions): mc_signal_reco rows with finite w_truth,w_reco in [0,1e4), times dataPOT/mcPOT;
pass_truth = rectangle (0<=pT<=4.5, 1.5<=p||<=60, edges inclusive) and atan2(pT,p||) < 20 deg; pass_reco = sim_pass and
reco in the rectangle. T = sum w_truth over pass_truth in the 14x16 grid (TH1 bins, upper edge out); M[reco,truth] =
sum w_reco over pass_truth & pass_reco, both in grid. B = mc_background, sim_background_pass, finite, 0<=w_bkg<1e6,
weight w_bkg*POT scale, binned at reco.
Controls (STOP if any fails):
 C-CV    CV build: M and T equal signed_data.npz (7a35b7e2...) to max|diff|/max <= 1e-9; B equals hBkgReco2D, rel <= 1e-9.
 C-TRUTH every lateral's T equals the CV T, rel <= 1e-9 (same events, truth unshifted).
Per file L: R_L = M_L/T, y_L = N - B_L (N = local hDataReco2D), fold f_L = R_L t_pub (t_pub as in foldtest).
Region statistic: weighted mean of y_L/f_L - 1 with the CV fold's combined-error weights (fixed across L), on
 COL = reco p|| 40-60 cells, ROW = reco pT 0-0.07 cells (data > 0).
Per band: points (s=-1?, 0, +1?) = (idx0, CV, idx1); quadratic through the three; sigma* = root nearest 0 in [-3,3].
Readings per band x region:
 CLOSES-2   a root with |sigma*| <= 2
 CLOSES-3   a root only with 2 < |sigma*| <= 3
 MOVES      no root, but a 1-sigma point reduces |stat| by >= 30% of the CV value
 NO-EFFECT  otherwise
Primary: COL with Muon_Energy_MINOS and MuonResolution; ROW with BeamAngleX and BeamAngleY. Others reported, secondary.
Descriptive: IBU K=10 (signed target, prior T, R_L) chi2/205 vs published for each L (CV 0.961); fold G per L.
