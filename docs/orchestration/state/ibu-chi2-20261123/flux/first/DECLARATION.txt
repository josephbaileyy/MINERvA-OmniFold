# Flux test: does our constrained nu_mu flux differ in shape from the one the 2021 paper used? (declared before computing)

Question. The 2021 paper (PRD 104 092007) cites Valencia et al. 2019 (PRD 100 092001) for its flux constraint: the FHC
nu-e constraint. Our MAT, with SetNuEConstraint(true) on ME playlists, loads
sorted_NuEConstraint_FHC_RHC_IMD.txt (FHC + RHC nu-e + IMD; FluxReweighter.cxx:504-506). The variant test found shipped
bare GENIE about 12 % above our tuned truth in shape at truth p|| 40-60. Is our constrained flux lower than Valencia's at
high E_nu, in shape?

Inputs.
- Valencia: arXiv 1906.00111v2 anc MINERvA_Flux_pdg14_500MeVBins.csv (sha 2f00972e...) and its covariance
  (sha 4693f406...).
- Ours: flux-g4numiv6-pdg14-minervame1{D,M,N}_rearrangedUniverses.root from
  MINERvA101/opt/lib/data/flux (the files the event loop logged), and the constraint file the event loop used. Our
  constrained CV = sum_i w_i phi_i / sum_i w_i over the 100 Flux universes, with w_i from the file's 'Flux i w' lines,
  as cov2d.flux_weights reads them. The unconstrained CV histogram is reported beside it.
- Both rebinned to 0.5 GeV over 0-100 GeV, where the binning allows.

Statistic. Shape ratio rho(E) = [ours(E)/sum_{2-20} ours] / [val(E)/sum_{2-20} val], normalized over 2-20 GeV. Region
means: LOW 2-6, MID 6-11, HIGH 11-20, VHIGH 20-50 GeV, flux-weighted. Valencia's diagonal error on each region is
taken from its covariance.

Readings, per flux period:
  HIGH-LOW   rho(VHIGH) <= 0.95 and the deficit exceeds 2 sigma of Valencia's VHIGH uncertainty.
  HIGH-HIGH  rho(VHIGH) >= 1.05 under the same sigma condition.
  SAME       otherwise.
Controls (STOP if failing): C-W, 100 finite non-negative weights; C-UNIT, the unconstrained CV integral over 0-100 GeV
within 30 % of Valencia's. That only guards against a units or binning mistake. A constrained/unconstrained CV ratio
is reported but not read.
Descriptive only: a link to the truth test needs the playlist POT mix and the E_nu to p|| mapping, which this test does
not do.
