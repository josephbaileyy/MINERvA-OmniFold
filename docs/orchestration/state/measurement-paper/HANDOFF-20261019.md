## 1. What this is for

The methods paper (the director's lane, [`../2d-closeout/stageF/METHODS-FRAMING.md`](../2d-closeout/stageF/METHODS-FRAMING.md))
presents the generator tests as conditional tests of stated hybrid hypotheses under a stated model. A
physics-measurement paper needs the conditions that make them physical results. The outside referee
(`../2d-closeout/stageF/plain-language/PLAIN-LANGUAGE-PASS2-REPORTS.md`, P20, major comments 2, 3 and 7–11) and the
triage (P7, and §5 of `../2d-closeout/stageF/PLAIN-LANGUAGE-PASS2-IMPLEMENTATION.md`) name four:

| id | question |
|---|---|
| **MP-1** | Can a calorimetric (hadronic-recoil) response uncertainty be propagated through the null calibration of the joint tests, from a documented source? |
| **MP-2** | Can the hybrid null hypotheses be shown close enough to the generators themselves, or replaced by nulls that are? |
| **MP-3** | How large is the carbon-versus-hydrocarbon effect for NuWro and GiBUU in the reported cells, and can it be removed? |
| **MP-4** | Is there any route, new estimator or regularization included, to validated five-dimensional uncertainties? |

The full record trail, with file:line at `a148f22f`, is the evidence map the director commissioned:
