You are an experienced Physical Review D referee and editor in experimental neutrino physics (unfolded cross sections, systematic-universe covariances, coverage studies). The authors plan a coverage test of their reported covariance and must decide now, before the result, how the paper will describe its scope. The author's specific worry: the proposed wording may ADD caveats to a paper already judged over-qualified (about 10 distinct caveats per 1,000 words, against 1.4-4.5 in published PRD neutrino papers).

The test (summary): at two held-out generator tunes (G18, AR23; G18 shares a 2p2h family with one generator in the model term, and the tunes share a quasielastic model), 300 pseudo-experiments per tune are drawn with every uncertainty group. Each systematic band is drawn at its +/-1 sigma endpoint, and the bands' event-weight shifts are ADDED (first order), matching how the covariance C_T is built: a sum of per-band blocks, each band varied alone, with no cross-band terms. The few negative expected counts (from two bands with negative weights) are clipped to zero; the clipping offset is measured per cell in sigma units. The pseudo-data come from the same simulated universes the covariance is built from. Each pseudo-experiment is unfolded by the production procedure; the covariance is remade by the same procedure at each tune's truth; grading is by per-cell in-interval fractions and a joint chi2 against a simulated null.

Below: (A) the proposal's section with the planned paper clauses (clause M, clause S additions, a "cannot support" line); (B) the paper's current coverage statement in Limitations; (C) an independent reviewer's suggested additions: state that the pseudo-data use the same universes as the covariance so missing sources are not probed, that negative counts were clipped, and that bands were drawn at +/-1 sigma.

## 2c. The narrower claim (B3): wording drafted for Joseph

The claim is now that C_T covers natures drawn from its own first-order model. That must reach the paper text, not
