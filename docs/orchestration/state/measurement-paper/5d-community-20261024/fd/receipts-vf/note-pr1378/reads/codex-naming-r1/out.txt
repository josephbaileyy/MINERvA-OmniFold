### 3. Naming

I’d keep the overall convention and make two local changes. Rename \(C_{\mathrm{total}}\to C_{\mathrm{sub}}\), defined as the **unscaled statistical, systematic, and prior-shape subtotal**. Replace “the baseline covariance” with **“the full sixteen-cell covariance with unscaled \(C_e\)”**, spelling out at first use that it includes the response and run-period terms. These changes distinguish the matrices readers could actually substitute for one another in a calculation. The principal-results caption already maps \(Q_R\) to the 2D densities and \(C_{\mathrm{rep}}\) to the cell integrals. Keep \(C_{\mathrm{model}}\) for the same prior-shape construction and put its different inclusion rules in that caption, where readers first compare the results. The analogy between \(C_{\mathrm{stat}}+C_{\mathrm{syst}}\) and \(C_e\) is at the level of source categories. Harmonizing \(C_{\mathrm{run}}\) and \(C_{\mathrm{PL},5\mathrm D}\) would be mostly cosmetic because the shared construction is already explicit. The purity covariance \(C_Z\) and its \(C_{EW}\) projection have a distinct diagnostic role. Those symbols can stay as they are, saving changes to macros, figures, and records.
