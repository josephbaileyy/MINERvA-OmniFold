### Suggested renames

- **$C_{\mathrm{total}} \to C_{\mathrm{base}}$** (baseline subtotal) so $C_{\mathrm{rep}}$ (or $C_{\mathrm{rep},16}$) is unambiguously the reported total.
- **$Q_R \to C_{\mathrm{rep},2\mathrm{D}}$** and **$C_{\mathrm{rep}} \to C_{\mathrm{rep},16}$** (or $C_{\mathrm{2D}}$ / $C_{16}$) to unify the reported-covariance notation across dimensions.
- **$C_{\mathrm{PL},5\mathrm{D}} \to C_{\mathrm{run},16}$** (and $C_{\mathrm{run}} \to C_{\mathrm{run},2\mathrm{D}}$ when disambiguating) to match `"Period"` and drop `PL`.
- **$C_{\mathrm{model}} \to C_{\mathrm{prior},2\mathrm{D}}$** and **$C_{\mathrm{prior},16}$** to distinguish dimensions and clarify that it measures prior-shape sensitivity.
- **$C_e,\,C_e^{\mathrm{infl}} \to C_{\mathrm{in}},\,C_{\mathrm{in}}^{\mathrm{infl}}$** (or $C_{\mathrm{stat+syst}}$ / $C_{\mathrm{stat+syst}}^{\mathrm{infl}}$) to reflect "varying the unfolding inputs" ([sec5_multidim.tex:52](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josephbailey-local-research-MINERvA-OmniFold/be6e903d-7ae7-440f-b5aa-de78760f31e0/scratchpad/naming-agy-20261126/inputs/sec5_multidim.tex#L52)), and **$C_Z \to C_{\mathrm{fine,pur}}$** for the exploratory fine-grid purity matrix.
