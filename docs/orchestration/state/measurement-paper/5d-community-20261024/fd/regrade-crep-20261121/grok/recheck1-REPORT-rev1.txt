## New

**MAJOR.** The sentence the reading licenses still names the wrong errors. R-χ² is the only reading wording may rest on (`REGRADE-DECLARATION.md:80`). Its fail sentence is "the toys' errors are larger on average than the tune-carried full covariance `C′` describes, at `M_full`" (`:93-94`). `M_full` is the conditional expected mean of `χ²(e+r)/ndf′` given the stored toys (`:87-90`), not the toys' mean. The toys' mean is `M′`. Under the model the declaration states, a right `C′` has `E[M′] = 1 − τ` (`:96`), so `M′` sits below 1 by about `τ` while `M_full` sits at 1. Example: `M′ = 0.80`, `τ = 0.40`, `SE(M′) = 0.05` gives `M_full − 1 = 0.20 > 0.15`, so the rule fires, while the stored toys' mean `χ²/ndf′` is `0.80 < 1`. The sentence is then false. The split printed with it (`:83-84`) adds to `M′`, not to `M_full`. The exact statement at `:87-90` does not have this error. The fail sentence should say that the full test's conditional expected mean exceeds 1.
