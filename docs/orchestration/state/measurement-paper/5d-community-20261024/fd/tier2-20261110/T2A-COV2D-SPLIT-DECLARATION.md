## 3. The computation (local, from E_<T>.npz and blocks_<T>.npz)

Let C = C_T, and λ_1 ≥ λ_2 ≥ … its eigenvalues with eigenvectors v_k (numpy `eigh`, reordered descending).

**The non-null subspace** is `fd_1b_null.chi2_nonnull`'s (`nd-unfolding/fd_1b_null.py:92-98`, RANK_REL 10⁻¹²), giving
ndf 204 (AR23) and 205 (G18). C⁺ is the pseudo-inverse on it.

1. **The split.**
   - The quantities: ē is the mean of E's 300 rows, and **S = (1/300) Σᵢ (eᵢ − ē)(eᵢ − ē)ᵀ (ddof 0)**.
   - The identity: mean χ² = ēᵀC⁺ē + tr(C⁺S), exactly.
   - Reported: both parts, both divided by ndf, and the bias share ēᵀC⁺ē / mean χ².
2. **Whitened components.** z_{ik} = v_kᵀeᵢ / √λ_k for k = 1..ndf. Per rank k:
   - b_k = z̄_k² (bias) and f_k = (1/300) Σᵢ (z_{ik} − z̄_k)² (fluctuation), so that b_k + f_k = mean_i z_{ik}²;
   - Σ_k b_k and Σ_k f_k equal step 1's two parts.
   - Reported per tune: a figure of b_k and f_k against k (log λ_k on a second axis), with the line at 1 (a component's
     expected mean square under the null), the table of k, λ_k/λ_1, b_k, f_k, and the cumulative sums at k = 73, 139
     and ndf.
3. **The truncated joint test,** at **k ∈ {73, 139}, the top k eigenpairs of C_T** (the director's ruling: fixed ranks).
   - The statistic is χ²_k,i = Σ_{j≤k} z_{ij}², the cut is χ²_ppf(P0, k) with P0 = erf(1/√2) (`fd_1b_null.py:42`), and
     the count is #{i : χ²_k,i ≤ cut}.
   - **The null at rank k** is `fd_1b_null.null_contents`' simulation (`fd_1b_null.py:109-126`) with its χ² replaced by
     χ²_k, over the same laws and Gaussian `cov2d_null.null_at_T` builds from `blocks_<T>.npz`
     (`2d-unfolding/cov2d/cov2d_null.py:50-58`). That is N_MC = 2,000,000 draws at seed `cov2d.NULL_SEED` = 5,742,000
     (`cov2d.py:48`), one null per (tune, k), four in all.
   - k_joint is `fd_1b_null.thresholds(null_k, n=300)["k_joint"]`, and the size is `fd_1b_null.size`.
   - Reported per (tune, k): the count, k_joint, q_k (se), whether count ≤ k_joint (printed as "at or below k_joint",
     with no verdict word), mean χ²_k/k, and its bias and fluctuation parts.
4. **C_T's own counts:** #{λ/λ_1 > 10⁻⁴} and #{λ/λ_1 > 10⁻⁶}, printed beside 73 and 139.
