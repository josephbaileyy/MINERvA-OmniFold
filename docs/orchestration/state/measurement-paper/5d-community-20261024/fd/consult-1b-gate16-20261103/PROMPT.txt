The same gate is now to be applied to a sister 5D test with 16 cells and three truths (two held-out tunes plus a plug-in truth). At 16 cells "95%" means all 16 must pass: noise-only STOP probability about 0.21 per truth, about 0.51 over three truths (assuming independent cells; cells share draws so they are correlated).

Options: (a) apply the 95% rule unchanged (fail-closed; roughly a coin-flip chance of a noise STOP, which returns the decision to the lead); (b) a 16-cell equivalent of the 2D tolerance, for example allowing k failing cells per truth.
