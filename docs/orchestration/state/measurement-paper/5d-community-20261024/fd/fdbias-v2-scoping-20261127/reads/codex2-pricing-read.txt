- **Construct it from signed residuals.** In cell-integrated cross-section units, estimate \(b_t=E[\hat x\mid t]-x_t\). Fit a positive-semidefinite approximation to \(\sum_t w_t(\hat b_t\hat b_t^\top-\operatorname{Cov}(\hat b_t))\), using the full covariance of each estimated mean. Keep the second moment uncentered so shared bias survives. Predeclare family weights so repeated variants and noise-free counterparts don’t get extra votes. For variations already in \(C_{\rm model}\), propagate their net error end to end and replace the overlapping contribution, accounting for correlations between the prior shift and residual bias.
- **Use (b) and (d) as sensitivity checks.** A diagonal envelope loses coherent bin correlations, and a scalar rescaling can only widen directions already in \(C_{\rm model}\). Use (c) as a replacement if the low-rank basis predicts bias directions in whole generator families held out of the fit.
- **Validate before adoption.** Freeze the construction and test it on fresh generator families with end-to-end pseudo-experiments that draw the relevant nuisances. Check joint, per-cell, and key projection coverage alongside interval widths, and compare mean residuals with toy scatter. If the gain requires broad inflation or the bias reflects poor recovery of plausible truth shapes, prioritize improving the estimator or narrowing the reported phase space.
