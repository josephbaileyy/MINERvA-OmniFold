#### Specific Answers to Directed Questions:
1. **Is C-OF able to detect a wrong cell order?** **Yes.** Cells in MINERvA have distinct physical cross sections and errors. If OmniFold cells are scrambled or transposed, `bias(E, σ)` will fail the $10^{-12}$ cell-by-cell equality check against `score_<T>.json`. *(However, C-OF does NOT check IBU cells).*
2. **Is C-OF able to detect a wrong toy-to-truth pairing?** **NO.** In `cov2d_readouts.bias(E, σ)`, $E = x̂^{\text{post}} - x_{\text{true}}$. The mean bias across toys is $\frac{1}{n}\sum_t x̂^{\text{post}}_t - \frac{1}{n}\sum_t x_{\text{true}, t}$. Because the truth enters as a linear unweighted sum over the ensemble, any permutation $\pi \in S_{300}$ of toy-truth pairings preserves $\sum x_{\text{true}, t}$ bit-identically. C-OF checks only `bias` and `pull_mean` (both sample means); it does not check `p_hat` or $\chi^2$. A complete shuffling of toy-to-truth pairs passes C-OF with zero error.
3. **Is C-OF able to detect a toy file mix-up?** **Partially for OmniFold, completely blind for IBU.** For OmniFold, swapping two toys with different surrogate terms $z$ introduces a non-commuting shift $\Delta \sim 0.014 \Delta x \Delta z / 300 \sim 10^{-6} \sigma_c$, which violates the $10^{-12}$ tolerance. But if two toys have nearly identical $z$, or if IBU toy files are swapped or permuted, C-OF cannot detect it because C-OF does not evaluate IBU outputs.
4. **Is C-REP able to detect a wiring error that only appears with `--data-file`?** **NO.** C-REP runs exclusively on the nominal `central` unit with NO `--data-file`. It does not exercise argument parsing for `--data-file`, does not stage pseudo-data, does not test `tfile_guard` on the staging data sub-directory, and does not test the driver's pseudo-data TTree loader. Any defect specific to `--data-file` will completely bypass C-REP.

### 4. STATISTICAL DESIGN AND READINGS AUDIT
* **Bootstrap SE & Pairing:** The paired bootstrap SE design ($B = 10,000$ resamples of toy indices $t \in \{1, \dots, 300\}$ drawn from `numpy.random.default_rng([5_760_000, k])`; [`IBUB-DECLARATION.md:148-154`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josephbailey-local-research-MINERvA-OmniFold/a4ec4b9a-c277-4847-8576-4e2ef26708b1/scratchpad/agy-decl-r1/inputs/IBUB-DECLARATION.md#L148-L154)) is statistically rigorous and properly pairs the two methods on the same toy resamples. Printing the analytic paired standard error $s_d / \sqrt{n}$ beside it provides an immediate sanity check.
* **Bonferroni 236-Test Family:**
  - *Cell and band count:* 205 cells + 14 $p_T$ bands + 16 $p_\parallel$ bands + 1 total = 236 tests. The sum of reported cells in the 14 $p_T$ bands ($9 \times 16 + 15 + 14 + 13 + 11 + 8 = 205$; [`IBUB-DECLARATION.md:162-163`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josephbailey-local-research-MINERvA-OmniFold/a4ec4b9a-c277-4847-8576-4e2ef26708b1/scratchpad/agy-decl-r1/inputs/IBUB-DECLARATION.md#L162-L163)) matches the 205 reported cells.
  - *Critical value:* $z^* = \Phi^{-1}(1 - 0.025/236) = 3.70443...$ is arithmetically exact for a two-sided normal test at familywise $\alpha = 0.05$.
  - *Conservative nature:* Because the 31 bands are linear combinations of the 205 cells, the tests are highly positively correlated. Bonferroni's union bound is therefore conservative, controlling the FWER at $\le 0.05$.
  - *Studentization nuance:* For $n=300$, a studentized t-statistic has critical value $t_{299}^{-1}(1 - 0.025/236) \approx 3.7505$. Using normal quantile 3.7044 is slightly lower, but the conservative Bonferroni correlation structure easily compensates.
* **Hotelling's $T^2$:**
  - The degrees of freedom for Hotelling's $T^2$ scaling on 205 cells with $n=300$ are correctly stated as $F(p, n-p) = F(205, 95)$ ([`IBUB-DECLARATION.md:185-187`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josephbailey-local-research-MINERvA-OmniFold/a4ec4b9a-c277-4847-8576-4e2ef26708b1/scratchpad/agy-decl-r1/inputs/IBUB-DECLARATION.md#L185-L187)). The declaration correctly notes that $S$ is noisy in 205 dimensions from 300 samples, and appropriately treats $T^2$ as descriptive only.

## NUMBERED LIST OF FINDINGS

### Finding 1 [MAJOR]: C-OF is Blind to Toy-to-Truth Mispairing
* **Citations:** [`IBUB-DECLARATION.md:193-205`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josephbailey-local-research-MINERvA-OmniFold/a4ec4b9a-c277-4847-8576-4e2ef26708b1/scratchpad/agy-decl-r1/inputs/IBUB-DECLARATION.md#L193-L205); [`cov2d_readouts.py:51-54`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josephbailey-local-research-MINERvA-OmniFold/a4ec4b9a-c277-4847-8576-4e2ef26708b1/scratchpad/agy-decl-r1/inputs/cov2d_readouts.py#L51-L54); [`cov2d_score.py:53-68`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josephbailey-local-research-MINERvA-OmniFold/a4ec4b9a-c277-4847-8576-4e2ef26708b1/scratchpad/agy-decl-r1/inputs/cov2d_score.py#L53-L68).
* **Issue:** Control C-OF reproduces COV2D's errors by checking that `cov2d_readouts.bias(E, σ)` and `spread_alone(...)["pull_mean"]` match `score_<T>.json` to $10^{-12}$. Because both quantities are unweighted sample means over the 300 toys ($\bar{E}_c = \frac{1}{n}\sum_t x̂_{tc}^{\text{post}} - \frac{1}{n}\sum_t x_{\text{true}, tc}$), the truth term enters as a linear sum $\sum_t x_{\text{true}, t}$. If `truth_xsec.npz` rows or toy-to-truth pairings are permuted in any order, the sum is bit-identically invariant. C-OF will pass with zero error even under a total mismatch of toys to truths.
* **Concrete Fix:** Update C-OF in §5 to check individual per-toy errors $E_{tc}$ or check the in-interval fraction `p_hat` and standard deviation `pull_sd` from `score_<T>.json`. In addition, mandate an explicit pre-readout seed assertion verifying that for every toy index $k$, $\text{seed}(x̂^{OF}_k) == \text{seed}(x_{\text{true}, k}) == \text{TOY\_SEEDS}[T][k]$.

### Finding 8 [NOTE]: Extreme Tail Bonferroni Normal vs Student-t Discrepancy
* **Citations:** [`IBUB-DECLARATION.md:167-169`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-josephbailey-local-research-MINERvA-OmniFold/a4ec4b9a-c277-4847-8576-4e2ef26708b1/scratchpad/agy-decl-r1/inputs/IBUB-DECLARATION.md#L167-L169).
* **Observation:** The critical value $z^* = \Phi^{-1}(1 - 0.025/236) = 3.7044$ assumes standard normal distribution. Because the variance is estimated from $n=300$ samples, the exact studentized ratio follows $t_{299}$, which has critical value $t_{299}^{-1}(1 - 0.025/236) \approx 3.7505$. While $3.7044$ is slightly lower, Bonferroni's union bound over 31 redundant kinematic bands provides ample conservatism. No operational change required, but noting the exact $t_{299}$ quantile documents statistical rigor.
