## 2. The identity rule, as `fig1_identity.py` applies it

Both producers run their own `main()` in one interpreter, on the same arrays: the `--npz` that `re2c_fig1.py` writes,
its `tmp/fig_arrays_q.npz`. The old producer is `make_figs.py` `4e18c266`, read from commit `7fc9ce93`, route A's.
At every save, the figure's drawn data are dumped with the fig-restyle campaign's `artists.py`
(`stageF/methods/fig-restyle-20261031/tools/`). Each data artist is kept as arrays, hashed with its colours and
styles, and each `Axes.hist` call's input values are kept too. Every text is recorded per axes, and the canvas is
rasterized at 300 dpi. Each check below is a FAIL:

- **F1:** each PDF has the digest given:
  - the old Fig. 1 is the committed figure (route A `1ead58a2`, amendment 3 `71a2ea48`);
  - the new Fig. 1 is the one `re2c_fig1.py` wrote;
  - fig2 and fig3 are route A's committed copies, in both versions.
- **F2:** fig2 and fig3 have the same items, the same texts and the same PDF bytes in both versions.
- **F3:** Fig. 1 has 7 axes. The six that are not the panel have the same multiset of item hashes: data, colours and
  styles, bit for bit.
- **F4:** the texts on those six axes are equal, except the colour bar's label, which goes from
  `(OmniFold $-$ published)$/\sigma_{\mathrm{published}}$` at 7.5 pt to `$r_i$` at 8 pt.
- **F5:** the old panel draws no data. The new one draws one `Axes.hist` call, and its values equal, bit for bit, the
  r_i computed from the arrays: `(ours_v - paper_v) / sqrt(diag cov_total)` on `mask_reported`, 205 values. Its bins
  are also checked:
  - they are 0.25 wide;
  - one is centred on zero;
  - they cover the minimum and maximum of r_i;
  - the counts sum to 205.

  Its only line is the vertical at zero.
- **F6:** the printed count, mean and SD equal the old panel's printed values and the r_i's `.3f` values.
- **F7:** every pixel that differs between the two rasters lies inside one of these boxes (2 px margin):
  - the panel's box, old or new;
  - the colour bar label's box, old or new.

unchanged.**
