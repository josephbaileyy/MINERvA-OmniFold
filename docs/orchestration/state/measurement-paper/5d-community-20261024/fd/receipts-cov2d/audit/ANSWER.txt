## Findings

**1. MAJOR – §13 (line 580) and §12 (line 561) claim more than the test supports.**
- Line 580 licenses "covered … at the nominal rate". The rule is one-sided (292), so a pass shows coverage at least at the nominal rate.
- The graded matrix C_T includes C_model_abs (217), but the toys never draw it. Nor do they draw C08 or the simulation-statistics part of C_stat (4.2 "Known difference"). Those components reach the null only as a Gaussian (258).
- C_model adds roughly 20% to the median per-cell σ. `RE2-PARTC-REPORT.md:254-256` gives 6.41% for Q_R against 7.90% for the total. A per-cell σ understatement of up to about 20% in the drawn blocks (52% flag probability at content 0.58) can therefore be masked.
- The size bound "at most 0.05 per tune" (293, repeated at 561) is unconditional. It holds only if the toys' error law is at least as spread as the null's. The toys draw Flux and the pair bands un-centred, while the null laws are centred (256). Clipping changes the law again. The un-centred offset is reported at 305 but is not part of the size claim.
- Suggested resolution: say the licensed sentence concerns the graded matrix, which is the drawn blocks plus the model term and excludes C_PL_R. Say "at least at the nominal rate". Make the size bound conditional, or drop it from the §12 paper text.

**2. MAJOR – the §6 (ii) "clipping may explain" reading (325-327) cannot fire.**
- The threshold is median |R_c| above 0.5 σ_c. C2's largest per-cell median is 0.043 σ_Q at G18 and 0.042 σ_Q at AR23 (`c2-clip2.json`).
- The one cell with a real effect, 216, has median 0 and p90 0.
- 0.5 is a new number. It is not in the consult (`c2_reading.txt` uses 0.2 and 0.4), and it was chosen after C2's numbers were known.
- Suggested resolution: fix a rule that can bind, such as median above 0.2 σ_c or p90 above 0.4 σ_c to match C2's gates, with a stated reason. Or state that the reading is inert by construction.

**3. MAJOR – no pre-toy control covers the drawn pseudo-data content, or the universe and lateral unfolds run through the new route.**

- §9.5 reproduces S5_R from part B's products. It does not show that a universe or lateral unfold via `--omnifile $UNI --data-file` matches part B's products.
- The realization check (309) is descriptive only.
- Suggested resolution: add INVALID gates. Run the writer path at the CV nature, with a toy total and reco histogram consistent with Σλ⁺. In the pilot, run one universe and one lateral with `--data-file` = $CV's own data and reproduce part B's U_x and lateral products to 1e-8. This costs about 0.3 node-h.
