COV2D clipping probe C1: erratum (2026-11-03, found by the cov2d session before any number reached Joseph; relayed to
the director the same day).

C1's truth-cell columns (clip.json: truth_cells_frac_over_sigma_stat, truth_cells_frac_over_sigma_Q and the
top10/over_cells fields built from them) are m_seen,c / N_c: the clipped seen mass over the cell's FULL composite truth
count. The unfolding corrects for efficiency with the nominal simulation, so at first order the unfolded result moves
by the seen fraction m_seen,c / S_c, with S_c the signed lambda over reco-kept rows whose truth lies in c. C1's columns
therefore understate option (a)'s offset by about 1/eps_c.

C1's clipped fractions, its reco-cell z values and its (a) counts are not affected.

Superseded for the offset by C2 (clip_probe2.py, clip2.json), which reports m_seen/S_c and option (1)'s residual.
Joseph gets C2's numbers only. C1's files are kept unchanged as the record of what ran.
