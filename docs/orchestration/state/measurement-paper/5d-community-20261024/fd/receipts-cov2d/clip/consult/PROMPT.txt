You are an independent statistician advising a neutrino cross-section analysis (MINERvA, 2D (pT, p||) result, 205 truth cells, unfolded with a LightGBM OmniFold-style estimator). You have no stake in any option. Answer in under 900 words. You may not read any files; everything you need is below.

THE TEST. A method-coverage test of the reported covariance procedure at two held-out generator tunes (G18 and AR23). For each tune, 300 pseudo-experiments ("natures") are drawn. Each nature's event weights are built by FIRST-ORDER composition of systematic universes: for each event row, a_D = a_u1 + sum_{j>=2} (a_uj - a_CV), in draw order, where a_uj is that row's weight in universe u_j drawn for band j and a_CV the central weight. This was chosen because the covariance under test, C_T, is a sum of per-band blocks with no cross-band term. The pseudo-data are Poisson draws from the nature's expected reco counts; the nature's truth (expected truth-cell counts) is what each unfolded pseudo-experiment is graded against. The covariance is rebuilt by the same procedure at each tune.

THE DECIDED RULE, option (a): a negative expected count is clipped, lambda+ = max(lambda, 0), in the pseudo-data, while the truth used for grading keeps the negative weights. It was chosen expecting few negative counts, with a rule that if the clipping offset is comparable to the per-cell uncertainty the lead decides again.

THE MEASUREMENT (descriptive, at expected counts, 300 draws per tune, both tunes nearly identical):
- Only about 0.27% of truth rows have any single universe weight <= 0 (mostly one pion-production band, universe weight exactly 0; a few low-/high-Q2 rows negative). Yet in an earlier pass about 38% of truth rows were negative in some draw. So nearly all negative composites come from rows where every single-band shift is above -100% but several downward shifts sum past -100%: a property of first-order composition with large single-band shifts.
- Clipped signal mass, sum(lambda-)/sum(lambda): median 0.6-0.7%, p90 about 1.5%, max about 2.7%. Background about 0.4% median.
- Per truth cell, the clipped mass relative to the cell's total uncertainty sigma_Q: largest per-cell median 0.26 sigma (16 cells above 0.2, none above 0.5); largest p90 about 0.5; single-draw max 1.2-1.4 sigma; about 50 cells have a max above 0.5 sigma. Relative to the statistical-only sigma it is several sigma (median up to 2.8, 99 cells with median > 0.5).
- The offset is one-signed: pseudo-data exceed what the graded truth implies. The worst cells are pT 0.33-0.47 GeV/c at high p|| (6-40 GeV/c).
- The per-cell resolution of the coverage readout is about 0.10-0.13 in coverage fraction.

THE OPTIONS put forward by the analysis lane (its recommendation first):
(1) Clip the nature consistently: w+ = max(w_D, 0) row by row in BOTH the pseudo-data's reco and background weights and the truth weights used for grading. The error is then measured against the truth of the sample actually drawn; the clipping artefact cancels at first order. C_T unchanged. Caveat: the reco and truth trees are separate trees, so row-wise clipping may not correspond exactly; the lane would quantify the residual mismatch first at no compute cost.
(2) Keep option (a); a pre-fixed reading labels cells whose miscoverage the measured offset could explain. Flags caused by the composition cannot be told apart from method failure.
(3) Stop this test and report method coverage as not tested.

QUESTIONS.
1. Is option (1) statistically sound for a coverage test of C_T? In particular: clipping makes the distribution of natures a truncated version of what C_T describes. Does testing coverage on clipped natures bias the coverage verdict, and in which direction? Is the bias smaller or larger than option (2)'s?
2. Is there a better standard option the lane did not list (for example a positivity-preserving composition such as multiplicative or log-space composition, rejection of draws, or capping the band shifts), and how would each interact with C_T having no cross-band terms?
3. What must be quantified before a choice is bound, and what threshold would make you prefer (3)?
4. The same composition is used in a sister 5D coverage test (with about 34-38 universes compounding on most rows). Should it run the same expected-count probe before its bulk run?
5. Your ranked recommendation, one line each.
