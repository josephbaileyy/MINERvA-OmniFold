You are an experienced Physical Review D referee. Under 500 words.

A MINERvA neutrino cross-section paper reports a sixteen-cell result whose covariance includes a run-period block built from three run-period groups. Its current paragraph (LaTeX source; \fvval{} are printed numbers) is below. A pre-declared diagnostic campaign then unfolded each of the twelve individual run periods ("playlists") separately and tested whether the twelve playlists' sixteen-cell offsets differ between the three period groups more than their playlist-to-playlist scatter would give, with a permutation test over all 9,240 ways to split twelve playlists into groups of 6, 3 and 3 (calibrated; false-positive rate at or below nominal). Result: the observed grouping is the most extreme of all 9,240 (p = 1/9240 = 1.1e-4); a second, era-based test gave p = 5e-3. A separate localisation of a fine-binned step was inconclusive (no clean pattern). The diagnostic cannot supply a cause.

The campaign's pre-declared rules license exactly one optional addition to the paragraph, and nothing else: "The offsets of the twelve playlists differ between the three period groups by more than their playlist-to-playlist scatter would give." Everything else in the paragraph (including "The cause is not known" and "its size is poorly determined") stays unchanged under every outcome.

Questions: (1) As a referee, would you want this sentence added, and why? Does it strengthen or weaken the paper's treatment of the run-period block (for example, does it support that a group-level block is the right structure), or is it note material? (2) If added, is the wording clear, and where in the paragraph should it go? (3) Would you, as a referee, ask for anything else to accompany it (for example the p-value)? One-line recommendation at the end.

=== THE PARAGRAPH ===
The reported covariance of the sixteen-cell result is $C_{\mathrm{total}}+C_{\mathrm{resp}}+C_{\mathrm{PL,5D}}$. The term $C_{\mathrm{total}}$ sums the statistical, flux, interaction, detector, lateral and normalization uncertainties of the joint-test estimator and $C_{\mathrm{model}}$. The model term $C_{\mathrm{model}}$ is built from the change in the unfolded data when the prior is reweighted to GENIE + MEC, NuWro and GiBUU in turn. Its contributions come from those generators' samples in their own definitions, with NuWro and GiBUU on carbon and GENIE CV as the common reference, not from the predictions compared in Sec.~\ref{sec:gen16}. The comparisons there with GENIE + MEC, NuWro and GiBUU therefore use covariance contributions derived from the same generators. It is a measure of unscaled generator-shape dependence, not a calibrated bound (\nsec{sec:fived16-cov}). The response term $C_{\mathrm{resp}}$ is the response of the result to a one-standard-deviation scale of the reconstructed recoil energy in the simulation. The scale is applied to proton, meson, electromagnetic and other deposits. $C_{\mathrm{resp}}$ does not cover neutron or muon deposits, nor species, energy-dependence or resolution effects beyond a scale. It enters only this result, its fake-data tests and its comparison in Sec.~\ref{sec:gen16}. The unfolded results of the three run-period groups, each relative to its own simulation, differ by more than their bootstrap scatter. The cause is not known, and these data cannot establish which period is correct. The run-period block $C_{\mathrm{PL,5D}}$ includes this inconsistency in the reported covariance. It is built from the double ratios among the groups, each group's unfolded result over its own simulated truth, with no group chosen as the reference, so it contains the differences by construction. It was added after these differences were seen, following an audit whose stopping criterion was fixed in advance. It has rank \fvval{pl-rank} and is not corrected for the bootstrap noise it contains, and with three groups its size is poorly determined: the 68\,\% interval on the ratio of its true to its estimated size is \fvval{pl-sigratio-lo}--\fvval{pl-sigratio-hi}. The median per-cell relative uncertainty is \fvval{sigmed-reported}\%, \fvval{sigmed-reported-nopl}\% without $C_{\mathrm{PL,5D}}$, and \fvval{sigmed-C_total}\% without $C_{\mathrm{PL,5D}}$ or $C_{\mathrm{resp}}$. The block raises the median by about \fvval{pl-medrise}\,\%, and the uncertainty of the most affected cell, \fvval{pl-cellrise-maxcell}, by \fvval{pl-cellrise-max}\,\%. The fake-data listing (Sec.~\ref{sec:fd16}) and the generator comparison (Sec.~\ref{sec:gen16}) are given both with and without it; the note gives the per-cell uncertainties both ways too, with the block's construction and audit (\nsec{sec:fived16-pl}).
