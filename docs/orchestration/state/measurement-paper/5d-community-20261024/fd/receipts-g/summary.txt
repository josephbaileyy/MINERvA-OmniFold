labels.covariance: C_total = C_e + C_model_abs from S1, with all three C_model,5D vertices (GENIE+MEC, NuWro, GiBUU) for every comparison. Each vertex is a data shift unfolded with a prior reweighted by that generator's ratio to GENIE 2.12.10 CV (pv-ladder/config.json), so the GENIE+MEC, NuWro and GiBUU comparisons, and through the ratios' reference the GENIE CV one, each carry a covariance built partly from the compared generator; C_resp is added to C_total in the reported comparison (labels.reported)
labels.shape_only: a free normalization of variance 100 relative to f, ndf n - 1; free normalization along f, Stage 0's precedent (ruled); a mode along g gives a different chi2 whenever f != g: review A's synthetic check measured 2.4 % (16 cells) and 7 % (8 cells)
labels.gibuu: GiBUU on the eight p_parallel < 4.5 cells only; no GiBUU value above p_parallel 4.5 is formed or shown, and no 16-cell GiBUU chi2 is computed
labels.not: no reading, claim or paper text (FD-PLAN section 4; DECISION-20261025 item 2)
