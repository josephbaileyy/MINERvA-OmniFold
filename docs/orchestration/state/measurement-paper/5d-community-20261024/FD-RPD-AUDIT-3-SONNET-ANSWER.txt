## 2. EC reachability toy (scope 1)

**Model.** 12 playlists, the stated shares (normalized), 16 cells, M = identity (also the whitening of any M whose noise is proportional to it). r_X = 1 + δ_X + ε_X, ε sd = s/√e_X per cell, independent, **s = 0.0016** (so playlist noise 0.43% for 1F … 1.42% for 1L; period-mean noise 0.22/0.29/0.38%, matching 5B's committed `g_5b_noise_sigma` medians 0.19/0.33/0.37% at the period shares). All 9,240 (6,3,3) partitions enumerated; π_flux = {1A–1F},{1G,1L,1M},{1N,1O,1P}; p_flux with ties counted. Σ_δ = Σ e g gᵀ/(1−V2) less the noise-only mean, PSD-projected; S0 at a ∈ {0, .5, 1, 2}; S1 = c·Ĝ_P(X) with c = √max(0, 1 − Q̄_noise/Q_b(π_flux)); β̂₁ = share with p_flux < 0.05; EC = calibrated, p_flux ≥ 0.05, β̂₁ ≥ 0.8; CLUSTERED = calibrated, p_flux < 0.05; rest INCONCLUSIVE.
**Differences from the declaration:** noise Gaussian, not resampled replica deviations; 100 outer datasets, **200 inner** per S0 point, per S1 and for the noise-only reference (declared 2,000 in R2, 200 at stage D); T_X MC variance not added; μ = 1 (shape-only). Monte Carlo error on a share p: ±0.05 at p ≈ 0.5, ±0.02 at p ≈ 0.05.
**Truths.** (a) exchangeable: δ_X iid N(0, τ²) per cell. (b) clustered: δ_X = G_P(X), G_P iid N(0, κ²) per cell and period. (c) mixed: each of (a) and (b) at variance ½·scale².
**Reference scale:** 5B's committed period offsets g_X have rms 0.55% (PA), 3.0% (PB), 5.2% (PC); C_PL,5D's per-cell σ_rel is 0.4–2.7%.

So the gate itself passes at the declared n.

**Reading.**
- **EC is reachable.** P(EC) is 0.74–0.98 under exchangeable offsets from τ ≈ 0.6% upward, which is below T1's scale (C_PL,5D's size, τ ≈ 1%) and below 5B's observed period offsets. So P(EC | T1) ≈ 0.97 and stage D's 0.2 flag would not fire on a design-time T1.
- **Where it is not:** noise-dominated offsets (τ ≤ 0.3%: EC ≤ 0.03), under any clustering of non-negligible size (EC ≤ 0.02 for κ ≥ 0.003), and in the mixed truth beyond a small band (EC 0.16 at 0.6%, ≤ 0.03 above).
- **Calibration and size:** P(CLUSTERED | exchangeable) = 0.01–0.05, the nominal 0.05 at most. The test is conservative, not liberal, in this toy.
- **Separation:** CLUSTERED and EC are nearly disjoint at every scale ≥ 0.006. At a mixed truth with equal variance, CLUSTERED wins (0.97): a half-clustered truth is not read as EC.
- **Not run:** a correlated metric M (the whitened statistic is what enters; a non-identity M with noise not proportional to it was not simulated) and the declared replica-resampled noise. Both are stage D's job.

## 4. Answers by scope item

**1. S1 held fixed.** Well defined (Ĝ_P = Σ e g/E per period; c scalar; Q̄_noise the noise-only mean of Q_b(π_flux)); the noise-only dataset count and seed order are the only gaps (A3). β̂₁ is meaningful only as a noise-floor check (m1). The circularity (Ĝ, c, p_flux from one g) is real but does not defeat the test in the toy (CLUSTERED \| exchangeable ≤ 0.05; EC 0.97). Revision 2's cap is gone: P(EC) reaches 0.98.

**2. Stage D.** No playlist data is used: R1's σ_u comes from stage 0's σ₀ (period data), R2's T2 from 5B's committed period offsets and noise. Not enough to run without choices (A3). The R1 rule is aimed at the wrong point (A2) and the SUBSET pattern is outside any rule (A1). The EC rule is consistent with §6.2/§10 in text, but its value depends on the gate (A4). The "login bound" holds for the readout and for stage D at 200 inner (about 67 min by my measurement under heavy load, inside 2 h) and fails if A4 is fixed by 2,000 inner (m7).

**3. N0.** Ratio-preserving `denom_nd` keeps completeness equal to ALL's in every cell where O_ALL > 0, as algebra and the code say: the unfold forms comp = Σ w_truth(pass_truth rows)/`denom_nd` at `s5c_unfold.py:138-142` (the trace class at `s5e_trace.py:286-290` does the same); T_X is `denom_nd`-proportional, so r_X = X/T is the push-weighted cell mean and carries no completeness noise, as declared. Σ_k `denom_nd`_k = ALL's holds exactly (Σ e = 1). The pseudo-inputs are valid for `s5p_numerics.py` if the npz carries the 13 `load_inputs` keys and the bkg dump's meta names the pseudo-npz (lines 160-172; `bkg_nd` is loaded but unread by `build_data`). A bootstrap would perturb O but not `denom_nd`; N0 has none. N0-P is decidable, not sufficient (m2). Thresholds, family level and powers reproduce; triggers are coherent with R1 and R2 except for §6.3's missing row (m4). Price reproduces (1.53, cap 3.0); N0's truths and split are unpriced (m5).

**4. Both defaults exist as cited (`nn_dump_inputs.py:33`, `s5p_input_dumps.py:202`); `lateral` also checks its flux against the nn npz (line 166). Gap: m6.
