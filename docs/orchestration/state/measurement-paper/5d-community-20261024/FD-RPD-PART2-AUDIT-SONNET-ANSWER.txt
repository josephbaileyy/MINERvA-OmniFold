## Findings

### MAJOR

**M1. N0's "effect" print is not what part 1 says, and it is not a declared deviation.** `fd_rpd.py:940-945`.
- Part 1 §4: "If SIZE-BIAS-g fires, R2's null is re-run with b̄ added to the three small playlists, and the induced change in F and p_flux is printed."
- Code: `gb = g.copy(); gb[small] -= b_cells; effect = {p_flux, F of gb}`. That re-tests the *observed* offsets with the bias removed. No null is re-run, and no α̂/β̂ with b̄ added is produced. The sign and object differ (observed minus bias, against null plus bias).
- Part 2 §6 lists two deviations; this is a third. Consequence is small (the INCONCLUSIVE consequence stands either way, per part 1), but it is a stated readout.
- Requirement: implement as written, or declare it as a deviation for acceptance.

**M2. Several part-1 "printed" items are absent from the readout; none is declared.** `fd_rpd.py:889-990`, `n0_reading` 1014-1050.
- Not produced: (a) "the detectable bias is printed in σ and in % of the real median |g_X| of 1L, 1B and 1O" (only `single_draw_sigma_cells` and `median_abs_g_small` are written; no detectable-bias value, no %); (b) "N0's 10 values of F are printed beside R2's null" (the null's F distribution is never returned by `R2.run`; only N0's F list is written); (c) "at readout the [stage-D OC] table is printed again with the measured replica σ" (§5, R1) – no code path; (d) ρ_raw and ρ_deb "each printed with ... its replica-noise reference" (R3) – none; (e) the stage-D "same table at t̄ = 7%" is produced (`at_7pct`), good.
- All are descriptive in part 1, so no outcome moves, but the wording table's licensed R2 sentence quotes ρ_raw and an interval and part 1 says each ρ carries its noise reference.
- Requirement: produce them, or list them as omitted with the reason.

**M3. The submit stage does not refuse "a path outside $R".** `submit_fd_ct.sh:101-102`.
- Part 1 §1: the submit stage "refuses any step whose argv lacks [--mcfile], or names a path outside $R, and a test covers that refusal."

A step that also names `/pscratch/.../MINERvA-OmniFold/...` (or a CFS path) in another argument passes. `$B` as a prefix also matches `$B-old/...`. The `--mcfile` check (line 104-106) is present and tested; the outside-path refusal is neither implemented nor tested (`Submit` class has no such test).

### MINOR

**m1. C1's measured_weights threshold (deviation 1) is not derived, and a single bin-crossing row cascades.** `fd_rpd.py:619, 696-715`.
- Deviation 1 says a row on a bin edge can fall in another bin. The effect is not "a few rows": one crossing changes `data_nd` in two bins, hence `target/d` for every row in both bins, so the share lost is (n_A + n_B)/N, not 1/N. 0.999 has no derivation.
- Toy (my script `t4.py`, 5 axes, ~10⁴ bins, gamma-distributed coordinates, float32 round trip, 20 realizations each): P(share < 0.999) = 0/20 at 5×10⁴ rows, 0/20 at 2.5×10⁵ (min share 0.99924, close), 1/20 at 10⁶ (min 0.99874). Real playlists are ~0.2–2.7×10⁵ rows. So the spurious-FAIL risk per playlist is of order 0–5% and not zero; a FAIL stops the campaign before job B.
- Not a scientific risk (config R ignores the key). Requirement: either measure the share on `lat_zero` itself at stage S/C before binding (read-only on staged data), or set the threshold from that measurement, or make the predicate "agrees with the rule evaluated on float64-reconstructed coordinates" if the dump can expose them.

**m2. N0's small set is by e<0.03; the thresholds are t₂₉-specific.** `fd_rpd.py:915, 54, 1014-1050`.
- Part 1 §4 defines the reading on "the three smallest-share pseudo-playlists (all with e<0.03)" -> 30 draws, t₂₉. The code takes `small = {e < 0.03}` and hard-codes 3.835/2.756. Part 1 §5 itself warns that 1O (≈0.0280) is within 7% of 0.03 "so the real value decides it". If 1O's real e is ≥ 0.03, n = 20 and the thresholds are wrong; if a fourth is below, n = 40. No assertion `len(small) == 3`; the quantile is not recomputed from n.
- Requirement: assert 3, or compute the t_{n-1} quantiles from n (and say so).

**m3. Q3 is only partly implemented.** `fd_rpd.py:905-910`.
- Part 1: "every unfold and MC-truth integral is positive and finite on L, on H and on each reporting cell." Code: cells all `> 0` (NaN fails, +inf passes: no `isfinite`); regions checked only for the seed-42 playlist unfolds and truths, not for the 360 replicas, not for N0's 120 unfolds and truths (N0's `Dr` regions feed t). `read_truth` has no finiteness test (`read_unfold` has).
- Requirement: `isfinite` and `>0` on cells and on L,H for every vector read.

**m4. F1 is stricter than declared, and e is not computed from the loops' POT.** `fd_rpd_inputs.py:318-331, 333-340`.
- F1 also compares `errors` and `edges` of each period flux file to 1e-12; part 1: "bin by bin ... with its POTs" (contents). 5B's committed period files' errors were built by a different script if their provenance differs; a FAIL here is a stop for a reason outside the declared F1. I could not check (no ROOT locally, flux files not in my read scope).
- Part 1 §5: e "is computed at stage S from the staged loops' data POT and the staged Φ_X". Code uses the baseline `runEventLoopData_X.root` `POTUsed` (`per_pl[pl]["pot_data"]`). `check` later ties the loop's `dataPOTUsed` to the flux file's `DataPOTUsedForFluxWeighting` at 1e-12, so e is equal to 1e-12 if C-stage passes; but e is frozen (F1.json) and used by stage D before job A runs, i.e. before that tie is tested.
- Requirement: say which; or read the loops' POT at stage S (a 1 s metadata read of the staged copies).

**m5. Gate b, gate a: weaker than "on this deploy's records".** `fd_rpd.py:813-837`.
- Gate b checks controls.json verdict, `fd_rpd.py` sha and the receipts' digests; it does not re-check F1 (verdict, code sha), the N0 receipts (hashed by controls but not re-hashed at the gate), or that `controls.json` was made *after* the receipts. Gate a checks existence and schema of stage0/stageD only (not their code sha, branch, or that stage D's rules were not run on a different F1). In a fresh `$R` with one deploy these are not exploitable; they are weaker than part 1's wording and than 5B's gate pattern.

**m6. Stage D's time budget is measured from the reachability loop, not the stage; the verdict test uses only T1's n.** `fd_rpd.py:522-571, 592`.
- The 2 h bound is on "each run"; `t0` starts after Grid, `common`, and both 20,000-draw OC tables. Overshoot ≲ one item. Also if the budget stops the loop, T2 and T3 have fewer outer datasets than T1 (outer-major, truth-minor); `ec_verdict` only checks T1's n ≥ 200 which is the declared rule; the probabilities for T2/T3 are printed with their own n. Fine; noting.
- My timing: one 9,240-partition sweep is 2.5 ms on this (loaded) laptop; 600,000 sweeps ≈ 25 min + Python overhead, well inside 2 h. Readout: 12,000 sweeps ≈ 30 s.

**m7. Outputs of the inputs driver do not go through the LeakGuard.** `fd_rpd_inputs.py` `write_json` for STAGED.json, F1.json, check receipts, N0 receipts.
- Part 1: "every output passes F0's LeakGuard". F1.json holds flux contents and POTs only, and the receipts hold digests and POTs; no unfold-derived number is in any of them, so nothing leaks. But the letter is not met and the truth npz (xsec_flat ~1e-39) sits unguarded under `$R`. Same as 5B, and the readers' JSONs are guarded. Declare it or ignore it.

**m8. `controls`' guard and stage D's guard are built with an empty integral list.** `fd_rpd.py:603, 810`.
- `cp.guarded(S["f"], [])` guards against f only (and f ratios), not against any X. Stage D and the controls write no X-derived numbers, so this is correct, but it means a later edit that adds one would not be refused. Same pattern in 5B? (`ct` readers). Informational.

## Correctness checks done (no defect unless listed above)

- **Grid / regions:** `wL`,`wH` are volume-weighted indicator vectors over fine cells that map to a reporting cell with `lo ≥ edge_lo, hi ≤ edge_hi`; fine pz grid has edges at 1.5, 8 and 15 (asserted at construction). Fine shape (14,16,7,7,6) = 65,856 cells; 500 vectors are ~0.3 GB: login memory fine.
- **Permutations:** `partitions((6,3,3))` = 9,240; `partitions((5,3,2,2))` = 83,160, all unique; observed π is found exactly once (`find`).
- **Stage 0 / OC:** `σ_u² = σ₀²/(1/E_PB+1/E_PA)` reproduces σ₀ (Var of an exposure-weighted mean of u ~ σ_u²/e is σ_u²/E). Rows of the linear map are right for t_X (leave-one-out), t̄, h_X.
- **Replica-noise resampling:** `dev = (v/mean − 1) × √(30/29)`, one replica per playlist per dataset at random, plus Gaussian T term from `Σw²`-based relative variance per cell.
