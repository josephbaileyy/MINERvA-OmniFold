## Question A: can a result be chosen because it looks better?

**A1. The flag at 2.3 has an operand that is not pinned to the ruling, and all signed Δ are printed.** Severity: should fix before binding.
- Ruling 4 (`BINDING.md:56-58`) says "would change the reported covariance's χ² by more than the GiBUU rise already disclosed (2.3)".
- The only disclosed 2.3 is step A's GiBUU rise of +2.29 (`FD-GENCMP-REPORT.md:66`, "47.55 … **+2.29**"). That is the with arm, total χ², generator definitions, 8 cells.
- The code flags on the maximum |Δ| over four predictions, two arms and two kinds (`fd_gencmp_e2.py:248-253`, `addendum:87-88`). The nopl arm, shape-only χ², and GENIE+MEC (χ² of about 229) can therefore trigger it.
- The report prints every signed Δ, so the director could still read the ruling as "with arm, total only, GiBUU only" after seeing the numbers. That is an open choice.
- Fix: state in the addendum which reading the flag takes, and that no other reading applies.
- Related minor point: 2.3 is the rounded 2.29, and the same record also shows 47.78 − 45.25 = 2.53 for the analysis-definitions corner. Say which "rise" is meant.

**A2. A stop has no stated rule for a rerun or repair.** Severity: should fix.
- `addendum:79,97` say a failed control means no E2 number is used and the caveat stays. Nothing says whether E2 may be rerun, re-staged or repaired after a stop.
- The "any control fails" row lists only (V, R1, R2, C1, C2, C3). It omits the stage, pin and product-digest checks (the S, U and C0 labels in the code). It also omits a job failure, timeout, a step that exits nonzero, or a numpy-version difference that fails C1's bitwise test.
- A repair round after seeing which control failed is how a result gets selected. Fix: add one row. "Any other stop, or a stopped run: no E2 number, no rerun without a new binding."
- Δ itself is never printed before the C-controls pass (`e2.json` is written after C1 to C3), so no Δ is seen on a stop.

**A3. "The four predictions" are not named, and `mnvtune_v1` is excluded without saying so.** Severity: minor.
- `chi2_rows` drops `mnvtune_v1` by name (`fd_gencmp_e2.py:194-195`). The other four are `genie_cv`, `genie_mec`, `nuwro` and `gibuu`. The addendum says only "four".
- `mnvtune_v1` is the prediction with the largest χ² and its χ² also moves with C_model. Excluding it follows declaration §8's "four corner predictions", but the addendum should say so, since the replacement sentence reads as general.

**A4. The 2.3 threshold is uninformative for large-χ² predictions.** Severity: minor, not a selection issue.
- E-1 shows GENIE+MEC at about 229 and 278. A shift of about 1% moves it by more than 2.3 in absolute χ².
- So the flag fires almost regardless of result. That is fixed in advance, so it is not result-dependent, but it means the flag does not discriminate.

**A5. Nothing in the addendum depends on the sign of Δ in an unfixed way.** Δ = χ²_CH − χ²_carbon, and Δ_max uses the absolute value. The only sign-dependent item is the optional "rise" wording in A1.

## Question B: controls and computations

**B1. R2 and the other run-time controls do not gate downstream steps.** Severity: should fix before binding. This is close to blocking, given `CLAUDE.md`'s "enforce rules in code".
- `cmd_unfold` writes `unfold.json` with `R2_bitwise` before calling `report("R2", …)` (`fd_gencmp_e2.py:167-169`).
- `cmd_chi2` reads `unfold.json` (line 218) and never checks `rec_u["R2_bitwise"]` (lines 217-223). It also checks no receipt that V or R1 passed.
- The job script (`x_e2_unfold.sh:30-43`) does not require R1 to have passed. `sbatch_pvl.sh:33-35` gates later jobs on a `gates/*.ok` file; this script has no equivalent.
- If R2 fails, `chi2` still produces `e2.json` with Δ values, with a failed R2 only recorded inside it. That contradicts `addendum:79`, "Any failed control stops E2".
- Fix: `chi2` should refuse unless `R2_bitwise` is true, and V and R1 should write pass receipts that the job and `chi2` require.

**B2. The CH unfold products' provenance and prior guards are never checked.** Severity: should fix before binding.
- The committed vertex products pass `pvl_analyze.check_meta` in `build_cmodel5d.load_products` (`build_cmodel5d.py:126`). That check covers the refinement ran at R's settings, the completeness and r-spread guards (≤ GUARD_TOL), same code and estimator params, ratio sha, seed, threads and iterations.
- `fd_gencmp_e2.py` never calls it. It records only the sha256 of the three products (line 163) and R2's `xsec_flat` equality for the carbon rerun.
- The two CH products could fail a prior guard, run without the refinement, or have used the wrong ratio file, and E2 would use them. Only R2 constrains the NuWro-carbon run.
- The addendum's "same estimator settings, seed and threads" (`addendum:48`) is thus unverified for the CH runs.
- Fix: run `check_meta` on all three products against synthetic task entries. Check each product's `ratio_sha256` against the `$R/prep` file for its task.

**B3. GiBUU's domain has no run-time control.** Severity: should fix.
- "GiBUU's p_∥ < 10 GeV/c domain … unchanged" (`addendum:46`) is checked only by a unit test of `config_ch` copying `pz_max` (`test_fd_gencmp_e2.py:77`).
- R1 compares only `tune_v1`, `genie_mec` and `genie_mec_sq`. These share the reference and gate with the CH vertices, so they catch common-path changes. They never exercise the GiBUU truth-cut path (`pvl_ratios.py:119,129-130`).
- R2 covers only NuWro.
- `cmd_ratios` does not even assert `truth_cut == {"axis":"pz","below":10.0}` on `gibuu.json`, or its absence on `nuwro.json`.
- Fix: assert the truth cut on the CH ratio files. Better, also re-derive the carbon GiBUU ratio with the same machinery and compare it to the committed `gibuu.json` (52438000…, pinned in `prep-SHA256SUMS` but not staged).

**B4. Controls that can fail, and the direction each was tested in.**
- **V:** implemented (`fd_gencmp_e2.py:89-94`), can fail, and tested both ways (`test_fd_gencmp_e2.py:47-56`). It is a strong control for the carbon arrays, the grid, and CH = combine(C, H). It does not pin step A's bytes; see S1.
- **R1:** implemented (lines 139-149) and can fail. The comparison function is tested (`test_fd_gencmp_e2.py:84-88`), but `cmd_ratios` itself is not. It uses 1e-12 relative tolerance, not "equal" (see S3). It cannot detect GiBUU-specific defects (B3).
- **R2:** implemented (lines 165-169), `np.array_equal`, so it can fail. No test, and not gating (B1). It covers only the NuWro path.
- **C1:** implemented (line 216), bitwise on S, can fail. It could fail spuriously on a different numpy build, and A2 says nothing about what then. No test.
- **C2:** implemented (lines 230-231), can fail. No test.
- **C3:** implemented (lines 241-245) over both arms and both kinds. It can fail on a wrong `C_rest`, and the nopl arm's C_resp is also checked. No test.
- Only V, the config repointing, `compare_numbers`, S's arithmetic, the χ² rows and the pins are tested. `cmd_vertices`, `cmd_ratios`, `cmd_unfold` and `cmd_chi2` have no tests, as `addendum:135` admits.
- The `Chi2` tests (lines 105-128) cover one hand-built C_rest. They do not test the two arms' C_rest assembly. C3 is the only check of that, and it runs only at run time.

**B5. The CH vertex changes only the target.**
- `config_ch` repoints paths only and copies each vertex dict (`fd_gencmp_e2.py:100-113`). It keeps the reference, `pz_max` and `stat_max`, and replaces only `path`, `sha256` and `label`.
- The CH file carries the carbon file's edges and replaces only `xsec_flat` and `sumw2_flat` (lines 95-96). It is read by `pvl_ratios.load_gen5d`.
- `pvl_ratios`' `checked()` verifies the file against `sha256(p)`, which is computed at write time (`fd_gencmp_e2.py:129`).
- Seed, threads and iterations match the PV ladder in the job. This part is sound, subject to B2 and B3.

**B6. The job script launches like the PV ladder, with these differences.** Severity: should fix, then minor.
- Same `srun -n1 -c 42 --mem=… --exact --cpu-bind=cores`, `mnv_guarded_run`, flags, 95%/3 memory split and environment. R2 can hold.
- **No `-o` or `-D`:** `x_e2_unfold.sh` has no `#SBATCH -o`. Slurm writes `slurm-<jobid>.out` into the submission directory, which can be outside `$R`. This conflicts with `addendum:107`, "Writes go only under $R". The ladder passed `--output=<OUT>/logs/…` on the command line. Severity: should fix.

- A failed `srun` step only echoes (line 42). The `unfold` call then fails on a missing product. This is acceptable.

**B7. S_CH uses `build_cmodel5d`'s own arithmetic.**
- `factor()` (lines 176-181) is `BC.factor`'s computation using `BC.integrals`, `P.rel_shift` and `BC.VERTICES`.

**B8. The χ² recompute is right.**

- `C = C_rest + diag(f) SᵀS diag(f)` (lines 184-191), with g from the readout's `g` and V_g = (sigma_Vg_over_f · f)² on each prediction's cells, which is a faithful inversion of `fd_g_compare.compare` (lines 171-172).

## Verdicts

**A:** There is no choice left open on Δ's definition, the arm or kind set, or the sign. Three things are not closed: the flag's operand (A1), the rule after a stop (A2), and the unnamed exclusion of `mnvtune_v1` (A3). A1 and A2 should be fixed in the addendum before binding.

**B:** V, C1, C2, C3, S_CH and the χ² recompute are implemented correctly and can fail. R1 and R2 can fail, but R2 does not gate `chi2` (B1), the CH products skip `check_meta` and its prior guards (B2), and the GiBUU path has no run-time control (B3). The job also writes its stdout outside `$R` (B6), and S1 is a false claim in the addendum. B1, B2, B3, B6 and S1 should be fixed before binding. I found nothing that blocks binding on its own, but B1 comes close.
