## Question A: can an outcome be chosen because it looks better?

**A1. The paper wording is asymmetric: good outcomes get fixed sentences, bad ones get deferral. Should fix before binding.**
- Step C: "Q26 | some quadrant 'differ' | … No paper text until they rule" (DECL:352). Every agreeing outcome has fixed text (DECL:349-351). The existing sentence at `paper_body.tex:119` ("only the three-observable marginal has been compared") would stay, so a failed comparison is never disclosed on a pre-fixed schedule.
- Step D: "agree" adds a clause (DECL:344). "Disagree" says the "paper keeps step A's CH rows… before any text" (DECL:345) and nothing is fixed for disclosure.
- Q10: "B0 fails → keeps its present sentence" (DECL:347). Nothing says the failure is reported in the paper.
- Fix: for each bad branch, fix a minimum disclosure sentence now.

**A2. Step D's "agree" is not defined across its two routes. Should fix before binding.**
- DECL:303-304 makes the same comparison in generator definitions and in analysis definitions. DECL:344 says "step D agrees" with no combination rule.
- The analysis route is optional on the CLI (`GC:590`, `--analysis` is `store_true`). The only line printed is `[D] agree (generator definitions)` (`GC:565`).
- If one route agrees and the other does not, the clause could be added or withheld after seeing the numbers.

**A3. Partial or stopped samples have no §7 row. Should fix before binding.**
- DECL:346-347 are per generator ("B0 passes for a generator"). B0 is per sample (nuwro_C12 and nuwro_H separately; `GC:292`, `GC:326-329`). A generator with C12 passing and H failing has no stated outcome.
- Which corner goes to Table IV in that case is also unstated. `GC:327-329` forms a carbon-only readout when only C12 passes.
- A B2 STOP (DECL:197-198) and a seed-spread flag have no paper row for the stop case.

**A4. Step C's primary covariance is not designated. Should fix before binding.**
- DECL:231-232 computes σ₁₆ for the reported matrix and for the matrix without C_PL,5D. `GC:502-503` gives an outcome for each.
- §7 says "agree" without saying which matrix governs. The code prints only the reported one at `GC:526`.
- The no-C_PL variant is also paired with a Q that includes C_PL (`total_plus_C_PL`), so it is not a like-for-like pair.
- Fix: state that the reported matrix is primary and the other is printed only.

**A5. One-sided discretion in Q10 wording. Minor.**
- DECL:346: "If the change is below every cell's 0.1 σ, the sentence may say so." The wording is permitted only when the change is small.
- DECL:354 requires the report to give numbers, not the paper.

**A6. The "agree" tier gets easier as σ₁₆ is inflated. Minor, a power note.**
- The unreported boxes add ±100% in quadrature to σ₁₆ (`GC:487-488`), and the "agree" band is 2|σ₁₆ − σ_low|. QA and QB therefore pass the strict tier more readily because they carry more uncertainty.
- The label does disclose this; the point is that there is no stated power for step C.

**A7. Sensitivity rows are not locked out in every case. Minor.**
- A-H and the GiBUU alt weight are marked "never in its place" (DECL:144). The GiBUU M_n variant (`ana_mn`, DECL:174) has no such statement, though no promotion route exists.
- The GENIE-hydrogen sample is thin: MP3-B B4 was not met, with 21 cells above 1/3 and a worst case of 1.41 (`phase1/mp3b/REPORT.md:148`). The A-H "spread" is largely that sample's own noise, and the declaration does not say so.

I found no open threshold, seed, card or variant choice beyond those above. Seeds (DECL:178), the k1 card, the frozen GiBUU weight, the 0.2σ flag, step D's 32.0 and 3.5, and C3 are all fixed.
