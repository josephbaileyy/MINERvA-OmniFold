Neutrino-Electron Elastic Scattering Special Sample
Neutrinos elastically scattering on atomic electrons is a standard candle process. MINERvA has good statistics and uses this as an important flux constraint.  Because it’s such a small cross section, we make a high statistics special sample.  The higher statistics support robust unfolding, migration matrix, and efficiency corrections.
This Monte Carlo sample is roughly 300 times the predicted data statistics.
