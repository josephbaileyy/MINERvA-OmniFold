1. **Truth-rate / high-$E_\nu$ flux discrepancy ($E_\nu \ge 45\text{–}50\text{ GeV}$) + unfolding regularization/binning at 40–60 (Most plausible):** Explains the pre-unfolding `13,283` vs `15,185` Tune v1 gap via flux (`flux.npz` bridges ~1,456+), the `K=4` vs `K=10` `40–60` fold behavior, the `E_nu_vis >= 50` turn-on even at `pz 20–40` (where `true >= 60` is zero), the `40–50` vs `50–60` tilt, the `40–60` `Eavail >= 5` excess, and the `good`-curvature dominance at `>=60`.
