# Conditional-Information Continuation: Pre-registration Amendment

I am operating as the durable `evidence_ablation_auditor` for session `4be5058b-7e1a-49f2-a102-04fe530e5f3a`. This amendment addresses the scientific-design gap in the initial synthetic matrix by defining bounded conditional stress fixtures.

## 1. Challenge: Independent Fixtures vs. Shared Factorial Fixture

**Decision:** **Five independent fixtures** are scientifically cleaner. 
*Reasoning:* A shared factorial fixture (injecting all five conditional signals simultaneously) risks capacity interference, where the model prioritizes the easiest-to-learn signal and ignores the others. It also confounds the gradients, making it impossible to confidently attribute an optimization failure to a specific feature representation. Independent fixtures guarantee that if the enriched arm fails to recover the ratio, it is strictly an optimization/architecture failure for that specific representation, not interference from a stronger confounding signal.

## 2. Calibration and Sample Size

*   **Minimum Sample Size:** To lift the signal cleanly out of the 100k-event noise floor observed in the prior campaign, this continuation requires either a **2M-event sample** or a deliberately amplified injection strength. 
*   **Calibration Smoke:** A single-seed calibration smoke test on 100k events must be run *only on the exact analytic formulas* to set the injection strength parameter ($A$) for each fixture. The criteria for freezing $A$ are:
    1. The true injected log-ratio RMSE must be at least 0.15 (to ensure it is measurable).
    2. The maximum injected physical ratio must remain $\leq 10.0$ and nonnegative.
    3. The calibration step must NOT train the models; it only sets the fixture weights. The constants are then frozen and committed before any training job is launched.

## 3. The Five Conditional Stress Fixtures

For all fixtures, the baseline evaluation population, common rows, split (0.7/0.15/0.15), estimator seeds (101, 202, 303), truth-frozen Step-2 representation, and optimizer budgets are strictly matched to the existing campaign.

*   **Injection Form:** $w_{\text{reco}} \times \exp(A \cdot z(\text{token\_energy}) \cdot \mathbb{I}(\text{view} == 2))$.
*   **Conditional Independence:** C lacks the view ID and cannot distinguish view-2 energy from view-1/3 energy. 
*   **Negative Control:** Arm C trained on this target must fail to recover the view-conditional shift, bounded by its ability to guess based on random chance.

### Fixture 2: Reconstructed Object Type
*   **Pair:** C (generic tokens) vs. D-typed (real object types).

### Fixture 3: Rich Globals
*   **Pair:** E-muon (muon globals only) vs. E-rich (audited rich globals).

### Fixture 4: Distinguished Muon Representation

*   **Conditional Independence:** E-muon has the muon globals but lacks the explicit spatial coordinates embedded *within the token cloud* to compute $\Delta R$ easily through attention. 
*   **Negative Control:** E-muon trained on this target.

### Fixture 5: Pre-Truncation Overflow Aggregate
*   **Pair:** C (truncated at $N$ tokens) vs. Overflow (truncated at $N-1$ tokens + 1 overflow aggregate token).
*   **Injection Form:** $w_{\text{reco}} \times \exp(A \cdot z(\text{overflow\_energy}))$.
*   **Conditional Independence:** Arm C discards the overflow energy. It can approximate it by subtracting the sum of retained tokens from the total calorimetric energy, but if total calorimetric energy is withheld from globals, C cannot reconstruct the exact lost energy.
*   **Negative Control:** Arm C trained on this target.

## 4. Mechanical Demonstration of Conditional Independence

For each fixture, **row-wise permutation** must be used to prove non-reconstructibility by the parent. 
1. Group rows that have identical direct-parent features (or bin them infinitesimally).
2. Permute the enriched feature (e.g., `view_id`) among the grouped rows.
3. The variance of the injected ratio within the group proves it is conditionally independent of the parent features.

## 5. Metrics, Pass/Fail Rules, and Gates

*   **Primary Metric:** Improvement in log-ratio RMSE recovery.
*   **Gate 1 (Representational Capacity vs. Optimization Failure):** The enriched arm must improve the log-ratio RMSE over the parent by at least **15%** (a threshold chosen to sit decisively above the noise floor). If the enriched arm fails this gate despite possessing the necessary feature, it is ruled an **optimization or architectural failure**.
*   **Gate 2 (Safety):** The enriched arm must not degrade global or tail ESS by $>10\%$ and must not increase extreme-tail logit-cap saturation.
*   **Pass/Fail:** To pass, an enriched arm must meet Gate 1 and Gate 2 consistently across all 3 estimator seeds. We do not select the "easiest" fixture post-hoc; each feature family is graded strictly on its own dedicated fixture.

## 6. Evidence Limitations

*   **Label:** `synthetic-fixture`.
*   **Limitation:** Passing a synthetic conditional stress test proves only that the PET2 architecture *has the mathematical capacity* to route and utilize the feature. It **does not** prove that this conditional information exists in real MINERvA G2 data, nor does it guarantee a physics benefit. It merely authorizes the feature for the final G2 evaluation.
