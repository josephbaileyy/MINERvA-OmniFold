## 1. What is decided

1. **Outcome (2) accepted.** The universal-bound approach is discontinued for this campaign. No
   further universal bound on projected objects is to be pursued unless direct checks reveal a
   specific need.
2. **Replacement scope authorized: design and independent review only.** A replacement criteria
   proposal based on direct checks of a fixed, declared set of publication consumers is authorized.
   This does not authorize adoption of the criteria, nor an amendment by implication.
3. **Two endpoints, kept separate.**
   - **A — covariance and projected-uncertainty release:** construction, provenance,
     numerical-reproducibility, and estimator-sensitivity requirements; `cause3_corr`'s remaining
     role is to be identified — retained with a justified criterion, or replaced with an explicit
     criterion of equivalent scientific protection.
   - **B — generator significances:** actual comparisons, inversion procedure, reference-distribution
     justification, sensitivity assessment.
4. **Threshold `T` is not universal.** Do not assume every claim needs it. If `T` is a significance
   threshold, name exactly which proposed claim requires it, and recommend either a justified
   threshold with the statement it supports, or descriptive comparisons without a threshold-based
   claim. `T` is not to be selected from observed results to obtain acceptance. Any other endpoint-A
   tolerance must be named and justified separately.
5. **Deliverable:** one concise, independently reviewed replacement decision packet. The rejected
   approach and its limitations are preserved as history; its tooling is not to be further extended.

No production compute, grading, gate movement, or publication change is authorized by the ruling
itself.
