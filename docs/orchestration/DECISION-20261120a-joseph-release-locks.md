## What is decided

1. **The 19g sentence may print in as many sentences as needed.** DECISION-20261119g's scope is unchanged: the descriptive fixed-IBU comparison, its four values, its placement, and everything it does not lift. Only the one-sentence count is released.
2. **The Training details paragraph may be split.** Its verbatim hold is released for sentence splitting under the meaning rules: no claim, number or qualifier changes, and two readers. Adding RDX's outcome wording is not decided here.
3. **Wording locks are released by default.** Verbatim holds and sentence-count limits on the note's text are released. Content stays protected by the meaning rules and the limitations register.
4. **Readers' suggestions are accommodated.** When Grok or codex-school2 flags something, the default is to apply the fix, or to make the text state the thing explicitly, and to rerun until both pass. A finding is recorded as "no change" only if the fix would break a decision, a register quote or a value. One exception stands: the stability test's timing qualifier stays out of the abstract, as Joseph chose directly; a body pointer is fine.
5. **Qualifiers may move to the records document.** A qualifier or limitation's full statement may move verbatim to the records document, with a pointer in the note. Each reported result keeps its headline limits in the note, one short sentence each (tab:validity).
6. **Editorial rules are guidance until a satisfactory paper exists.** This covers the floor rule, word budgets as caps, verbatim and sentence-count locks, and reader verdicts as merge gates. Readers still run, and their suggestions are accommodated. Re-adding hard rules is a later decision, Joseph's.
7. **Kept as error checks, not editorial rules:**
   - build and LaTeX errors;
   - values matching their receipts (make_values, the numbers diff), with moved values printing in the records;
   - no new claims, since claims stay Joseph's;
   - every register limitation located in the note, or in the records with a pointer;
   - no undefined references.
