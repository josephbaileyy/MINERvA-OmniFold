# DECISION 2026-10-25 — Joseph: caveats in the paper follow PRD practice

**Recorded by** the paper-tighten lane, as director.

**What led to it.**
- `DECISION-20261025-joseph-r18-history-to-note.md` set r18's brief for the passages the raters quoted. The director
  then described to Joseph how PRD papers present caveats:
  - the final procedure, in the present tense;
  - validation of the final method only;
  - superseded versions left to a companion document;
  - deviations from a predeclared procedure stated briefly, with their consequence;
  - each caveat stated once, quantified and positive-first where the facts allow.

Joseph's question, verbatim:

> can we handle caveats the same way PRD papers do?

sha256 of the text plus a final newline begins `2ee72b8f25bc8032`.

The director answered yes, within the limit in item 3. This record takes the question as Joseph's instruction to
adopt the convention.

## What is decided

1. **From r18 on, every caveat in the methods paper is sorted into one of five kinds:**

   | kind | the PRD treatment |
   |---|---|
   | (a) the history of superseded work | it leaves the paper. It prints in the note, and the internal records keep the trail |
   | (b) a deviation from a predeclared procedure | one sentence where the result is reported: what changed, and its consequence. The mechanics go to the note |
   | (c) an untested property or an effect that is not included | it is stated once, in Sec. VIII A or the systematics discussion, as a plain declarative and quantified where a number exists |
   | (d) scope | it is stated once, in the abstract and Sec. I (R1) |
   | (e) process vocabulary | it is removed: development history, configuration bookkeeping, "later", "rebuilt", "post hoc" except in (b) |

   - **Apply it to the note in its own rounds,** as the fully qualified companion. The note keeps (a) and the
     mechanics of (b).
2. **The guardrails are unchanged:**
   - a qualifier stays where leaving it out would make a printed sentence false as read;
   - no number or claim changes, and no fact leaves both documents;
   - agy agrees on every printed passage;
   - the blind pairwise comparison and P1 decide adoption.
3. **The limit.** PRD papers often turn a caveat into an assigned uncertainty, such as a non-closure systematic. Here
   that would change numbers and claims, so the register rounds do not do it.
   - For example, the paper states that the residual regularization bias is in no quoted uncertainty.
   - The paper rewrite lane may list caveats where that conversion would be possible, as options for Joseph. Each
     would need its own decision, and compute where a measurement is missing.
