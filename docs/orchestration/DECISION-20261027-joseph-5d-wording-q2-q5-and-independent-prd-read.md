## What is decided

1. **Q2.** The 5D χ² table prints χ² with ndf and shape-only, and no p-values. The four "no covariance-based
   significance" sentences are scoped to the configuration-(a) covariance, with a pointer to the sixteen-cell comparison.
2. **Q5.** The passing truths are described by the criteria: "At the other 9 truths the bias stays within the reported
   uncertainty by both criteria: no cell beyond 2σ and p ≥ 0.01." The criteria's wording is checked against FD-PLAN and
   the F0b report, and agy agrees.
3. **Q3, Q1, Q4, Q7 and Q9 wait for an independent PRD-language read.** The paper rewrite lane runs it, before Joseph
   answers.
   - **The readers** are independent raters on allowed accounts, the lane's calibrated P1/P2 raters. They see the
     printed text of the paper's new 5D subsections and its changed sentences, and no internal record or
     recommendation.
   - **Q3:** a blind pairwise comparison of the three sensitivity variants, in both orders, and each reader's judgement
     of which reads as PRD.
   - **The qualifications Joseph noticed:** for each qualifying clause of the new 5D text, the readers say whether
     comparable PRD papers would state it, state it more briefly, or leave it to a companion document. This covers Q1's
     "unscaled and not a calibrated bound", Q7's coverage sentence and Q9's envelope sentence. Q4, integer χ² in the
     paper, is asked alongside.
   - **The guardrail stands.** A qualifier stays where leaving it out would make a printed sentence false as read. For
     each qualifier the readers would cut, the lane states whether cutting it leaves the sentence true.
   - The result comes to Joseph with the remaining questions (Q6 and Q8).
