## 2026-08-09 — Scope history

**SCOPE — FIRST-CLASS, AND THE LARGEST LEVER BY 8x (promoted out of the caveat list 2026-08-09).** Until 2026-08-09 the *weighting* choice carried the 'load-bearing' label (caveat (iv)) while the far bigger *scope* choice sat as caveat (iv-d). That was backwards: scope moves the ceiling **0.190187** (`0.808415 -> 0.618228`), weighting moves it **0.023733** (`0.633208 -> 0.609475`), and the injection — the only genuinely free parameter — only **±0.02**. **THE ARGUMENT, not just the pin:** the criterion scores `recovery = 1 - Σ_b|unfolded_b - target_b| / Σ_b|prior_b - target_b|`, a ratio of L1 sums over cells. If cell *b*'s displacement `d_b` is recoverable only to `r_b = φ(a_b) = 1-(1-a_b)^k`, then achievable recovery is `Σ_b r_b d_b / Σ_b d_b = E_d[φ(a)]` — a **displacement-weighted mean of per-cell dilutions**. The retired bar used `φ(E[a])`, the dilution of an *aggregate* acceptance: a different functional of the same map. The criterion's own structure selects the first, and note it settles the weighting question too — the denominator **is** `Σ_b d_b`, so displacement-weighting (0.633208) is correct and truth-mass (0.609475) is the wrong weight for this criterion rather than an alternative. **And the direction is FORCED, not incidental:** `φ''(a) = -k(k-1)(1-a)^(k-2) < 0` on (0,1) for k>1, so Jensen gives `E[φ(a)] ≤ φ(E[a])` for **every** acceptance map — the scalar scope must overstate, with equality **iff acceptance is uniform across cells**. MINERvA's is strongly non-uniform (~0.05–0.80 across the 285 cells), so the scalar reading's error *is* the acceptance spread. Verified: φ''<0 over 2e4 samples; a two-cell demo at fixed mean opens 0.086 at ±0.22 spread and 0.241 at ±0.37. So the scope choice is **correct, not merely stable** — `test_scope_error_is_signed_by_concavity` asserts the concavity and the Jensen ordering rather than pinning a string, which is the only version that survives a referee.

## 2026-08-10 — Residual (vii)

**(vii) THE CRITERION IS SELF-ADJUSTING IN k — ONE DIRECTION ONLY. Added 2026-08-10 on Joseph's instruction, because it is the objection someone will raise about re-opening `niter` after adopting a k-dependent ceiling.** The bar is `f × ceiling(k)`, and `ceiling(k)` is **monotone increasing** in k (`φ(a) = 1-(1-a)^k` rises with k for `a ∈ (0,1)`): per-event `0.4236 / 0.5642 / 0.618228 / 0.6441 / 0.6592 / 0.6691` at `k = 1..6`, so the bar is `0.338880 / 0.451360 / 0.494582 / 0.515280 / 0.527360 / 0.535280`. **Therefore NOBODY CAN BUY A PASS BY ADDING ITERATIONS.** Precisely: `margin(k) = measured(k) − f·ceiling(k)` improves only if the estimator captures **more than fraction f** of the headroom each additional iteration opens. `k=3 → 4` opens `+0.025872` of ceiling and the bar takes `+0.020698` of it, so the estimator must gain more than `0.020698` in recovery merely to HOLD its margin. That is the protective property and it is real. **BUT THE PROTECTION IS ONE-DIRECTIONAL, AND THE OPEN DIRECTION IS THE LIVE ONE.** Lowering k *lowers* the bar: `k=3 → 1` drops it from `0.494582` to `0.338880`, a fall of `0.155702`. And *fewer* iterations is exactly what the 2026-08-09 step-1 trajectory favours on normalization — fold-forward `push dev` is `−2.79%` at `k=1` against `−34.46%` at `k=3` (`STEP1_TRAJECTORY.slurm-56525829.json`). So **"buy a D2 pass by REMOVING iterations" is NOT blocked by this property** and needs its own guard. **Adoption condition (e), added here and EXTENDED 2026-08-10 by Joseph:** if `niter` is ever lowered, the D2 verdict must be re-stated at the new k *and* the shape/normalization trade-off measured. **But re-statement alone is insufficient, because a relative criterion cannot adjudicate a choice of k** — lowering k lowers the bar, so a lower-k configuration can post a BETTER relative margin at a WORSE absolute recovery. **So CLM-012 must report the ABSOLUTE recovery and the CEILING alongside the relative margin, at whatever k is in force, always and in the same place.** Then a lower-k pass cannot be read as an improvement: the absolute number and the shrunken headroom are in plain sight while the relative one rises. That is what stops the gaming, not the re-statement. Enforced by `powered:absolute_recovery_reported_with_ceiling`. A lower-k configuration that passes a lower bar is not evidence of a better estimator. **THE TRADE-OFF, NOW IN BOTH CURRENCIES** — this converts the shape-recovery open item into a quantified choice: `k=3 → 1` moves the fold-forward deficit from **−34.46% to −2.79%**, buying **31.67 percentage points** of normalization, while the reference achievable recovery falls from `0.618228` to `0.4236`, forfeiting **0.194628** of shape headroom. **ONE CORRECTION to the framing, per this claim's own caveat (i):** the ceiling is a **REFERENCE curve, not a hard cap** — `(1-a_b)^k` assumes step 2 resolves cells independently, a smooth learner can transport across cells and beat it, and BEN-038 measured the top acceptance band at `E_w[r] = 1.0333`. So the 0.194628 is a fall in the REFERENCE, not a proven loss of attainable performance. **AND THE SHAPE LEG IS STILL ONE-SIDED:** the recovery actually achieved at `k=1` (or `k=2`) has never been measured, so what is quantified is 31.67 points of normalization against 0.194628 of reference headroom. Converting it into a decidable trade needs the low-k recovery MEASURED.
