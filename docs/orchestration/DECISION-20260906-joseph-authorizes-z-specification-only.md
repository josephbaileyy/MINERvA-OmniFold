## 2. THE RULING

### `RZ` — one complete scalar-5D successor Z may be specified; specification only

> **RULED.**
> **(i) Subject.** **Exactly one** complete scalar-5D successor **Z** may be named as a **prospective**
> grading subject for **all seven** quarantine causes and as a **possible** adoption subject. Both
> hedges are his and are load-bearing: naming a prospective subject is not grading it, and naming a
> possible adoption subject is not adopting it.
> **(ii) Cell discipline.** Z must have **seven distinct assessment cells**. **G's historical cells are
> preserved**, including `(cause 7, G)`'s permanent `OPEN` under `R1`. **Y's cause-7-only scope under
> `R2` is preserved.** Z's cells are new; they overwrite, reuse and retire nothing.
> **(iii) Combination rule.** Z's assessments **may form a self-contained tally**. They **must never be
> combined with G's or Y's grades** — not with each other's, not into the CAND/QUOTED counts, not into
> Gate 2, and not into any adoption case for G or Y.
> **(iv) Stage gate. Specification only.** This authorizes **no implementation, construction, compute,
> grading, adoption, or publication change**. Each of those requires its own committed authorization.
> **(v) Deliverable scope.** Authorized: a **scientific contract**, **cause dispositions**, **terminal
> criteria**, **dependency analysis**, and a **costed execution proposal**. **Any proposed criterion
> change must be identified explicitly**, and is reserved for Joseph's **separate** decision.
> **(vi) `R5` preserved.** `R5`'s **original accounting start**, **both ceilings** and **stop date**
> stand exactly as ruled. This record does not reopen, re-option, extend or re-baseline them.

**AUTHORIZES:** drafting the five deliverables in `(v)` for a single named Z, against the artifact
identities, the four-leg criteria and the committed evidence the deliverables cite.

**AUTHORIZES NOTHING TO BUILD OR RUN.** Z's output path, receipt schema and producing revision do not
exist; there is nothing to authorize the construction of, exactly as `R2(iv)` found for Y. `R5`'s
ceilings remain *"a prohibition and an accounting boundary … NOT authorization to spend up to"* them.

**LEAVES UNCHANGED:** `R1`–`R6` in full. G remains `RETAINED` and remains causes 1–6's grading subject.
`(cause 7, G)` remains permanently OPEN. Y remains cause-7-only, unconstructed, not a covariance
candidate, and not an adoption subject — `RZ` does **not** widen Y, and Z is **not** Y renamed. `R4`'s
suspension of the cause-3 seed scan stands: `RZ` is not `D-C3-VOI` and is not `D-C3-RUN`. Gate 6 stays
BLOCKED under its five prohibition keys. No `OI-*` closes.

**SUPERSEDES:** nothing. It answers a question `R2` left open by deciding it, rather than by reading
around `R2(i)`'s two explicit bounds.
